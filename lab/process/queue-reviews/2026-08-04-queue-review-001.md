# Queue review 001 — 2026-08-04

## Why this artifact exists

**queue**. The program's single largest measured efficiency loss was a queue
defect, not a wave defect: every individual wave D–J passed its own hostile
review while the whole stack sat on an undetermined Layer-0 real-form fork that
Wave K then flipped
(`lab/process/science-council-program-efficiency-2026-08-04.md:126-142`,
`:553-557`). This is the first artifact positioned to catch that class of loss.

**Covered window.** Fifteen wave dispositions, A → K77-B3, discovered by the
rule stated in `process_gates/queue_review_freshness_audit.py` and ordered as
the council orders them
(`lab/process/science-council-program-efficiency-2026-08-04.md:103-117`).

**Honesty note on independence.** This review is written by the same agent that
built its gate, on the same day, from a council's findings rather than from an
independent pass over the fifteen dispositions. It is therefore a *seeded*
queue review: its value is that the four questions are now on the record with
answers, not that the answers were independently derived. Queue review 002
should be adversarial to this one.
