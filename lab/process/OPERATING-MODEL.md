# GU Formalization Operating Model

GU work is selected from the current research portfolio and progressed under
the construction-fork and North-Star firewalls.

```text
frame -> choose construction and method -> formalize or compute ->
falsify or corroborate -> harden -> package or bank
```

The portfolio object remains authoritative. Formal proof, model-building,
collision testing, empirical prediction, and paper preparation are distinct
methods inside the applicable Lane, not interchangeable evidence and not
automatic new Lanes. Each result records the construction used, evidence
grade, live uncertainty, and the portfolio object or decision receiving it.

Lane A reranks the portfolio, protects the North Star from quick-payoff drift,
checks claim/evidence alignment, and keeps unresolved returns owned.
