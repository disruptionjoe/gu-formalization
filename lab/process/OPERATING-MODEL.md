# GU Formalization Operating Model

GU work is selected from the current research portfolio and progressed under
the construction-fork and North-Star firewalls. Purpose lives in Lanes 1, 2,
3 and A. The ratified functional-channel contract routes the method inside a
Lane through Build, Compose, Source or Verify:

```text
load purpose and current ledger -> type the object/source -> choose Build,
Compose, Source or Verify -> construct/cross-compose/inspect/attack ->
reconcile ledger and high-fanout premises -> package, bank or return
```

The portfolio object remains authoritative for ranked scientific work. The
conditional-physics ledger is authoritative for the compositional denominator,
typed mapping, row distance, residue and adverse-row escalation. Formal proof,
model-building, collision testing, empirical prediction, source reconstruction
and paper preparation are distinct methods inside the applicable Lane, not
interchangeable evidence and not automatic new Lanes. Each result records the
construction used, evidence grade, live uncertainty, receiving portfolio
object, functional channel and affected ledger rows.

Lane A reranks the portfolio, protects the North Star from quick-payoff drift,
checks claim/evidence alignment, and keeps unresolved returns owned.

Dispatch is condition-based, not percentage-based. Source/Compose leads when
the target or premise is uncertain; Build leads when the typed distance is
constructible; Compose runs after three material Build outputs or any immediate
high-fanout trigger; Verify attacks only changed/high-fanout claims or a named
integrity risk. A held GU owner claim permits useful disjoint read-only work,
not a competing write or an unchanged replay presented as progress.

silently absorbing the cosmological sector: Einstein tensor, matter
stress-energy, constant `Lambda g`, variable olive/`varpi`/VEV distortion and
observable dark energy remain separate until the source-first Layer-0 split is
completed and the append-only ledger is migrated.
