# Science council: blockbuster potential

**Calibration note, stated first because it governs everything below.** A
"blockbuster potential" question invites inflation, and this repository's
discipline is anti-inflation. Today three dispositions were proposed off one
correct fact and **all three were wrong** — the appetite for a big result is
exactly what generated them. Every ceiling below is paired with a blocker and a
frankly-guessed probability, and the guesses are not measurements.

---

## C-1 Program-target seat — the only true blockbuster

**GU's blockbuster is a source action. Nothing else qualifies for THIS
programme.** Every diagnostic result — signature, parity, reality type — is
instrumental to that. The repository has never had an action.

The Ward-closure result (2026-08-08, `32abf4ed`) is a real step: the physical
four-dimensional Ward graph closes with **zero defect in all three causal
classes**, and the previous rank-four defect was diagnosed as an artefact of
reusing a timelike-frozen operator under spacelike and null labels. The fourth
direction is longitudinal with zero spin-connection image, so nothing has to
manufacture it.

- **Ceiling: very high.** An action would change the programme's status
  categorically — from reconstruction to theory.
- **Blocker: four named gates remain** — `K_loc` formal adjoint, Green identity,
  action Euler/Noether, presymplectic classes. The artifact says so itself and
  does not claim the Noether identity.
- **Probability the full chain closes: unknown, and nobody should quote one.**
  What is knowable: this is the first step in the chain with zero defect.

## C-2 Generation-count seat — highest-ceiling diagnostic

**If the generation count is the index whose parity Kramers constrains, then the
horn decides whether three is reachable at all.** `(9,5)` quaternionic forces
even multiplicity; `(7,7)` real permits odd. That would convert a decades-old
"why three?" into a structural statement.

- **Ceiling: high.** It is the programme's central target.
- **Blocker, and it is severe:** the argument is **doubly conditional**, and
  `RA-D2` records that the index/zero-mode route survives only *as a type*, with
  **no GU-side realisation**. If there is no realisation, the parity argument has
  no object to constrain and is vacuous rather than false.
- **Probability: low-to-medium.** The 2026-08-08 hostile review named exactly
  this as its third ground for falsifying the MW resolver.

## C-3 External-value seat — the only result with life outside GU

The `{J,K}` dichotomy — an antilinear `J` commuting with the dynamics forces
K-balanced eigenspaces (hence an underdetermined C-operator) **iff it
anticommutes with the Krein form**, while `[J,K]=0` leaves signatures fully
informative — bears directly on Bateman–Turok (arXiv:2607.00096, July 2026) and
on pseudo-Hermitian QM generally.

- **Ceiling: modest.** A short, clean criterion, not a programme.
- **Blocker: novelty is UNCHECKED.** Bender's C-operator theory is decades old
  and the interaction of antilinear symmetries with Krein structure is precisely
  what that field studies. **Assume known until checked.**
- **Probability it is new: genuinely uncertain; the proposer's own guess is
  well under half.**

## C-4 Negative-result seat — blockbusters are not only positive

Two results today kill classes of future work in advance, which is worth more
per token than most positive findings:

1. **Signature-blindness.** `Cl(p,q) ⊗ ℂ` depends only on `p+q`, so **any**
   complexified computation is provably incapable of deciding the ambient fork.
   This explains `M-H9`'s failure retrospectively and forbids a whole category of
   future attempts.
2. **The base-sign exclusion.** Isometry group, maximal compact, Levi-Civita,
   geodesics, curvature and causal structure **provably cannot see** the base
   sign. Any resolver must reach the Clifford/spinor layer.

- **Ceiling: moderate but CERTAIN**, which is rare. These are theorems, not
  conditionals.
- **Value: highest immediate ROI on the board.** They redirect effort now.
