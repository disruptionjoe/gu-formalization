## Charge 1 — where the summary outran the artifact

The first draft called the repaired point a “full stationary action
background.” The certificate differentiates `B` and `T` over all 1,470
low-grade directions, but it does not differentiate the metric-dependent
Hodge, `Phi`, density and observation factors at fixed connections. That
wording was retracted. The surviving statement is: **all connection Euler
directions and raw Upsilon vanish; direct metric Euler remains open.**

The 14 nonzero entries at the old point are support, not fourteen independent
parameters or equations. They form one invariant trace covector. The report
now says that explicitly.

## Charge 2 — where rigor defended a superseded or mistyped object

The proposed 125-field Hessian was a mistyped target. Every one of those
directions is coefficient-grade two, while the action already owns a
nondegenerate 196-dimensional grade-one sector. The newly serialized moving
epsilon block has rank 91 and couples exclusively to that omitted sector.
Computing a beautifully exact 125-by-125 Hessian would therefore defend a
constrained slice whose missing mixed block is already known to be live.

Prior art makes this a composition failure, not a discovery that the repo had
no grade-one machinery. The action path-average versus `F_A` distinction was
recorded in v0.54, and the grade-one Hessian was closed in v0.36. The new work
is the exact application of both to the later v0.105 fixture.
