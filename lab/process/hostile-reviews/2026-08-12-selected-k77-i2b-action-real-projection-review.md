# Hostile review: I2B action-real projection

## Pre-review charge

The tempting summary was “the source action contains `P_+A`.”  The review
tried to kill it by separating the nonlinear residual, the real quadratic
action, its first variation and its section-level Euler/Green return.  It
refuted the nonlinear statement and retained a narrower exact theorem.

## Lens findings

- **Layer 0:** `A`, `P_+A`, `I_R(A)` and `dI_y A` are different objects.
- **Prior art:** v0.205 proves naturality and image membership, not variation.
- **Source criticism:** the source owns norm-square and adjoint grammar, not
  the repository `H_q` or projector.
- **Variational bicomplex:** the correct identity is a factorization of the
  first derivative at a fixed-real residual.
- **Clifford/Krein:** the tested form is exact and indefinite; no positive norm
  or fundamental symmetry is inferred.
- **Real-form geometry:** the involution makes fixed and anti-fixed sectors
  orthogonal for the real part of the bilinear pairing.
- **Principal-bundle geometry:** a fibrewise primalizer does not choose a
  global Spin lift, source `epsilon`, connection or curvature.
- **Symplectic geometry:** an Euler primalizer is not a presymplectic current,
  BFV charge or reduced phase space.
- **Analytic PDE:** the formal adjoint, Green identity, closed domain,
  positivity and spectrum remain open.
- **Contrary path:** the non-fixed residual control restores the anti-fixed
  sector and kills the global replacement.

## Three charges

### 1. Where does the summary outrun the artifact?

“The action derives the projected nonlinear residual” outruns the artifact.
The exact theorem is

```text
dI_y(A delta x)=B_R(P_+A delta x,y),  tau y=y.
```

It is an Euler-covector factorization at a specified stratum.  It is not the
identity `I(A)=I(P_+A)` and not the field equation `P_+A=0`.

### 2. Where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

Continuing to ask whether target image membership alone makes `P_+` physical
would rigorously solve the wrong layer.  The residual-square action reads the
pairing dual.  Conversely, treating the Euler factorization as a residual
replacement would discard the exact rank-195 anti-fixed action sector.

### 3. If this stands, what else must change?

- v0.205 pointwise naturality — **survives**.
- `P_+` wholly unowned — **dissolved only at the fixed-real Euler grade**.
- `P_+A` nonlinear source residual — **needs-recheck and remains unowned**;
  the generic replacement is refuted in the selected action.
- chosen Spin/source-`epsilon` lift and global real bundle — **needs-recheck**.
- moving `H_q`/Hodge/Shiab derivatives and Euler/preboundary —
  **needs-recheck**.
- RA-E1, RA-E3 and LT-SM6 — **distance/evidence only**.
- P1/P2/P3, residue, verdict counts, canon and public posture — **survive**.
- Dissolved canon rows — **none**.
