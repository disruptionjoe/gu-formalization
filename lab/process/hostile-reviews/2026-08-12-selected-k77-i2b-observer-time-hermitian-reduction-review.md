# Hostile review: observer-time Hermitian reduction

## Pre-wave

- No action-parent fork is assumed.
- The finite response test is decidable wholesale; global selection,
  basicness and analytic domain are not.
- Success exposes a possible future-unit observer field but does not license
  adopting it.
- Success kills automatic ownership by the vertical trace, not the coupled
  contact or observation-owned routes.

Required lenses: Layer-0 semantics, prior art, Clifford/Krein analytic,
principal-bundle geometry, variational and symplectic.

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

Saying that the **action owns** `H_u` outruns the certificate.  The probe shows
only that the induced adjoint pairing works on the finite live principal
response.  It does not derive `u` from the action, prove a global connection
preserves it, or build a Green/presymplectic form.  Also, the v0.214 phase-even
pairing and `Q_u` agree on this response up to an overall sign; they are not
proved equal on the entire algebra.

## Charge 2 — rigor defending a mistyped object

The phrase "moving fundamental symmetry" was too coarse.  `H_u` is an
indefinite Hermitian form of inertia `(64,64)`, not a positive Hilbert
fundamental symmetry.  More importantly, the geometry-owned vertical trace
and observed time vector are homonyms only at the level "non-null Clifford
vector".  The trace lives in the normal metric fibre; `u` lives in the
observed Lorentz tangent.  The exact rank `0` versus `4` result proves that the
typing difference is load-bearing.

## Charge 3 — downstream disposition

- **dissolved:** the claim that the canonical trace `q_g` itself completes the
  rank-four kinetic pairing; raw coordinate phase-even Frobenius as the only
  available rank-four candidate.
- **survives:** coupled contact/expanded parent; physical scalar descent;
  Euler/Green/preboundary construction; analytic domain and spectrum;
  distinction among two carrier halves, block subgroup, full parent and
  independent connections.
- **needs-recheck:** whether source epsilon/observation or the existing
  soldering reduction supplies `u`; whether the final action is basic under
  changes of observer; whether a three-function conditional observer field
  has positive constraint surplus.

## Controls and dissent

The spacelike-axis control gives a positive four-block form rather than a
Lorentz form.  The rational boosted observer changes fixed-frame coefficients,
blocking a false independence claim.  The `U(1,1)` plant preserves the
pseudo-Hermitian value and breaks the raw Frobenius value.

The strongest dissent is conceptual: a future-unit field may be ordinary
observer gauge and therefore should not be counted as datum.  The current
certificate cannot discriminate that claim because it proves joint-family
covariance only indirectly and does not prove basicness.  Accordingly the
three-function cost is conditional and unbooked.
