## Charge 1 — where does the summary outrun the artifact?

The strongest overreach would be “GU derives the Higgs potential.”  The probe
derives only the coefficient norm of one conditional algebraic eddy.  It does
not apply the selected moving Shiab/Hodge/Krein contraction, derive its sign or
normalization, supply a negative quadratic term, solve the Euler equation, or
compute a physical Hessian.  The report therefore uses **quartic carrier**, not
potential or vacuum.

The second overreach would be global uniqueness.  `c=1` is unique only inside
the smallest two-leg family `H theta_q + c JH theta_Jq`.  It does not classify
the complete 90-dimensional soldering kernel.

## Charge 2 — where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

V0.198's common-leg zero remains exact, but its prose treated all four real
Clifford coefficients as though they had the same fixed-unitary phase.  That
typing is superseded.  Three angular real coefficients fail maximally; their
`i` multiples pass.  The radial cell has the opposite phase.  Preserving the
old all-real phrase would turn a correct curvature-zero certificate into a
mistyped connection claim.

The review also rejects conflating the odd angular Higgs coefficients with the
even spin compensators that move `q`.  Both are indexed by the same three orbit
directions, but their Clifford grades and jobs differ.
