# Hostile review — K129

## Strongest objections

The repository already has a grade-one Schur analysis, so K129 could be a
duplicate. Conversely, the nondegenerate quadratic `T` norm could make `C`
invertible and render the remaining domain cautions ceremonial.

Both objections fail, but for different reasons. The earlier Schur packet is
the Hessian of a selected nonzero-`Phi1` stationary branch and cannot be
ported to the K127 `T=0` germ. At `T=0`, the correct block is
`C=kappa_1 K+E(D_B)`. Nondegeneracy of `K` proves pointwise zero-momentum
invertibility when `kappa_1` is nonzero; it does not prove a uniform inverse
for the covariant first-order operator, choose a boundary adjoint, or create a
common closed domain.

## Adversarial checks

1. Action-degree differentiation removes the cubic `T` term from `D2I1B|_0`.
2. The earlier nonzero-branch irreducible eigenvalues are not copied.
3. The held `d_BT` formal Euler block is reused at its exact ranks rather than
   rediscovered: cross ranks `12/12/11`, parity-completed ranks `24/24/22`.
4. Same-grade derivative zeros are typed as Shiab-parity selection; live raw
   images prevent an absent-map reading.
5. `A` retains the exact nonnull/null curvature-linearization ranks `6/6/4`
   and the Ricci-flat diffeomorphism radical.
6. K127's generic Weyl fixture still leaks outside the selected TT plane, so
   a two-polarization compression is not a closed subsystem.
7. For each fixed finite symbol the determinant is a nonzero polynomial in
   `kappa_1`, but its exceptional set can vary with covector and says nothing
   by itself about a global inverse.
8. `kappa_1=0` and nonzero `kappa_1` are kept as distinct forks; neither is
   selected by stationarity.
9. The formal Schur expression retains the operative adjoint, Green current,
   constraint, gauge, and domain burdens.
10. No characteristic kernel is promoted to a particle, physical graviton,
    quotient, spectrum, or positive cohomology.

## Verdict

Accept the T=0 operator classification and the exact causal adjacent-grade
rank packet. Reject transfer of the nonzero-branch Hessian, pointwise-to-global
inversion, a fitted `kappa_1`, silent pseudoinverse or quotient, and any BFV or
spectral promotion. K130 must build the complete covariant characteristic and
Green/domain packet before the coupled constraint complex can be reduced.
