## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

"Gauge invariant" would be too wide.  The certificate proves exact diagonal
Spin/frame naturality on the current finite response and a universal trace
identity on its generated Clifford support.  It does not prove invariance of
the full `U(64,64)` source action, basicness of a bundle functional, a global
section, or independence from the geometric observer.  Likewise, covering
all 256 current response pairings is not a spectrum or nonlinear field-space
theorem.

## Charge 2 — rigor defending a mistyped object

Two mistypings were available:

1. treating the boosted Hermitian form as an involution and writing
   `H A^dagger H` instead of `H^-1 A^dagger H`; and
2. treating an adapted Lorentz plane as the first four plus-sign axes rather
   than one plus and three minus axes.

Both would create convincing but false calculations.  The final probe uses
the `(0,7,8,9)` plane and the inverse-form adjoint.  A further typing fence is
still necessary: the finite observation projector is not the unbuilt complete
`epsilon_IG` flag.

## Controls and dissent

be collapsed into observer independence.

more structure than the coarse projector.  The review agrees, but that is the
open full-`epsilon_IG` horn, not evidence that the present projector selects a
line.  A second dissent is that an observer can be ordinary gauge; this remains
possible only if the selected action is shown basic or has the corresponding
Ward/BV reduction.
