# Hostile review: selected-K143 fixed-action subprincipal-owner obstruction

## Strongest overclaim attack

The absence of an owned subprincipal coefficient on K141's compact joint
family is not evidence that the physical subprincipal symbol vanishes. K143
correctly distinguishes three zeros: the frozen Schur equation on its
radical, K142's projected graph derivative, and a hypothetical lower amplitude
endomorphism. Only the first two exist, and neither is the third.

The result also does not invalidate finite-band elimination. For fixed
`kappa_1`, the annulus remains a legitimate compact frequency interval with a
uniform inverse. What fails is its promotion to a conic high-frequency family
of one fixed local action.

## Strongest contrary construction

One can define `kappa_1=mu |D|_aux` using a positive auxiliary norm and a
pseudodifferential quantization. That makes the joint family an operator and
may allow a subprincipal calculus. It does not contradict K143 because it adds
exactly the currently missing data: auxiliary geometry, quantization, adjoint,
action custody and an equivalence or replacement statement.

The Lorentz scalar `sqrt(|g^{-1}(xi,xi)|)` does not supply a counterexample;
it vanishes on the null cone and cannot realize `13<=|mu|<=14` there.

## Symbol-order and basicness attack

After `kappa_1=rho mu`, the mass contribution is `rho mu K`, homogeneous of
degree one. Calling `mu K` lower order after factoring out `rho` confuses a
normalized principal family with the subprincipal coefficient of the
unfactored operator. K143's order classification is exact.

The original zero-order `kappa_1 K` is action-owned on the distortion carrier,
but that does not make it an endomorphism of the separate five-class metric
quotient. K132's null-identity obstruction and K139's `106634`-dimensional DN
quotient block that shortcut. The planted good and bad lower maps show why
principal quotient data alone cannot decide radical/gauge basicness.

## Weakest reproducibility seam

The conclusion depends on treating `kappa_1` as the fixed scalar coupling
printed in the local action and `rho` as covector scale. If a new semiclassical
parameter is declared independently of covector dilation, its calculus must
be stated explicitly and K143 replayed there. No such declaration is present
in the current action or repository state.

The next fixed-action curved coefficient audit must not reuse K141's compact
annulus as a conic domain. Conversely, a future pseudodifferential owner must
not claim equivalence merely because its normalized frozen matrices match.

## Verdict

Pass at the stated ceiling. K143 proves an owner/type obstruction, preserves
the compact finite-band graph, and leaves both the fixed-action curved
coefficient route and an explicitly new pseudodifferential/gauge/boundary
route open at their honest assumptions.
