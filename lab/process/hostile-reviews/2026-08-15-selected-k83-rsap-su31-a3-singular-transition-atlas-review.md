# Hostile review

## Imported-configuration attack

Copying the nine `SU(2,2)` configurations would be false because five require
at least two negative directions. The probe rebuilds the signature census and
retains only real `3+1`, `2+1+1`, four real lines, and complex-plus-two-lines.

## False orbit-count attack

The four configurations and nine singular families are Jordan/eigenvalue
incidence types, not real adjoint orbits. Eigenvalues and sign characteristics
remain. Twenty-five admissible signed controls are tested explicitly.

## Moving-centralizer attack

Equal full centralizer dimensions do not license reuse of `(2,2)` moving
centralizers. The probe reconstructs `m_H` for each signature-`(3,1)` form and
recomputes every full and moving rank. All rows saturate the pointwise bound.

## Complex-boundary attack

The complex-plus-two-lines chart is not isolated. Exact paths cover imaginary
part collapse to semisimple `2+1+1`, real-line collision, and the combined
`2+2` boundary, all inside one fixed `(3,1)` fibre.

## Tautological-cocycle attack

Formal telescoping would be empty without one common factor. Explicit
determinant-one rational congruences normalize all four forms to
`diag(+,+,+,-)`. The induced moving maps are invertible; all six primitive
tests and all four base/cotangent triangles close.

## Cross-form and comparator attacks

No relative ambient embedding is supplied, so no cross-real-form transition
is inferred. Higgs, generation, chirality and other particle comparators do
not type this classical pseudo-Hermitian atlas and are not used.

## Verdict

Accept closure of the local `SU(3,1)/SO(3,1)` singular transition atlas at
canonical-configuration, sign-control and cotangent grade. Do not promote its
nine families to a real-orbit count or infer any cross-real-form edge. Proceed
within compact `SU(4)/SO(4)`.
