# Hostile review

## Strongest objections

1. **`I_selected` obviously means the sum of every named GU action.** The
   source and repository distinguish the first action, second residual square
   and observer `||II||^2` functional. Summing them is a new action unless a
   source/action owner selects the sum and its coefficients.

2. **The observed scalar is plainly the intrinsic radial field.** The
   intrinsic D3 artifact explicitly marks that identification uncertain. A
   typed tangent map and normalization must be written; a shared scalar label
   is not a map.

3. **Knowing `hh` and `vv` should determine a symmetric two-field cubic.** A
   symmetric first-order pencil contains two symmetric matrices, hence six
   coefficients. Two diagonal projections leave four dimensions.

4. **Ward covariance fixes the missing coefficients.** Ward identities
   constrain the complete action and its field map. They do not identify
   distinct action layers or supply unmeasured `h-v` response. The exact
   congruence completion is Ward-compatible as a coordinate change and still
   defeats uniqueness.

5. **A field redefinition is physically trivial and should be discarded.**
   Its purpose is not to propose new physics. It is a countermodel proving
   that the granted projections do not identify a spectrum.

6. **The full pointwise `u(64,64)` action bank already closes K118.** That bank
   is a field-direction covector at fixed pointwise geometry. K118 asks for a
   third pullback derivative on the observed `(theta,h,v)` legs, requiring the
   action layer and first/second field-map jets.

7. **At residual zero, the `I2B` Hessian determines every higher derivative.**
   It does not. Its cubic includes `D2Upsilon` and `DQ` terms. The Hessian owns
   only `DUpsilon^!QDUpsilon`.

8. **Proceed to the spectral connection and let it choose the completion.**
   That reverses ownership. Spectral convenience cannot select an action
   coefficient; the action-derived pencil must exist first.

## Verdict

Pass at exact owner-sufficiency and minimum-interface grade. K118 does not
construct the full moving cubic; it proves why the current inputs cannot do
so and names the smallest missing object. K119 must select one primitive
action layer and construct a stationary observed-to-native two-jet map before
another coefficient or spectral swing.
