polynomial belongs to a source-guided eddy-square rival, not yet a complete
published or action-selected formula. Its nonzero branch is conditional on a
co-moving background `rho E_q` with `rho<0`, and neither `rho` nor `J` is
selected.

orbit-flat directions and one positive radial direction, but the kinetic,
presymplectic, gauge-reduction and photon-kernel calculations remain open.

the construction is a moving reduction whose physical coordinates are one

physical action. It survives as a carrier diagnostic but is superseded as the
action coefficient by the exact moving-family Hodge calculation.
