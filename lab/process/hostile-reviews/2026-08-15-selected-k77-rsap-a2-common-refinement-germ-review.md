# Hostile review: adjacent-A2 common-refinement germ

## Strongest overclaim

An exact transition in regular Darboux--Casimir coordinates is not a global
algebraic transition between the homogeneous cotangent presentations. The
result is therefore explicitly limited to contractible regular germs. Global
sections, monodromy, discriminant loops and singular extension remain open.

## Strongest contrary construction or counterexample

Local primitive equality does not preclude a nontrivial global closed
one-form or section cocycle. The quadratic gauge control proves that ordinary
symmetric local section shifts are exact, but it does not classify global
transition functions. The next gate targets precisely that seam.

## Weakest reproducibility or propagation seam

The executable certificate verifies the complete frozen normal-form matrices,
including the 13-component target, but the existence of relative-Darboux
normalizations is theorem-level. The result text now separates those roles and
does not claim a finite group-coordinate construction.

## Triple challenge

The triple test is not reduced to commuting factor permutations. Its selected
pair maps do not commute, yet all three are defined through one universal
minimal realization, so the ordered Cech product is exactly the identity.
This closes only the regular germ; it gives no global atlas cocycle.

## Verdict

`PASS_WITH_GLOBAL_CEILING`. The type-missing pair object and first
noncommuting triple now construct locally with exact symplectic, moment and
primitive certificates. Globalization and singular extension remain the live
obstructions.
