# Hostile review: split/mixed `A2` real-form Cartan transitions

## Strongest overclaim

“Complete regular locus” would be false. The packet closes the complete
regular-semisimple locus, type by type. Regular nilpotent values are regular
for the moment-map rank calculation but are not semisimple and admit no Cartan
diagonalizer. The wording and registry preserve that boundary.

## Strongest contrary construction or mistyping

The dangerous mistake is to treat the split diagonal centralizer as connected
or to invent periodic angles for its noncompact directions. Its identity
component is `R^2`, its compact lattice has rank zero, and its four sign
components are discrete. The complex-pair `SL(3,R)` and split `SU(2,1)` types
have one compact circle; the compact `SU(2,1)` type has two. The certificate
uses those ranks separately and gives discrete components cotangent actions
rather than new conjugate pairs.

## Cross-type challenge

Distinct real Cartan types meet only at the discriminant, outside the regular-
semisimple locus. No same-locus overlap was silently omitted. What is omitted
is exactly the nonsemisimple bridge at that discriminant, which is named as
the next construction rather than assumed from continuity.

## Primitive and lattice challenge

For every same-type overlap, charge and conjugate coordinates transform by
inverse cotangent representations. This preserves the tautological pairing.
Compact logarithm triples close modulo the appropriate cocharacter lattice;
the rank-zero split type has no such period. No weight-lattice condition or
prequantum interpretation is used.

## Reproducibility seam

The exact probe checks the four Cartan-type records, four split centralizer
components, integral Weyl/component actions, inverse-transpose pairing,
lattice ranks, the regular nonsemisimple Jordan control, dimension/rank
schedules, links, and all claim ceilings. It does not prove the missing smooth
regular-centralizer transition.

## Verdict

`PASS_WITH_NONSEMISIMPLE_TRANSITION_REQUIRED`. Split and mixed regular-
semisimple transitions close classically at `98D`. Full regular globalization
and higher roots remain unlicensed until the nilpotent bridge constructs.
