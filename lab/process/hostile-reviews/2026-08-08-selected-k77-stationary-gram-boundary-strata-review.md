Krein domain, a physical boundary phase space, or an odd BFV complex. Any use

rectangular. Its formal transpose produces an equation dual. A field Riesz is
needed only to represent that covector as a field-valued operator, not to make
the action Hessian exist. Likewise, the separately owned first action cannot be
combined with this second action while its stationary fixture differs.
