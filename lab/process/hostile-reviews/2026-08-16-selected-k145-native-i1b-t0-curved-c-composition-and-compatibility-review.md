K133 already proves that the selected Euler symbol is not square-zero. Any

neighborhood inverse.

kernel or as a distortion KT/BV resolution. Diffeomorphism invariance supplies

compositions. Homological exactness, distortion reducibility, KT/BV, BFV and
physical cohomology remain false or unselected as stated.
