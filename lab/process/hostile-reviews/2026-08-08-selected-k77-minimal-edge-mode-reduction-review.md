solves only the finite local characteristic-reduction problem obtained from
the v0.69 contact boundary form and the ten inherited nonzero K77 weights. It

## Symplectic-geometry lens

The construction passes all four relevant tests: the extended form is closed;
its contraction with both endpoint gauge generators vanishes; its kernel is
exactly the gauge span; and the quotient form is nondegenerate. Rank alone
would not establish the third test, so kernel equality is essential. The
coefficient solution `(-1,+1)` is unique, and the two planted coefficient
errors retain a live contraction.

The quotient is meaningful but local. Its nondegeneracy is symplectic, not a
positive-state-space claim.

`theta -> theta + delta B` leaves `Omega=delta theta` unchanged because

boundary functionals are irrelevant to the variational principle. The edge
extension works because it enlarges field space and adds a boundary one-form
whose exterior derivative is nonzero.
