diagram, orthogonal six-plane or source-owned endpoint embedding. The artifact

second triple as another support, or state their intersection. The artifact
uses the `(5,2)` result only as an owned invariant and not as a fabricated

calls the transition type-missing and routes the factors as alternative local

between them. The next swing remains productive inside the `SU(2,2)` factor.

## Comparator attack

support maps and are irrelevant to this classical incidence gate. The
