K77 `A`, `B` and `C` blocks are held fixed. It does not say every source-family
four-field operator has the same determinant, because the draft does not
select one Shiab. It also does not prove that the K95 `11/12` coefficient
repairs K77 hyperbolicity; rank equality is not Jordan or symmetrizer equality.

The null rank-`1024` result is one orbit representative. Its `896`-dimensional
right kernel is a characteristic solution carrier, not a global physical state
space and not BV cohomology.
