# Hostile review — K127

## Strongest objection

A local algebraic Ricci-flat two-jet is not the source-global GU background
that the reverse superposition scaffold ultimately requires. Moreover a
two-by-two TT compression can look closed while the actual Hessian sends the
chosen polarizations into metric constraint or gauge components.

Both objections fire. They narrow rather than erase the K127 result.

## Adversarial checks

1. The curvature fixtures satisfy both pair antisymmetries, pair exchange and
   the first Bianchi identity exactly.
2. Their Ricci tensor and scalar curvature vanish coefficientwise.
3. The normal-coordinate metric two-jet reconstructs each algebraic curvature
   tensor, so the fixtures are realizable local metric jets.
4. After the correct internal-index raising, the full selected K77 Shiab
   response vanishes, not only its radial pairing.
5. At `T=0`, direct `B`, metric-coefficient, pairing and density derivatives
   retain `T`; in source variables, metric-induced `delta T` pairs with the
   zero translation Euler row.
6. The aligned family gives the exact operator compression `24 K_perp I_2`
   after K124 normalization.
7. Distinct exact values of `K_perp` preserve stationarity, so no coefficient
   is selected by the stationary equation.
8. The off-diagonal electric-Weyl fixture is Ricci-flat and K77-stationary but
   sends plus/cross outside the TT plane while its compression is zero.
9. A symmetric algebraic potential cancels from the Green identity, but this
   does not select a global operator domain or BFV charge.
10. No `I_sc`, ordinary Higgs/VEV, family-index, chirality or particle-model
    coefficient enters the construction.
11. The `-12 Box I_2+24 K_perp I_2` compression is the one-radial
    `C_t_h_h` response, not the pure TT Hessian at the `T=0` germ.

## Verdict

Accept the exact local fixed-boundary stationary family, the aligned
one-parameter TT compression and the generic leakage counterexample. Reject
wording that calls the family a selected source-global background, calls the
compression a closed two-field Hessian or the pure TT Hessian at `T=0`,
interprets `K_perp` as a mass or
scale, or promotes the local current to a global BFV/positive-cohomology or
superposition result.

K128 must test source-global legality and the full metric constraint/gauge
closure on the same Ricci-flat Weyl family before spectral or physical work.
