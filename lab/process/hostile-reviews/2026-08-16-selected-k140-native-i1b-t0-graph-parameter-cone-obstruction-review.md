# Hostile review: selected-K140 graph/parameter-cone obstruction

## Strongest overclaim attack

Failure of uniform recovery as `mu=kappa_1/rho` tends to zero does not erase
the exact fixed-frequency Schur reduction. Wherever `C(rho,n)` is invertible,
the distortion Euler equation defines an action-derived graph and the full
kernel is exactly represented by the metric Schur kernel. K140 therefore may
not say that the five classes are artificial or that no effective transport
can ever be defined.

The licensed conclusion is narrower: this graph is not a uniform
Douglis--Nirenberg principal reduction of the original fixed-`kappa_1`
ultraviolet problem, and it is not a constraint projector on K139's complete
homogeneous principal quotient.

## Strongest contrary construction

Scale `kappa_1=rho mu` and restrict to a closed parameter cone with `mu`
bounded away from zero and all spacelike exceptional ratios. Then
`C=rho(iC_1+mu K)` has an order-minus-one inverse and the graph reconstruction
has the expected relative order one. This is a mathematically coherent
parameter-dependent family and K140 preserves it.

It is not a recovery of fixed nonzero `kappa_1`: that ultraviolet limit forces
`mu` to zero, where the full null inverse has a fifth-order parameter pole and
the metric-supported graph has a third-order pole. The closed cone excludes
the limit it would need to establish equivalence.

## Weakest reproducibility seam

The new local coefficient ranks `4/3/1/0/0` reuse K135's exact null packet and
K134's Clifford engine rather than forming the complete `229376`-square
inverse. This is appropriate because the metric image closes in that packet.
K140 re-executes the construction and also replays K139. A change to the
selected Shiab, Hodge mass, metric image, or null packet invalidates the ranks
and triggers complete replay.

## Mistyping and preservation attack

An exact graph projector at one Fourier covector is not automatically a
differential constraint or an invariant microlocal projector. K132's concrete
tangential block has a 24-dimensional normal kernel but only an 11-dimensional
common normal/tangential kernel, and the nonzero mass term turns principal-null
rows into live lower-order equations. The action owns only four metric
diffeomorphisms, not the further `106629` quotient directions.

## Verdict

Pass at the stated ceiling. K140 keeps the fixed-frequency graph and the joint
parameter cone, but blocks both from being mislabeled as the original
fixed-`kappa_1` principal five-carrier. A five-by-five Dencker endomorphism for
that original calculus remains undefined. K141 may test a compact
shell-avoiding parameter annulus as an explicitly band-limited effective
family, without claiming ultraviolet equivalence.
