## Charge 1 — where does the summary outrun the artifact?

four-manifold need not admit a Lorentz observation section.  It does not deny
global sections on physically admissible `X`, and it does not prove a complete
classification of Lorentz manifolds.  The report uses it only to replace
“arbitrary `X`” with an admissible-sector condition.

### Jet geometry

The polynomial fixture proves local first/second-jet holonomicity.  It does not
prove atlas gluing, a global section, a spin lift, or nonlinear all-order
integrability.  Conversely, first-jet holonomicity cannot change the universal
rank of `s*:T*Y -> T*X`.

### Variational PDE

The charge passes only because the prior source-action calculation supplies a
nonzero Euler covector in that same kernel.  The witness is conditional on
nonzero `kappa_1` and the displayed full local translation domain.  A derived
constrained domain remains a live rival.

### Symplectic geometry and BV/BFV

system at fixed section jet; it is not a reduced phase space.  The rival BV
horn is not a quotient until an explicit differential, coisotropic constraint,
moment map and cohomology own the conormal sector.  No sixth quotient is booked.

### Krein/operator theory

The obstruction is fibrewise rank and action image, independent of choosing a
positive or indefinite norm.  It establishes no closed domain, positivity,
hyperbolicity, Green operator or constraint propagation.
