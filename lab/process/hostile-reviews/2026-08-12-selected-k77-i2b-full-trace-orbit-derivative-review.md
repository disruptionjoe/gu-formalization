# Hostile review: I2B full normalized trace-orbit derivative

## Pre-review charge

The tempting summary was “the moving Higgs/reality field is now complete and
touches 392 physical modes.”  The review attacked “complete,” “field,” the
meaning of the joint rank, stabilizer invariance and the full/unitary-half
source typing separately.

## Findings by required lens

- **Layer 0:** a normalized trace orbit is not arbitrary DeWitt variation; a
  carrier decomposition is not a decomposition into two connection groups.
- **Prior art:** v0.207 supplied one rank-56 derivative and the associated
  descent.  This wave closes the other 12 orbit directions and their joint
  image; it does not rediscover the formula.
- **Homogeneous spaces:** the exact `91=78+13` split is the complete
  infinitesimal orbit decomposition at fixed norm.
- **Principal bundles:** the trace stabilizer need only preserve the reduced
  class.  It may rotate a residual vector inside that class.
- **Clifford/Krein:** all 13 ranks, differentiated identities and action
  adjoints are exact on the full real target.
- **Variational bicomplex:** these are frame-owned terms in a composite first
  variation, not the whole Euler operator.
- **Symplectic geometry:** no presymplectic potential, boundary charge or BFV
  quotient follows from a fibrewise projector derivative.
- **Analytic PDE:** no closed domain, Green return, positivity, spectrum or
  vacuum follows from the finite exact certificate.
- **Source criticism:** the source states `C^(32,32) + C^(32,32)` carrier
  halves and separately a full `U(64,64)` arena.  It does not state two
  independent `U(32,32)` connections.
- **Contrary path:** freezing `P_+` fails in every orbit direction; radial
  scaling fails the fixed-norm tangent test.

## Three charges

### 1. Where does the summary outrun the artifact?

“Complete trace derivative” means complete only on the 13-dimensional
fixed-norm normalized trace orbit.  It does not cover arbitrary metric,
Hodge, Shiab, connection, observation or field variation.  Joint image rank
392 means the derivative family reaches the whole target collectively; it is
not 392 physical particles, fields or degrees of freedom.

### 2. Where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The first draft assumed the 78-dimensional trace stabilizer fixed the chosen
residual pointwise.  Exact calculation refuted that: 12 stabilizer generators
move it, while every moved target remains in the `P_+`-fixed class.  The
correct theorem is covariance of the class, not isotropy of one vector.

Likewise, “two `U(32,32)` halves” mistypes the source statement.  The source
object is a `C^(32,32) + C^(32,32)` carrier split.  A block-preserving
`U(32,32) x U(32,32)` subgroup is a derived compatible symmetry, not two
source-declared action connections.

### 3. If this stands, what else must change?

- one-generator-only trace derivative burden — **dissolved**;
- extra datum for the normalized trace orbit — **dissolved**;
- v0.207 associated descent — **survives**;
- v0.206 nonlinear-replacement refutation — **survives**;
- arbitrary DeWitt/Hodge/Shiab/connection/observation/field derivatives —
  **needs-recheck**;
- source-`epsilon` identification — **needs-recheck**;
- full `U(64,64)` operative action parent — **needs-recheck**;
- source `C^(32,32) + C^(32,32)` carrier split — **survives as source
  statement**;
- block-preserving subgroup as selected action connection — **needs-recheck**;
- two independent connection fields — **needs-recheck**;
- complete Euler/preboundary, Green/domain and physical vacuum —
  **needs-recheck**;
