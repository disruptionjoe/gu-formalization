# Hostile review: full-contact observer identifiability

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The artifact does not compute the actual full contact and does not prove that
the actual source jet destroys, preserves or creates the observer line.  It
proves only that the current restricted data cannot decide among those
outcomes.  “Full contact fails” and “the observer survives full contact” both
outrun the certificate.

## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend a mistyped object

The paired scalar contacts are independence witnesses, not candidate physical
jets.  Treating either coefficient as an action parameter would replace the
missing construction with a fit.  The first-action moving-metric Hessian and
the radial first derivative also belong to different variational grades from
the residual-square observer tensor; neither may be silently imported as the
missing normal jet.

## Charge 3 — downstream disposition

- **dissolved:** the claim that already-built restricted pullback and
  coefficient transport uniquely determine the full observer contact; the
  claim that `A=0` is protected after arbitrary admissible contact; the broad
  successor “assemble all moving terms.”
- **survives:** the v0.218 conditional line path; exact co-moving Ward
  covariance; actual source-normal-jet construction; full non-scalar contact;
  global common-line, arrow, BV, domain, positivity, stability and spectrum
  burdens.
- **needs-recheck:** the observer stratum after the actual
  `J1_normal(Upsilon_B)` is built; whether the source jet is constrained beyond
  `SO(3)` equivariance; whether the coupled tensor is physical stress-energy.

## Required lenses and dissent

- **Layer-0 semantics:** passes only with restricted derivative, normal jet,
  total derivative and physical stress kept distinct.
- **Prior art:** the observation-owner theorem already established the normal
  jet as missing; this wave adds the exact effect on v0.218, not a new claim
  that observation is a graph.
- **Variational bicomplex:** a chain-rule jet is the correct owner; an
  independent observation action column is rejected.
- **Principal-bundle geometry:** the scalar witness is stabilizer-equivariant,
  so covariance alone cannot remove the ambiguity.
- **Symplectic:** no quotient, preboundary class or BFV phase space is built.
- **Clifford/Krein:** v0.218's inverse-adjoint tensor is replayed unchanged.
- **Analytic:** generic local persistence does not establish global selection
  or passage through the discriminant.
- **Dissent:** the actual source geometry may force the scalar coefficient to
  zero or forbid that scalar channel.  That is precisely why constructing the
  source-native jet is the next experiment; it does not restore
  identifiability from the present restricted surface.
