## Findings and repairs

### H1 — material: the first synthesis gave a false exhaustive fork

The first draft said the receiver must either force the primalized Euler image
horizontal or retain ten normal components.  That was exhaustive only inside
the class “primalize upstairs, then apply an ordinary section receiver.”

The repository already contains a third construction route in
`unified-source-datum-variational-emission-map-2026-07-30.md`: a moving
codimension-ten defect with an explicit first variation, including both
intrinsic-density and support-motion terms.  A genuine defect/current or
induced-density reduction can be applied to the action before variation,
producing a different four-dimensional Euler system.

**Repair:** the report, registry, source receipt, probe fences and next gate
now carry three routes:

1. source-action-derived horizontal Euler image;
2. honest defect/density action reduction followed by variation; or
3. a typed tangent-plus-normal equation receiver.

No route is promoted.

### H2 — material propagation finding: old top-form pullback notation is not literal

An older reconstruction surface writes `s*(dvol_Y)` and describes it as a
four-dimensional volume form.  Literal differential-form pullback of a
14-form to a four-manifold is zero, exactly like the 13-form case.  The
intended construction may be an induced density on the section, but that is a
different functor and its variation matters.

**Repair:** the present report records the typing correction without rewriting
the older provenance.  The next gate must name current pairing, induced
density, or fibre pushforward before reusing a reduced-action formula.
