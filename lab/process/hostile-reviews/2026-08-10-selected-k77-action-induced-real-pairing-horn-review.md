The **selected scalar trace** chooses `B_Re`. Likewise, “orientation selects
the horn” is false: if combined parity were imposed it would select the rival
`B_Im` line. The report now states the narrower conditional theorem.

“GU derives the pairing” would also outrun the evidence. The source tells us to
norm-square `Upsilon`; the repository chose the scalar-Clifford-trace parent.
