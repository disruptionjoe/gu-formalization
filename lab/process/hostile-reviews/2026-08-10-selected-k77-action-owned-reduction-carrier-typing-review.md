# Hostile review: action-owned reduction versus fermion carrier

## Pre-wave

- **Fork assumed:** labelled K77 only; neither bosonic parent nor fermion `W`
  is assumed physical.
- **Search space:** the predecessor's wholesale `16384` connection basis and
  the separately named `1664`-dimensional RS split. The type question is
  decidable; full nonlinear action selection is not.
- **New un-owned object:** an induced odd K77 action operator or BV differential
  on the common RS bundle is required for the carrier test. The ordinary
  associated-bundle covariant derivative is available as grammar, but its
  physical projector/kernel is not built.
- **What dies:** only direct use of the bosonic projector as the five-way
  fermion discriminator and any reading of local closure as unique selection.

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The primary report initially called the moving reduction a “legitimate bosonic
connection subtheory” without always repeating that the exact closure theorem
is a **local first-order/fixed-background gate**. It does not prove nonlinear
closure, global constraint propagation, a common domain or BV closure. The
hostile disposition therefore narrows “consistent truncation” to
`LOCAL_LINEARIZED_CONSISTENT_TRUNCATION_CANDIDATE`.

The stronger negative statement also needed narrowing. A principal connection
does canonically induce a connection on an associated fermion bundle once its
representation is fixed. What is absent is not every induced connection; it is
the action-derived K77 RS operator, physical domain/cohomology and intertwining
theorem that would preserve or select the particular `j=1` projector.

## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend a superseded or mistyped object

The v0.131 queue asked a rank-`8128` connection-coefficient projector to accept
or reject subspaces of a rank-`1664` spinor-valued one-form carrier. Those are
different vector bundles. `D_varpi chi=0` only preserves the two Weyl halves;
the exact block counterexample proves it need not preserve a proper subspace
inside a half. Running five “carrier controls” before constructing the induced
fermion operator would give numbers for an undefined application.

Conversely, the review rejects a retreat to the old fixed `W` Casimir. The
corrected task is to construct the odd operator, not to rehabilitate the
non-discriminating selector.

## Charge 3 — downstream disposition

- **Dissolved:** the combined priority sentence claiming the current bosonic
  moving-reduction variation itself can discriminate `W`/mirror/random/
  `640`/`832`; any unique-parent inference from sector closure or common

- **Survives:** the moving rank-`8128` bundle, full-U comparator, exact
