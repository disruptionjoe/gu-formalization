# Hostile review

## Abstract cotangent reduction mistaken for action ownership attack

The parent is not introduced merely because every homogeneous cotangent bundle
has one. The selected epsilon preboundary variation has already been proved to
equal the canonical potential on formal `T*Spin_0(7,7)`. K97 starts from that
action-owned formal parent. The right-`H_bal` reduction is repository-derived,
not attributed to the source.

## Subcarrier mistaken for reduction attack

`T*(G/H_bal)` is not inserted as `G x p_0` inside `T*G` without quotienting.
The exact zero level has dimension `182-42=140`, and quotienting its `42D`
right-`H_bal` characteristic orbits gives `98`. The global associated-bundle
quotient and descended moment map are explicit.

## Formal attachment mistaken for dynamical selection attack

The success of the reduction proves that the K96 carrier attaches canonically
to the owned parent. It does not prove that the action imposes the right moment
constraint or treats the right action as gauge. K97 separately checks all
current bare-boundary horns and finds no such selection law.

## Free boundary zero mistaken for the required constraint attack

Free variation forces all of `lambda` to zero. Although zero lies in `p_0`,
this is only a single fibre value and cannot supply the globally surjective
RSAP. The required law allows every `lambda in p_0` while killing precisely
its `h_bal` components.

## Dirichlet symmetry mistaken for gauge attack

Fixed epsilon data leaves momentum unrestricted. Transformations moving the
fixed datum are physical boundary symmetries unless an additional completion
makes them gauge. Quotienting them by declaration would add the missing owner.

## Current-action obstruction promoted to source-global impossibility attack

The obstruction exhausts the currently certified bare boundary dispositions,
not every future completion. A sourced or derived boundary functional, edge
system, coisotropic constraint or BFV charge can reopen selection if it
produces exactly `J_R,H_bal=0` and exactly the right-`H_bal` characteristic
orbits.

## Verdict

Accept the global formal attachment of the K96 carrier to the action-owned
epsilon cotangent parent. Accept nonselection by the current bare action.
Keep physical phase-space attachment open, and make the next gate the explicit
construction or obstruction of the missing right-`H_bal` constraint-and-gauge
law.
