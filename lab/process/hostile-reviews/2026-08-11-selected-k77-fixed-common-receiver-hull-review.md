## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The finite bank proves rank `384` for its three representative causal strata
and both supplied Pin constructions. It does not prove that every nonzero or
global covector adds no further direction. “The global common hull is rank
384” would outrun the certificate; “the fixed common hull on the tested causal
bank is rank 384” is licensed.

The matched rank-`384` left hull proves finite action-dual typing only. It does
not show that the written source action selects, preserves or globally closes
the hull.

## Charge 2 — where rigor defended a superseded or mistyped object

`D_varpi chi_epsilon=0` against graph/mirror/192/640/832 conflicts with the
already-promoted scope correction: those conditions live on bosonic
connection-parent data. The candidate carriers and the new receiver live in

were never subspace equality, and the exact pairwise join now refutes it.
