# Hostile review: selected-K138 null-stratum covariant transport

null frame.

**Disposition.** Rejected by a second action-derived exact computation. The
rationally rotated null direction closes in a `224`-coordinate distortion
packet, has nilpotent power ranks `118/63/8/4/0`, and its exact finite inverse
returns `S_null=-48 ell_n ell_n^T`. A rational Lorentz transformation then
carries the full radical and gauge image between the two frames.

## Strongest K137-conflict attack

**Attack.** K137 already proved that no smooth five-bundle exists.

**Disposition.** K137 proved that one rank-five bundle cannot extend through
fibres of dimensions zero, five and `46481`. K138 stays on the constant-rank

through a different stratum; the results are complementary.

## Strongest three-jet attack

**Attack.** A changing Weyl tensor should rotate the five classes, so the
three-jet is indispensable even for the projector.

**Disposition.** The exact Brinkmann family has two independent
curvature-gradient parameters and is Ricci flat. Neither parameter enters the
principal outer-square functional or the diffeomorphism image. Curvature
gradients may enter lower amplitude coefficients, but they do not change the
principal quotient. The artifact preserves that lower-order opening.

under the full action-specific Dencker connection.

**Disposition.** Sustained and load-bearing. The source Hessian is mixed order.
Until a covariant first-order or Douglis--Nirenberg reduction, left inverse and
invariant subprincipal convention are declared, the action-specific Dencker
endomorphism is not a typed matrix. K138 books only geometric quotient/frame
transport and assigns the exact missing five-by-five test to K139.

## Strongest physical overclaim attack

**Attack.** A covariant five-dimensional characteristic quotient means five
propagating physical modes.

**Disposition.** Rejected. The quotient is finite-symbol and modulo only the
four action-owned diffeomorphisms. No closed domain, KT/BFV cohomology,
positive pairing or state space exists at this grade. “Five classes” is not
