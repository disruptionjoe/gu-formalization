# Hostile review: real-primalizer phase gate

## Pre-wave charges

- **Fork assumed:** none globally. The calculation is conditional on the
  selected K77/Hq response and compares action-parent symmetries rather than
  choosing one.
- **Search space:** the four live responses and the finite grade-two tensor
  carrier can be decided wholesale. A global moving reduction, domain and
  physical quotient cannot.
- **New unowned object:** the phase-even primalizer is a candidate only. It is
  not booked as a field, selector or datum.
- **What dies on success:** the claim that the geometry has no pairing-only
  rank-four route. Automatic ownership by the present noncompact-unitary
  action does not follow.

## Charge 1: where does the summary outrun the artifact?

“`Q_B` repairs the Higgs kinetic term” outruns the certificate. The exact
artifact proves only that a conjugation-sensitive finite-fibre pairing gives
rank four on the selected four-real response. The source does not select this
pairing, and the probe explicitly refutes its noncompact-unitary adjoint
invariance. The surviving wording is therefore **conditional rank-four
candidate**, not completed action.

The phrase “phase-even” also must not be read as “positive Hilbert.” Its base
factor remains Lorentzian, the full residual carrier is indefinite, and no
closed positive domain is constructed.

## Charge 2: is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The current rank-two calculation is not superseded: its complex-bilinear real
trace is the invariant comparator and is reproduced exactly. But treating its
radical as forced by the response geometry was too strong. It is forced only
within that pairing and within the already-owned fixed-real projectors.

The two-half control is correctly scoped. On the live grade-two products,
the two chiral traces are exactly equal, so two scalar half weights reduce to
their sum and cannot repair rank. This does **not** classify arbitrary moving
block connections or a symmetry-breaking fundamental field. The source
`C^(32,32)+C^(32,32)` carrier, its block subgroup and independent connections
remain distinct.

## Charge 3: what else must change if it stands?

- **Needs recheck:** the next construction must derive a moving fundamental
  symmetry/reduction from the selected action and test whether it transports
  the phase-even pairing covariantly.
- **Survives:** coupled metric/section/gauge contact and expanded total-residual
  parents remain rival repairs.
- **Survives:** observation basicness, BV/BFV reduction, analytic domain,
  positivity, spectrum and physical vacuum remain open.
- **Dissolved:** another search over only the eight displayed Shiab selectors,
  fixed `P_+`/`P_-`, or two scalar Weyl-half weights is no longer informative.
