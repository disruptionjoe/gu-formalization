## Charge 1: where does the summary outrun the artifact?

The strongest overreach would be to say the joint W/mirror action is now
constructed.  It is not.  The matrix theorem constructs the unique natural
action of an anti-linear half exchange on the parent center.  The source does
not publish that bosonic transformation, and the selected GU action has not
been varied on a nonzero central-flux background.

The phrase “invariant ring” also needs scope.  The exact probe decides parity
under simultaneous sign reversal and proves every restriction to a curvature
line is even.  It does not enumerate all tensor contractions of a many-
component two-form on `Y^14`.  The parity result is enough for sign selection;
it is not a complete curvature-invariant classification.

Finally, the 14D index sign is a donor theorem conditional on compact spin
Fredholm data.  Calling it the GU matter index would be false.  The exact 4D
control goes the other way: ordinary conjugate line twists have equal indices.
That equality is consistent with Weinstein's explicitly non-chiral total
theory; it merely fails to construct the asserted low-curvature luminous/dark
separation.

## Charge 2: where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The center must be typed at the correct parent.  `z_+` and `z_-` are separately
central only in the block-preserving subgroup.  The relative line ceases to be
central in full `U(64,64)`.  Treating it as a second full-parent gauge field
would repeat the known mistake “two carrier halves = two independent
connections.”

The central `U(1)` is also not automatically hypercharge.  A name-level match
would bypass the entire internal-group reduction, charge normalization and
anomaly problem.  The artifact correctly refuses that identification.
