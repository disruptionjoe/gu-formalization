## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The tempting sentence “H640 is the observed carrier” is false. The exact join
rank is 768, intersection rank 512 and complement graph rank 128. The surviving
statement is narrower: in the chosen flat K77 section, observation restricts
isomorphically to H640 and therefore supplies a canonical graph presentation.

The source does not state this graph, and a flat coordinate retract is not a
global observation section. The result therefore cannot be called a global
source-selected physical carrier.

## Charge 2 — where rigor defends a mistyped or superseded object

A fixed-subspace invariance test defends the wrong object under moving frame or
gauge transformations. The correct tensorial test moves the graph projector,
and its chain rule passes. Conversely, moving covariance cannot be used to
erase the physical compatibility burden `D_varpi P_H=0`; that would replace
one mistyping with another.

The rank-128 individual lower-order residuals do not falsify the complete
source operator. Its sixteen cells, barred adjoints and lower-left equations
may cancel only in the coupled graph/Riccati system. Treating individual-port
failure as full-operator failure would defend a superseded ablation.
