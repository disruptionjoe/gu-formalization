# Hostile review: selected K77 primitive-epsilon common bank

## Pre-review

- **Fork assumed:** the computation is conditional on the selected real K77
  Spin-native action parent. It does not settle `SIGNATURE-AMBIENT`,
  `REAL-CLIFFORD-FORM` or the expanded action-parent fork.
- **Search space:** 91 declared bivector epsilon directions appended to the
  existing 34 field directions; exact finite principal-symbol arithmetic can
  decide this horn wholesale.
- **New unowned object:** none. `epsilon` and its primitive Euler/Green chain
  were already source-owned. The missing object was the common serialization.
- **What dies on success:** only the claim that independent epsilon principal
  columns are absent. Full nonlinear epsilon, shared first action, BV/domain
  and parent selection survive.

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

Calling the result “the complete epsilon block” would be false. The certificate
constructs the principal `-q eta` augmented-torsion response. The source-owned
moving-Shiab term `delta Phi=[Phi,eta]` is zeroth order and remains unserialized
on the common coefficient bank. The report has been narrowed accordingly.

The doubled ranks `220/220/32` also do not define BFV phase spaces. They are
radical quotients of a finite normal Gram form with no action-derived trace
soldering, tangential operator, maximal domain or odd charge.

## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend a superseded or mistyped object

The predecessor's phrase “missing independent epsilon” was too broad. Prior
work had already constructed the primitive epsilon Euler chain and compact
Green owner. Reconstructing those would have solved yesterday's problem. This
wave builds only the missing common K77 principal serialization.

There is also a serious gauge-typing risk: epsilon may ultimately lie partly
in an action-derived gauge/BV characteristic distribution. The fifteen raw
kernel directions support that concern, but no BV differential currently
licenses deleting them. Keeping all 125 fields before reduction is therefore
the conservative choice; calling them physical degrees of freedom would be a
mistake.

## Required lenses

- **Layer-0 semantics:** passes after separating primitive epsilon, physical
  Ward orbit, gamma orbit and `Xi`.
- **Prior art:** fires constructively; primitive epsilon itself was already
  built, while its common bank was not.
- **Analytic/Krein:** exact non-null image nondegeneracy and null isotropic
  excess are finite-symbol facts only. They do not imply positivity,
  hyperbolicity or a domain.
- **Symplectic:** refuses a sixth quotient booking until the trace soldering
  and action characteristic distribution exist.
- **Variational:** refuses direct composition of 34- and 125-field action
  symbols or zero-padding epsilon.
- **Real Clifford:** accepts exact K77 ranks; expanded unitary parents remain
  uncomputed.
- **Complex/path-integral:** cannot discriminate beyond confirming that no
  complex contour or measure claim was made.

## Dissent

An operator-theory reading could decline the entire 125-field Gram as
premature because the lower-order epsilon coefficients are absent. That would
throw away valid principal-symbol information: principal rank and
characteristic stratification are independently meaningful. The dissent is
accepted as a fence against full-domain promotion, not as a reason to discard
the exact symbol.

A gauge-theory reading could quotient epsilon immediately. That is rejected:
the repo has not yet derived the BV differential on this enlarged action
tangent, and Schur or raw-kernel elimination is not a substitute.
