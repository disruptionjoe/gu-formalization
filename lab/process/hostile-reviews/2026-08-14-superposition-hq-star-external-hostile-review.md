# External hostile review — H-Q* and the superposition scaffold

Requested as an adversarial re-examination from outside the lane. A twenty-lens
council already exists inside the lane; duplicating it would add nothing, so
this review is charged only to **attack** and then to **reassess**.

**Reviewer limits, stated up front.** Read at section depth in one pass. The
internal derivations of VRS-1..VRS-4 — in particular VRS-3's Hessian reduction
and VRS-2's endpoint moment-map argument — are **not** independently verified
here. Nothing below should be read as impeaching those computations. The
attacks are on framing, hypothesis space, test design, and the interpretation
of results, which is where an outside reviewer can actually add value.

## What is genuinely strong, recorded first so the criticism is calibrated

- **VRS-4 is model discipline.** It states necessary-and-sufficient descent
  conditions and then reports plainly that `J10` does not meet them. Stating
  the bar and then failing your own candidate against it is the right shape.
- **Council conclusion 2** — the moving split/ghost sector is contractible,
  hence a simplification mechanism and not a state count — is a real guard
  against a classic error.
- **Pairing `H-Q*` with a mandatory null `H0`** is better practice than
  declaring a winner.
- **`Result first` plus explicit dispositions** makes the artifacts auditable
  by an outsider in one pass, which is exactly why this review was possible.

## The twenty hostile lenses

Each lens is charged to break something, not to comment.

1. **Deformation theorist.** `ker(L)/im(K)` is a subquotient of a vector space.
   It is a **linear space by construction**. Deriving "a linear state space"
   from it is not a derivation.
2. **Foundations-of-QM specialist.** Superposition is three claims: a
   `C`-linear state space, *linear dynamics on it*, and the Born rule. `H-Q*`
   addresses only the first. It could fully succeed and deliver no superposition.
3. **Classical field theorist.** GU's field equations are nonlinear; their
   solution set is a nonlinear variety. The linear structure `H-Q*` finds is
   the **tangent space at a background**, which every classical field theory
   has. This cannot distinguish GU from vacuum GR.
4. **Experimental-design specialist.** There is **no negative control**. Run
   the identical apparatus on a theory known not to derive superposition
   internally. If it yields the same structure, the apparatus measures nothing.
   **This is the single cheapest decisive test available and it is missing.**
5. **Same, second charge.** There is also **no positive control**. Does the
   machinery recover the known Hilbert space for a theory where the answer is
   known? If not, a null on GU is uninformative.
6. **Lakatos / philosopher of science.** Track the hypothesis wording across
   revisions: "positive physical cohomology of the total-residual complex"
   became "**gauge-dressed** positive physical cohomology of the **full
   bulk-plus-boundary** residual complex." Each failure added a qualifier. That
   is the signature of a **degenerating problemshift**, not sharpening.
7. **Statistician.** A 7–7 "tie" between a challenger and a null is not a tie.
   Ties go to the null. The table's visual symmetry does rhetorical work that
   the "routing signal, not a probability" caveat does not undo.
8. **Same, second charge.** Twenty lenses reading one evidence base are
   **strongly correlated**. Vote counts over correlated lenses are not an
   epistemic measure of anything, and should not be tabulated as if they were.
9. **Bayesian.** Seven-plus swings, zero positive instantiations: SR-1
   negative, SR-2 blocked, SR-3..SR-7 queued, VRS-1 three kills, VRS-4
   type-missing. That record is **evidence**, and it should move the posterior
   toward `H0`, not merely route the next swing.
10. **Survivorship-bias auditor.** `J10` is called "the strongest surviving
    internal complex candidate." It survives because it has not yet been tested
    at the grade where its predecessors died — VRS-4 says so explicitly
    ("fibrewise gauge covariant only"). That is survivorship, not strength.
11. **Falsifiability auditor.** State the single computation that would kill
    `H-Q*`. There isn't one: every failure is absorbable as "the total complex
    is not instantiated yet." An unkillable challenger is not a challenger.
12. **Sunk-cost auditor.** `H-B` is protected because "endpoint momentum and
    Green data are already live." That is evidence of **activity**, not of
    promise. Protection should require a materially distinct route with a
    decisive test.
13. **Test-symmetry auditor.** Every swing is designed to kill `H-Q`. **No
    swing is designed to confirm `H0`.** Asymmetric test design manufactures
    the appearance of a live contest.
14. **Operator-algebraist.** The hypothesis space has **no algebraic family**.
    The standard internal route to linearity is GNS: an observable algebra plus
    a positive state yields a Hilbert space, deriving superposition without
    ever linearizing field equations. Its absence is a hole in the census.
15. **Geometric quantizer.** The hypothesis space also has **no geometric-
    quantization family** — and this is the costly omission, because the
    repository *already owns* an exactly symplectic structure on
    `T*Spin_0(7,7)` with rank-91 moment differential. See the reassessment.
16. **Positivity specialist.** "Positive physical cohomology" is doing heavy
    lifting undefined. Positive with respect to *which* pairing, on which
    domain, with what completion? VRS-4 lists the pairing as type-missing, so
    the central noun of the hypothesis is currently undefined.
17. **Real-form specialist.** A complex structure `J` on a *real* deformation
    complex gives `C`-linearity of the complex, not of *physics*. The step from
    "the tangent complex is `C`-linear" to "states superpose" is never argued.
18. **Ghost/BRST specialist.** Council conclusion 2 correctly excludes the ghost
    sector as a state count. Apply the same standard to the boundary sector:
    what rules out `H-B`'s endpoint data being similarly non-physical
    bookkeeping? The asymmetry is unargued.
19. **Scope auditor.** RF-1/RF-2 kills are "at the declared local K77 scope."
    Conclusion 1 promotes them to "fixed-`J` is not the live route" with "very
    high conviction." That is a scope promotion from a local result to a global
    routing decision.
20. **Honesty auditor.** The lane's own grades say it plainly — "NO GU
    SUPERPOSITION RESULT", "NO PHYSICAL COHOMOLOGY", "TYPE MISSING". The
    scaffold's framing ("strongest live internal mechanism") sits in tension
    with its own certificates. The certificates are right.

## The two structural killers

**(1) Linearity is assumed, not derived.** The deformation complex is the
*linearization* of gauge symmetry and field equations at a background. Its
cohomology is automatically a vector space. So `H-Q*`'s success condition —
"reduce to a nontrivial positive physical cohomology" — recovers linearity that
was inserted by the act of linearizing. The physical content of superposition
is that *nature* is linear, not that *linearizations* are. Until this is
answered, `H-Q*` is close to tautological.

**(2) No control, either sign.** Lens 4 and lens 5 together are the cheapest
decisive move in the whole programme. Apply the identical apparatus to vacuum
GR or pure Yang-Mills. Both have deformation complexes, gauge sectors, boundary
data and positive pairings, and neither derives superposition internally. If
the apparatus returns the same verdict there, it has **zero GU-specific
content**, and every swing to date is uninformative about GU.

## Reassessment — the strongest potential superposition hypothesis

`H-Q*` is not it. The reassessed candidate is not on the current family list:

> **H-GQ (geometric quantization).** If GU derives superposition internally, it
> does so by **geometric quantization of its own action-owned symplectic
> structure** — not by cohomology of a linearized complex. The complex
> structure enters as a **Kähler polarization on phase space**, and the Hilbert
> space is the space of polarized sections of a prequantum line bundle.

Why this is stronger than `H-Q*`:

- **It does not smuggle in linearity.** Geometric quantization constructs a
  linear state space over a *nonlinear* phase space. That is exactly the step
  `H-Q*` skips, and it is the whole difficulty.
- **It uses a structure GU already exactly owns.** `CURRENT-STATE` records the
  full canonical cotangent parent `T*Spin_0(7,7)` with a 182-dimensional
  two-form that is *exactly symplectic*, with rank-91 moment differential. That
  is precisely the input geometric quantization needs, and it is already
  certified.
- **It explains the failure pattern.** The `J` hunt keeps failing on
  deformation complexes because `J` is being sought in the wrong place. In
  geometric quantization `J` is a polarization on phase space; the moment map
  and charge data that VRS-2 found indispensable are exactly the reduction data
  geometric quantization expects. The programme's own hardest-won positives
  (exact symplectic form, moment map, endpoint charges) are geometric-
  quantization inputs, not deformation-cohomology inputs.
- **It has a sharp, cheap, decidable first gate.** Prequantizability:
  `[omega / 2 pi] in H^2(M, Z)`. This is an integrality condition, exactly
  decidable with the symplectic form the repository already owns, and it is a
  genuine kill condition — a non-integral form kills the route outright with no
  further construction. Contrast `H-Q*`, which has no such gate (lens 11).

`H-GQ` does **not** rescue the programme by itself, and this review does not
claim it succeeds. It relocates the question to a place where GU owns the input
and where failure is cheap and legible. `H0` remains the correct default, and
lens 9's point stands: the swing record to date favours it.

**Secondary candidate, also absent from the census:** `H-ALG`, the GNS route
(lens 14) — build the gauge-invariant observable algebra, ask whether GU
supplies a `*`-structure and a positive state, and derive the Hilbert space
from those. Weaker than `H-GQ` only because the `*`-structure relocates rather
than resolves where complexity enters, but it deserves a row in the family
table.

## Recommended next moves, in cost order

1. **Run the negative control** (lens 4). Cheapest, highest information, and it
   audits every result to date.
2. **Test prequantizability** of the owned symplectic form (`H-GQ`'s first
   gate). Cheap, exactly decidable, genuinely kills or opens a route.
3. **Add `H-GQ` and `H-ALG` to the family table** and re-run the vote over the
   corrected hypothesis space. A census that omits the two standard internal
   routes to linearity cannot claim exhaustion.
4. **Define "positive"** (lens 16) before it appears in another hypothesis
   statement.
