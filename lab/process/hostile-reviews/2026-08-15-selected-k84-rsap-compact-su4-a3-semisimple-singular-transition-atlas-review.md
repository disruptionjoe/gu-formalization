# Hostile review

## Illicit compact-normality attack

Compact normality is not copied from a slogan. The probe reconstructs the
`6+9` decomposition at `H=I_4` and checks that the moving basis is precisely
real symmetric and trace-free. The real spectral theorem then supplies the
semisimple exclusion; it is not extended to the indefinite or quaternionic
forms.

## Incomplete-stratum attack

The probe recursively enumerates all fifteen set partitions of four labels.
Their block sizes yield exactly `1+1+1+1`, `2+1+1`, `2+2`, `3+1` and `4`.
Removing the regular type leaves exactly the four registered singular
multiplicities.

## False orbit-count attack

Multiplicity type is not an adjoint-orbit count. Continuous eigenvalue data
remain. The result claims exhaustion of compact spectral multiplicities, not
four singular orbits.

## Imported-rank attack

Every representative is checked directly against `sl4` and the compact
moving space. The resulting centralizer pairs are `5/4`, `7/5`, `9/6`, and
`15/9`; all four target/map schedules independently saturate the `98D`
pointwise bound.

## Incomplete-closure attack

The eight paths cover every arrow in the registered multiplicity closure
graph. Every path is checked to stay symmetric and trace-free, and every
endpoint recomputes the centralizer pair of its destination stratum. This is
stronger than merely displaying one regular approach per stratum.

## Fake-global-frame attack

The four rational `SO(4)` frames are not advertised as a global finite cover.
They form an exact noncommuting nerve testing the transition law on admitted
overlaps. All six inverse-transpose primitive tests and all four base and
cotangent triangles close, while the one global compact cotangent moment map
forces zero moment defect.

## Cross-form and comparator attacks

No relative ambient embedding is supplied, so no cross-real-form transition
is inferred. Higgs, generation, chirality and other particle comparators do
not type this classical compact spectral atlas and are not used.

## Verdict

Accept closure of the local compact `SU(4)/SO(4)` singular atlas at complete
spectral-multiplicity, exact-rank and cotangent-frame grade. Do not promote
the four multiplicities to orbit counts or the four test frames to a global
cover. Proceed within the six-configuration quaternionic
`SU*(4)/SO*(4)` factor.
