# Hostile review

## Strongest overclaim

The dangerous sentence is “the two BRST cohomologies differ.” The result does
not compute either total cohomology, its dimension, its topology, or a
canonical comparison map. It proves a narrower marked-class statement: the
specific exact decaying mode survives both hit quotients and is not an element
of the miss middle domain. The final artifact uses that ceiling.

The second dangerous promotion is from a linear middle-stage gauge quotient
to quantum BRST cohomology or physical states. No ghost measure, completion of
the full BV complex, positivity, probability rule, source-selected domain or
observable algebra is present. Those claims are explicitly rejected.

## Strongest contrary construction

Could the witness lie in the closure of the global gauge range even though it
is not a symbol-level gauge vector? No. The bounded projection to tangential
Fourier mode `e4` and vector components `1,2` kills every distributional
gradient: the selected mode has `k_1=k_2=0`. It fixes the nonzero witness.
Continuity makes the same separator kill the closure of the gauge range. This
is stronger than an unproved closed-range assertion.

Could the folded `L_hit`/`L_miss` domains be silently treated as stage
domains? They are not. The result constructs separate closed middle graph
extensions and closed pullback gauge domains subordinate to the respective
Witt trace lines. It does not claim that the whole folded domain is invariant
under the differential.

## Weakest analytic seam

The stage extensions are deliberately finite-dimensional graph extensions of
the minimal middle operator. They are closed and trace-compatible, but they
are not proved Hilbert self-adjoint, Fredholm, maximal, unique, or source-
selected. The maximal terminal realization is chosen because the
distributional Noether identity makes it domain-compatible; it is not a
physical boundary condition.

The Green-line separation requires that the witness be null but not radical.
The exact certificate verifies `K_n v != 0`; the noncharacteristic rank
sequence fixes the middle radical as `im A_n`. This prevents the Witt line
from collapsing into the minimal graph quotient.

## Hostile controls

- A planted longitudinal vector does not pass the transverse separator.
- A planted normal component fails the terminal Noether kernel.
- Reduced survival is not inferred from algebraic nongauge support.
- No total cohomology, closed-range, positivity, source, particle or GU
  conclusion is admitted.

## Verdict

The marked linear middle-stage class survives both algebraic and reduced hit
quotients and is absent from the miss middle domain. The result is bankable at
closed stage-domain and marked-class extension-sensitivity grade. Positivity
and physical interpretation remain a separate, source/action-unselected gate.
