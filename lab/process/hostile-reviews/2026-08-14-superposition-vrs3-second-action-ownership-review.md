# Hostile review: VRS-3 second-action ownership

## Strongest overclaim

Calling `D Upsilon^! Q D Upsilon` a physical Hermitian form would silently
import both a complex structure and a positive completion. The object currently
owned is a real symmetric covector-valued Hessian, and the selected receiver
need not be positive.

## Strongest contrary construction

A separately supplied `J` compatible with a nondegenerate Hessian can define
`omega(u,v)=H2(Ju,v)` and a complex sesquilinear package. This is a legitimate
conditional construction, but it proves the opposite ownership point: `J` is
an independent premise and must also descend through the total complex and
boundary domain.

## Weakest reproducibility seam

The finite probe certifies tensor type and logical independence, not the full
GU functional Hessian. The GU result therefore remains conditional on
residual zero and records every omitted off-shell and boundary term explicitly.

## Disposition

The result is decision-grade at type level. It kills the standalone
Hessian-to-`J` route, preserves the Hessian as real quadratic/differential
input, and licenses VRS-4 without unblocking `SR-2`.
