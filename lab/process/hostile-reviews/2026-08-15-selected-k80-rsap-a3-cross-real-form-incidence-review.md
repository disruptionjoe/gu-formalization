# Hostile review

## Representation attack

Equal dimension and common complexification do not identify real ambient
embeddings. The artifact uses the real orthogonal models
`sl4(R) ~= so(3,3)` and `su(2,2) ~= so(4,2)`. Exact bases place both in
`so(7,7)`. Their support signatures differ and are preserved by ambient
orthogonal conjugacy, so the nonconjugacy claim passes.

## Generic-overlap attack

Different support signatures alone would not exclude a shared lower-rank
operator. The artifact limits the disjointness claim to full-support controls:
rank six recovers the six-plane as the image. It separately constructs shared
rank-four controls. The generic-versus-special distinction passes.

## Relative-position attack

One convenient coordinate placement cannot establish the selected incidence.
The certificate deliberately supplies three placements with the same two
individual support signatures and different common-support invariants:
`0`, `(2,2)`, and `(3,2)`. Their embedded algebra intersections have dimensions
`0`, `6`, and `10`. This proves the missing datum rather than hiding it.

## False-refinement attack

The existing `A1 x A1` factor makes the four-plane model tempting, but the
five-plane model yields a distinct `B2` bridge and the transverse model yields
none. Selecting the banked factor for convenience would add an unowned
relative orbit. The artifact correctly performs no moment, primitive or
cocycle calculation without a selected base incidence map.

## Scope attack

Relative-orbit underdetermination is not failure of either individual
principal factor and is not a global all-strata RSAP obstruction. Complete
nonsplit singular atlases, deeper ambient strata and zero charge remain open.
No particle comparator or physical interpretation enters.

## Verdict

Accept the individual ambient classes, generic separation, explicit special
incidence controls and the no-refinement disposition. Require the actual `D7`
root/source embedding to select the relative six-plane orbit before any
cross-form symplectic gluing.
