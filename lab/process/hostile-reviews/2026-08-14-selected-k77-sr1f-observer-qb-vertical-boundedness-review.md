after selected Shiab and Hodge. The values are `+16,-16` at the adapted
observer, not the fixed-natural `-16,+16`; the candidate swaps the displayed
signs but retains their opposition.

### One observer representative is not the moving family

Rejected. All four observer-axis coefficients and all six polarizations are
computed. The exact polynomials are `+16 c(u)` and `-16 c(u)` with no cross
terms. A rational nontrivial boost fires at `+656/9,-656/9`.

Rejected within this family. On the unit-timelike hyperboloid
`c(u)=1+2|v|^2>0`. Choosing `u=u(T)` changes the magnitude pointwise but never
the sign opposition. This is precisely why the field-dependent horn needed an
exact full-carrier test.

### The residual thirteen-form is not normal to the observer plane

Rejected as a type confusion. The input point-carrier rays are vertical. The
selected residual is a `Lambda^13 Cl1` cell, whose thirteen-form necessarily
contains observed axes; after the action-owned Hodge adapter it is a normal
one-form `Cl1` cell, which is the object contracted by `Q_u`.

### `H_u` is a positive fundamental symmetry, so the answer must be positive

Rejected. The predecessor explicitly classifies `H_u` as an indefinite
Hermitian form with balanced inertia, not a positive Hilbert majorant. Its
involution property alone supplies no positivity theorem.

### This proves no moving fundamental symmetry can ever work

Rejected. The result covers the existing conditional observer-Hermitian
family and any field dependence obtained solely by selecting `u`. A genuinely
different action-derived primalizer outside that family is not classified.

### Lower-order action terms could still create a local minimum

Accepted only as a local-stationarity possibility, not a boundedness repair.
The released first action is at most cubic, so the negative quartic remains a
runaway at large amplitude. No canonical critical orbit is licensed.
