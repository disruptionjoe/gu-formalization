# Hostile review: K77 physical-boundary disposition selector

## Verdict

`CONDITIONAL_EDGE_SELECTOR_SURVIVES__SOURCE_AND_ACTION_SELECTION_CLAIM_REJECTED`.

The exact edge horn is uniquely selected by two explicit predicates. Neither
the source nor the local action establishes the first predicate—full endpoint
gauge redundancy—so the result cannot be reported as a source/action-selected
physical boundary theory.

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

“Full tilted gauge symmetry selects edge modes” is too broad. Weinstein's
double-coset statement concerns the inhomogeneous gauge configuration grammar;
his boundary discussion expressly leaves the multiple-time problem as
technical debt. On a boundary, nonvanishing gauge transformations may act as
charged physical symmetries. The artifact therefore says only:

```text
IF all endpoint transformations remain gauge
AND generic nonzero action momentum remains admissible,
THEN the minimal edge horn is unique locally.
```

The second overreach would be to read a nonzero rank-ten endpoint bank as a
physical-domain theorem. A Neumann-like restriction can consistently set the
endpoint momentum to zero; the action bank shows what is available, not which
boundary values nature admits.

## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend a superseded or mistyped object

The most dangerous wrong object is the bulk quotient `A/G` or tilted double
coset treated as if it were already a boundary BFV quotient. Bulk gauge
equivariance, boundary characteristic reduction and asymptotic symmetry are
three different objects. An exact proof of the bulk quotient cannot decide the
last two by notation.

Likewise, the prior zero-charge and small-gauge horns are not mathematically
falsified by the nondegenerate action bank. They remain conditional domains.
The new result ranks the edge horn for construction because it preserves more
of the GU grammar, not because those domains are inconsistent.

## Dissent

A strict covariant-gauge reader may argue that the source's insistence on the
full double quotient should automatically extend to every boundary. That is a
reasonable construction preference and motivates taking the edge horn first.
It is not yet an inference: theories with boundaries routinely convert bulk
gauge transformations into charged boundary symmetries. The charged horn must
therefore remain until the actual global GU boundary and observable algebra
are built.
