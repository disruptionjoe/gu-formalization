# Hostile review: K77 branch-Hessian discriminator

## Pre-wave

No action-parent or branch-selection fork is assumed. The finite homogeneous
search consists of two algebraic branches, two separately sourced actions and
the displayed scalar `(B,T)` slice; that slice is decidable wholesale, while
the full functional `(g,varpi,epsilon)` tangent and three parent scopes are not.
Success introduces no object. If successful, only the shortcut that ports one
branch or selects it from the displayed reconstruction Hessian dies.

## Charge 1: where did the summary outrun the artifact?

The tempting matrices have different real inertias: one is a saddle and the
other is negative definite. But neither branch is critical in the independent
`B` direction. A Hessian is coordinate-invariant at a critical point; away
from one its second derivatives gain a term proportional to the nonzero first
derivative. The exact control constructs `B=B0+x+c*x^2` and chooses `c` so the
displayed Hessian determinant becomes zero. Calling either matrix a branch
Morse type therefore outran the source tangent and the invariant mathematics.

On the actually owned scalar `varpi` direction, both first-action coefficients
are negative, with positive ratio `7-4 sqrt(3)`. For the distinct residual-
square action, both restrictions are positive rank one, up to a nonzero parent
pairing orientation. Neither calculation selects a branch.

## Charge 2: where was rigor defending a mistyped object?

The exact two-coordinate calculation was rigorous but defended independent
`B` at fixed `T`, which v0.111 already labels a reconstruction diagnostic. It
is not one of the source variables. Galois conjugacy also does not preserve the
ordering of real embeddings, so it cannot transfer positivity or Morse data.
The one-dimensional source slice is valid but incomplete: it does not replace
the metric, epsilon, off-slice varpi, parent, gauge or ghost blocks.

## Charge 3: what changes if this stands?

- **Dissolved:** the naive real-Morse branch selector and any one-branch
  Hessian-transfer shortcut based on Galois conjugacy.
- **Survives:** both nonzero branches; the exact boundary symplectomorphism;
  both distinct source actions; the selected Spin parent, separate
  `U(32,32)` halves and full `U(64,64)` comparator; all six operator owners.
- **Needs recheck:** every branch-specific first-action and residual-square
  block beyond the scalar source line, including metric/epsilon cross terms,
  parent pairing, gauge fixing, ghosts and the closed domain.
