## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend the wrong object

The seven polynomial values are orbit labels, not seven physical degrees of
freedom.  Their nonzero derivatives do not say that a successor needs exactly
seven new canonical pairs, nor that the 84 orbit coordinates are physical.
The dimension and symplectic form of a carrier spanning multiple orbit values
remain to be derived.  A union of coadjoint orbits is generally Poisson, not
automatically one symplectic phase space.

Likewise, simultaneous coefficient scaling is a valid tangent of the affine
connection/distortion coefficient space at the frozen fixture, but no claim is
made that it satisfies field equations, boundary conditions, positivity, or a
closed domain.
