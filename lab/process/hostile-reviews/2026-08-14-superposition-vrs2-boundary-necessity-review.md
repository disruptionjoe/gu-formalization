# Hostile review

## Strongest overclaim

The live nonzero fixture does not prove that every possible GU background has
a physical boundary. Free variation supplies an exact zero-charge escape, and
a boundaryless topology or future solution family is not exhausted. The kill
is restricted to a bulk-only realization of the selected nonzero branch.

## Strongest contrary construction

An action-owned generated boundary functional could define a nonzero
Lagrangian graph and change the horn. It is not presently owned and would have
to pass seven independent invariant tangencies. The review preserves this
route rather than treating source silence as nonexistence.

## Weakest reproducibility seam

Finite Green-symbol isotropy is not an analytic closed-domain theorem. The
certificate checks the exact base/normal and rank-eight mixed-gauge controls,
but does not infer a Calderon projector, self-adjoint extension or positivity.

## Disposition

Accept boundary necessity and the bulk-only kill for the live nonzero branch.
Reject a universal boundary theorem, physical BFV cohomology, complex Hilbert
space, positivity, superposition, ledger, canon or public-posture movement.
