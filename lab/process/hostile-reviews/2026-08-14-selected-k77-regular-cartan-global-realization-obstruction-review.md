# Hostile review: regular-Cartan globalization obstruction

## Strongest overclaim

The result could be misread as proving that every 98-dimensional global
equivariant realization is impossible.  That statement is unsupported and is
rejected.  The cohomology argument assumes a globally untwisted product
identification of the orbit family and its fibre classes.  A twisted,
monodromic or differently coupled minimal Hamiltonian space remains open.

## Strongest contrary construction

The best surviving contrary route is a nontrivial Hamiltonian fibration whose
coupling form absorbs Cartan/Weyl monodromy while retaining total dimension
98 and an equivariant submersive moment map.  No such space is constructed
here.  The exact two compact directions prevent silently substituting the
maximally split product model.

## Weakest seam

The root classification is exact and finite.  The globalization consequence
uses standard orbit-topology and fibrewise-cohomology reasoning rather than a
machine formalization of the full fibration theorem.  The durable claim is
therefore kept at the narrow product-family obstruction.  A later successor
must explicitly construct the total space, closed symplectic form, group
action and moment map or prove a topology theorem that covers all such spaces.

## Verdict

`SCOPED_SURVIVES__UNIVERSAL_NO_GO_REJECTED`.  The `(5,2)` Cartan classification
and untwisted-product obstruction survive.  Minimal global dimension, source
