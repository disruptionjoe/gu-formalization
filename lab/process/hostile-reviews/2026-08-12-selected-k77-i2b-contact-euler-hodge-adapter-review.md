# Hostile review: I2B contact/Euler Hodge adapter

## Required lenses

- **Layer-0 semantics:** the identity and raw pairing fail before rank is
  interpreted. Hodge repairs exterior degree for the principal bank only; it
  does not repair the lower bank's Clifford grade.
- **Prior art:** v0.201 already identified `(12,12)+(13,13)` as the
  `J`-completed radial tangent, v0.212 already owned the arbitrary-field Euler
  bank, and v0.221 owned the trace-`H_q` contact. This result is their typed
  composition, not a new primitive.
- **Variational bicomplex:** an Euler covector evaluated on a variation is not
  the value of an ambient normal jet. The report keeps the moving coupled
  prolongation open.
- **Symplectic geometry:** a principal carrier intersection and zero physical
  Green row supply no presymplectic current, momentum map or BFV quotient.
- **Krein/real structure:** trace-`H_q` is not the source-unspecified `Q_B`.
  The zero on the stationary branch is an Euler zero, not positivity or
  residual vanishing.
- **Principal-bundle geometry:** the sparse two-cell preimages are pointwise.
  The v0.221 response truncation was already not closed under the full
  stabilizer, so no global subbundle is inferred.
- **Analytic/PDE:** the finite exact ranks establish no closed domain,
  hyperbolicity, energy, stability or spectrum.

## Charge 1 — where the summary could outrun the artifact

The strongest overreach would be “the action fills the missing contact
direction.” It does not. The action owns the Euler factor on the Hodge
preimage, and that factor is zero on the current restricted stationary branch.
The report therefore says the carrier fits and the fixed action owns the row,
not that a nonzero on-shell contact is selected.

Calling `e3` radial is licensed by prior art: v0.201 explicitly identifies the
correlated `(12,12)+(13,13)` pair as the `J`-completed radial tangent. Calling
it a full Higgs equation would overreach because scalar descent, Yukawa
placement and physical quotient are open; the report avoids that phrase.

## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend a superseded or mistyped object

The v0.212 fixed-`H_q` Euler bank is not the complete moving `Q_B` action. A
large exact solve on that bank could become rigor applied to the wrong final
object. Its proper use here is a baseline: it isolates the radial row and
shows exactly what moving terms must change. The next run must differentiate
the moving pairing/metric/section/Shiab data rather than repeat the frozen
bank.

The primary carrier remains two `C^(32,32)` halves. Their
`U(32,32)xU(32,32)` subgroup, the full `U(64,64)` parent and two independent
connections are not interchanged.

## Charge 3 — if this stands, what else must change

- **Survives:** v0.221 rank-120 source contact, pointwise scalar availability,
  the restricted Mexican-hat potential and the exact contact discriminant.
- **Needs recheck:** moving Hodge derivative, `Q_B`, Shiab, metric, section and

- **Dissolved:** the untyped direct composition of the 16 contact coordinates

  the idea that the missing local contact direction is an arbitrary new
