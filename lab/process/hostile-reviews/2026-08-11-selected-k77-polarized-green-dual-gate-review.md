## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The tempting summary is that `ker N` is “the domain.” That outruns the artifact
because the action owns two independent fields. On
`ker Nsharp x ker N`, the Green pairing has `128`-dimensional radicals on both
sides. The report therefore retains “one-sided principal evolution
ingredient” and rejects “complete selected-action domain.”

Likewise, a perfect algebraic dual is not automatically a physical quotient.
The quotient statement is exact, but its ownership is absent.

## Charge 2 — where rigor may defend a mistyped object

A long exact quotient calculation could defend the wrong object if it silently
renamed `im Nsharp` as gauge. The source supplies no such tangent/BV
differential. Worse, the naive observation map has rank `128` on the proposed
quotient directions, so the quotient is not observationally basic.

The purely observed-frequency sample supplies a second warning: the natural
dual kernel itself retains observation rank `512`, not `640`. This is a
direction-sensitive dual effect, not permission to fit a new observation.

## Dissent

`Vbar/im Nsharp` and declare the lost naive observation irrelevant. That is a
consistent abstract pairing, but it changes the observed-field interface and
introduces an unowned quotient. It is a construction option, not something
the present action or datum has selected.
