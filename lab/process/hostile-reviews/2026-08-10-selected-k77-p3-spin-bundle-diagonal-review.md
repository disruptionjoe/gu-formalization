“P3 supplies the source reduction” outruns the certificate. P3 supplies the
correct isomorphism class in one horn. A connection-preserving map into the
actual source parent and the reduced action are still absent.

Conversely, “the diagonal adds a function-valued physical datum” also

function-valued multiplicity is not a physical parameter after a valid gauge
quotient.

topological class now matches. The live object is the differential connection
orbit. Equal `c2` cannot decide it, and the fundamental `p1=-2c2` must not be
compared directly with the adjoint framing `p1=-4c2`.

The other superseded object is the old unreduced stationary family. Its two
`su(2)` factors remain a valid kill of that family, but it cannot be projected
into the newly reduced theory after solution.
