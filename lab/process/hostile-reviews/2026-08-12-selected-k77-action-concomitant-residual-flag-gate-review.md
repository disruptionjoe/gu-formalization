the current Lorentz-invariant natural zero-order class cannot.  Nonhomogeneous
curvature and full `II` can reduce the stabilizer and are not covered.

The rank obstruction is also not a generation-count theorem.  Rank four is a
required flag carrier in the RB5 construction; no multiplicity is read as a
count of generations.

## Charge 2 — where could rigor defend a mistyped object?

the section was described as constant.  The ambient metric-bundle geometry
already proves constant sections are not totally geodesic.  Replacing the
false zero with the full commutant class makes the result stronger and correct.

frame transformation equal to the identity at a point but with nonzero first
jet changes its value.  Curvature and `nabla P` are the tensorial successors.
