# Hostile review: K77 gamma-trace graph dynamics gate

## Verdict

`SCOPED_SURVIVES`. The exact rank result survives, but it supports a narrower
conclusion than “the graph is a dynamical physical carrier.” It is an
action-active **non-closed** reduction candidate. No BV, physical, index or
count promotion is licensed.

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The full-rank induced Green matrix proves a nonzero local restricted kinetic
bilinear. It does not prove hyperbolicity, positivity, a Green inverse, a
closed domain or a physical propagating mode. Its rank 128 at the tested null
covector actually means this restricted symbol does not inherit the parent
null characteristic there; because receiver closure already fails, that fact
cannot yet be priced as a physical dispersion law.

Likewise, fourteen full-rank current matrices do not say every nonzero pair of
barred/unbarred amplitudes sources the connection. Indefinite bilinears can
vanish on special pairs. The supported claim is only that the current map is
not identically zero and generic zero-fermion stationarity does not extend for
free.

## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend a superseded object

Building a BV complex directly on the 128 graph would rigorously analyze the
wrong carrier: the full principal Euler residual misses its receiver by rank
128 in every tested causal stratum. The v0.157 algebraic graph remains exact,
but its role as a standalone field domain is superseded by this receiver
failure.

The local `diag(eta_i B,B)` pairing is sufficient to type the independent bar
as an action covector. It must not be promoted to the unbuilt global
Hodge/Krein/reality adjoint or to a positive Hilbert majorant.
