## Charge 1 — where the summary can outrun the artifact

global K77 BV manifold, solve the classical master equation beyond the minimal
closed algebra, classify physical cohomology, or produce a closed operator
domain. B2C9 supplies the action-level first-jet Ward prior art; this Run
composes rather than re-derives the full native action.

spinor factor of spinor-valued forms. A larger symmetry that acts on form slots
could reduce the multiplicity commutant, but it would be a distinct source-
owned construction, not ordinary `Gau(P)`.
