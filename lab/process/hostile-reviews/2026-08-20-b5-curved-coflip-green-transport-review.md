# Hostile review

conormal boundary coefficient and its algebraic coflip covariance, but does
not supply a boundary/end geometry, trace topology, Calderon projector,
maximal-isotropic subspace, closed range, or global realization for the
noncompact ultrahyperbolic ambient operator. The artifact preserves that
boundary and routes the next work to a named end model rather than claiming
domain closure.

The coflip statement is also covariance `B_n -> B_(t n)`, not invariance on an
arbitrary fixed hypersurface. A boundary is coflip-invariant only when its
normal and end data are preserved. The result states this explicitly.

prevents transfer of the two non-null inverse statements to characteristic

most tempting overextensions.

retains the null adverse class. It also plants an illicit mass contribution
