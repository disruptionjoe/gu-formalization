The exact local mathematics survives. Given a supplied source-`Y` horizontal

```text

```

`nabla q_H` term, and a nowhere-null `q_H` makes the Clifford factor invertible
on both ambient halves. A nonzero null section retains the bundle type but
drops to half rank, while `q_H=0` kills the adapter.

The reverse calculation also survives under its declared local density,
pairing, metric, and connection conventions. With `B`-skew Clifford action,

```text
(gamma(q_H)d0)^times alpha
  = gamma(q_H) nabla^a alpha_a
    + gamma(nabla^a q_H) alpha_a.
```

The principal and lower terms have the same convention sign; the source-
shaped outer minus negates both together. The lower term is forced by the
Green identity and cannot be deleted, sign-flipped independently, or confused
with the algebraic symbol dual. This is a formal density transpose, not an
identification of the source star, an analytic adjoint, a reality map, or a
relation among the independent barred fields.

The adverse result is path-decisive. A generic source-`Y` first jet of `q_H`
has normal variation and gives a nonzero additional zero-order map on the
pure-normal H210 Z port. Thus a generic source-`Y` horn is **killed as an
isolated reverse-H210 completion**: H210 is no longer the sole zero-order
`Z -> nu` route. It is not killed as a well-typed full formal transpose.

There is an exact escape condition,

```text

L_q(z) = sum_i gamma(nabla^i q_H) z_i.
```

This condition is necessary and sufficient for the new derivative-generated
lower term to vanish on the declared Z subbundle. `nabla_N q_H=0` is a
sufficient stronger condition; full `nabla q_H=0` is stronger still. These
are mathematically coherent conditional branches. Nothing in CB-2--CB-8,
however, supplies the source-`Y` split, the connection on `q_H`, or either
vanishing condition. The exact kernel condition is therefore a useful
classifier but, in this lane, an additional stipulated horn rather than an
emergent compatibility result.

Consequently, a source-`Y`, nowhere-null, vertically parallel branch is a
coherent **local conditional full-operator type** with the reverse lower term
kept wherever it remains on horizontal one-forms. It is not a global source
operator theorem and is low fertility: it requires more unowned structure
than the original H210 horn, and seeking an owner for that structure is
explicitly off limits. The isolated zero-order CB-2--CB-6 H210 chain remains
the strongest banked result.
