## Two mandatory charges

### 1. Where does the summary outrun the artifact?

Six candidate overclaims were rejected or narrowed:

1. **“The Krein construction fails.”** Too broad. Only a multiplicative sign
   extension of the free two-field spectral involution fails on the declared
   `theta h^2` vertex. General field mixing, nonlinear grading and nonlocal
   interacting `C` remain open.
2. **“The full cubic action was tested.”** False. The owned term is the
   constant-`theta` TT expansion of `(a+beta theta)R`. Other ambient K77 cubic
   vertices remain unassembled.
3. **“All local theories fail.”** Too broad. The theorem covers the constant
   mode of finite local derivative completions with fixed zero-order
   coefficient. Boundary/global constraints are intentionally outside it.
4. **“The global horn solves the cosmological-constant problem.”** Rejected.
   It screens a constant source by a supplied projector and therefore exposes,
   rather than solves, the domain/measure-selection burden.
5. **“The projector is GU-native.”** Rejected. It is unique only after a
   finite domain and invariant measure class are supplied; the source does not
   supply them.
6. **“The new functional is P2.”** Rejected. Its type is now explicit, but no
   identification map to the existing unknown P2 has been constructed.

### 2. Where is rigor defending a superseded object?

The inherited phrase “full odd super-IG interacting cohomology” was the
superseded object. The source-corrected repository already separated algebraic
super-IG descent from action symmetry. Continuing the odd BV campaign would
have solved a stricter problem than the source asserts while leaving the real
interaction-positivity question untouched.
