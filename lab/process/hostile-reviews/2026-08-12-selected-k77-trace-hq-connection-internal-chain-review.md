## Charge 1 — where does the summary outrun the artifact?

Three tempting summaries are rejected.

1. Exact `D H_q=0` at the K77 split-spin layer does not prove the operative
   full `varpi` connection is this reduced connection.
2. `Spin(6)xSpin(3)` contains an abstract SM Lie algebra, but subgroup
   dimensions do not recover hypercharges or the chiral 16-state branching.
3. A rank-nine connection defect is neither a four-real scalar doublet nor an
   action-selected Higgs merely because both are related to symmetry breaking.

The result is therefore connection compatibility plus a scoped shortcut kill,
not action-parent or Higgs construction.

## Charge 2 — where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The first draft of the inference could have reused trace q as the repository's
known Pati-Salam breaking vector.  Prior art blocks that move.  Trace q is in
the normal vector `(1,2,2)` and preserves diagonal `SU(2)` inside `Spin(4)`;
the established SM-breaking vector is in `(4,1,2)` and preserves `SU(2)_L`
while generating the hypercharge combination.

The exact real-linear intersection has dimension `9`, not `12`.  This is not a
refutation of `SC-GRP-03`, because the source claim uses full Pati-Salam
intersected with `U(3,2)`, not a fixed-trace shortcut.  Rigor must now defend
the source-typed intersection rather than an easier but mistyped stabilizer.
