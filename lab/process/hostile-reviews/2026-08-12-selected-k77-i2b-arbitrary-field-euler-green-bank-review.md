## Charge 1: where could the summary outrun the artifact?

“The Higgs has no kinetic term in GU” outruns the certificate.  The exact zero
is for the `S_q/H_q` residual family, selected real bilinear, fixed geometry,
and current selected parent.  Moving Hodge/Shiab/projector/section contact

The claim that the arbitrary Euler bank is complete is also limited to
connection variations.  It does not include independent metric, section,
epsilon, matter, or background variations.

## Charge 2: is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The initial expectation that the curvature-principal bank must be nonzero was
mistyped.  The principal response operator is nonzero, but the Green
coefficient is its pairing with the background residual.  Exact orthogonality
can kill the latter without killing the former.  The repaired probe keeps
those objects separate and adds nonzero principal self-pairing and off-family
Green controls.

The complex-bilinear comparator is not the action.  The probe uses its real
part, as required by v0.206; otherwise intermediate imaginary coefficients
would be mistaken for a real field covector.
