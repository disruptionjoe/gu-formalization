# Hostile review: P3 normal/tangential support

## Verdict

`CURRENT_NORMAL_SUPPORT_DIAGONAL_FALSIFIED__ABSTRACT_CLASS_RESULT_SURVIVES_RESCOPED__REPLACEMENT_ROUTES_OPEN`.

The candidate connection diagonal does not exist on the bases currently
written in the packet. The horizontal source bundle restricts trivially to
P3's vertical normal four-cycle, whereas the nontrivial P3 horn has `c2=1`.
This kills the present support identification, not all P3-like data and not a
future source-owned soldering construction.

## Three charges

### 1. Where does the summary outrun the artifact?

v0.147's phrase “one P3 orientation matches the positive chiral spin bundle”
is correct only on an abstract common model `S4`. Reading it as an actual
support map outruns the artifact. The prior calculation did not include
`pi o i_N`; after that map is restored, the tangential class pulls back to
zero.

The inverse overreach would be “P3 cannot work.” The exact kill covers the
current framed normal-cycle support and internal-gauge identification. A
tangential support datum or a newly built horizontal-normal soldering is not
tested.

### 2. Where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

Further instanton-moduli or homogeneous-connection calculations on one
abstract `S4` would defend the wrong object. Before connection equality, the
two bundles must live over the same actual base. Their equal rank and equal
model-space `c2` do not repair a constant pullback map.

Calling the planted horizontal-normal permutation “gauge” would also mistype
the repair. It changes the exterior-form carrier and observation projector;
it is soldering/reduction data and must be action-owned.
