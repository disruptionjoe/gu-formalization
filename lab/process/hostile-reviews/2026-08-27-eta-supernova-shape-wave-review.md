actual thirteen-dimensional link. It does not. The proof uses a chosen
trivialization, product metric and product horizontal distribution on the fixed
`RP3 x S6` model. The global normal family over `P(TX)`, its spin/Pin lift,
horizontal distribution and base integral remain unconstructed and are stated
as such in the title, typed block, scope, proof and register consequence.

does neither. The additive magnitude/H0 direction is projected out exactly;
the tested object is the existing reconstruction-grade `M2=8`, `f0=0.125`
background, not a source-owned GU action or the whole model family.

## Strongest contrary construction or mistyping

The old reflection shortcut is not accepted as a Spin-family proof. The
coordinate reflection is orientation reversing on `S6`; without extra Pin and
orientation data it cannot be called a Spin automorphism. The product-family
argument instead uses invertibility, chirality and zero horizontal curvature.

For the supernova leg, changing `Omega_m` is the strongest simple alternative.
It does not rescue the comparator: the same-`Omega_m` LCDM control is better by
`19.20577`, while the SN-only best flat-LCDM fit is better by `21.13734`.
The result is therefore shape-driven at this comparator ceiling.

## Weakest reproducibility or propagation seam

The DES release files are external and are not vendored. Reproducibility is
bounded by exact release commit, paths and SHA-256 digests; the executable fails
closed on a digest mismatch and replays the official inverse-covariance and
offset-marginalization prescription. The result artifact carries all regression
numbers. A later release must be treated as a new input, not silently substituted.

The eta certificate is a scope/theorem-premise certificate rather than a
computer proof of Bismut superconnection theory. Its value is that every invoked
premise is explicit and the global promotion is mechanically rejected.
