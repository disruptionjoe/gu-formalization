# Hostile review: P3 characteristic-class amplitude selector

## Verdict

`SURVIVES_AS_CONDITIONAL_CONSTRUCTION__CURRENT_P3_SELECTION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

The exact theorem is sound: on a fixed nonzero characteristic sector, a
curvature family `F_t=(t^2/3)Omega` has only finitely many allowed nonzero
amplitudes. The proposed disposition is narrowed twice. The current P3 object
is an auxiliary operator twist, not the source connection, and the even
characteristic equation cannot choose the sign.

## Three charges

### 1. Where does the summary outrun the artifact?

“P3 selects the GU vacuum” is false. The artifact proves an implication after
adding a diagonal characteristic-class ownership map and a fixed nonzero
pairing. Neither is in the source or current action packet. “Topology predicts
dark energy” is also false: the selected `t` is not yet converted to an
observed dimensionful density or tested cosmologically.

### 2. Where could rigor defend a mistyped object?

The strongest danger is identifying the BPST curvature on `H_n` with `F_B`
because both are curvatures on the same framed cycle. They act on different
bundles and enter different operators. A second danger is calling a local
coefficient a Chern number before global descent. A third is treating the
normalization `C_B` as fixed while quietly fitting the invariant polynomial,
embedding or cycle scale.

### 3. If this stands, what else changes?

- `LT-GR1`: **needs-recheck**—a discrete global sector may select magnitude,
  but observed Hilbert stress and the common domain remain open.
- `LT-GR2b`: **survives**—the one-amplitude curvature/distortion family is the
  object being constrained.
- `LT-GR2c`: **needs-recheck**—topological selection is a live external horn,
  not an action-derived source equation.
- `LT-GR2d`: **needs-recheck**—fixedness can be replaced by a discrete sector,
  but vacuum-shift response and units remain open.
- `LT-GR6`: **needs-recheck**—the characteristic sector must descend through
  observation and the physical domain.
- all other rows, P1/P2/P3 assignments and canon verdicts: **survive unchanged**.

## Required lenses

- **Layer-0 semantics:** passes only for the conditional implication; rejects
  auxiliary-twist/source-connection identity.
- **Prior art:** the datum and general flux mechanism already existed. The
  delta is their typed application to amplitude magnitude.
- **Chern--Weil topology:** the degree-two scaling and finite-root result pass.
  The native pairing and embedding index remain uncomputed.
- **Analytic/Krein:** no claim about a closed domain, determinant, positivity
  or spectrum is licensed.
- **Symplectic/BV--BFV:** the sector constraint is not a quotient; it must be
  preserved by the eventual bulk-boundary differential.
- **Source:** `SOURCE_SILENT`; no Eric quotation owns this bridge.

## Dissent

The strongest dissent says the route is too constructed to count as GU. That
is fair if the diagonal map is added solely to hit a desired value. The answer
is not to dismiss supplied structure reflexively but to price it: preregister
the finite embeddings, compute `C_B` blind to the target amplitude, and demand
that the same datum satisfy other independent rows. Without that positive
constraint surplus, the route is merely a topological restatement of a fit.

No trap is added. The auxiliary-versus-source curvature error is already an
instance of the standing Layer-0 homonym/type rule.
