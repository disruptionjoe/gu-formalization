## Required lenses

### Layer-0 semantics

The endpoint covector, its evaluation on a named infinitesimal orbit, and the
moment map are distinct objects. Residual adjoint motion and primitive epsilon
endpoint motion coincide generically only if a soldering theorem says so; no
such theorem is present. The split is load-bearing and exact.

### Prior art

v0.101 supplies the boundary-horn theorem and v0.102 supplies the generic
residual moment map `[Theta,P]`. v0.112 supplies the actual branches. This wave
composes those results; it does not replace them. In particular, the nonzero
generic v0.102 fixture survives outside the aligned `Phi1` line.

### Symplectic geometry

Evaluating the branch covector on the actual orbit is the correct test. The
edge extension has unique coefficients `(-1,+1)`, rank four and kernel two,
so the finite local characteristic reduction is not vacuous. It is still not
a global BFV phase space or a selected polarization.

### Variational bicomplex

Integration by parts creates an endpoint functional of independent epsilon
traces. A bulk Noether identity does not erase it. Conversely, its nonzero
coefficient does not prove a charge for an adjoint orbit on which the
Hamiltonian evaluates to zero.

### Gauge and differential geometry

The aligned branch lies in the stabilizer of its own momentum, so the adjoint
moment map vanishes. Derivative-bearing endpoint transformations act through
independent boundary values and remain charged. Calling both simply “gauge”
would recreate the type error.

### Representation and Clifford algebra

The exhaustive 16,384-generator check is stronger than a selected-basis test,
while the planted misalignment proves the zero is branch-specific. This does
not identify the Spin-native carrier, the two-half `U(32,32)` product or full
`U(64,64)` as the action parent.

### Analytic, PDE and Krein domain

No trace theorem, closed maximal domain, Green inverse, hyperbolicity,
reflection positivity or functional measure follows. The rank-14 endpoint
bank is a finite coefficient-space fact only.

### Source criticism
