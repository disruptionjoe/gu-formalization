# Hostile review: real-structure projection candidate

## Pre-review charge

The exciting reading was that v0.202's complex solution descends after a
canonical real projection.  The review tried to kill both “descent” and
“canonical.”  It killed the additive-cohomology story and the frame-independent
promotion, while preserving one exact local q13 witness.

## Lens findings

- **Layer 0:** a fixed-output projection, a real restriction and a naturally
  transported action map are different.
- **Prior art:** v0.203 proves the unmodified full pointwise map excludes the
  target at q13 and q12.  This review does not retract that theorem; it tests a
  new post-composed map.
- **Source criticism:** the source owns the residual action grammar and moving
  conjugated ingredients, not this `H_q` projection.
- **Real/Galois descent:** additive `H^1` is zero by averaging.  Naming a
  nonzero class before checking equivariance was a type error.
- **Clifford/Krein:** the target is fixed and the exact map has mixed reality;
  `rank(P_+A)=170`, `rank(P_-A)=195`.
- **Category/functoriality:** fixed points do not commute with the unmodified
  selected image.  Post-composing with fixed points changes the morphism.
- **Principal-bundle geometry:** q13 admission plus q12 failure refutes naive
  naturality.  A moving compensator remains a legitimate but unbuilt escape.
- **Variational bicomplex:** no action variation owns `P_+`; no Euler or
  preboundary equation follows.
- **Symplectic geometry:** no presymplectic or BV reduction follows from a
  pointwise projected image.
- **Analytic PDE:** no domain, propagator, spectrum, positivity or vacuum is
  inferred.
- **Contrary path:** degree-shifted reality, alternate Shiab and compensator
  transport remain open.

## Three charges

### 1. Where does the summary outrun the artifact?

“The complex solution has a real descent” outruns the artifact.  The artifact
says a different real-valued map, `P_+A`, contains the target at one frame.  It
does not say the original map has a fixed-point preimage or that the new map is
natural or action-owned.

### 2. Where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The proposed `H^1(Z/2,ker A)` route defended a cohomology class that cannot be
nonzero for additive real vector-space coefficients in characteristic zero.
The correct diagnostic is whether the operator intertwines the involutions;
the exact rank-390 realified defect says it does not.

### 3. If this stands, what else must change?

- v0.202 “complex succeeds, real fails” interpretation — **needs-recheck:**
  retain the ranks, replace additive-descent language by nonintertwining.
- v0.203 unmodified-map exclusion — **survives** exactly.
- `P_+A` physical supplier — **needs-recheck:** local q13 witness only; q12
  naturality fails.
- moving compensator and alternate target reality — **survive** open.
- RA-E1, RA-E3, LT-SM6 — **needs-recheck:** migrate distance/evidence only.
- P1/P2/P3, canon, verdict counts and public posture — **survive** unchanged.
- Dissolved items — **none**.

## Controls that fired

1. The expected “both components fail, hull succeeds” pattern was refuted:

3. The held-out q12 control refuted the strongest naturality reading.
4. The anti-fixed component excludes the target, proving projection does not
