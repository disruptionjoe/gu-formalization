establishes a general local theorem conditional on a real invertible `A(q)` and
an already supplied symmetric graph. The actual nonlinear selected-action
coefficient, bundle overlaps, Grassmann completion and closed domain have not
been assembled. The summary is narrowed accordingly.

is its independent-dual anti-dualizer specialization. Second, the old H-linear
K95 APS/Calderon reconstruction is a different carrier and cannot certify a
real-K77 analytic domain.
