# Hostile review: B5 strict-eight-block plus Euler separation

## Strongest overclaim

The separation does not construct an action. A strict exact complex and a
separate symmetric full-nine Hessian can coexist simply because the current
packet contains no map relating them. Calling that coexistence an
"action-owned completion" would replace the missing bridge with notation.
The result correctly reports compatibility plus nontransfer, not completion.

The full-nine Hessian witness is also not the historical preferred Shiab and
not a source-derived B5 operator. It is an exact counterexample to coefficient
transfer: nilpotence of the strict complex cannot normalize data that live on
the separate Hessian object.

## Strongest contrary route

A filtered field/antifield graph embedding could change the folded support and
place an apparent `S -> S` term inside a larger BV differential. That route is
not excluded. It requires an explicit embedding, degree bookkeeping and a
master-equation proof; none currently exists. The present result therefore
chooses the strict separation as the executable route without claiming the
alternative impossible.

## Mistyping attack

The most dangerous substitution is

```text
ordinary symmetric Hessian  ==  nilpotent BV differential.
```

It is false without a specified odd symplectic form, action and Hamiltonian
vector-field construction. The artifact keeps `d`, `Q8`, `H9`, `G` and the
missing action bridge separate and assigns each equation to exactly one of
them.

A second mistyping would carry the full-nine Euler family's
`EXTERNAL-VIA-GRAM` formal-adjoint verdict over to the strict eight-block
differential. No bridge licenses that transfer. The review requires the two
field-(iii) statuses to remain distinct.

## Reproducibility and controls

The exact probe passes `26/26` with rational arithmetic. Its negative controls
break a strict middle arrow, reject a planted `SS` cochain block, and delete
the Euler `SS` term while leaving strict exactness unchanged. These controls
test both the strict-complex failure path and the object-separation claim.

The probe reuses the prior exact acyclic complex and Stage-B rank-two Hessian
as deliberate reproduction-before-use. It does not claim novelty for either
witness; the new result is their typed nontransfer and the packet
restatement.
