## Dissent

The construction can be criticized as tautological: constrain by the exact
minimal-polynomial defect and the defect vanishes. That criticism is decisive
only if constraint surplus is nonpositive or the condition has no independent
compatibility burdens. Here it pays rank 128 and still owes Green, overlap,
BFV, nonlinear and source-selection constraints. The present success is
therefore informative but weak: compatibility was not guaranteed, yet most of
the surplus has not been tested.
