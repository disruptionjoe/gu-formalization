## Charge 1 — where does the summary outrun the artifact?

The relative theorem proves nonemptiness and global invariant-trace patching.
It does not construct a Fréchet/Hilbert manifold of all sections, a smooth
slice, a common bulk evolution domain or a quantum measure. “Globalizes the
edge construction” is acceptable only with the word **topologically**.

Likewise, the identity section proves that the configuration space is
nonempty. It does not freeze the edge field to identity. Doing so would erase
the target-gauge compensation that v0.102 and v0.114 require.

## Charge 2 — where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

v0.72 and v0.102 used `u -> u h` correctly for the active target-gauge action,
then reused its shape as a patch law. Those are different objects. When read
passively as `u_j=u_i g_ij`, the formula is a global trivialization and is not
available on every principal bundle. Exact local rank and moment-map tests
could not detect that topological obstruction.

The repair is not to replace `u` by an ordinary section of `Ad(P)` without
comment. It is a relative isomorphism from the physical copy to the `A0`
reference copy. Its local conjugation law is a consequence of that two-sided
typing. This preserves the reason the right target action is characteristic.

  the hidden homonyms. The repair passes only after they are separated.
- **Prior art:** the source and repository already own `P_H`, `A0` and the
  local dressing; the relative topology theorem itself is new.
- **Differential topology:** a principal bundle has a global section iff it is
  trivial. A section of `Isom(P,Q)` exists iff `P` and `Q` are isomorphic.

- **Complex/path integral:** no contour, reflection positivity, determinant or
