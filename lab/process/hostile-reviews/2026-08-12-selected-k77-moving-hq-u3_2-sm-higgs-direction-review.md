## Charge 1 — where does the summary outrun the artifact?

The first summary said the exact 16-state branch was already certified by a
prior script.  That script constructs the standard list and argues conjugacy;
it does not restrict the actual chiral spin representation.  The new probe was
repaired to compute the half-signed chiral-spin weights directly.  The 16-state
claim then survives.

Three further overreaches are rejected:

The strongest surviving claim is a Higgs-carrier result, not a Higgs-action
result.

1. Four real carrier components do not constitute a physical Higgs without an
   action-owned radial field.
2. The existence of `J` does not select one member of the 20-dimensional
   `O(6,4)/U(3,2)` family.
3. The finite breaking pattern does not derive a potential, VEV, mass,
   positivity, domain or quantum theory.

## Charge 2 — where is rigor defending a mistyped object?

v0.195 correctly found a dimension-nine joint stabilizer but compared it to
the 12-dimensional *pre-Higgs* SM and therefore treated it as evidence against
the construction.  Once the source's `U(3,2)` intersection is inserted first,
the twelve-dimensional group is exact; fixing q then leaves an algebra with
derived dimension eight and center dimension one.  The same nine is the
post-Higgs `SU(3)xU(1)` stabilizer.  The arithmetic survived; the stage label
did not.

This repair does not identify trace q with the independent `(4,1,2)` `v_PSB`.
It places q later in the chain, inside the weak doublet of the already formed
SM.  An explicit conjugator between repository coordinate presentations is
still useful but not required for the existence-grade theorem.
