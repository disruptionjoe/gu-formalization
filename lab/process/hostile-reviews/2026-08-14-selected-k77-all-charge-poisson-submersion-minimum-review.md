   vertical-dimension inequality.

   Poisson maps at singular charges are retained as a weaker, separately typed
   class and are not excluded.
4. **The mathematical carrier became a physical edge theory.** Rejected. No
   source/action ownership, boundary law, domain or quantization is supplied.
