# Hostile review

## Verdict

`SCOPED_PASS__EXACT_WITNESS_OBSTRUCTION_ONLY`.

The computation survives as an exact obstruction to invariant `W` and
`W-plus-mirror` carriers for the existing q-repaired family under any freely
ranging parent that contains the tested witness. It does not survive as a
no-go for the complete source operator, an action-selected smaller orbit, BV
cohomology, or domain selection.

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The raw result could be summarized as “zero order cannot select the carrier.”
That is too wide. The probe uses one fixed observation split, one nonzero
connection one-form sample, the shared q-repaired middle family, and one
direction from each parent class. A witness is sufficient to refute invariance
under an unrestricted parent, but not to show that the selected action permits
that direction or that a different zero-order adapter cannot work.

Disposition: **needs-recheck** for the action-owned orbit; **survives** for the
finite witness obstruction.

## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend a superseded or mistyped object

`W` is a representation subspace of the one-form carrier. The source's
equation 9.16 is a four-field sixteen-cell candidate, and a physical carrier is
a cohomology/domain object. Treating those as the same object would repeat the
repo's standard Layer-0 failure. Likewise, source-full `U(64,64)`, two
`U(32,32)` halves, moving Spin, and the quaternionic physical gauge group are
not interchangeable group labels.

Disposition: the standalone-invariant-subspace demand **survives** as a
necessary test only when the parent acts freely; the claimed physical-carrier
interpretation is **dissolved**.

## Charge 3 — what else changes if this stands

- **survives:** v0.135's principal result; the principal symbol recognizes
  natural modules but cannot distinguish `W` from its mirror.
- **needs-recheck:** the connection-parent choice must be derived from the
  selected action rather than inferred from the source's largest named group.
- **needs-recheck:** the complete four-field/BV complex may cancel or quotient
  the leakage that the one-form family exposes.
- **dissolved:** the recorded next step “assemble the exact zero-order family,”
  because that family was prior art and has now been composed with the carrier.
- **survives:** no verdict, residue, quotient, datum, or canon claim moves.

## Required lenses

- **Layer-0 semantics:** passed after separating matrix, family, invariant
  subspace, doubled closure, and physical cohomology.
- **Prior art:** passed; novelty is only the W/mirror/parent composition.
- **Analytic:** scoped; finite exact ranks do not prove a closed domain,
  spectrum, Green operator, index, or K-definite physical space.
- **Symplectic:** scoped; no presymplectic or BV quotient is inferred.
- **Representation theory:** passed for the three explicit projectors and
  parent witnesses; no claim over all possible lower-order intertwiners.
- **Variational/PDE:** open; action ownership of the allowed connection orbit

## Repeatability traps

1. “Leakage into the mirror” is not the same as “closure of W plus mirror.”
   The explicit outside-pair rank must be computed. It is 64 at the best ratio.
2. A one-direction counterexample kills invariance under an unrestricted
   parent but not an action-selected orbit that may omit that direction.
3. Equal W/mirror fingerprints do not turn a 384-dimensional direct sum into a
   physical quotient.

No new path-dependency chain is needed; these traps are local to the recorded
carrier-selection scope correction and are encoded in the probe and audit.

## Dissent

The strongest dissent is variational: the selected action may own a much
smaller connection orbit than any named parent algebra, so algebra-wide
invariance may be the wrong physical demand. The result remains valuable
because it turns that dissent into the next exact construction burden rather
than treating “action selection” as a verbal escape.
