# Hostile review: SR-1C action-owned top-symbol reduction

## Strongest overclaim

The rank-thirteen block is the principal symbol of the action-owned primitive
epsilon row, not the full `j^1(E_B-E_T)` evaluator and not a physical mode
count. The result must not be described as completion of the common 196-row
`O_SR1C` bank.

## Strongest ownership objection

The earlier rank-thirteen calculation belonged to the source-printed rival.
Reusing its matrix is legal only after independently deriving that the actual
action Euler has the same top block. The new derivation supplies that bridge:
the half-adjoint contribution squares to zero at exterior principal grade,
leaving minus one-half times the already-exact selected-Shiab matrix. Lower
orders remain different and untransferred.

## Strongest contrary cancellation

The selected K77 tensor coefficient of the remaining metric `epsilon^3` slot
could vanish through covariance, Bianchi identities or moving-Shiab
cancellation. The scalar order control proves only that source order does not
force that vanishing. It is therefore retained as admitted and uncomputed,
not declared nonzero.

## Disposition

Accept the branch-independent action-owned rank-thirteen primitive top block
and the structural zero certificates for metric `g^4` and `varpi^3`. Reject
completion of `O_SR1C`, a branch kill, stationary background, physical
cohomology, positivity or superposition.
