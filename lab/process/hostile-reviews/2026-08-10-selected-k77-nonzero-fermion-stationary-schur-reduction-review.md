## Pre-review

- **Fork assumed:** selected real K77 only as a conditional construction lane;
  no full-`U(64,64)` versus moving-Spin versus two-`U(32,32)` selection.
- **Search space:** the finite theorem is wholesale under two rank hypotheses;
  the actual source coefficient image and domain are not.
- **New un-owned object:** the actual source-owned residual map and reality on
  the 64-dimensional defect; no new datum is admitted.
- **What dies:** automatic stationarity from the southeast zero and selection
  by plain coefficient conjugation, not every source reality or operator.

## Required and adversarial lenses

**Layer-0 semantics:** The displayed matrix, stationary kernel, principal
characteristic kernel, BV cohomology, closed-domain zero modes, Fredholm index
and generation count remain separate. `64` is a residual dimension, not a
count.

**Prior art:** The principal discriminator already supplies exact 192/128
sector dimensions and equal desired/mirror characteristic fingerprints. The
zero-fermion Hessian wave proves why this branch is separate. Neither contains
the stationary quotient theorem or actual draft-9.16 lower-order map.

**Analytic/operator:** A finite kernel does not establish a closed operator,
normalizable mode, Green identity or Fredholm index. The result is an algebraic
front-end for that future construction.

**Symplectic/BV--BFV:** `ker C` and `coker B` are ordinary linear-algebra
objects. They are not a gauge quotient, reduced presymplectic space or BFV
cohomology; no scoped quotient is added.

**Variational/fermion parity:** A nonzero solution of the odd classical Euler
equation is not automatically a Lorentz-invariant fermion condensate. The
coupled bosonic Euler equation and fermion-parity interpretation remain open.

## Charge 1: where the summary outruns the artifact

Calling the next calculation "the K77 64-by-64 operator" would overrun the
artifact. The `64` follows only if the actual zero-order `B` is injective and
`C` surjective. The prior characteristic rank `224` does not establish either
separate rank. The safe statement is "the exact residual size on the maximal-
offdiagonal-rank horn."

Likewise, equal nullities are proved for ordinary coefficient-conjugate maps
with real `B/C`. The source extraction explicitly keeps barred and unbarred
fields independent before reality. The result does not kill a different
source `C`-reality, Krein projection or domain asymmetry.

## Charge 2: where rigor could defend a superseded or mistyped object

Using the 96-dimensional characteristic kernel as the stationary kernel would
defend the principal symbol against a lower-order question it cannot answer.
Using a finite c-number kernel as a physical fermion vacuum would similarly
mistype an odd field configuration. Both shortcuts are rejected.

The full `U(64,64)` parent and the two `U(32,32)` halves are not interchangeable.
They share the tested principal derivative coefficient but can have different
lower-order cells and reality maps. No parent is promoted here.

## Dissent

Equivariance may force `B` or `C` to have nonmaximal rank, in which case the
64-by-64 horn is not the actual source problem. That does not falsify the
theorem; it makes the immediate next calculation the separate ranks and the
generalized residual complex. A second dissent is that the final antilinear
reality may exchange more than coefficients and evade the plain-conjugation
no-selector. Both are reasons to compute the source cells, not reasons to
promote this finite result.
