## Charge 1 — where could the summary outrun the artifact?

The result must not say that `D omega=0` erases all displayed connection
coefficients for a moving split.  It erases the intrinsic tensor
`K_omega=(D omega)omega/2`; frame coefficients can be transported by `d omega`.
The corrected artifact states this distinction.

It must also not turn zero local constraint surplus into a proof that no
multiplier theory exists.  A moving-`omega` multiplier can cancel either finite
Euler covector locally.  What is absent is an independently derived multiplier,
positive surplus, its own global Euler solution and preservation of the current
Higgs-like carrier.

## Charge 2 — where could rigor defend a mistyped object?

A fixed projector is not the tangent of a moving reduction.  Treating
`dS=0` after declaring `S` dynamical manufactures a kernel that the exact
compatible first-jet graph does not have.  Both moving graphs project
surjectively to the full connection tangent.

A penalty term is likewise not a multiplier.  At compatibility its first
variation is zero even when its Hessian is full rank.  Using Hessian rank to
claim cancellation of an existing Euler covector would defend the wrong
variational object.

Finally, the current Cl1 radial carrier is a conditional repository mapping,
not a unique authorial identification.  The review restricts the carrier-loss
claim accordingly.
