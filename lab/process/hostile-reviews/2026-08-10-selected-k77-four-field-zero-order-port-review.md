## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

“The zero-form field cancels the leakage” is too broad. The probe proves that
the leakage image lies in the ordinary connection-port image and that this
condition selects one projective ratio per parent class. It does not construct
a graph map `G`, check the lower-left equation, solve the nonlinear graph
identity, or derive a BV differential.

Disposition: necessary port inclusion **survives**; cancellation by a complete
four-field solution **needs-recheck**.

## Charge 2 — where rigor could defend a superseded or mistyped object

The port image is `span(a) tensor S` for every invertible spinor parent. Its
rank therefore cannot select moving Spin versus two halves versus full U. A
rigorous inclusion proof on one restricted parent would defend the wrong
object if reported as source-full closure. The source-full parent contains
both ratio requirements and remains conflicted.

Disposition: restricted-parent necessary gates **survive**; source-full parent
closure **needs-recheck**.
