# Hostile review: selected-K136 microlocal characteristic and local-domain obstruction

## Strongest overclaim attack

**Attack.** A singular frozen Fourier symbol does not prove that every boundary
value problem on every geometry is non-Fredholm. Compact domains discretize
frequency, pseudodifferential projectors may be nonlocal, anisotropic spaces can
change estimates, and a different background changes lower-order transport.

**Disposition.** Sustained and incorporated. K136 restricts its theorem to the
frozen translation-invariant whole-space operator and unbounded realizations
with local finite-order trace conditions and the same interior operator. It
explicitly leaves compact, nonlocal, anisotropic, different-background, and
new action-boundary-term routes open.

## Strongest contrary construction

**Attack.** Remove the 27 exact shell hypersurfaces from Fourier support and
invert on the complement.

**Disposition.** Rejected as a domain construction. Exact shells have measure
zero in `L2`; removing only them does not control normalized packets supported
in shrinking neighborhoods, whose residual tends to zero. Removing a full
neighborhood is a nonlocal spectral cutoff and is neither selected by the
local action nor tested for adjoint, covariance, and boundary compatibility.

## Strongest mode-typing attack

**Attack.** Calling all five quotient classes propagated physical waves would
confuse a frozen normal-symbol kernel with a solution bundle. Conversely,
calling the three `A`-visible classes constraints would infer propagation from
their algebraic compensation.

**Disposition.** Sustained and incorporated. The exact result is only two
`A`-null TT finite-symbol classes plus three `A`-visible compensated
finite-symbol classes. K132's tangential counterexample blocks automatic
propagation. No class is labeled physical, constraint, boundary, gauge, edge,
or cohomological beyond the four proved diffeomorphisms.

## Weakest reproducibility seam

**Attack.** The Weyl-sequence consequence is analytic and is not certified by
enumerating finitely many matrices.

**Disposition.** The probe certifies its finite hypotheses: all 27 predecessor
shells, a non-gauge `a=4` kernel, the exact nested null quotient, and the
continuity/locality premises explicitly used. The artifact states the standard
normalized shrinking-patch construction and narrows the conclusion to the
operator class for which that construction applies.

## Verdict

Pass at the stated ceiling. K136 closes the ordinary frozen unbounded local
Fredholm/Green route and exactly types the five gauge-reduced null symbol
classes. It does not construct or exclude every possible domain and does not
promote the classes to propagated or physical modes.
