## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The phrase “the selected-action Noether identity is complete” would overrun
the evidence in two ways. The new executable calculation proves the universal
moving pairing/density and variational-boundary theorem; ownership of the K77
coefficients comes from immutable predecessor receipts. It does not recompute
every full Clifford coefficient on the physical graph. The report is narrowed
to **local selected-action composition at exact receipt-plus-QQ theorem grade**.

Likewise, presymplectic basicness holds only for boundary-vanishing matched-q
transformations. The unrestricted transformation is a Hamiltonian boundary
symmetry with a live moment map, not a gauge direction already available for
quotient.
