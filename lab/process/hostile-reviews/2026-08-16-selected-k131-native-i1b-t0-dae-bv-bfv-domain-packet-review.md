# Hostile review — K131

## Strongest objections

The exact Green radical might be quotiented conormal by conormal, suggesting
that K131 has already built the desired reduced theory. Alternatively, one
could call every radical direction gauge and introduce enough ghosts to force
a BV complex.

Both moves exceed the data. The quotient dimensions jump from 24 to 22 on the
null stratum, so the fibrewise quotients do not form one regular vector bundle
across that jump. Nullity is also not a Noether generator. At `T=0` the known
distortion gauge column is zero, while the radical has dimension 196 or 198.

## Adversarial checks

1. Kernel, cokernel, radical and quotient dimensions are recomputed from the
   held `196 x 24` cross map; none is inferred from prose.
2. Fixed-rank reduction is stated as a quotient; arbitrary complements are
   not promoted to canonical projectors.
3. The two-dimensional null rank jump is retained and used only for the
   constant-rank bundle obstruction.
4. A same-normal-symbol toy pair proves that tangential coefficients can turn
   a zero left-null equation into a live constraint; normal rank alone does
   not prove propagation.
5. Metric diffeomorphism gauge, null TT characteristics and distortion
   radical are kept as three distinct objects.
6. The DN family satisfies all three live order equalities. Its common shift
   is not presented as physical freedom, and the `a=0` representative is a
   mapping scale rather than a selected domain.
7. The boundary class includes the second-order mixed `A` concomitant as well
   as K130's first-order distortion Green term.
8. Classical trace burdens are not silently applied to weak maximal graph
   domains.
9. Formal Euler-antifield slots are distinguished from a KT resolution;
   missing compatibility operators block nilpotency and reducibility claims.
10. No radical quotient is called physical BFV cohomology, and no global PDE,
    Fredholm, inverse, positivity or spectrum theorem is claimed.

## Verdict

Accept the exact stratified symbol quotient, the obstruction to one regular
cross-null radical bundle, the relative DN weights, and the formal
action-owned boundary/BV generator inventory. Reject a global propagated
constraint split, arbitrary distortion ghosts, regular BFV reduction, closed
domain, inverse or physical cohomology. K132 must supply the all-grade
tangential/subprincipal Noether complex and test regularity stratum by stratum.
