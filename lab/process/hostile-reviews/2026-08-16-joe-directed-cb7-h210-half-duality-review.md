# Hostile review — CB-7 H210 full-cell half/duality classifier

## Verdict

`PASS_AFTER_THREE_MATERIAL_SCOPE_REPAIRS`.

CB-7A's central result survives exactly. For every relevant displayed cell,
the derivative and H210 terms share the same row, column, and form-degree
incidence but have opposite ambient-half parity. Any common **homogeneous**
row/column primalizer contributes the same parity shift to both terms, so the
two typing equations reduce locally to `0=1`. Same-half duality types only the
derivative; opposite-half duality types only H210. No summand-blind homogeneous
half/degree convention makes even one complete cell homogeneous, and the same
obstruction occurs in both forward and both reverse-shaped cells.

CB-7B's current-Clifford classification also survives at pointwise
representation grade. The canonical tautological trace receiver is vertical,
not horizontal. On the H210 internal RS port, bare internal `q` leaks gamma
trace; right/Pin repair restores the RS condition but flips internal chirality
and breaks full PS to a stabilizer. A horizontal Clifford vector instead
commutes with internal PS, preserves internal chirality and the H210 RS
condition, and changes ambient parity. Applying it on the H210 side therefore
passes parity but misses the banked `144bar/144` source slots. Applying it only
after the derivative leaves the H210 source-module port untouched and is the
only current **algebraic parity-and-custody candidate**.

The phrase “surviving branch” requires three material qualifications:

1. the F2 theorem exhausts homogeneous parity primalizers, not arbitrary
   grading-destroying linear automorphisms or enlarged full-Dirac codomains;
2. a moving horizontal **line** alone does not define an untwisted map
   `S+ -> S-`, and the exact rank claim fails for a null generator; the horn
   needs a coherently transforming vector/covector section (or an explicit
   line-valued codomain) and, for retained rank, a nowhere-null section; and
3. `gamma(q_H) o d0` is currently certified only as a conditional pointwise
   bundle/principal-symbol type. A full forward operator needs the exact base,
   bundle, transition law, Clifford-compatible connection, and relation of the
   horizontal split to equation (9.16). Its reverse formal transpose is
   `d0* o gamma(q_H)*`; expanding it differentiates `q_H` and creates a
   lower-order `nabla q_H` term. The present probes do not test that term or a
   complete forward/reverse naturality square.

Accordingly, CB-7C's two parity classes are exact, and its CB-6 functor-order
ledger is sound, but its `D-ODD/OPP` row is not yet a completed four-cell
differential operator. It is a declared conditional principal-symbol/bundle-
type receiver. The isolated H210 zero-order CB-2--CB-6 chain remains banked;
`SC-GEN-53`, canon, and public posture do not move.

## Sixteen-lens hostile attack

### 1. Conditional-build scope — survives with a horn-name repair

The artifacts assume H210 and do not try to derive the action, family row,
background, selector, reduction, or quotient. Treating `q_H` as a declared
conditional input is allowed by the reverse scaffold. It must not be described
as already owned, selected, or built. Because a bare line does not define the
needed untwisted operator, the safe horn is not merely “a moving horizontal
line”; it is a **supplied compatible section and its transition data**, or an
explicitly line-twisted reformulation. This is a stronger conditional input,
not a new work queue to construct that datum.

### 2. Prior-art novelty and v0.140 transfer scope — survives

The 2026-08-04 SAT calculation concerned three principal classes in different
cells. CB-7A instead proves a local same-cell contradiction, so it is not a
rerun. Ledger v0.140 killed canonical trace-`q`/Pin degree primalization on the
rank-384 W/mirror closure by the upper joined-rank-`256` obstruction. That
result remains valid and remains carrier-scoped. CB-7B correctly tests the
current H210 internal port rather than importing the W/mirror kill wholesale.

The new H210-specific vertical/horizontal distinction is useful. CB-7C is
mostly integration: its new content is the source-slot comparison, reverse-
coherence ceiling, and preservation of CB-6 order, not a second independent
parity theorem.

### 3. Summand-blind F2/SAT completeness — survives after wording repair

The local equations

```text
h_i+r_i = h_j+c_j+0,
h_i+r_i = h_j+c_j+1
```

are contradictory for every common homogeneous row/column parity assignment.
This proves more than the finite `16` and `256` enumerations: degree-, slot-,
cell-, and half-dependent homogeneous flips all fail.

It does not literally enumerate arbitrary invertible maps with even and odd
parts, noninvertible projections that delete one summand, or a target enlarged
from one source-labelled half to full `S+ direct-sum S-`. Those moves destroy
or change the grading question and are different operator grammars. Therefore
“any invertible primalizer” must always mean “any invertible homogeneous
source-half primalizer in the declared F2 class.” Within that class the no-go
is exact and complete.

### 4. Product-grading semantics — survives

Both terms have the same form-degree shift. With
`G=(-1)^form J`, the derivative has product parity one while H210 has product
parity zero. Product grading diagnoses the mismatch; it does not repair it.
Ignoring ambient half can call both terms degree-odd only by discarding the
identity-grade section-11.2 target labels.

### 5. Current `Cl(7,7)` signs and raised/lowered conventions — survives with a null fence

The probe tests the four coordinate representatives of the Lorentzian

`g(q_H,q_H) != 0` pointwise. Null and vanishing strata remain adverse controls,
not silently excluded by the word “line.”

### 7. External-q RS, PS, rank, and both halves — partial

For a supplied horizontal covector, pointwise RS trace, internal Weyl custody,

### 8. `144/144bar` custody and H210-side branch — survives

Horizontal Clifford multiplication preserves internal chirality. Therefore
an H210 A-output remains `144bar`, but same-half row typing sends it toward the
source `zeta+` slot carrying `144`; the conjugate B-output fails analogously.
The H210-side branch is parity-SAT but source-module-mistyped. Internal q is
worse: bare left placement leaks RS, while right/Pin placement exchanges
internal chirality and reduces PS. Leaving H210 untouched is the only current
way to preserve the banked CB-2 source slots.

### 9. Forward/reverse density dual versus adjoint/reality — material downgrade

The source bars remain independent Berezin fields. A row primalizer and an
algebraic transpose do not impose field reality or prove a Hilbert/Krein
adjoint. CB-7C is correct on that boundary.

For a full differential operator, however, the reverse of

```text
D_q = gamma(q_H) o d0
```

cannot be certified by parity flags alone. Given compatible pairings, its
formal transpose has the order

```text
D_q^t = d0^t o gamma(q_H)^t.
```

When applied to a field, `d0^t` differentiates the moving coefficient and
produces a lower-order `nabla q_H` contribution. Whether that term lands in
the declared reverse source module, combines with the displayed zero-order
coefficient, and transforms on overlaps is open. If “reverse dual” means only
the algebraic transpose of the principal symbol, then the four-cell result is
principal-symbol coherence, not a complete reverse operator.

`gamma(q_H)` changes spin parity and not the `Omega0 -> Omega1` form degree.

For a supplied smooth section `q_H` of the same base bundle as `d0`,


artifacts do not decide whether `q_H` lives globally on the equation-(9.16)

