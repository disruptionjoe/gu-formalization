## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

“`H640` is not invariant” is too wide. It is exactly invariant under the three
observed spatial evolutions and is the correct ordinary-pullback graph in this
flat conditional model. The failure occurs only when transverse `Y^14`
directions are allowed to act nontrivially.

Likewise, a full rank-1920 ambient hull does not prove that a vertical Higgs
channel is impossible. It proves that such a channel cannot be represented by
ordinary pullback or by silently reusing the fixed `H640` graph.

field disappear: its transverse covector has pullback rank zero. Treating graph

restricted coefficients while every transverse direction still leaks rank 128.

The complete sixteen-cell lower-order system is therefore not yet the right
next solve on a fixed graph. Its first burden is to construct the moving
vertical adapter or accept the full ambient carrier.
