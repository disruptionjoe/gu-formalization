## Charge 1: where does the summary outrun the artifact?

“The central flux route is dead” is too broad.  The theorem closes the
**determinant/absolute first-Chern** route on the connected-Spin-induced
spinor bundle.  It does not classify every `U(64,64)` bundle, every lift of its
projective structure, every flat connection, or relative boundary cohomology.

It would also overreach to say the central connection is flat.  A connection
on a trivial line can have nonzero exact curvature.  The conclusion is only
that every closed-cycle first-Chern flux vanishes.

Finally, determinant triviality is not faithful on the scalar center:
`det(z I_128)=z^128`.  The finite kernel `mu_128` and any independently chosen
128th root are invisible.  This qualification is load-bearing and is now in
the result, probe and next gate.

## Charge 2: where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The dangerous object substitution is

```text
full U(64,64) frame group
    -> arbitrary U(64,64) bundle topology.
```

The source calls the object a unitary spinor-frame bundle.  A frame group says
which frame changes and connections are allowed; it does not erase the Spin
reduction or change the isomorphism class of the bundle being framed.  The
theorem is correctly restricted to that source-induced reading.

The second substitution is determinant line = scalar central root.  They are
related by the 128th power and are not equivalent global objects.  The result
keeps the root/finite-holonomy question open.

The third is torsion = curvature.  `H^2(RP^3;Z)=Z/2` while de Rham `H^2=0`.
The torsion class can support a flat line, not nonzero smooth curvature flux.
