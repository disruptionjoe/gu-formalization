# Hostile review: P3 replacement surplus

## Pre-wave

- **Fork assumed:** none.
- **Search space:** the two replacement families opened by v0.148; wholesale
  decision is possible only for natural zero-order H-N maps and universal
  tangential `c2=1`, not for action-derived reductions.
- **New un-owned object on success:** an admissible observation background or
  real action pairing if it is not already supplied by the source construction.
- **What dies:** calling an unowned H-N field cost-free, and treating the
  tangential chiral class as universally `+1`.

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

The exact zero intertwiner space is only a theorem about **natural zero-order
`SO(1,3)` maps**. It does not exclude a derivative-dependent map, symmetry
breaking, a solution-dependent moving reduction, or an action equation that
determines `u`. The report therefore says the H-N route has negative surplus
**as presently unowned**, not that geometry can never derive it.

The `S4/T4` control refutes universal `c2=1`; it does not prove that no
physically admissible background has `c2=1`. Likewise `*^2=-1` excludes a real
Lorentzian `+1` self-dual eigenspace, not the complex chiral `SL(2,C)` bundle or
its integral second Chern class.

## Charge 2 — where rigor may defend a superseded object

The compact `SU(2)+` language in the tangential-fork canon is Riemannian/Spin(4)
language. Applying it literally to the Lorentzian observation bundle would
defend the wrong real form. The current review keeps the complex Lorentzian
chiral bundle and the compact BPST model distinct. It also refuses to treat the
local principal Levi-Civita soldering already built in prior work as a global
`H -> N4` reduction; those maps have different domains and ownership.

## Charge 3 — what else must change if the result stands

- **Dissolved:** universal tangential `n=+1` selection from bundle identity;
  zero-cost unowned H-N soldering.
- **Survives:** a background-conditioned tangential identity; an action-derived
  H-N reduction; the v0.148 normal-support obstruction; all P1/P2/P3 assignments.
- **Needs recheck:** actual `c2(S_X^+)` on an admissible Lorentzian observation
  background; reality of the characteristic coupling in the selected action;
  global source ownership and the BV constraint surplus of any H-N field.

## Required lenses

- **Layer-0 semantics:** complex `SL(2,C)` chirality is not a real compact
  Euclidean `SU(2)+` self-duality condition.
- **Prior art:** the same-stratum weld and local physical soldering existed;

- **Analytic:** the real action pairing, closed domain and Lorentzian reality

- **Symplectic/BV:** pointwise fields count as residue until the action-derived
