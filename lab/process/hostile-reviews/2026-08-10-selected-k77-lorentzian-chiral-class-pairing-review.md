# Hostile review: Lorentzian chiral class and real pairing

## Pre-wave

- **Fork assumed:** none. Lorentzian `(1,3)` is tested as the source-confirmed
  observer sector without settling the ambient-signature or real-Clifford forks.
- **Search space:** absolute degree-four classes on closed Lorentzian spin and
  globally hyperbolic spacetimes, plus invariant real pairings on
  `sl(2,C)_R`. These families can be decided wholesale; relative APS/BFV data
  cannot.
- **New un-owned object on success:** a relative trivialization, boundary
  framing, compact-support condition or real-pairing ratio.

  tangential `c2` as though it were available on physical spacetime.

## Charge 1 — where the summary outruns the artifact

`H^4(R x Sigma3)=0` kills the **ordinary absolute bulk class only**. It does
not kill `H_c^4`, relative cohomology, APS transgression, framed bordism or a
boundary edge charge. The report scopes the kill accordingly. Conversely, the
closed calculation excludes `c2=+/-1`; it does not prove every absolute chiral
class zero, because multiples of 12 remain topologically possible.

The two-dimensional invariant-pairing calculation proves that real action
densities can be written. It does not prove their reality conditions,
stationarity, positivity, source ownership or quantum admissibility. “Real
pairing exists” must not be inflated to “the action is now real and selected.”

## Charge 2 — where rigor may defend a superseded or mistyped object

The old index exploration says Rokhlin applies only in Euclidean signature and
that simple connectivity is required. Those are incorrect scopes for the
topological divisibility theorem. What changes in Lorentzian signature is the
analytic Dirac problem (hyperbolic rather than elliptic), not the intersection
form or Rokhlin's theorem on a closed smooth spin manifold. The appended
correction preserves the old provenance while preventing it from defending a
superseded bypass.

The v0.149 `S4/T4` controls also defended a sign formula they could not test.
The K3-style nonzero-signature control catches the mismatch. Fortunately the
v0.149 conclusions that depended only on `S4=1`, `T4=0` and nonuniversality
survive unchanged.
