# Hostile review: K77 Zorro/DeWitt trace-curvature obstruction

- **Fork assumed:** authorial K77, explicitly.  The vertical theorem is also
  checked under the mirrored base sign and is identical.
- **Search space:** two named nonzero branches against one exact canonical
  connection-metric family.  It is decidable wholesale for that family, not
  for all unspecified Zorro completions.
- **New unowned object:** none.  A rescue would introduce or derive a different
  induced-`Y` metric/connection and must own it explicitly.

  `b_plus Phi1` and `b_minus Phi1` branches as backgrounds of the canonical
  Zorro/DeWitt connection metric.

## Charge 1: where does the summary outrun the artifact?

The tempting overstatement is “Zorro forbids both branches.”  The exact
artifact proves “the canonical connection-metric reconstruction forbids both
branches.”  Weinstein supplies the chain and its roles but not the coordinate
metric or uniqueness.  The report and registry retain that qualifier in their
status, result and successor.

The point-independence claim is licensed inside the reconstruction because the
pure-vertical metric jet is the same DeWitt homogeneous-space jet at every
fibre point after choosing normal coordinates.  It is not a claim about every
possible metric on `Y`.

## Charge 2: where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The old full jet was computed on `(9,5)`.  That could have made it stale after
the authorial K77 correction.  The new independent calculation uses base
signature `(1,3)`, certifies fibre `(6,4)` and total `(7,7)`, and proves exact
base-sign invariance of the vertical metric and curvature.  The result does
not rely on the old real spin carrier.

The frozen branch connection, the dependent `B(epsilon)`, and the independent
source connection `varpi` are not identified.  The comparison tests a necessary
condition for the first to instantiate the second.  Failure redirects the
vacuum search to `T=varpi-B_Z`; it does not remove `varpi` or the source action.

## Strongest attacks

1. **Could epsilon mix trace with traceless directions?**  It can rotate the
   internal value, but the gauge curvature law leaves the labelled tangent
   inputs fixed.  Zero on the natural trace plane remains zero.
2. **Could an observation/diffeomorphism relabel the plane?**  The metric-trace
   line is defined naturally at every fibre point.  A diffeomorphism transports
   the distribution rather than deleting its mixed-curvature zero.
3. **Could the full connection metric add an extrinsic vertical curvature?**
   In normal coordinates its pure-vertical LC block depends only on the fibre
   metric jet.  An independent contraction of the older full 14D Riemann jet
   returns no nonzero trace-mixed leg; the new calculation independently
   certifies the fibre block and its K77 sign port.
4. **Does this kill every native background?**  No.  It kills two homogeneous
   candidates for one reconstructed dependent connection.  A nonhomogeneous
   `T,varpi` solution or a separately derived Zorro completion remains open.
