# Hostile review

## Strongest overclaim

“The split singular atlas is complete” would be false. The result attaches one
generic opposite-sign split root. It does not attach same-sign/compact roots,
another `A3` real form, adjacent two-wall intersections, deeper orbit types or
zero charge. The artifact consistently states this narrower ceiling.

## Strongest typing attack

The earlier wall model lives in `so(7,7)*`, while the completed regular factor
is written as `sl4* x R4_zero`. The bridge is not a coincidence of ranks. At
the selected subregular point, the `sl4` centralizer is `sl2 + R2`; adjoining
the already present `R4` gives exactly `sl2 + R6`, and the common leaf changes
from `72+12` to `72+10=82`. The embedded root is across opposite `(2,2)` signs,
so its isotropy is `so(1,1)`, not `so(2)`.

## Strongest gluing attack

Cotangent naturality on the regular atlas does not by itself build a singular
chart. The singular chart was independently constructed by the predecessor,
including its smooth zero section, local target surjectivity and both regular
Cartan overlaps. This result uses naturality only after that genuine wall-to-
Cartan arrow exists, then composes it with the complete block-pivot arrows.

## Primitive and topology attack

The wall gauge degenerates at the zero section, so no regular coordinate map
is asserted there. Gluing is required only on the nonzero regular overlaps;
the wall chart itself remains smooth across zero. On those overlaps both maps
are actual cotangent lifts, so the tautological primitive is strictly natural.
No contractibility or choice of logarithm is smuggled into the proof.

## Reproducibility seam

The exact probe verifies the embedded involution, centralizer dimensions,
cotangent moment ranks, full `98D` schedule, banked-artifact compatibility and
the chain-rule triple. It does not independently re-enumerate all `306`
regular atlas sectors; that census remains the predecessor's separately
reproducible result.

## Verdict

Accept at the stated grade. The first split-`A3` singular attachment and its
wall/Cartan/block-pivot cocycle close. Require a separate adjacent-`A2`
two-wall attachment before any deeper-strata claim.
