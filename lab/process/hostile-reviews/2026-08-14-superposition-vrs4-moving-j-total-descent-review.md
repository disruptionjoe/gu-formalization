# Hostile review: VRS-4 moving-J total descent

## Strongest overclaim

An algebraic quotient theorem is not an instantiated GU physical complex.
Writing the two intertwining squares does not supply the missing background,
operators, reducibility tower, boundary domain, completion or positivity.

## Strongest contrary construction

A field-dependent associated-bundle `J` can descend even when no fixed matrix
is basic. The theorem explicitly preserves that route by allowing typed moving
`J_G,J_E,J_R`; it rejects only the inference that covariance alone supplies
those intertwiners.

## Weakest reproducibility seam

The finite control uses closed finite-dimensional images, whereas GU's physical
quotient is expected to require closure and a common operator domain. The
result therefore states topological closure and boundary-domain invariance as
premises rather than claiming the finite model proves them.

## Disposition

The conditional theorem is sound at its declared scope. Moving `J10` remains
the leading internal complex component, but current GU truth is type-missing at
every cross-sector and analytic seam. `H-Q*` survives; `H0` strengthens without
proof. VRS-5 is the exact next forward enabler.
