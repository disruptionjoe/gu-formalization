# Hostile review: K77 tilted edge-bundle type bridge

## Charge 1: where does the summary outrun the artifact?

The exact fixture proves the tilted affine cocycle and ordinary edge-frame
cocycle, not an actual global `Y14` edge theory. It does not prove descent of
the preboundary potential, two-form, moment map or characteristic kernel. The
report therefore books two closed coordinate-cocycle conditions and one killed
identity bridge—not a new global quotient.

## Charge 2: is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The largest risk is precisely mistyping: the affine part of `tau_A0` is an
ad-valued one-form, while the v0.70 edge coordinate is a zero-form/group frame.
Treating both as “gauge shifts” would make a careful cocycle calculation defend
the wrong object. The constant-`xi` control at the admitted `A0=0` fixture
prevents a universal direct identification; it is not a computation of every
nonzero-reference-connection bridge.

## Symplectic-geometry lens

The wave correctly refuses to infer symplectic descent from coordinate descent.
A global promotion requires a dressed preboundary potential or two-form whose
overlap transformations are exact/canonical, kernel equality in every
trivialization, and a nondegenerate reduced form. The v0.70 local quotient is
retained, not enlarged.

## Principal-bundle and group-cohomology lenses

The noncommuting triple-overlap order is correct:
`c02=Ad(h12^-1)c01+c12`. Reversed order and wrong adjoint-side plants fire.
The group-valued edge frame also satisfies its own cocycle. This establishes
that the failure is not a bad transition convention; it is a form-degree and
infinitesimal-action mismatch between two legitimate objects.

## Variational-bicomplex lens

No bridge is admissible merely because it maps coordinates. It must intertwine
the preboundary one-form/two-form and moment-map contraction. A differential
can change form degree but also changes jet order. A group-valued dressing can
absorb gauge action but changes the field-space geometry. Both must be varied,
not only declared.

## Variational-PDE lens

Inverting `D_A0` introduces a constant zero mode, basepoint/boundary data and an
analytic domain. A normal contraction imports a boundary normal and does not
recover constant `xi`. These are construction burdens, not proof that no bridge
can exist.

## Krein/operator lens

dissipativity or common Green domain follows from the exact cocycle result.

## Source-criticism lens
