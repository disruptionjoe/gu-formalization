## Findings and repairs

### H1 — material: “the full action image is not horizontal” was too broad

The initial synthesis said the full action image had been killed as a
horizontal object. The exact fixture establishes a narrower theorem. It uses
the displayed local translation directions, one constant commuting
Lie-algebra generator, vanishing local curvature contribution, and nonzero
`kappa_1`. A source-derived constrained variation domain could exclude the
fixture, and the `kappa_1=0` stratum is not decided by it.

**Repair:** every result surface now says automatic horizontality is killed
only for nonzero `kappa_1` on the displayed full local translation stratum.
The global admissible source domain remains open.

### H2 — material: the receiver is not yet the localized action

The map `(s*,res_s^V)` is a fibrewise bundle isomorphism along a section, and
its inverse transpose is the exact equation dual at a fixed section jet. That
does not define the induced vertical density/current, vary its metric and
support, or establish a tilted Ward/BV identity.

**Repair:** “defect Euler receiver built” is explicitly restricted to the
field/equation carrier and first-variation pairing. The next gate owns the
complete source-action localization and moving variation.

### H3 — passed: the field map is canonical and source-compatible

For every bundle section `s`, `d pi ds=1` gives
`TY|s=ds(TX) direct-sum V`. Restricting a covector to these two summands is
therefore an isomorphism without a metric choice. In graph coordinates its
matrix is triangular with determinant one. This is not the predecessor's
chosen metric-normal decoder and introduces no projector or parameter.

The field map also already exists in N1 as `A_X=s* A` plus
`res_s^V(A-A0)`. The artifact therefore does not defend a superseded generic
normal-receiver proposal.

### H4 — passed: the equation map follows from variation rather than rank

The receiver `M^{-T}` is forced by

`<M^{-1} delta q,e_A>=<delta q,M^{-T}e_A>`.

Exact rational controls verify the full pairing, recovery
`M^T M^{-T}=I`, the `e_V-J e_H` graph correction and movement to a second
section jet. Plants using `M^T`, freezing the old receiver after the jet moves,
dropping the correction, and dropping the vertical equations all fail.

### H5 — passed with boundary: exterior degree is correct

The identity

`Lambda13(H+V)* = Lambda3 H* tensor Lambda10 V* + Lambda4 H* tensor Lambda9 V*`

has dimensions four plus ten. The probe independently checks the top-form
signs and equality of the ambient and defect variation pairings. A vertical
density rather than an oriented vertical volume avoids consuming P1.

What remains open is the variation and normalization of that density in the
full nonlinear action. It is not written as literal pullback of a 14-form.

### H6 — passed: the `kappa` witness is not vacuous

The graph-conormal augmented torsion is nonzero, ordinary pullback kills it,
and vertical coefficient restriction recovers it exactly. On the
one-generator constant fixture, `dT=0` and `[T,T]=0`; the nonzero Euler row is
therefore emitted independently by the quadratic `kappa` term rather than by
an untracked curvature term. Setting the curvature fixture to zero does not
erase it.

### H7 — passed: no physical over-identification

The ten outputs remain sections of `s*V*Y tensor ad P`, and their equations
remain bundle-valued four-forms after density reduction. The report does not
call them Higgs components, Standard Model scalars, auxiliary constraints,
gauge directions or dark-sector fields. Those identifications require later
representation, vacuum and domain gates.

### H8 — passed: analytic and status boundaries hold

No closed Krein domain, Green hyperbolicity, constraint propagation, BV/BFV
phase space, physics row, P1/P2/P3 use, lane promotion, canon verdict or public
posture is claimed. Wave 3 remains closed.
