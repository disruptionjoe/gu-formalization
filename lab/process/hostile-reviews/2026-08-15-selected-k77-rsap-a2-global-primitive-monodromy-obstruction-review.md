# Hostile review: compact-A2 global primitive monodromy

## Strongest overclaim

Failure of a global diagonalizing group section and its exact relative
primitive transition is not failure of a global symplectic realization. The
compact cotangent factor itself retains its global tautological one-form, and
local moment maps may glue equivariantly. The result is narrowed to the
selected one-section, one-single-valued-transition route.

## Strongest contrary construction

A torus-bundle atlas with a nontrivial Cech--de Rham primitive class can retain
the passing local symplectic and moment data. A separately constructed
prequantum line could cancel the class at integral charges. Neither route is
killed; both are the exact continuations named by the result.

## Weakest reproducibility seam

The quotient from group representatives to `SU(3)/SO(3)` changes the natural
circle normalization because the root circle meets `SO(3)` in a finite
subgroup. The proof therefore uses pullback: exactness on the quotient would
imply exactness on the group-section cover, where the unit Hopf winding and
period `2 pi mu_alpha` are explicit. Finite quotienting cannot turn the
nonzero pulled-back class into zero.

## Charge challenge

The primitive period could vanish if `mu_alpha=0`, but that condition is the
simple-root eigenvalue collision. It is the discriminant wall, excluded from
the complete regular overlap being tested. The obstruction is therefore
pointwise across that regular component, not a generic-only sample.

## Verdict

`PASS_WITH_ATLAS_CONTINUATION`. The compact group-section exact-relative-
primitive route is killed at the full regular-overlap grade. A different
global transition and the regular multi-chart symplectic/moment realization
remain open and form the required next gate before any singular extension.
