# Hostile review

## Strongest overclaim

The dangerous wording is “the split `A3` regular locus globalizes.” The
construction covers one complete connected component of the identity Gauss
big-cell overlap after fixing minor signs and eigenvalue order. Regular points
in other sign components or lower Bruhat cells are not thereby covered. The
artifact and registry say this explicitly and leave their cocycles open.

## Strongest contrary construction

Another component may carry a nontrivial discrete transition, or its overlap
with the selected component may circle the discriminant after quotienting by
Weyl data. Contractibility of the selected cell cannot kill that class. The
present result removes monodromy only inside the selected component.

## Typing attack

The `O6` transfer could be mistaken for six imported degrees of freedom. It is
instead the three orbit Darboux pairs moved out of the `78D` `A2` common leaf
when the shared leaf is reduced to `72D`. The full source remains `98D`; the
target remains `sl4* x R4_zero`; seven invariant pairs are present on both
sides.

## Reproducibility seam

The exact probe certifies the frozen `26D` universal normal form, complete
moment square, primitive, noncommutation and triple product. The passage from
Gauss factorization to a global chart uses the standard uniqueness of LU/Gauss
coordinates on a fixed nonzero-minor sign component and the ordinary
cotangent-lift theorem. It is not a machine enumeration of all real regular
components.

## Verdict

Accept at the stated grade: one complete connected split big-cell transition
and its first mixed-rank triple are constructed. Reject any inference to every
split component, another real form, a singular attachment, zero charge,
all-strata RSAP, physical phase space or public claim.
