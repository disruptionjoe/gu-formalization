# Hostile review — K125

## Strongest overclaim

Exact connection/frame covariance does not evaluate the complete curved
`I1B` coefficient bank. K125 proves that a correctly transported fixed-chart
packet is equivalent to the co-moving principal description. It does not give
the three zero-order entries of the curved two-polarization TT endomorphism.

## Strongest contrary construction

The exact family `-12 q^2 I_2+[[a,c],[c,b]]` shares K124's principal symbol
and Green current for every `(a,b,c)` but has generically different determinant
and spectrum. This forbids promotion from principal closure to a unique full
pencil.

## Weakest reproducibility or propagation seam

The fixed-chart naturality witness is finite-dimensional and local. It tests
the exact transformation laws and second-jet bookkeeping, not every one of
the 1,274 off-shell K77 coefficients on an arbitrary curved background. K126
must instantiate the actual background jet in the same common basis.

## Adversarial checks

1. The explicit symmetric-frame `D2B_LC` is nonzero and eta-skew.
2. Dropping `dB` breaks the planted curvature covariance control.
3. Freezing the noncyclic coefficient map breaks scalar invariance.
4. The Green current uses the induced moving-frame connection.
5. A Cartan improvement may have nonzero field-space curl; only its spacetime derivative is exact.
6. Exact local improvement is not equated with a vanishing edge integral.
7. No Ward-fitted, `I_sc`, `I2B`, `I_II`, or old `14/3` coefficient is used.

## Verdict

Accept the fixed-chart curvature/coefficient naturality theorem, the
frame-covariant principal Green current, and the exact Cartan representative
class. Retain the curved lower-order endomorphism, unique pencil, spectrum,
global domain and BFV charge as open.
