# Hostile review: selected-K137 curved transport rank/jet obstruction

## Strongest overclaim attack

**Attack.** Variable multiplicity does not forbid all propagation across a
crossing. Hyperbolic systems can have mode conversion, transmission matrices,
or singular normal forms at eigenvalue crossings.

**Disposition.** Sustained and incorporated. K137 proves only that the stated
fibres are not one smooth constant-rank five-dimensional vector bundle and
that ordinary bundle parallel transport does not cross them without extra
matching data. It explicitly leaves singular transmission and stratumwise
transport open.

## Strongest contrary construction

**Attack.** Stay on the smooth null cone, use `H_q`, and parallel-transport the
five K136 basis vectors with the Levi-Civita connection.

**Disposition.** This constructs a frame transport, not the system's amplitude
transport. The three compensated classes depend on the distortion inverse and
the full system projector; K132 already proves normal-null rows need not remain
tangential-null. Invariance of the five-dimensional quotient under the actual
subprincipal connection must be calculated, not assumed from tensor transport.

## Strongest jet-sufficiency attack

**Attack.** Subprincipal symbols are pointwise, so the K127 two-jet should be
enough.

**Disposition.** A pointwise subprincipal value does not determine its values
along a bicharacteristic. K127 selects no background neighborhood. Metric germs
with the same two-jet and different third jets preserve the point curvature and
frozen symbol but change the first neighborhood variation of connection and
curvature coefficients. K137 therefore does not claim that no pointwise
subprincipal expression can be written; it rejects a complete transport law
from one unselected point two-jet.

## Weakest source-owner seam

**Attack.** Fixed-boundary variation might be read as the action selecting a
Dirichlet boundary condition.

**Disposition.** Fixed boundary is the declared class of test variations used
to isolate the bulk Euler row. The serialized `I1B` functional contains no
boundary functional or Euler boundary equation. Treating an admissibility
choice as a dynamical owner would invert the variational logic.

## Verdict

Pass at the stated ceiling. Exact rank changes obstruct one cross-stratum
five-bundle; current point-jet and action data do not select amplitude or
boundary transmission. Smooth-stratum propagation with newly derived owned
coefficients remains open and is the K138 gate.
