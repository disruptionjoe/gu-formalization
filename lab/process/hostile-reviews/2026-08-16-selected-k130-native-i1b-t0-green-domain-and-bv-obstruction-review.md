# Hostile review — K130

## Strongest objections

Nonzero `kappa_1 K` makes the zero-momentum block invertible, so perhaps it
also regularizes the boundary problem. Conversely, the repository already has
a nonnull K77 Green inverse and local symmetric-hyperbolic domain, so perhaps
K130 is duplicative.

Both objections confuse typed layers. `kappa_1 K` is zero order and cannot
change the principal or Green coefficient. The K77 nonnull inverse belongs to
a distinct four-field `1920`-dimensional fermionic symbol, not the present
`196+24` distortion carrier.

## Adversarial checks

1. The tracked carrier dimensions are read from the held `d_BT` registry, not
   inferred from the K129 prose.
2. Cross ranks `12/12/11` give off-diagonal ranks `24/24/22` and exact
   radicals `196/196/198` in dimension `220`.
3. Same-grade zeros remain type-selection zeros; inserting diagonal principal
   blocks fires a planted control.
4. The Green coefficient is the skew off-diagonal boundary concomitant and
   has the same ranks as the principal block.
5. Algebraic `kappa_1 K` changes neither integration-by-parts boundary terms
   nor the characteristic rank.
6. Every conormal is called characteristic only on the tracked carrier; the
   unsupplied all-grade source totalization is not claimed.
7. The nonnull/null distinction is retained through the two-dimensional null
   rank drop.
8. Failure of an unreduced invertible time coefficient does not kill every
   constrained hyperbolic formulation; it requires an owned split.
9. `ord(A)=2` and `ord(C)=1` prevent an unexamined common first-order domain
   or ordinary matrix-symbol treatment.
10. No Green radical is identified with gauge, silently quotiented, or
    promoted to BFV cohomology or a physical mode.

## Verdict

Accept the exact tracked-carrier Green/radical packet and the resulting
ordinary elliptic, unreduced symmetric-hyperbolic and direct-polarization
obstructions. Reject mass-as-principal repair, fermionic-domain transfer,
all-grade overreach, silent radical reduction and formal-Schur promotion.

BV/KT/BFV packet before a closed inverse or cohomology can be tested.
