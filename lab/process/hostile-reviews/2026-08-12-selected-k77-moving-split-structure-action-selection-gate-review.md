## Charge 1 — where does the summary outrun the artifact?

Three tempting promotions are rejected.

1. `K_omega` is an exact half-exchanging real endomorphism component, but this
   probe does not prove that its representative lies in source `u(64,64)`.
   That requires the still-underived Hermitian form and external complex
   scalar.
2. The source says `varpi` hosts Higgs-like and Yukawa functions; it does not
   say that `K_J` or `K_omega` is the Higgs or a Yukawa matrix.  The report was
   repaired to call this a candidate arena only.
3. The inherited Hessian is pointwise and branch-specific.  It shows that the
   current action keeps the breaking banks nonradical and does not select the
   reduction there; it is not a global no-selection theorem.

## Charge 2 — where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The initial successor language risked treating every nonzero `D J` or
`D omega` as a defect that had to be killed.  That is too narrow.  A full
connection relative to a moving reduction naturally decomposes into a
compatible connection plus tensorial off-diagonal fields, and the source
places Higgs-like functions inside `varpi`.  The correct question is first
what those tensors are and how the action uses them, not whether they all
vanish.

The review also retains the v0.189 fence: the K77 vector connection and the
full unitary `varpi` are not the same object.  The exact decomposition is a
spin-carrier theorem until a compatibility map is built.
