# Hostile review: K77 boundary-edge Lie closure

## Standing charge 1 — strongest overclaim or hidden assumption

The result could be misread as deriving a 40-component physical edge field.
It derives only the local homogeneous orbit required to move the W
polarization covariantly under the full gauge algebra. Existence of a global
section, action ownership, dynamics, preboundary form, BFV charge, analytic
domain and physical interpretation are all absent. The result is narrowed to
an algebraic carrier-and-closure theorem.

## Standing charge 2 — strongest contrary construction

A source-owned reduction to the 51-dimensional split stabilizer would preserve
W and mirror without a coset field. The current evidence does not exclude that
route; it proves only that the present full ordinary-gauge complex has no such
reduction and that an eight-direction truncation is not closed. This contrary
route remains the coequal next ownership gate.

## Standing charge 3 — weakest reproducibility or propagation seam

The generator closure is exact and finite, but the passage from a local
homogeneous orbit to a global boundary field can fail topologically. Even a
global field would not imply a functional BFV phase space or a closed Green
domain. Those propagation seams are explicit and no domain claim is made.

## Additional disciplinary charges

- **Lie algebra:** the eight mixed directions close to 15, and the active
  `17+8` set closes to all 91; the inactive tangent kernel is not an ideal.
- **BRST/BFV:** a rank-eight-only ghost sector fails the bracket test. The
  91-dimensional gauge algebra and 40-dimensional coset coordinate count are
  not conflated.
- **Homogeneous geometry:** `dim G/H=40` is local. No global triviality or
  section is asserted.
- **Variational physics:** moving-projector covariance is kinematic and does
  not make the projector action-owned or dynamical.
- **Real structure:** the mirror-associated family exists equally; no member
  selection or chirality follows.

## Verdict

`SURVIVES_WITH_STRICT_ALGEBRAIC_AND_OWNERSHIP_CEILING`.

The exact closure result and the rejection of eight fitted edge coordinates
survive. Any claim of a derived physical edge field, BFV cohomology, analytic
domain, luminous half, chirality or count is rejected.
