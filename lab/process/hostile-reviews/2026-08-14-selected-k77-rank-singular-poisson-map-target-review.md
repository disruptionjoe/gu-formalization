# Hostile review

## Strongest overclaim

Typing RSAP does not construct one. The 98-dimensional regular atlas is not an
RSAP candidate because it omits singular charges. The lower bound 98 is
necessary, not sufficient.

## Strongest contrary construction

The 182-dimensional cotangent parent remains the exact all-charge Poisson
submersion and therefore an RSAP example only in the uninteresting
non-smaller dimension. A genuinely below-182 example must extend the regular
atlas across singular walls while permitting controlled critical points.

## Weakest reproducibility seam

The cube map proves only that surjectivity need not imply submersivity. It is
not a Lie--Poisson or `so(7,7)` model. The registry and result fence that scope.
The first mathematical test is an actual codimension-one discriminant-wall
attachment with matching Poisson and symplectic data.

## Findings

- The 98 lower bound uses only the regular submersion hypothesis.
- The 182 zero-orbit bound is not transferred after its hypothesis is removed.
- Smooth-manifold and stratified-domain targets remain distinct.
- No all-charge carrier, edge theory or physical subcarrier is claimed.
