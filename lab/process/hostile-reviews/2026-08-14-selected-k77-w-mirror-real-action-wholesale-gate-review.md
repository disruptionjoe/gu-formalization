## Charge 1 — where does the summary outrun the artifact?

“No real action can produce chirality” would be false. The theorem requires a
`tau`-fixed stationary background and either separate W/M invariance or a
well-typed combined complex. A real invariant action can have two non-fixed
stationary vacua exchanged by `tau`; at either chosen vacuum its component
Hessian entries can differ. The exact polynomial control demonstrates this.

Repair accepted: the result is always called a fixed-background class theorem,
and spontaneous vacuum, BV/BFV and analytic-domain exits remain explicit.

## Charge 2 — where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

source-faithful frozen object is already an explicit residual-square action,
so its Hessian is formally Helmholtz symmetric by construction. The first

owner wave had already closed.

physical stationary solution is fixed under the real structure and whether
the physical differential preserves or breaks the W/mirror decomposition.

- **Representation:** the general block form is a theorem of the anti-linear
  involution, not a sample of current Clifford generators.
- **Variational:** ordinary Hessian symmetry must be read as graded symmetry
  for fermions; conjugation covariance, not ordinary symmetry alone, carries
  the W/mirror conclusion.
- **Complex descent:** a non-fixed vacuum and its conjugate are two distinct
  solutions. Equality of their action values does not select either one.
- **Symplectic/BV:** a `tau`-breaking quotient could distinguish physical
  cohomology even when the unreduced Hessian cannot, but the differential and
  reduction must be action-owned.
- **Analytic:** asymmetric boundary conditions can evade the finite theorem;
  they must form a closed, well-posed domain and cannot be inferred from a
  fibre matrix.

  rejected precisely because it is not homogeneous under the real structure.
