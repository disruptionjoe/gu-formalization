# Hostile review

## Wrong quaternionic quotient attack

The atlas uses `SU*(4)/SO*(4)`, whose moving space is nine-dimensional and
whose cotangent factor is `18D`. It does not revive the dimensionally
insufficient `SU*(4)/Sp(2)` Cartan quotient.

## Incomplete-family attack

The probe replays the K79 exact real-form census and retains its six
quaternionic canonical spectral/Jordan families exactly. The origin is tested
as a common terminal stratum, not counted as a seventh family.

## False orbit-count attack

Canonical families are not asserted to be real adjoint orbits. Continuous
spectral parameters can remain inside a family. The conclusion is family
exhaustion at the K79 canonical-form grade.

## Imported-rank attack

Every full and moving centralizer is recomputed in the exact quaternionic
`6+9` basis. The `5/4`, `7/5`, and `15/9` schedules are not copied from a
pseudo-unitary factor, though the resulting pointwise bounds agree.

## Fake closure attack

Twelve exact linear paths remain complex symmetric and quaternionic-real.
Three exact nonzero controls on every path retain the registered source
centralizer, and each endpoint recomputes the destination pair. The displayed
diagonal and square-zero formulas certify the same type for every nonzero path
parameter. Both regular families approach singular families, and every
singular family reaches the origin directly or through a registered
intermediate.

## Fake global frame attack

The four rational `SO*(4)` Cayley frames form a finite exact consistency nerve,
not a claimed global finite cover. The probe checks quaternionic reality,
orthogonality, determinant one, noncommutation, all six primitive tests and all
four base and cotangent triangles.

## Cross-form and physics attacks

No relative ambient embedding is supplied, so no cross-real-form transition is
inferred. Particle comparators do not type this classical cotangent atlas and
are not used.

## Verdict

Accept closure of the local `SU*(4)/SO*(4)` singular atlas at canonical-family,
exact-rank, degeneration and cotangent-frame grade. Proceed to the first
deeper ambient `so(7,7)` singular stratum without claiming a cross-form edge.
