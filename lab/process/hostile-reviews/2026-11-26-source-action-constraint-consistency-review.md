lift is such an n0. Every other local primary lift is n0+k, k a section of Z.
Define the fixed initial-field source

    F0 = DG(Bv0 + E n0).

In a stationary adapted coefficient frame write

    L = L0 + sum_(a=1)^13 La partial_a.

At a point p define

    T_p : N_p + Z_p + (T*_p Sigma tensor Z_p) -> N_p,
    T_p(w,k0,k1) = M_p w - L0_p k0 - sum_a La_p k1_a.

Then the descriptor equations admit compatible first time-derivative jets at
p, after an allowed choice of the primary kernel germ k, IF AND ONLY IF

    [F0(p)] = 0 in coker T_p.                         (FC)

Equivalently the finite matrix is the augmented block matrix

    [ M_p | -L0_p|Z | -L1_p|Z | ... | -L13_p|Z ].

There is no full-R inverse or M inverse in this criterion. For the owned
first-order Q lift n0(v), F0=DGBv+L n0(v) has order at most2 in v. Thus the
actual finite input is J^2_p(Q), with freely variable J^1_p(Z) multipliers and
the single N-valued derivative n_t(p). The naive third derivative is absent
by the full operator-order reduction, not by discarding lower terms.

### Proof

The R equation fixes the initial velocity germ

    v1 = -G(Bv0 + E(n0+k)).

Differentiating the N equation once in time gives, at the initial time,

    M n_t = DG(Bv0 + E(n0+k)) = F0 + Lk.

Therefore any compatible data supply w=n_t(p), k0=k(p), and
k1_a=partial_a k(p), proving necessity of (FC).

Conversely, suppose T_p(w,k0,k1)=F0(p). In the constant-kernel frame, choose an
analytic Z-valued germ with the specified value and first spatial derivatives;
an affine coordinate polynomial suffices. Set n_initial=n0+k and define v1 by
the complete R equation above. Choose a local n1 germ with n1(p)=w. Then

    v(t,x)=v0(x)+t v1(x),
    n(t,x)=n_initial(x)+t n1(x)

satisfy the primary equation at t=0 on the initial patch, the R equation at
t=0, and the first time derivative of the primary equation at p. This proves
sufficiency for precisely the claimed finite jet, without asserting an exact
solution or a neighborhood consistency equation. Smooth compactly supported
kernel germs with the same finite jet are also available on a local patch if
that is the existing test-data category; no support condition is needed for
the proposition.

## Intrinsic and lift-independent formulation

The first-order operator L restricted to sections of Z induces a bundle map

    ell_p : J^1_p(Z) -> N_p.

The intrinsic augmented map is M_p direct_sum (-ell_p). Choosing a connection
or kernel frame merely splits J^1(Z) into value plus derivative coordinates;
a frame change mixes these by an invertible triangular map. Thus the cokernel
class (FC) is independent of that choice. A frame in which the embedded kernel
basis varies requires its derivative in L0; it may not be silently discarded.

Replacing n0 by a different primary lift n0+k_star changes F0 by Lk_star, which
lies in the augmented image. Hence [F0] is also independent of the selected
primary lift. This is a genuine constraint invariant rather than an artifact
of the existing particular Q preimages.

For explicitly time-dependent coefficients use instead

    F0 = DG(Bv0+E n0) - D_t v0 - M_t n0,
    L = DGE - M_t.

The same finite-jet argument applies in a fixed time-adapted trivialization.
Changes to that trivialization must transform all derivative terms together.

## Lower-order formula suitable for a cheap native implementation

In spatial coordinate derivatives, write

    D = sum_i D_i partial_i + D_0,
    E = sum_j E_j partial_j + E_0.

With actual coefficient derivatives included, the second-order terms cancel,
and the exact first-order remainder is

    L^j = sum_i D_i partial_i(G E_j) + D_0 G E_j + D_j G E_0,
    L^0 = sum_i D_i partial_i(G E_0) + D_0 G E_0.

Here L^j and L^0 act on N-valued coefficients. In a noncoordinate frame the
antisymmetric second-derivative coefficient additionally contributes

    (1/2) sum_ij D_i G E_j [e_i,e_j].

If covariant derivatives are used, their curvature contribution must also be
retained. The owned affine/Nomizu geometry gives these terms; setting all
coefficient derivatives to zero merely because the geometry is homogeneous is
not a valid replacement. Homogeneous adapted frames and ordinary coordinate
frames have different lower terms.

There is also an exact formal-adjoint check. For the stationary Hessian in the
native density pairing, E^dagger=D and G^dagger=-G. Therefore

    (DGE)^dagger = -DGE.

This is a strong implementation check on the retained first-order remainder,
not a reason to set it to zero. It is formal skew-adjointness of a differential
operator, not pointwise skewness of every displayed lower matrix.

## Dual witness: no nondegenerate kernel required

Finite-dimensional linear algebra makes (FC) equivalent to the vanishing of
lambda(F0(p)) for every covector lambda in N_p* satisfying

    lambda M = 0,
    lambda L0|Z = 0,
    lambda La|Z = 0 for every spatial a.

A single such lambda with lambda(F0) nonzero proves a real first-consistency
obstruction for those initial jets, independent of every kernel multiplier.
With the full native nondegenerate pairing on N and M self-adjoint, lambda can
be represented by a vector in ker M. This identification never assumes that
the pairing RESTRICTED to ker M is nondegenerate.

This dual form can avoid constructing a full matrix or inverse, but an actual
witness must annihilate ALL of im M and all kernel-jet images. A vector outside
the currently certified inverse subspace is not such a witness by itself.

## Exact limits and falsification relevance

1. Failure of (FC) excludes the specified primary initial jet, even after all
   allowed kernel germs. It is stronger than failure to invert an optional
   unrestricted-R source. To exclude a whole free-data class, show an
   obstruction for an admitted member under that class's claimed freedom; to
   exclude every nonzero member requires the stronger corresponding statement.
2. Success of (FC) establishes only pointwise first-time-derivative
   compatibility. It does not solve the spatial multiplier PDE on a
   neighborhood, differentiate the next constraints, establish a closed
   evolution system, or give a physical propagating negative mode.
3. Constant rank of M provides free kernel FIRST JETS. It does not guarantee
   constant rank of the augmented map without an additional covariance check,
   and even augmented surjectivity at every point is not by itself a theorem
   of local PDE solvability or higher-time compatibility.
4. The finite pointwise matrix does not imply that the full differential
   constraint algorithm stabilizes in a finite number of steps. Each further
   stage can involve higher spatial jets. A fixed-frequency/frozen finite
   matrix argument is a separate category and cannot be substituted for the
   variable-coefficient native equations.
5. A new constraint on Q or larger diagnostic R data remains candidate-level.
   Q was not a source-selected physical state space; it is not a GU
   falsification. To turn a data exclusion into a physical/source obstruction
   requires an independently established requirement that the excluded jets
   must be admitted by the source-intended physical quotient/domain.
6. No new choice family is introduced. The proposition explicitly retains
   choices ALREADY present as undetermined auxiliary-kernel multipliers.

Disposition: the augmented finite-jet criterion is an exact and useful native
structural theorem under the owned premises. It closes the FORMULATION of the
first-consistency test and separates an actual obstruction from missing inverse
coverage. It does not close the substantive propagation question.

## Stronger native identity from varying the exact time function

The existing closed-time theorem supplies more than the quadratic principal
sandwich. Let K=sum K^a nabla_a be the actual pure differential connection
operator (all native covariant lower terms included), and H=K+C with
C=kappa I+2N(T0,.). For EVERY local closed non-null time form tau=dt, it proves

    P_tau K P_tau = 0                                  (CT)

as an identity of differential operators on the full coefficient bundle. This
is exactly §1 of source-action-kappa1-constraints, not an additional parallel-P
assumption. K itself does not depend on the choice of time function.

Take any scalar f and tau_epsilon=dt+epsilon df. This stays closed and locally
non-null for sufficiently small epsilon. Let dotP be the pointwise derivative
of its native orthogonal kernel projector. The owned pencil formulas are

    dotP P = -G K_df P,
    P dotP = -P K_df G.

Differentiate (CT) as an OPERATOR identity and multiply by P on both sides:

    0 = P dotP K P + P K dotP P
      = -P K_df G K P - P K G K_df P
      = -[P K G K P, f].

The last equality uses [K,f]=K_df. Multiplication and differentiation in these
operator products retain derivatives of every P,G,K_df coefficient. Since f
is arbitrary, the differential operator P K G K P commutes with all scalar
multiplications and is therefore order ZERO. This proof automatically includes
the moving-projector terms that a normal-frame calculation must assemble; it
does not need them to vanish separately.

Expanding DGE=P H G H P now yields the exact first-order symbol

    sigma_1(DGE)(xi) = P K_xi G C P + P C G K_xi P.       (LS)

The pure geometric two-K term contributes only at order zero. Both mixed terms
and P C G C P retain their ordinary coefficient derivatives at order zero.
There is no assertion that DGE vanishes.

### Horizontal multiplier derivatives vanish in the cokernel

At the actual homogeneous roots, T0 and C are full horizontal-Lorentz
invariant at p. As tau varies through horizontal timelike covectors, the
endomorphisms M(tau)=P_tau C P_tau on N(tau) are related by Lorentz transport
and irrelevant positive rescaling of tau. Their kernels therefore form a
constant-rank smooth family. For w,z in Z=ker(M|N), choose transported z_epsilon
in ker M(tau_epsilon). Differentiating M_epsilon z_epsilon=0 and using
self-adjointness gives

    h(w, dotM z)=0.

The projector formula gives, on those kernel rows,

    h(w, dotM z)
      = -h(w, (P K_xi G C P + P C G K_xi P)z).

Together with (LS), this proves

    pi sigma_1(DGE)(xi)|Z = 0

for HORIZONTAL spatial xi, where pi:N->coker M. Equivalently those derivative
columns of the augmented finite-jet map lie in im M. This argument uses the
nondegenerate full N pairing and self-adjoint M; it does not assume that the
pairing restricted to Z is nondegenerate.

Thus the three horizontal derivative columns can be omitted AFTER passing to
coker M. The zero-order kernel column and all ten normal derivative columns
remain. The latter have no proved constant-rank/conjugacy argument in tau, and
normal kernel jets are available even when the tested v source has only
horizontal derivatives. Omitting them would silently change the allowed data
class. This is not yet a multiplier-independent source obstruction.

Independent disposition: accept the order-zero geometric identity and the
horizontal-cokernel cancellation under these explicitly owned hypotheses.
The proof is a short consequence of the existing closed-time theorem and
horizontal Lorentz invariance; it is not a new universal claim about arbitrary
descriptor systems.
