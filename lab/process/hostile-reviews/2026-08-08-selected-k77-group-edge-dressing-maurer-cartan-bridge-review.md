# Hostile review: K77 group-edge dressing and Maurer-Cartan bridge

## Charge 1: where does the summary outrun the artifact?

The exact theorem is universal and finite. It proves a cotangent dressing and
kernel equality for a generic rational `GL(2)` fixture. It does not prove that
the full K77 gauge group, chosen representation, invariant trace or
action-owned preboundary potential has exactly this form. The report therefore
closes the universal dressing/basicness gate, not the physical BFV gate.

## Charge 2: is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The likely mistyping is to call both `u^-1 d u` and arbitrary `varpi` “the
connection.” The first is flat and pure gauge. A nonzero curvature plant proves
the theorem cannot cover every affine one-form. The result also keeps base `d`
separate from field-space `delta`, so the differential bridge cannot be used as
the symplectic variation without another calculation.

## Symplectic-geometry lens

The important advance is exact kernel equality, not coordinate invariance.
The Jacobian has rank eight, the pulled-back two-form has rank eight, and its
four-dimensional null space is exhausted by four independent right `gl(2)`
generators. The inert-momentum plant shows this is not automatic. Promotion
still requires the same theorem for the action-owned K77 potential and trace.

## Principal-bundle and group-cohomology lenses

`q=xu^-1` and `pi=p u^T` are the correct right-action invariants, and
`u^-1 d u` obeys the correct affine transformation law. The noncommuting
triple-overlap product rule passes. This supplies a differential bridge to the
tilted pure-gauge component without reversing the v0.71 type separation.

## Variational-bicomplex lens

At identity, the canonical potential gives the exact `delta x-delta u` sign.
That is the correct comparison with v0.70. The proof does not yet show that
the K77 action's degree-13/14 Green potential is the pullback of this canonical
form, nor that its moment map matches the boundary charge.

## Variational-PDE and Krein/operator lenses

No inverse derivative was introduced, which avoids the earlier zero-mode and
basepoint debt for the pure-gauge component. This does not construct a common
Green domain, polarization, fundamental symmetry, positivity or
self-adjoint/maximal-dissipative realization.

## Representation-theory lens

`GL(2,Q)` proves the algebraic pattern and catches ordering/sign errors. It is
not evidence that the actual K77 `H` action on all boundary owners is free,
faithful or equipped with the needed invariant cotangent pairing. That is the
next representation-specific gate.

## Source-criticism lens

The sources support the tilted subgroup, Maurer-Cartan affine component and
Levi-Civita/Zorro reference connection. They are silent on the edge frame,
cotangent dressing and presymplectic completion. The return code is correctly
split.

## Final disposition

Pass with scope narrowing. Preserve the exact universal group-edge dressing,
kernel-equals-gauge theorem, v0.70 sign recovery, noncommuting affine bridge and
flatness fence. Do not promote an actual K77 `H` instantiation, arbitrary
varpi bridge, global BFV phase space, common domain, P1/P2/P3, canon verdict or
public posture.
