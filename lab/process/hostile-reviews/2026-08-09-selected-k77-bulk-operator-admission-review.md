# Hostile review: K77 bulk-operator admission

## Pre-wave

No action-parent or ambient-domain fork is assumed. The finite field-type
search space is the three source variables `(g,varpi,epsilon)` and their nine
linearized Euler blocks; it can be audited wholesale only as a derivative
**upper-bound grammar**. The actual full coefficients cannot be decided
wholesale because the parent-specific global tangent, gauge fixing and branch
ports do not exist. Success introduces no object; it prevents `Dmax/Dmin` and
the H7/H8 trace from being promoted too early. If it succeeds, only the prior
one-scalar-order reading dies.

## Charge 1: where does the summary outrun the artifact?

The first draft risked saying the action *is* a mixed-order operator. The exact
calculation proves less: the source density admits safe jet weights `(2,1,1)`
after covariant reduction, hence the Euler blocks admit the matrix upper bound
`[[4,3,3],[3,2,2],[3,2,2]]`. Integration by parts, Bianchi identities and
stationary cancellations can lower actual top coefficients. The result is an
admission grammar and a construction order, not a principal-symbol theorem.

Similarly, uniform weight `(2,2,2)` is compatible. It is rejected only as a
*derived minimal scalar order*, not as a possible over-regular graph topology.
The final result now states both qualifications.

## Charge 2: where is rigor defending a superseded or mistyped object?

The rank-91 epsilon/Cl1 cross and the 34-field stationary Gram are exact, but
they were computed on earlier restricted/background surfaces. The two current
QQ(sqrt(3)) source-stationary branches and three action-parent candidates came
later. Reusing those blocks unchanged would defend yesterday's background.
The ownership matrix therefore marks them `OWNED_PARTIAL_NEEDS_BRANCH_PORT`,
not components of a complete present operator.

The H7/H8 boundary carrier is another near-mistype. Its strong form and
relative descent are exact, but its exponents came from a kinematic nonlinear
regularity choice. They are targets for an operator trace theorem, not outputs
of the still-unbuilt operator.
