## Strongest overclaim

“The edge completion constructs the `98D` RSAP while retaining the charged
boundary sector.” It does not. The exact edge extension is `224D`, has rank
`182` and a `42D` diagonal kernel, and therefore reduces to `182D`. The
relative edge coordinate and `lambda_h` survive as a canonical pair. Reaching
`98D` requires the additional `lambda_h=0` constraint and its `42D`
characteristic quotient, which removes the generic charge the edge mode was
introduced to retain.

A coadjoint-orbit edge system could cancel one fixed charge orbit and may use
fewer variables than a full `T*H_bal`. It still does not retain arbitrary
`lambda_h` across the endpoint dual: choosing the orbit is already a charge
selection. For the exact unrestricted cotangent parent tested here, the
minimal Stueckelberg form adds 42 edge coordinates and pairs the existing 42
momenta with the relative endpoint-edge positions. Its 42-dimensional
diagonal quotient has zero net reduction and leaves the generic charged
quotient `182D`. A fixed coadjoint orbit is a different conditional boundary
theory, not a counterexample to this unrestricted-charge census.

The exact probe uses local canonical normal forms, not the full
field-theoretic differential operator. That suffices for the stated
characteristic and dimension census because K98 proves the right action free
and K102 fixes the coefficientwise moment-map typing. It does not establish a
global collar, closed analytic domain, polarization, positivity or
quantization. Those remain outside the claim ceiling.
