## Findings and repairs

### R1 — “the common action is built” needed a grade qualifier

The field-theoretic two-layer formula is written and its variational chain
rule is exact. The complete moving K77 coefficient expansion is not. The
result title, grade, registry, and campaign now say **formula with exact
variational control** rather than “full K77 action varied exactly.” Moving
Hodge/Krein/density variation is displayed as an owned extra term and remains
unassembled at full field grade.

### R2 — norm-square non-selection was initially too global

The exact implication is

```text
Upsilon_c = 0  =>  E S_2L = 0
```

for every fixed coefficient. This proves that source-stated redundancy cannot
select the coefficient **on the first-order solution locus** and that the
field Euler complex contains no coefficient row. It does not prove that
future boundary, vacuum, nontrivial-solution existence, or observation
conditions cannot favor a coefficient. Those possibilities are restored to
the held-out boundary.

### R3 — the optional modulus model is a control, not the actual K77 action

The two exact stationary roots, `173/13` and `1/38`, demonstrate that simply
varying the ratio in the finite common-action control gives field-dependent
answers. They do not prove that every possible modulus completion fails. The
report now requires a modulus owner, potential/kinetic term, symmetry/BV
placement, and datum invoice before that rival can move.

### R4 — one-vector rank three is sufficient, but only as a lower bound route

The three actual K77 quadratic operator tensors are evaluated on one dense
exact field. Their three output vectors have rank three. Evaluation is linear
and cannot raise the rank of the operator tensors, while three is the maximum;
therefore the operator tensors are independent on the tested principal
family. No claim is made about a full global operator domain or spectrum.

### R5 — middle-symbol scalarity is not a Dirac-square theorem

The probe now checks anticommutator scalarity on all fourteen basis covectors,
so linearity extends it to every covector. This is a real Laplace-type lead.
It remains insufficient because a Dirac/Lichnerowicz condition concerns the
composed full operator with form indices and lower-order curvature, not one
middle block. `alpha=beta` is retained as a candidate, not promoted.

### R6 — the source has a cancellation directive, not the needed adapter

Portal `02:03:07` explicitly names up/back/over paths and cancellations. Repo
archaeology also finds the draft deformation-complex locators
`delta_2^omega delta_1^omega=Upsilon`, but no actual real-K77 fermion/boson
path map identifying those routes with `gamma(q)A` and `A gamma(q)`. The next
gate therefore builds an existing source obligation rather than proposing a
new action or rediscovering the need for one.

### R7 — source branches must remain separate

Draft section 9.2 most directly norms the bosonic residual. Portal can be read
as norming the full first-order replacement, while the square-root language
suggests a composed-operator target. The review keeps all three as explicit
rivals. Blending them would manufacture a selector.
