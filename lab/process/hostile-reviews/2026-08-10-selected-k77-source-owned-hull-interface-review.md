# Hostile review: K77 source-owned hull interface

## Pre-review

- No ambient-signature settlement is assumed; this review is conditional on
  the selected real-K77 construction.
- The finite input covers two Pin placements and representative timelike,
  spacelike and null covectors. It cannot decide a global nonlinear action or
  all covectors wholesale.
- Success introduces no new ambient field type. A selector, moving reduction,
  global adjoint, BV differential or domain would be a new unowned object.
- Success kills only the statement that the extra paired directions are new
  fundamental fields. It does not kill the selector, closure, domain, chirality,
  index, anomaly or datum burdens.

## Charge 1: where the summary outruns the artifact

The source types the ambient barred and unbarred carriers. It does not identify
the 128 directions selected by the exact receiver calculation, and it does not
show that the three separately rank-256 causal-stratum receivers are the same
subspace. The exact common-hull rank is therefore recorded as `UNTESTED`.

The surviving claim is only that the missing directions can live in an
already-declared field type. Calling the finite hull source-selected, invariant,
variational, physical or unique would outrun both source and certificate.

## Charge 2: where rigor defended a mistyped object

v0.159 correctly priced the need for 128 additional paired left directions,
but summarized that as fields the source did not own. The source's explicit
four-field grammar shows that field-type ownership and subbundle-selection
ownership are different questions. The old sentence defended the right rank
burden under the wrong ownership type.

The correction does not rescue the graph by fiat. It moves the live unknown
from `NEW_FIELD_TYPE` to `FIXED_ACTION_OWNED_MOVING_REDUCTION`.

## Charge 3: what else must change

- **Needs recheck:** one fixed common receiver hull across the tested covectors,
  rather than three equal-rank fits.
- **Needs recheck:** preservation by the action-owned moving reduction, proposed
  as `P_epsilon u=u` and `D_varpi chi_epsilon=0`, against the graph, mirror,
  random graded 192s, 640 and 832.
- **Survives:** the exact v0.159 southeast and gauge-image failures.
- **Survives:** BV/domain, physical observation, mirror, anomaly, index/count,
  P1/P2/P3 and all rendezvous burdens.
- **Dissolved:** only the claim that the added paired directions require a new
  ambient fermion field type.

## Required lenses

- **Layer-0 semantics:** field type, selected subbundle, equation receiver,
  action restriction, quotient and datum remain distinct.
- **Prior art:** the frozen four-leg interface, 27-row requirements and unified
  source/datum packet already own the general demand; this wave adds the exact
  v0.159 dependency order.
- **Analytic/operator:** finite ranks do not provide a common domain, Green
  inverse, Fredholm index or positivity theorem.
- **Symplectic/variational:** the paired left directions must arise from the same
  action before variation; post-variation projection fails the gate.
- **BV:** the null rank-64 overlap remains only a half-complex, not an off-shell
  differential.
- **Representation/anomaly/cosmology:** observed particles, anomaly admission
  and cosmological amplitude stay rendezvous tests and cannot select the local
  hull retroactively.
- **Adversarial summary audit:** equal dimensions do not imply equal subspaces.

## Verdict

The construction remains worth pursuing because the source already permits
the ambient field types. The next run must test an action-owned fixed reduction
and must stop if closure requires a fitted covector-dependent projector or a
new unrestricted function-valued map. No canon or public verdict moves.
