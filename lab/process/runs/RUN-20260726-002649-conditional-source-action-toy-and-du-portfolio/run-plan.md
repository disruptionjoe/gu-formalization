# Conditional source-action toy construction program

## Objective

Turn the known source-action shape into a graded construction ladder that
separates:

1. compatibility engineered by penalty terms;
2. conditional realization of a selected chiral boundary sector;
3. source-owned dynamic selection;
4. regulator-level index, anomaly, and mirror control; and
5. an eventual program-native GU lift.

## Construction fork

Every rung must name whether it uses standard positive-Hilbert
domain-wall/overlap physics or the program-native `(9,5)` Krein, gimmel,
shiab, and `ker Gamma` construction. A result on one side does not transfer
without a stated map or theorem.

## Strongest absorber

Any desired solution can be made a minimum of a reverse-engineered action.
Therefore existence alone is not explanatory. The useful discriminator is
whether a small target-blind source law selects the result while admitting
zero, non-three, mirror, and non-chiral outcomes.

## Finite falsifier and stop

Stop at conditional hosting if chirality, winding, flux, boundary orientation,
rank, or flavor matrices are chosen from the observed target. Stop the
program-native lift if the standard construction cannot be transported to the
physical GU carrier and domain.
