## Pre-registered expected shape

The G2/G3 distortion, reduction, metric, Ward, preboundary, and partial BV
packet should be the native parent for several conventional shadows. Maxwell,
Yang--Mills, and Einstein are expected to remain candidate reductions rather
than established equalities. Dirac/RS, Higgs/Yukawa, anomaly, generation, and
cosmology are expected to have native carriers or parent slots but no complete
Eric-lane equation yet. The useful result is a construction graph, not a
claim that these equations have already been recovered.

## Kill conditions

1. A standard equation may not be copied into the native column.
2. A connection/Bianchi identity may not be promoted to Yang--Mills dynamics.
3. A Clifford symbol may not be promoted to a physical Dirac equation.
4. A scalar carrier may not be promoted to the Higgs.
5. Pullback without lift, equation dual, leakage, and domain may not be called
   a four-dimensional reduction.
6. Standard Model groups, charges, count, or PP3 may not select the stationary
   orbit, Hessian modes, projector, or domain.
7. Anomaly cancellation is a downstream quantum consistency test, not a
   native replacement equation.
8. No construction item may return only “external datum/source action needed.”
   It must use the G2/G3 parent and carry declared data conditionally.
