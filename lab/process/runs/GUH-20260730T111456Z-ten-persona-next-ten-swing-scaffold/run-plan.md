The campaign is a nested candidate-family construction. Every swing inherits
one explicit packet and must add an object or remove a stratum. Gap discovery
alone does not count.

The ten scaffolded swings are:

1. unified source-action and external-datum packet v0;
2. actual-\(\operatorname{Sym}^2\) odd-bilinear orbit screen and formal
   Euler-hull knockout;
3. variational five-leg emission map;
4. curvature-irrep and source-EOM/open-BV factor;
5. unified quadratic five-leg candidate-variety knockout;
6. nonlinear BV/higher-bracket completion;
7. curved lift and ansatz-relative gravity/DE/causality/UV solve;
8. full-20 Green/BFV domain and graded transport;

10. actual twisted-RS analytic index realization plus held-out tournament.

N2a and N4a may run in parallel after N1; N2b and N4b wait for N3's actual
variation. N5 and N7 are hard re-evaluation gates. N7 produces only
ansatz-relative local/formal stationarity until N8 checks the boundary/domain
variation. The P3 twist is fully instantiated from N1, while its integer is
not read before N8/N9 freeze the domain and physical operator.

- Leave-one-leg-out is a cross-consistency ablation. Only observables sealed
