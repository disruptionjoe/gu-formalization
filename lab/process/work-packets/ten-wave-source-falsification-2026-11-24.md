## Corrections that prevent false falsifications

1. Equation9.16 is candidate matrix grammar, not automatically a fully fixed
   principal symbol or unique action. Whole-field covariance needs an owned
   realization or explicitly quantified tensor family. Independent bars need not
   be Hermitian adjoints.
2. Source scalar-curvature language concerns an effective background limit.
   A scalar-flat off-shell counterexample only decides a uniform off-shell claim
   if that stronger quantifier is actually required. Source equation ideals must
   be real, current and independently owned; algebraic witnesses need actual
   solution/domain admission before physical inference. Vanishing on the scalar-zero
   locus, being determined by scalar alone, and uniform decay as scalar tends
   to zero are separate claims; divisibility by scalar proves only the first.
3. Allowed invariant mixing does not establish generated counterterms, occupied
   physical states, a nonzero observed rate or failed effective chirality.
4. Static parameter families require powers of the **whole fixed generator**.
   Arbitrary ordered switching words assume stronger controls. A bilinear current
   is not automatically a linear measurement map. Exact darkness and small
   measured response are different questions. A lower bound tending to zero
   proves no suppression; that needs an appropriate upper bound.
5. Strong hyperbolicity and wavefront results need their precise observed-system
   hypotheses. Failure of a sufficient pullback criterion is not an impossibility
   theorem; an actual admitted singular witness or norm failure is needed.
6. The optionalSU normal determinant is different from the spinor determinant.
   Absolute existence is different from a selected reduction, preserved connection
   or extension of prescribed end data.
