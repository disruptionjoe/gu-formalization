\begin{abstract}
Section 3.1 of Nguyen and Polya's response to Geometric Unity associates
complexification of the connection and gauge group with nonunitarity or an
energy spectrum unbounded in both directions. We distinguish its literal
wording from a stronger contextual reading involving propagating
Yang--Mills fields. The cited work of Witten explicitly separates ordinary
Yang--Mills theory with an indefinite invariant kinetic form from complex
Chern--Simons theory. We exhibit Gauss-compatible data with arbitrarily
negative energy for the former, state the assumptions and limitations of
the latter, and examine the source-native Geometric Unity actions.
Neither the Yang--Mills obstruction nor the Chern--Simons construction has
an established physical transfer to those actions. Thus the passage does
not establish a Geometric Unity no-go, while a rebuttal based only on
Chern--Simons theory does not discharge the physical concern. The
note also summarizes a direct calculation in one selected curved
first-order reconstruction: after its imaginary-packet primary constraints
are solved, the classical quadratic energy is unbounded in both directions
on a compact phase core, for either stationary coupling sign. The
metric-coupled real packet admits a consistent nonlinear restriction,
but an exact primary reduction leaves a nondegenerate metric-acceleration
block. Its Hamiltonian consequence depends on the boundary completion;
no positive physical completion is proved.
These bounded results support the energy concern on that reconstruction;
they are not a quantum spectral theorem or a universal GU no-go.
\end{abstract}
