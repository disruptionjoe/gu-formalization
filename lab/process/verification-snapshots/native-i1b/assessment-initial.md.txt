# The strongest objection, assessed end to end

**The response does not establish that Geometric Unity must be nonunitary or
have energy unbounded below. Its inference from complexification alone is
refuted by the very Witten paper it cites. But a positive, stable quantum
physical sector for GU is not constructed in the audited sources or
repository.** These are separate
conclusions. This assessment rebuts the asserted general implication and
establishes why the available GU construction does not yet decide the more
substantial physical question.

The repository is useful for this task: it supplies source records, exact
Clifford arithmetic and reproducible tests that distinguish a failed repair
from a theorem about GU. It does not contain a completed quantum GU theory.
The useful contribution of these tools is a checkable argument and a sharp
boundary on what remains unproved.

## 1. The actual objection and the standard for answering it

Nguyen and Polya's February 23, 2021 response, §3.1, pp5–6, makes two
arguments. First, the proposed identification of the adjoint bundle with
real Clifford data needs additional real/complex structure. Second, p6
asserts that complexifying connections and the gauge group forces either
nonunitarity or a Hamiltonian unbounded in both directions, citing Witten
[15]. The response predates the April 1 GU draft; explicit formulas in that
later draft cannot be treated as omissions from the earlier talk it reviewed.

The real-form calculation has a definite target. The repository's
[42-check certificates](../nguyen-c1c2-real-form-certificates-2026-08-12.md)
confirm the obstruction to the claimed natural identification at the full
real Clifford layer. They also distinguish the complex structure available
at the split layer. The April draft explicitly introduces complex factors
and `U(64,64)` (pp23–24, 42). Neither the algebraic obstruction nor the later
complex construction proves a statement about the physical Hamiltonian.

The live question associated with **SA-U1/H59 and SC-META-53** is therefore:
can the proposed theory have a nonzero physical state space with a positive
probability pairing, unitary evolution and energy bounded below? All three
properties must refer to the same action, constraints and domain. A positive
matrix on auxiliary coefficients does not answer it. The source's own
maximal-compact shielding uncertainty is preserved in the
[source-custody record](../source-uncertainty-custody-wave-2026-08-27.md).

This applies the [claim-indexed doctrine](../claim-indexed-verdict-doctrine-2026-08-12.md)
and [banked-mathematics register](../nguyen-objection-banked-mathematics-register-2026-08-13.md):
the blanket implication fails; the exact real-form calculation on its named
carrier remains banked; the GU positivity question remains open. No verdict
on §3.2, §3.3 or the theory as a whole is inferred.

## 2. The cited source does not prove the asserted implication

The recovered **IASSNS-HEP-89/65** report underlying Witten's 1991 paper
separates ordinary Yang–Mills with an indefinite invariant form from
Chern–Simons theory. The former has unbounded energy; the latter can be
unitary with zero bulk Hamiltonian on a closed spatial surface. The theorem
on preprint p21 proves unitarity, for real `s`, of the specified projectively
flat quantum connection on the Hilbert bundle with fiber
`L²(M,L^k)`; §4.1 gives its positive preserved pairing. Constraints and
polarization enter the construction. The alternative imaginary-`s` branch
has narrower qualifications and is unnecessary here. These locators refer
to the recovered 44-page report, not an independently inspected published
typesetting. [Witten primary report](https://lib-extopc.kek.jp/preprints/PDF/1990/9009/9009085.pdf).

Write the disputed implication as

```text
complexified gauge group  ==>  nonunitarity OR energy unbounded below.
```

A theory with such a group, a positive physical Hilbert space and `H=0`
violates its conclusion. This is a counterexample to the implication as
written. The physical space need not be one-dimensional or empty merely
because the bulk Hamiltonian vanishes. If the intended premise was instead
**the conventional propagating Yang–Mills action with an indefinite kinetic
form**, that additional action premise must be established for GU. Section
3.1 does not establish it. This inference failure does not prove GU shares
the counterexample's dynamics.

A recent explicit donor reinforces the distinction. **Han,
[arXiv:2504.16367v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16367v1)**, constructs a
physical multiplicity space and rigging-map quotient for complex
`SL(2,C)` Chern–Simons theory on a punctured sphere (§§8.4, 8.6). Its
assumptions include `k=2N>0`, `|b|=1`, `Re b, Im b>0`, and specified boundary
labels. The spectral measure is a positive absolute square. Completeness
uses the cited spectral theorem; it is not a new all-group result or a
four-dimensional GU Hamiltonian. Versioned custody is in the
[source receipt](../../lab/sources/noncompact-quantization-primary-pack-2026-10-08.json).

## 3. GU's proposed escape has a real donor, but no established transfer

GU p29 footnote 5 recalls a Bar-Natan–Witten modification involving the
maximal compact subgroup and Hodge operator, while saying the construction
was not reconstructed. The
[author preprint](https://www.math.toronto.edu/~drorbn/papers/NonCompact/NonCompact.pdf)
provides the missing reference: §4, pp14–16, defines a compact-reduction
involution `J`, equal to `+1` on the compact algebra and `-1` on its
complement, and uses `*J` to obtain a positive form. It derives new
gauge-fixed operators; §3 explains why changing the pairing while retaining
the old operators is insufficient. Equations 4.37–4.39 add a local
counterterm to restore independence of the compact reduction. Its setting
is perturbative Chern–Simons theory, not a positivity theorem for GU's
propagating fields.

The concrete GU transfer would have to derive the reduction, changed
operators and any counterterms from its real action and constraints, and
then establish physical energy separately. No equality between those
operators and GU's I1B or I2B has been proved here or in the audited chain.
The source footnote motivates this construction; it does not supply it.

Likewise, complex coordinates do not specify a physical real integration
cycle. Witten's [analytic-continuation paper, §2.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.2933v4)
distinguishes real cycles inside the same complexified connection space.
A convergent cycle alone is not a positive probability interpretation.
The [earlier 2026 positivity intake](../../lab/sources/quantum-positivity-primary-pack-2026-10-07.md)
also supplies no action-preserving GU transfer: tree amplitudes, selected
composite correlators and off-shell renormalization have narrower claims.

## 5. Constructive tests, including a necessary correction to the flat route

### What the earlier flat calculations actually establish

The [flat scalar calculation](../conditional-build/native-i1b-scalar-closure-2026-10-08.md)
finds a coupled growing mode of a declared constant-coefficient K77 I1B
completion, satisfying every lifted linearized metric and distortion row:

```text
gamma² + |p|² = 17 kappa² / 113,     kappa != 0.
```

The native distortion gauge variation vanishes at `T=0`; treating it as
`d chi` would incorrectly discard the mode. A positive conserved norm cannot
retain an exponentially growing eigenvector: if `Av=gamma v`, `gamma>0`,
then `A*W+WA=0` implies `2 gamma <v,Wv>=0`, contradicting `W>0`.
Admissibility in a source-global physical domain was not established.
The [constant replacement test](../conditional-build/native-i1b-constant-background-2026-10-08.md)
rejects radial, diagonal and five-field repairs **of that same flat
completion** for nonzero coupling. Its exact ideal remains valid there.

**Scope correction:** a flat observing metric does not make the canonical
fourteen-dimensional connection metric flat. Nor is moving a point in the
metric fibre the same variation as changing the observing metric. These
are essential distinctions, not small curvature corrections.

### The actual reconstructed geometry over a flat observing metric

Let `g` be the independent observing metric on `X`, and `h` the metric-fibre
coordinate at a fixed ambient point `(x,h)`. The source-inspired canonical
[Zorro reconstruction](../eric-curt-wave3d-b2c15p-source-epsilon-tangent-zorro-dewitt-2026-08-02.md)
is

```text
G_Y(g) = h_ij dx^i dx^j + D_h(theta,theta),
D_h(A,B) = tr(h^-1 A h^-1 B) - 1/2 tr(h^-1 A) tr(h^-1 B),
theta_ab = dh_ab - (Gamma(g)^c_ia h_cb + Gamma(g)^c_ib h_ac) dx^i.
```

The explicit coordinate completion is repository-derived; source uniqueness
is not asserted. The calculation selects the plus-first representative
`h=diag(1,-1,-1,-1)`, giving K77 with this DeWitt form; it does not settle
the source's signature-convention bridge. At constant flat `g`,
`Gamma(g)=0`, but `h` still varies
across `Y`. Conversely, `Gamma(sg)=Gamma(g)` for constant `s>0`, so
`G_Y(sg)=G_Y(g)` at a **fixed fibre point**. Its density variation in this
direction is zero. Moving the fibre point `h -> s h` instead gives the
previous `-8` logarithmic density derivative. Therefore the prior `-8 L=0`
metric equation is not transferred to the observing-metric variation of
this canonical geometry. The owner notes now carry this correction.

The new [geometry probe](../../tests/channel-swings/native_zorro_geometry_probe.py)
derives the full connection by the Koszul formula, checks torsion,
metricity and Bianchi, and obtains

```text
Ricci^sharp = (1/4) I on horizontal plus vertical-trace directions (5),
             (-5/4) I on vertical-traceless directions (9),
scalar curvature = -10.
```

A vertical traceless plane has sectional curvature `-1/2`. Thus setting
`F_B=0` would discard nonzero curvature of this reconstruction even when
the observed geometry is flat. The result agrees with the earlier
[pure-vertical curvature obstruction](../conditional-build/selected-k77-zorro-dewitt-trace-curvature-obstruction-2026-08-14.md)
and adds the horizontal and mixed connection terms.

### Full distortion equations reject the simplest curved replacements

Use the selected `comm/symi/symi` Shiab and the first bosonic action

```text
I1B = <T,S(F_B)> + 1/2<T,S(D_B T)> + 1/3<T,S(T wedge T)>
      + kappa/2<T,*T>.
```

The [action probe](../../tests/channel-swings/native_zorro_i1b_probe.py)
lifts the full tangent curvature to spin curvature, checks every
commutator, and independently compares the direct Shiab contraction with
the Einstein endomorphism. At `T=0`, the nonzero Euler forcing has fourteen
grade-one rows:

```text
f_horizontal = f_trace = -21/4,     f_traceless = -15/4.
```

So zero distortion is not stationary here. Try the three intrinsic
constant coefficients
`T(v)=gamma(a P_H v + b P_V0 v + c P_trace v)`.
Covariant differentiation of the projectors and the actual formal adjoint
of the quadratic derivative term produce necessary bivector equations
(the receivers are `e^0 gamma_0 gamma_4` and `e^0 gamma_0 gamma_10`)

```text
(-a+b)/2 = 0,       (a-c)/2 = 0.
```

The complete derivative matrix has rank two and kernel `(1,1,1)`.
Curvature, mass and cubic terms cannot cancel these two equations: their
contributions to these rows vanish, as checked by full quadratic
polarization and Clifford parity. Hence `a=b=c=t` is necessary. The field
then becomes the parallel solder form times `t`, and its grade-one equations
are

```text
312 t² + kappa t - 21/4 = 0,
312 t² + kappa t - 15/4 = 0.
```

Subtracting gives `-3/2=0`. **No member of this three-coefficient family
solves the full distortion equations, for any real kappa.** Since necessary
normal equations already fail, no assumption of nonlinear truncation or
metric stationarity is needed. Other Clifford components, nonconstant
coefficients, other observing backgrounds and the I2B fork are not excluded.
This is an exact failed construction, not a no-go for GU.

## 6. Why observation cannot by itself finish the positivity argument

There is a precise obstruction beyond an inventory of missing tasks.
For fixed observing section `s_g`, let `P` record all smooth section jets.
Choose a nonzero smooth compact bump `f` supported in a coordinate ball in
`Y` disjoint from the section. Then `P(fU)=0` for every order of jet. In a
local K77 orthonormal frame choose the two rank-one grade-one distortions

```text
U_+ = e^0 gamma_4,       U_- = e^0 gamma_10,
< U_+, *U_+ > = +1,     < U_-, *U_- > = -1.
```

Their wedge squares vanish. Levi-Civita differentiation preserves Clifford
parity, while this Shiab reverses it, so `<T,S(D_B T)>=0` for grade-one `T`.
The cubic term vanishes for these rank-one fields; the curvature-linear
term cancels between opposite amplitudes. Consequently

```text
I1B[epsilon f U_±] + I1B[-epsilon f U_±] - 2 I1B[0]
    = ± kappa epsilon² integral_Y f² vol_G.
```

For nonzero `kappa` these nonzero values occur in the kernel of **all**
section jets. The algebraic signs and parity witnesses are exact probe
checks; bump support and parity give the analytic argument for varying
frames. If `I1B` factored through `P` on this smooth field space, the left
side would have to vanish. It does not. Bare observation is therefore not
an action quotient on this selected completion.

This is an off-shell failure of action descent. It is **not** a Hamiltonian
instability proof, a claim about physical particles, or an exclusion of
integrating out fields, solving constraints, or imposing another justified
domain. Analytic fields would require a different support argument. A
physical restriction must supply exactly the additional dynamics or domain
that the bare observation map lacks; it cannot be inferred from visibility.

This also explains the demonstrated underdetermination. The available
classical and observational data do not specify a common tuple

```text
(admissible solutions, constraints/reduction, physical real structure,
 physical state completion, positive pairing, self-adjoint H and its domain).
```

Replacing missing entries by a preferred positive norm changes the question;
identifying the entire observation kernel with gauge directions contradicts
the explicit action-descent test above. The external Chern–Simons and
indefinite Yang–Mills examples show that group data alone permit opposite
energy outcomes, but are not asserted to be two completions of the full GU
action. The obstruction to a verdict is thus a definite unconstructed
physical reduction and operator/domain, not a claim that all future GU
quantizations are impossible. This is underdetermination of the specified
physical object, not an undecidability theorem about the existence problem.

## 7. Verdict and the evidence that would change it

**Scoped rebuttal:** the implication from complexification alone in §3.1
fails, and its cited source supplies a counterexample. This removes that
argument as a proof of GU inconsistency.

**Surviving obligation:** a source-supported GU physical sector satisfying
all three positivity requirements remains unproved. The flat instability is
conditional; its transfer to canonical Zorro geometry fails. Direct curved
tests exclude a natural replacement family. Bare observation fails an exact
action-descent condition. These are substantive tests, but none identifies
an interacting physical GU Hamiltonian or proves that every possible one
fails. SC-META-53 stays `UNCERTAIN`; SA-U1/H59 stay open.

The smallest useful new mathematical input is an **action-derived physical
reduction on an actual stationary background**, specifying what happens to
the observation-invisible directions and a common invariant domain. With
that in hand, one can test the induced pairing, generator, energy and
interaction closure. A complete rebuttal of the live physical objection
would additionally need quantization with its declared regime of validity.
A fatal theorem would need a bad mode proved to survive that physical
reduction, or a no-go under explicit indispensable assumptions. Neither can
be replaced by another group decomposition or a fitted positive matrix.

explicitly distinguishes its Chern–Simons construction from indefinite
ordinary Yang–Mills. The appropriate conclusion is that **complexification
alone does not establish GU inconsistency**. A counterexample in a different
theory does not prove that a GU-specific no-go, with additional action and
domain assumptions, would be false. No numerical confidence in GU's physical
validity is inferred.
