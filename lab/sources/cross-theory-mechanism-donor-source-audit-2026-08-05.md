# Cross-theory mechanism-donor source audit

## Return

## Primary anchors and admitted claims

### Connes--Chamseddine noncommutative geometry

- A. H. Chamseddine and A. Connes, *The Spectral Action Principle*,
  arXiv:hep-th/9606001, https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9606001.
- A. Connes and A. H. Chamseddine, *Inner fluctuations of the spectral action*,
  arXiv:hep-th/0605011, https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0605011.

Admitted: a spectral triple packages algebra, Hilbert space and Dirac data; its
spectral action and inner fluctuations yield tightly coupled gauge, scalar and
fermion control conditions in the almost-commutative setting.

Not admitted: that GU's simple quaternionic carrier is the finite Standard-
Model algebra, or that a GU connection fluctuation automatically supplies the
finite order-one Dirac/Yukawa data. The local `gu-as-ncg` swing already rejects
that object identification.

### Loop quantum gravity / projective connection-space integration

- A. Ashtekar and J. Lewandowski, *Projective techniques and functional
  integration for gauge theories*, arXiv:gr-qc/9411046,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9411046; J. Math. Phys. 36 (1995) 2170--2191.

Admitted: projective limits of compact Hausdorff spaces can construct measures
on completed spaces of connections modulo gauge and support diffeomorphism-
invariant integration.

Not admitted: that such a measure is the normalized covariant Lorentzian
observer/domain functional required by the GU zero-mode construction. The
carrier, group, observable and normalization question must be mapped first.

### Asymptotic safety

- K. Falls, D. Litim, K. Nikolakopoulos and C. Rahmede, *Further evidence for
  asymptotic safety of quantum gravity*, arXiv:1410.4815,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.4815.
- K. Falls, C. King, D. Litim, K. Nikolakopoulos and C. Rahmede,
  *Asymptotic safety of quantum gravity beyond Ricci scalars*,
  arXiv:1801.00162, https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00162.
- G. de Brito, N. Ohta, A. Pereira, A. Tomaz and M. Yamada,
  *Asymptotic safety and field parametrization dependence in the f(R)
  truncation*, arXiv:1805.09656, https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.09656.

Admitted: high-order curvature truncations provide evidence for interacting UV
fixed points and finite relevant directions; parametrization and regularization
choices can materially change fixed points and critical exponents.

Not admitted: a theorem that the actual GU action has a UV fixed point. The
repo's FRG work remains truncation- and scheme-conditional, and a new run is
premature while the selected action/domain is moving.

### Higher gauge / String-group integration

- J. Baez, A. Crans, D. Stevenson and U. Schreiber, *From Loop Groups to
  2-Groups*, arXiv:math/0504123, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0504123.
- A. Henriques, *Integrating L-infinity algebras*, arXiv:math/0603563,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0603563.

Admitted: Lie 2-/L-infinity algebras with higher Jacobi data can be integrated
to higher group objects under stated hypotheses; period/integrality data can be
load-bearing, as in String-group models.

Not admitted: that Weinstein's super-IG bracket has already been constructed,
that its period class is integral, or that a String-group level can be chosen
without adding a datum. The translation to super-IG is a repository proposal
and must fail closed at each of those gates.
