# K77 exact coefficient-bank source custody

## Layer 0

This artifact separates three objects that must not be collapsed:

1. Weinstein's source grammar for the moving metric, frame, Hodge, `Phi`,
   Shiab and observation structures;
2. the repository's selected-Spin, first-transgression, fixed-operator
   grade-two coefficient construction; and
3. the serialized exact bank used to avoid recursively rebuilding (2).

The primary source does not specify a cache format, coefficient-bank API,
construction hash or replay policy. The return is therefore `SOURCE-SILENT`:
this Run is process infrastructure around a repository construction, not an
Eric-lane mathematical claim.

## Custody chain

The one-time builder records SHA-256 hashes for its producer, every nested
repository script loaded by that producer, and both source-reinspection
receipts used by the v0.122/v0.123 construction. The consumer verifies those
hashes before returning any coefficient. A changed producer or source receipt
therefore makes the cache stale rather than silently changing its meaning.

The bank remains conditional on:

- the real selected `Spin(7,7)` parent;
- the first-transgression action rather than a residual norm square;
- the selected scalar-Clifford-trace Shiab point;
- the fixed-operator grade-two receiver; and
- the existing distinction among selected Spin, two `U(32,32)` halves and
  full `U(64,64)` parents.

It does not source-select an action parent, supply an external datum, establish
a quotient or port the result to either unitary parent.
