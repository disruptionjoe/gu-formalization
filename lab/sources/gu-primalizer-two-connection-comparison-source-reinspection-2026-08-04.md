## Layer 0: three maps, not one

The source collision separates three objects:

1. the released D916 fermion operator on the rolled four-field carrier;
2. the unreleased two-connection cyclic fermion completion/rival;
3. the common-action mixed Hessians between Bose and Fermi Euler sectors.

The third object cannot be identified with the second merely because Portal
uses path language and TOE later displays a two-by-two mnemonic. In
particular, the planned tensor-to-spinor central-character `Hom` test answers
the wrong question for this gate and is withdrawn rather than reported as a
negative result.

## Primalizer source boundary

The draft supplies density-dual equation rows and the repository already
supplies the split spin pairing, K77 Hodge signs and trace-reversed metric
geometry. Their product canonically constructs pseudo-musicals on the
admissible associated-bundle sector:

\[
 \flat_{g,B,\mu}:\Omega^p(S)\longrightarrow\Omega^{14-p}(S)^!,
 \qquad
 R_{g,B,\mu}=\flat_{g,B,\mu}^{-1}.
\]

Using the absolute metric density `mu_g` does not choose an orientation and
does not consume P1. Rewriting the same construction with ordinary top forms
would carry the orientation line; that is an optional representation choice,
not a datum required by the density-valued action.

The sources do not establish an arbitrary `Y^14` atlas, a closed analytic
domain, Green operators, or a full cyclic two-connection action. Those remain
construction burdens.
