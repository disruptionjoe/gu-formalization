## Ward locus

The source's intended equivariance makes the naturality identity

`D Upsilon · R_xi = rho_*(xi) Upsilon`

the correct theorem target. At a zero-residual background its right-hand side
vanishes. This identifies the *four orbit columns* that a physical
diffeomorphism Ward check must kill. It does not supply the six transverse
metric columns, the residual pairing `K`, an adjoint, a Green operator, or a
BFV quotient.

The exact source claim is therefore narrower than “build the whole Frechet
bank in order to know whether the Ward orbit vanishes,” and broader than
“tensor covariance alone is already the complete selected-action
coefficient calculation.” The current wave tests and preserves that middle
position.

The exact Clifford calculation adds a conditional selector:

- `(7,7)` has `p-q=0 mod 8`, ordinary real Majorana--Weyl halves, and a

Majorana--Weyl carrier without an extra symplectic doublet**. The requirement
is a named condition, not a theorem that every
possible GU reconstruction must adopt. K95 remains a useful pseudoreal
comparator; its Clifford/Krein structures may not be ported into K77.
