# Weinstein GU primary-source packet

## Purpose

This is not another general synopsis of Geometric Unity. It asks a narrower
question:

> Do Weinstein's own public materials constrain any of the exact missing maps
> in the frozen N1/N3 source-action and external-datum construction?

The frozen comparison objects are:

- [`unified-source-datum-packet-v0-2026-07-30.md`](../../explorations/unified-source-datum-packet-v0-2026-07-30.md);
- [`unified-source-datum-variational-emission-map-2026-07-30.md`](../../explorations/unified-source-datum-variational-emission-map-2026-07-30.md); and
- the eight missing maps listed in N3, beginning with the typed
  \(J_{\rm bridge}\) disposition, the connection-current Riesz map, and the
  full zero-order \(P_0/\rho(\Phi)/Y_{K,C}\) placement.

The source review uses five dispositions:

- `SOURCE-EXPLICIT`: the source displays the relevant object or formula;
- `SOURCE-BOUND`: the source narrows a candidate class but does not supply the
  complete typed map;
- `SOURCE-NEGATIVE`: the source says the needed object is open, missing,
  prospective, or unreleased;
- `ALREADY-ABSORBED`: a neighboring repo artifact already owns the result; and
- `NEW-CONSTRUCTION-PROMPT`: a source-grounded comparison against the frozen
  N1/N3 packet has not yet been executed.

None of these labels means that the displayed GU construction is
mathematically or physically correct.

## The construction-bearing source rows

### `WGS-01` — Weinstein did write a concrete first-order bosonic action

The 2021 draft, Section 9.1, equations (9.1)--(9.7), declares

\[
I^B_1:\mathcal G\times \operatorname{MET}(X^{1,3})\longrightarrow\mathbb R
\]

and writes, in normalized notation,

\[
I^B_1
=
\left\langle
T_\omega,\,
*
\left[
\odot_\omega\left(
F_{B_\omega}
+\frac12d_{B_\omega}T_\omega
+\frac13[T_\omega,T_\omega]
\right)
+\frac{\kappa_1}{2}T_\omega
\right]
\right\rangle ,
\]

where

\[
T_\omega=\varpi-\epsilon^{-1}d_0\epsilon
\in\Omega^1(Y,\operatorname{ad}P).
\]

The displayed translation-direction variation is

\[
\left.\frac{d}{ds}\right|_{0}
I^B_1(\epsilon,\varpi+s\alpha)
=
\langle\alpha,\Upsilon^B_\omega\rangle,
\qquad
\Upsilon^B_\omega
=\odot_\omega F_{A_\omega}+*\kappa_1T_\omega .
\]

**Disposition:** `SOURCE-EXPLICIT` and `ALREADY-ABSORBED`.

The action, its Chern--Simons-like completion, and its variation were already
transcribed and typed by:

- [`hourly-20260626-1003-cycle3-tau-source-locator-packet.md`](../../explorations/research-cycles/hourly-20260626-1003-cycle3-tau-source-locator-packet.md);
- [`hourly-20260625-0301-cycle3-rendered-dgu01-identity-transcription.md`](../../explorations/research-cycles/hourly-20260625-0301-cycle3-rendered-dgu01-identity-transcription.md); and
- [`W161-lens-foundational-action-2026-07-14.md`](../../explorations/W161-lens-foundational-action-2026-07-14.md).

What remains useful is not rediscovering the action. It is comparing this
source-native functional class to N1's different sum of Yang--Mills, parent,
distortion-current bridge, full-20, and defect terms.

**What it does not answer:** the source does not declare the complete
admissible \((\epsilon,\varpi)\) variation domain, the native
\(\operatorname{Sp}(32,32;\mathbb H)\) real-form translation, a Krein/Riesz
map, a global domain, or identity with N1's fields.

This matters for N3's current fork. The source-native organization places the

`ALREADY-ABSORBED`; using it as a rival to N1's separately inserted
distortion-current bridge is a `NEW-CONSTRUCTION-PROMPT`.

This is an inference about action architecture, not a proof that N1's bridge
is wrong. Before any cancellation claim, Layer 0 must construct:

\longleftrightarrow
\delta_A S_{20}
=J_D+J_F

including Hodge/density conversion, invariant adjoint pairing, projector and

### `WGS-03` — the quadratic completion is not optional in the source

At `02:35:10` in the Portal/Oxford transcript, Weinstein says that the
Shiab-applied curvature alone is not exact and therefore cannot be the
differential of a Lagrangian. He says a quadratic “eddy” tensor must be added
to obtain total swervature. The 2021 draft gives the corresponding
Chern--Simons-like terms explicitly:

\[
\frac12d_{B_\omega}T_\omega+\frac13[T_\omega,T_\omega].
\]

The same point appears earlier at `01:43:32--01:45:25`: the curvature and
augmented-torsion tensors must be assembled so that the relevant object is
exact.

**Disposition:** `SOURCE-EXPLICIT`, locator ownership
`ALREADY-ABSORBED`, and current N1/N3 comparison
`NEW-CONSTRUCTION-PROMPT`.

N1 contains \(\lVert F_A\rVert^2\), a parent term, and
\(\frac1{2\kappa}\lVert\theta\rVert^2-\langle\theta,J\rangle\), but it does
not yet establish that its connection Euler covector is the differential of
the source-native completed residual or that its quadratic terms are the
same objects as the source eddy. The efficient test is therefore an
exactness/no-double-counting comparison, not another term inventory.

### `WGS-05` — the source names a concrete zero-order mass carrier

Section 12.9 is explicitly introduced as a stylized example. Equations
(12.12)--(12.17) couple left and right Weyl components by

\[
\slashed D_A\psi_L=\frac{R(y)}4\psi_R,
\qquad
\slashed D_A\psi_R=\frac{R(y)}4\psi_L.
\]

The text calls \(R(y)/4\) a mass in a region where scalar curvature is
approximately constant and calls the coupling a zero-order scalar-curvature
term.

\(\Omega^1(Y,\operatorname{ad})\) contains a true-spin-zero invariant
subspace coming from a \(\Lambda^1\otimes\Lambda^1\) component, says the
field playing the role of fundamental mass is part of the gauge potential,
and gives the three-way link

\[
\text{cosmological ``constant''}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{spinless gauge field}
\longleftrightarrow
\text{fermion mass}.
\]

The draft's summary further says the cosmological constant is the VEV of the
field playing the role of fundamental mass.

**Disposition:** the scalar toy and three-way link are `SOURCE-EXPLICIT`;
their use as a full-20 zero-order placement is `SOURCE-BOUND` and a
`NEW-CONSTRUCTION-PROMPT`.

The mass/VEV idea is already owned at coarse level by
[`escape-corners-campaign-RESULTS.md`](../../canon/escape-corners-campaign-RESULTS.md)
and related tests. What has not been executed against N3 is the exact
placement branch:

\[
\Pi_{\rm spin\,0}
\operatorname{res}^V_s(T_\omega)
\longrightarrow
\operatorname{End}(E_{20})
\]

with the separate \(K\)-sesquilinear and \(C\)-bilinear reality conditions,

The scalar-curvature formula and the spinless gauge-potential statement must
also clear Layer 0 with each other. The source presents them in one stylized
