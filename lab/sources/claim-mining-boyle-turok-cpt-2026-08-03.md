## The nine claims, verified

### C1 — "The universe is CPT-symmetric: after the bang it is the CPT image of the universe before it; a universe/anti-universe pair emerging from nothing."

**VERIFIED AS A POSIT, not a derivation** (1803.08928). Abstract: "We
propose that the state of the universe does not spontaneously violate CPT."
The CPT condition is a **selection principle imposed on the state**, motivated
by the τ → −τ isometry of the analytically extended radiation-era metric. The
"emerging from nothing" line is an interpretive gloss via Stueckelberg
("it becomes natural to reinterpret the contracting half of our universe as an
anti-universe... emerging from nothing!") immediately followed by the caveat
that converting "this suggestive, semi-classical picture into a fully quantum
one... is an important task, beyond the scope of this Letter." Distortion
risk: presenting CPT symmetry of the state as derived. It is proposed.

### C2 — "The Big Bang is a mirror; the fields continue analytically through the singularity."

**VERIFIED WITH TWO CORRECTIONS.** (i) The bang is **not resolved**: it
remains "a topologically enforced singularity," characterized as a mild
Weyl-type singularity; the authors explicitly call nonsingular-bounce models
"misguided" (1803.08928). What continues through is the **analytic
continuation** of the conformal-factor-stripped data, with quantum
backreaction explicitly deferred ("A fuller treatment of the singularity to
include the trace anomaly and quantum back-reaction requires semiclassical
methods"). (ii) The word "mirror" is **not in the 2018 papers**; the
bang-as-mirror framing arrives in 2109.06204 ("an exotic mirror (a spacelike
surface which reflects in the time direction)") and 2208.10396 (title). In
2109.06204 the reflecting character is derived from the analyticity demand:
analyticity at the bang forces "either Neumann or Dirichlet conditions" —
a reflecting boundary condition — while no such condition holds at the de
Sitter boundary. The analyticity demand itself is posited.

### C3 — "Dark matter is a stable right-handed neutrino at 4.8 × 10⁸ GeV."

**VERIFIED, with the epistemic arrow corrected** (1803.08930, §4). One of
the three ν_R (ν_R,1) is stabilized by demanding the first row of the
neutrino Yukawa matrix vanish — equivalently "a global Z₂ symmetry under
ν_R,1 → −ν_R,1" — which decouples it from everything but gravity. Its
abundance is **gravitational production from vacuum mismatch**: the
CPT-invariant vacuum differs from the late-time adiabatic vacuum by a
Bogoliubov angle, so "late-time observers see heavy sterile neutrinos
emerging from the bang, for the same reason that distant observers see
Hawking radiation." The mass M₁ = 4.8 × 10⁸ GeV is then **fixed by matching
the observed dark-matter density** — a prediction of the mass *given* Ω_DM,
not an independently derived mass (with inflation added, an appendix moves it
upward by about an order of magnitude). Companion firm predictions, verbatim
from the abstract: "(i) that the three light neutrinos are majorana; (ii)
that one of these is exactly massless; and (iii) that, in the absence of an
epoch of cosmic inflation, there should be no primordial, long-wavelength
gravitational waves." Note for the foil contrasts: the stability condition
is an **exactly vanishing Yukawa row** — maximal breaking of any SU(4)_c-type
quark-lepton Dirac-mass relation for that generation (see the exploration's
number-contrast rows).

### C4 — "The matter–antimatter asymmetry is explained."

**CORRECTED: reinterpreted globally, outsourced locally.** The global
statement is an interpretation (the asymmetry on our side of the bang is
mirrored by the opposite asymmetry on the other side; the pair is CPT-neutral).
The local asymmetry on our side is produced by **standard thermal
leptogenesis** from the decays of the two unstable ν_R — "we expect the usual
thermal leptogenesis predictions to hold" (1803.08930). Nothing
program-specific generates the asymmetry.

### C5 — "Anomaly cancellation forces #(Weyl fermions) = 4 × #(gauge fields) = 48 ⇒ exactly three 16-fermion generations." [the campaign's claim 6 — the foil's core]

**VERIFIED AS LINEAR ALGEBRA; the words "anomaly," "forces," and "three
generations" each need a fence.**

- **Which anomalies do the forcing (task check a).** NOT gauge anomalies.
  SM gauge anomaly cancellation is per-generation and cannot fix the
  generation number — and the papers do not claim otherwise. The forcing
  quantities in 2110.06258 are the **free-field vacuum energy** and the
  **two Weyl (trace) anomaly coefficients a and c** — global, additive-over-
  species quantities (their Eqs. 13, 16a, 16b):
  E: n₀ − 2n_{1/2} + 2n₁ + 2n₀′ = 0; a: n₀ + 5.5n_{1/2} + 62n₁ − 28n₀′ = 0;
  c: n₀ + 3n_{1/2} + 12n₁ − 8n₀′ = 0. Because these are totals over the full
  field content (unlike per-multiplet gauge anomalies), they CAN constrain a
  total count — that is the mechanism's genuine structural insight.
  Crucially, E = a = c = 0 is **not a QFT consistency requirement** (trace
  anomalies do not make a theory inconsistent); it is a demand imported from
  the CPT/conformal-bang cosmology, which needs quantum Weyl invariance for
  the continuation through the Weyl singularity. Video-level distortion risk:
  flattening "consistency of their cosmological boundary posit" into
  "consistency of quantum field theory."
- **The forcing, exactly.** Three independent homogeneous conditions on four
  species numbers have the unique solution ray
  (n₀, n_{1/2}, n₁, n₀′) ∝ (0, 4, 1, 3) — re-derived by hand in the survey
  Addendum. Given n₁ = 12 (SM gauge group, an input): n_{1/2} = 48,
  n₀′ = 36, n₀ = 0. Within the stated truncation this is theorem-grade;
  the authors' own scope words: "only an initial exploration at the level of
  free quantum fields on a curved spacetime background," with interactions
  ("strongly affected") and dynamical spacetime explicitly deferred.
- **"Three generations" (task check c).** The conditions count **species,
  blind to gauge representations**: they fix the total 48, not the partition
  3 × 16. The generation reading imports family organization (complete
  SM + ν_R sixteens) from observation plus per-generation gauge anomaly
  freedom. The authors' abstract states it as a requirement-given-the-SM:
  "given the standard model gauge group SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1), it requires
  precisely 48 Weyl fermions, i.e., three generations of standard model
  fermions, including right-handed neutrinos." Status: **a uniqueness
  theorem inside a posited demand and truncation, plus an identification** —
  not a numerological fit, and not an unconditional theorem about nature.

### C6 — "36 dimension-zero scalars, trivial upon quantization (Gupta-Bleuler-like)."

**VERIFIED; 36 is DERIVED, not chosen** (task check b). n₀′ = 3n₁ = 36 falls

power spectrum of vacuum fluctuations"; once the local gauge symmetry is
accounted for, "the theory turns out to have no local degrees of freedom at
all. All local, gauge invariant operators commute hence the theory has only a
single physical state – the ground state or vacuum." The Ostrogradsky-ghost
worry for the fourth-order operator is the subject of a live continuation

epicycle; they are triple-loaded (cancellation + spectrum + no particles).

### C7 — "The Higgs is emergent/composite."

**VERIFIED AS A FORCED CONDITIONAL.** n₀ = 0 is part of the unique solution
ray: an elementary dimension-one scalar would spoil the cancellation, so the
abstract's conditional — the cancellation works "if the Higgs field is
emergent" — is load-bearing. The papers do not supply a composite-Higgs
model; the authors note the results "would appear to support this
speculation." A confirmed elementary Higgs kills the mechanism as stated.

### C8 — "The primordial spectrum is derived from SM parameters: n_s ≈ 0.958 (and the amplitude)."

**VERIFIED WITH THE AUTHORS' OWN HEDGES ATTACHED** (2302.00344,
preprint-only). Tilt: n_s ≈ 1 − 7α₃(M_P)/π = **0.958** — the QCD coupling
run to the Planck scale supplies 95% of the deviation from 1; inputs from
Buttazzo et al. Amplitude: predicted A = 12.9 ± 4.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ vs Planck's
21 ± 0.3 × 10⁻¹⁰ — "less than 2σ away." Two corrections against
video-level readings: (i) the Planck value they match (0.9587 ± 0.0056) is
the **extended fit** with running and running-of-running freed; baseline
Planck ΛCDM is 0.9649 ± 0.0042, ~1.2σ above their prediction; (ii) the
authors label the analysis "heuristic" and name the unverified assumptions —
a 30-orders-of-magnitude extrapolation in comoving scale and constancy of
n_s with scale ("we caution the reader that our result rests on some key
theoretical assumptions which we have yet to verify"). Status: derived
formulas inside posited assumptions; consistency at the 1–2σ level.
