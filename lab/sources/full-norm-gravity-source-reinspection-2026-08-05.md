# Full-norm gravity source reinspection

## Question located before evaluation

The predecessor asked for a cancellation that would leave **one pole total**.
The repo's older gravity map instead says that the favored full
second-fundamental-form norm has one massless Einstein pole **and** one
distinct massive spin-two pole. The source question is therefore narrow:

1. Does Weinstein actually choose the full tensor norm `|II|^2` over the
   trace-square `|H|^2`?
2. Does he say the complete GU gravitational propagator must have only one
   pole, or only that Einstein gravity must be recovered inside it?

## Disposition

**Decisive return: `SOURCE-SILENT`.**

The sources support a two-layer/norm-square architecture and require recovery
of Einstein-like gravity, but they do not settle the action-norm premise P2 and
do not impose a one-pole-total propagator. The full-`|II|^2` completion is a
repository construction backed by the exact Gauss identity and conditional
physics map. It may be tested and carried as the favored horn, but it cannot be
attributed to Weinstein as a released selection.

This return corrects two possible overreads at once:

- `norm square of Upsilon` is not automatically `norm full II`; and
- `recover an Einstein pole` is not `erase every non-Einstein pole`.

No source silence is converted into a failure. P2 remains the one-bit
action-norm fork already charged in the ledger.
