## SOURCE-CONFIRMS

multiple-time problem. For the fourteen-dimensional theory he says there are
no ordinary codimension-one initial conditions, that one instead has boundary
conditions, and that he does not know how to push Hamiltonian dynamics in
multiple temporal dimensions. He later calls the multiple-time and indefinite
quantization problems technical debt to be paid back.
