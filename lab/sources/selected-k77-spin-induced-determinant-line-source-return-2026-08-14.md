# Source return: spin-induced determinant-line gate

## Source confirms

- The checked 2021/Portal material directs the construction to the **unitary
  spinor-frame bundle**.
- Weinstein's 2025 TOE discussion supports the full `U(64,64)` principal-group
  arena.
- Curt's exposition distinguishes two `64`-complex-dimensional Weyl carriers,
  each of Hermitian signature `(32,32)`, from the separate full `U(64,64)`
  parent.
- The source places gauge and Higgs-like functions in the connection one-form
  `varpi`.
- Weinstein explicitly describes the total theory as non-chiral and claims an
  emergent low-curvature separation into luminous and dark chiral-looking
  sectors.

## Source is silent

The checked loci do not:

- replace the spin-induced frame bundle by an independently twisted
  `U(64,64)` bundle;
- choose a determinant line or a 128th root of it as extra global data;
- construct a nonzero first-Chern flux;
- construct finite `mu_128` holonomy or a disconnected-group character;
- provide the global fundamental-group, observation, BV/BFV or boundary data
  needed to make such holonomy physical;
- select one holonomy/flux sector;
- identify the central channel with hypercharge;
- derive the claimed luminous/dark decoupling from this global structure.

## Disposition

`SOURCE-CONFIRMS` applies to the unitary spinor-frame reading and the full
connection arena.  Triviality of the connected-Spin determinant character is
a repository theorem about that reading.  An independent twist, scalar root,
finite holonomy, boundary charge, selection law and physical decoupling remain
`SOURCE-SILENT`.

The operative distinction is:

```text
unitary frame bundle of the source-induced spinor bundle
    is not
an arbitrary unitary bundle with freely chosen determinant topology.
```

Compatibility with the abstract group is not source ownership of the global
bundle class.
