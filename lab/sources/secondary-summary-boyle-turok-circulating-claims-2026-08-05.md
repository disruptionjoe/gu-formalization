## Why this is kept

Two reasons, neither of which is "it is a source of claims."

1. **It records what circulates.** The repo learned on 2026-08-04 that a
   much-quoted line about the imposter appears nowhere in Weinstein's
   draft — a paraphrase that entered circulation and was cited as though
   primary. Keeping verified records of *what secondary material asserts*
   is how that gets caught earlier.
2. **It proposes work this repo already did**, and its recommendation
   differs from the answer we got. That contrast is the useful part.

## Its recommendation, and our answer

The summary advises: *"explicitly check the Boyle–Turok fermion–gauge
counting route as a potential escape or class-exit"* from Located-Not-
Forced, and *"flag any claim that no known mechanism forces three
generations."*

**We ran exactly that check on 2026-08-03, with both outcomes
preregistered. Verdict: OUTSIDE the delimited class — EK1 does not fire.**
All three class criteria fail; the decisive one is that every standard
species contributes positively to the c-anomaly, so **without the imported
36 scalars, cancellation is not merely unforced but unexpressible — the
entire forcing power resides in the import.** Layer-0 also bites: their 48
is a chirality-blind species multiplicity, not the net chiral index the
interior-even theorem governs.

The result was a sharpened fence, recorded in
`papers/drafts/no-go-class-relative-survey.md`: *a count can be forced by
quantum consistency-with-added-content, never by interior kinematic
operators.* Boyle–Turok is the strongest published example of the other
route — which makes it a valuable boundary marker, not a threat.

Surplus counted symmetrically at the time: theirs ≈ 0 to +1 nominal, −3
hostile; this repo's envelope ran +3/−3 on the same accounting. Neither
may cite the other's hostile count without quoting its own.
