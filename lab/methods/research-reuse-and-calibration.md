# Research reuse, calibration and autonomous pivots

Authority: Joe direct chat, 2026-11-27(c). This implements a bounded process
update under `program_focus`; scientific acceptance and source conventions
remain with their existing owners. It adds no scientific counter credit.

## One lookup, existing owners

Before a wave, query `python3 scripts/research_context.py --reuse TEXT` in
`lab/process/research-reuse-index.json`. Use `--hypothesis FILE.json` to compare
an explicitly declared object, carrier, convention, class and premises. The
reader reports declarations and differences; it cannot establish logical
coverage or choose work. Read the linked theorem and its hypotheses before
reusing it. An exact signature match is insufficient if the tested question,
domain or proof assumptions differ. A missing result requires a targeted
repository search, not a claim of novelty. Compute only the uncovered delta.

The index is curated for the current queue, not an exhaustive history. Each
entry points to its owner, certificate, exclusions, survivors and revival
condition. Keep correction and supersession history. When an authored coverage
check proves that a queued successor is already settled, mark it consumed or
covered beside the evidence in its existing packet; do not delete history or
use string similarity to infer theorem coverage. Existing path-dependencies
own causal traps; the index points to slow, misleading or redundant approaches
there or in their reports rather than creating a second narrative ledger.

`--check-cache` reads artifact hashes and declared verifier metadata without
running or importing the verifier. Distinguish a recorded successful receipt,
currently matching artifact bytes, and a fresh execution. Missing input,
argument, verifier or environment coverage stays unknown. Changed dependencies
require revalidation; unchanged hashes alone do not prove complete dependency
coverage or fresh correctness. Heavy checks remain serialized.

## Decide before scaling a computation

Check owned theorems and source/literature evidence first. Browse for a genuinely
new applicability or prior-art question; do not redo an already-covered search.
Prefer a class theorem or the smallest model that can decide the typed question.
A toy dimension, frozen symbol or Euclidean comparator is useful only within
its scope until the transfer is proved. Run cheap independent sign, bound or
symmetry probes before committing to an expensive calculation when they can
change the decision.

In the existing wave evidence, record one compact selection entry:

- Prior lookup, tested statement, signature and changed premises.
- Mandatory basis, class covered, source relevance and weakest unproved bridge.
- Outcomes and the action **or evidence/scope change** each would cause.
- Expected decisiveness and source reach per remaining cost, strongest eligible
  alternative, forecast delta and cost (label judgment versus measurement).
- Actual delta and measured cost when available; unknown costs stay unknown.
- Exclusions, survivors, consumed successors and exact next discriminator.

Skip a test only when it offers no useful decision or evidence gain. The same
next action can follow a scientifically informative result. Do not invent
precise probabilities or claim calibrated estimates before observations exist.
Use the assumption view and source claims for an “if GU succeeds” target list:
each necessary consequence needs a source page/equation, polarity and typed
bridge to the tested object. A stable connected branch, canonical energy
positivity or a particular conservation formula is not automatically a GU
requirement merely because a comparator uses it.

## Calibrate and quantify honestly

A new diagnostic needs a known-answer comparator and, where useful, a planted
failure before GU inference. Register its tested scope in the same index.
GR, positive-mass Stelle gravity and Dirac distinguish raw constrained energy,
physical quotient/norm, signed solutions and well-posedness. A negative energy
sign is not by itself a quantum ghost or an evolution failure. A conservation
identity for one tensor does not calibrate a different theta dictionary.
Comparator success checks the diagnostic, not the source-to-candidate bridge.

Source-sensitive results cite the actual page/equation and conventions. Test
both interpretations when a convention is genuinely ambiguous; an authenticated
source convention remains primary and alternatives remain comparators. Preserve
K77/K95, full-U/optional-Sp, total/observed/physical grades and contraction maps.

Record dependencies on the existing choice families. Use STRUCTURAL only for a
quantified class, or explicitly say “structural across tested variations.” Use
BUILD-SPECIFIC when an admitted countervariation changes the conclusion.
Dependence on a coefficient alone does not establish that countervariation.
Use UNDETERMINED for unresolved coverage. Check these failures before selecting
a successor; do not rebuild an excluded class under a new name.

## Continue and learn

An investigation defaults to three scientific waves. At that budget, or after
two consecutive waves without a decision-relevant delta, rerank and pivot
within the program. A bounded extension needs an uncovered discriminator and
remaining-cost rationale. This is never a global stop or a Joe checkpoint.
Finish started Tier-1 validation, preserve explicit authority and ordinary LC
cadence, and record the revival input. New evidence can reopen a route without
renewed permission. This does not authorize external or destructive actions.

About every ten scientific waves, use the existing weekly metrics for a short
review: forecasts versus actual outcomes/costs, repeated work, slow routes and
useful shortcuts. Update estimates and the existing queue. Do not create a new
schedule, duplicate cost ledger or retroactively fabricate forecasts.

Tag claim reach as candidate / structural class / source. Before publishing a
source-level negative claim, flag an external expert check; it is a release
condition, not a research waiting condition or permission to contact anyone.
GU-independent results may be tagged in the existing opportunity register for
the paper track; a bounded prior-art check precedes dedicated writing. Neither
a tag nor a literature search establishes world novelty or authorizes release.
