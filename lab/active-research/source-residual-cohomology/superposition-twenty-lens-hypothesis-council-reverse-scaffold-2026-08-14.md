# GU superposition twenty-lens hypothesis council and reverse scaffold

## Correction of scope

The first twenty-lens pass treated the lenses as an audit checklist for the
current `SR-1C` background construction. That was too narrow. The intended use
is a hypothesis council over everything learned in the superposition channel:

```text
each lens states its strongest current hypothesis;
the lenses vote among the resulting hypothesis families;
high-conviction minority positions remain visible;
the synthesis determines the next series of swings.
```

The `SR-1C` source-coordinate variational prolongation remains useful, but it
is one forward enabling packet inside this larger scaffold. It is not the
channel scaffold itself.

## Evidence the council inherits

The council starts after, and does not erase, these exact results:

- fixed split-natural `J4` and `J10` fail at the tested local K77 scope;
- the proposed `+/-Jhat10` bit lies in one connected source-frame redundancy
  orbit at that scope;
- the forty moving-split tangents and forty mixed ghosts form an exact
  contractible local Stueckelberg cone;
- `J10` nevertheless descends fibrewise on the associated spinor bundle;
- the split-orbit tangent itself has no invariant real complex structure;
- the canonical-`B_Z`, nonzero-`T` branch passes residual, translation Euler
  and Bianchi rows at field one-jet grade for two exact algebraic amplitudes;
- primitive epsilon and total fixed-`varpi` metric stationarity are not
  determined at that jet order;
- the first `SR-1C` execution object is now typed as `O_SR1C`, a common-basis
  evaluator for `j^1(E_B-E_T)` and the fixed-`varpi` metric derivative slots;
  current receipts do not serialize it on the two-root branch;
- no complete stationary GU background, total `K/L`, physical cohomology,
  positive pairing, common domain or intrinsic superposition law exists yet.

## Reverse conditional-building rule

The retained Variancer invariant for this channel is: start with the
superposition hypothesis and reason backward through its necessary conditions.
The scaffold is therefore:

```text
T0  intrinsic physical superposition preserved by evolution
     implies
T1  internally owned complex physical state space
     implies
T2  nontrivial positive physical cohomology with descended J and pairing
     implies
T3  J/pairing preserve ker L and closure(im K), including boundary reduction
     implies
T4  one common total bulk--fermion--boundary K/L complex and domain
     implies
T5  an action-owned stationary background and compatible local/global solution
```

At every arrow:

```text
failure of a necessary condition -> scoped kill;
survival                      -> NOT-YET-FALSIFIED only;
exhaustion of every internally owned route -> support H0;
missing lower data            -> launch the smallest forward enabling swing.
```

Forward construction serves the reverse scaffold. It does not replace it.

  is their forward support packet.
