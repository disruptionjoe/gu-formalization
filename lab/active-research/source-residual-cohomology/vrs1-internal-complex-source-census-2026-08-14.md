## What the exact inherited results decide

### Fixed and moving K77 structures

RF-1 gives the finite fixed-family exhaustion. The complete split-equivariant
commutant contains only the four square-minus-one units in the table. The
rolled `J4` route fails the owned observed principal operator, while fixed
`J10` is broken by a live rank-eight subimage of the selected rank-25 gauge
image. The two signs of the moving rolled `J10` lie in one connected
background-fixing source-frame redundancy orbit; they do not define a local
physical bit.

RF-2 then distinguishes a repaired carrier from a physical result. The forty
moving-split variables and forty mixed ghosts form an exact local
contractible pair. `J10` is well-defined fibrewise on `G x_H S`, but the
split-orbit tangent has no invariant real complex structure. Thus the moving
reduction repairs the frozen spinor descent defect without making the whole
coupled deformation complex complex-linear.

### Boundary and BFV structures

The action owns nontrivial real preboundary symplectic and endpoint moment
data. Fixed, free and charged endpoint horns do not impose the same reduction
or Green problem. None of those exact facts selects a positive compatible
complex polarization. This makes `H-B` necessary to test, not already proved:
the boundary supplies an owned reduction arena, not a Hilbert space.

### Second action and Hessian structures

At residual zero, the second-action Hessian conditionally factors through the
first residual linearization as the symmetric Gram-type object
`L^! Q L`; away from residual zero an additional residual-dependent term is
present. This gives `I2` a privileged ownership role for the differential and
quadratic control. It does not turn the Hessian into a complex structure or a
symplectic/Hermitian form. `H-D` therefore survives only as a discriminator
inside the composite route.

### Twistor structures

The base local-twistor carrier and normal homogeneous-twistor fibre are exact
but distinct. A curved Bach-flat detour comparator can form a differential
complex whose cohomology may later be tested, yet no action-selected total GU
twistor-plus-normal connection, common Lorentzian domain, endpoint admission
or positive quotient is owned. The twistor family is retained as a possible
evolution/intertwining adapter, not promoted to the action owner.

### Moduli and nonlocal structures

`SC-ACT-06` asserts rich classical moduli and a Euclidean elliptic deformation
complex after redundant equations are removed. The register explicitly marks
Euclidean ellipticity and rich moduli untested for the selected Lorentzian K77
program. A claimed family is not yet an integrable complex family or a
positive physical state space.

Likewise, non-Markovian reduction, channel and memory-kernel language remains
an external transfer until GU supplies the action-owned state algebra,
generator and reduction map. Present analogies are killed as intrinsic
sources; the class of future action-derived nonlocal constructions is not
exhausted.
