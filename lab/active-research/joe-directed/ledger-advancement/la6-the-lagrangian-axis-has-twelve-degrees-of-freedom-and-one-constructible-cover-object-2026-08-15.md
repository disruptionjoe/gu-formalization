## 1. THE COMPUTATION

### 1.1 Method

Model the axis as a linear control system. The 21 active rows are the state
coordinates. The **atoms** are the open objects the rows currently wait on,
named in the ledger's own vocabulary. `B` is the 21x13 incidence: `B[i][j] = 1`
iff row `i`'s v0.258 text names atom `j` as an open requirement.

**Effective degrees of freedom := `rank_Q(B)`** — the dimension of the reachable
subspace of the 21-dimensional row-state space under arbitrary independent
variation of the atoms. If `rank_Q(B) = r`, then no combination of grants can
move the axis in more than `r` independent directions, and `21 - r` is the
dimension of the space of *row combinations that are constant under every
possible grant*.

The incidence is a **declared typing**, not a machine inference, so it is made
auditable rather than asserted: every 1 in `B` carries an exact substring that
must occur in that row's own v0.258 text, and the script fails on the first
miss. Ten negative controls bound the tokens so an over-broad substring cannot
silently inflate `B`.

**The certificate has demonstrated power against its own author.** A first-pass
`LT-GR5 / H_ABSOLUTE_SCALE` entry was asserted and **rejected**: the
`NOT_SCALE_SELECTOR__NORMALIZED_FUNCTIONAL_OPEN` tokens belong to `LT-GR2c`'s
frontier grade, not to `LT-GR5`, and `LT-GR5`'s v0.258 text contains no
occurrence of "scale" in any case. That entry is removed and the exclusion is

```text
pi_3( Spin(6) x Spin(4) )  =  pi_3(SU(4)) (+) pi_3(SU(2)) (+) pi_3(SU(2))
```

**Finding — exact, and it is a counting statement, not a value statement.** The

## 5. POSTFLIGHT

### 5.1 Strongest overclaim in this artifact

**"Effective degrees of freedom = 12."** The number is exact given the matrix,
and the matrix is a declared typing by one reader in one session. `rank_Q(B)`
measures the rank of *my vocabulary's* incidence, not of GU's. A different atom

is normalized"; or coarser, merging `B` and `C` — returns a different integer.
The conditioning sweep bounds single-cell error at 85-90% but says **nothing**
about basis error, which is the dominant term.

**The defensible core, stated at the width it can carry:** the minimum cover is
2 and unique, the dual gap is 0, and both facts survive 90% of single-entry
perturbations. Those depend only on `A` being nearly universal and `LT-GR2a`

**Quote the 2, not the 12**, if only one number travels.

### 5.2 Strongest contrary reading

*"`rank(B) = 12 < 21` is not a finding; it is arithmetic. Any 21x13 matrix has
rank at most 13. You have discovered that 13 < 21."*

This is the sharpest objection available and it is partly right. The rank bound
`min(21, 13) = 13` is free; the content is only the drop from 13 to 12, which is
one dimension, produced by the two coupled pairs and the zero row. **One
dimension is a thin finding to hang a headline on.**

The reply — and it is a reply, not a dismissal — is that the informative
statistic was never the rank. It is the **cover**: 2, unique, gap 0. That number
is not bounded by the matrix shape (a 21x13 incidence can easily need 7 or 8
atoms to cover), it is not free, and it is the number that changes what should be
scheduled. The rank is reported because it was asked for and because it prices
the "21 independent advances" plan at 12; it should not be the headline. §5.1
already says which number to carry.

A second contrary reading, weaker but real: the coupled pair
`{LT-GR1b, LT-SM3b}` may be an artifact of both rows being *terminal*
(`OVER_DETERMINED`) and therefore under-described. A row that has been
adjudicated is not written to the same depth as a live one, so its atom set is
thin for editorial rather than structural reasons. That would make the coupling
spurious. **Not resolved here.**

**The atom `A_ACTION_OWNED_BACKGROUND` is doing 18 of the work and it is the
