## 2. THE THREE ADJUDICATIONS

### Adjudication A — `LT-SM1b`'s second-atom `reason_kind`

**The contested claim** (LA-7 §3.2): Route A (2026-08-12 surplus attempt,
`explorations/lt-sm1-horn-surplus-attempt-2026-08-12.md:460-468`) recommends
`REAL_PARAMETER`; Route B (LA-6 §2.9 IT-2 incidence pricing) recommends
`MISSING_CONSTRUCTION`. LA-7 reports the disagreement and declines to pick.

**Picked: `MISSING_CONSTRUCTION` today, with `REAL_PARAMETER` recorded on the
row as the named successor kind behind the gate "the 1→3 branching is built
and the relative normalization becomes computable."**

Grounds:

1. **Both routes agree the branching is unbuilt** — Route A names the unbuilt
   branching alongside `g_A^{-2}` and its own outcome was
   `SURPLUS-UNCOMPUTABLE` *because both sides of the subtraction are
   unresolved*; Route B prices the atom set as one priced bit plus one
   **unpriced construction**. A `REAL_PARAMETER` demand asserts "supply the
   number(s)"; today there is no defining equation whose number could be
   supplied. The kind types the row's *current* blocker, and the current
   blocker is a construction.
2. **Non-laundering direction.** `MISSING_CONSTRUCTION` sits farther from
   dischargeability than `REAL_PARAMETER`. Where two honest readings disagree,
   the channel's standing rule takes the more indebted one and names the
   upgrade gate — the same discipline as LA-7's own refusal of `ONE_BIT` on
   `LT-SM1a`.
3. **Route A is subsumed, not overridden.** Its `REAL_PARAMETER` describes the
   post-gate state; recording it as the successor kind preserves Route A's
   content exactly where it becomes true. This is LA-7's own "least-committal
   reading consistent with both," now asserted rather than offered.

**Reversal conditions.** (i) Build the branching (or any construction making
the relative normalization computable from the source action): the row
re-types `REAL_PARAMETER` by its recorded successor note. (ii) A proof that
the branching is *unconstructible from the selected action* re-types toward

`REAL_PARAMETER`-today would have been wrong in the other direction as well.

### Adjudication B — placement of the LT-GR6b successors: `rows`, one carrier

**The open question** (packet item 1, closing paragraph; LA-11 Lens Q3):
whether the object belongs in `rows` at all, given CB-B §PRE-3's

1. **The register cannot carry a debt.** `layer0_objects_compared` is a list
