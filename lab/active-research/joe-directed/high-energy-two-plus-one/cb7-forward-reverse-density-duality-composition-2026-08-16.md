## 2. Why degree and product grading diagnose but do not repair

Write half parity additively in `F_2`. For any one cell let `r` and `c` be the
row and column primalizer bits. Homogeneous typing requires

```text
r + c = parity(d0) = 0,
r + c = parity(H210) = 1.
```

The contradiction is local to one displayed sum. Giving `r` and `c` arbitrary
dependence on form degree, source row, source column, or conjugate half does not
help because the same `r+c` acts on both summands.

The exact finite classifier exhausts:

- all `16` degree-only row/column conventions; and
- all `256` invertible row-slot/column-slot half-flip conventions.

Every convention types exactly one of the two terms per relevant cell and
zero of four complete cells. The product grading `G=(-1)^form J` swaps the
reported parity classes of the two terms; it does not make them equal. A
uniform odd Clifford factor on the whole cell does the same thing. The repair
must be summand-specific.
