Every preflight must state all five rules:

1. **Declare the horn.** Use `H210`, `H54`, `HBOTH`, or `HNONE`; never imply
   that the lane derived which horn nature or the action selects.
2. **Build downstream.** Test carrier inclusion, intertwiner/Clebsch structure,
   equation-9.16 half/cell compatibility, observation/quotient survival, and
   the family kernel under the assumed horn.
3. **Action and external datum are off-limits.** Do not design a new action,

   Record those as dependencies only.

   chiral index or delete the conjugate half.
5. **No physical promotion.** An algebraic rank-one row is not yet a massive
   third family, a named family, an observed threshold, or a prediction.
