# CB-7 integration and reprioritization

## Result first

The printed H210-bearing equation-(9.16) cells do not have one homogeneous
source-half type under any summand-blind homogeneous row/column or degree
primalizer. In each of the four relevant cells,

```text

H210 varpi      is ambient-half odd.
```

Both terms have the same row, column, and form-degree incidence. Every common
homogeneous convention therefore shifts both equally and leaves their relative
parity mismatch unchanged. Same-half duality types only the derivative;
opposite-half density duality types only H210. Product grading detects the
mismatch rather than repairing it.

There is exactly one current pointwise escape that also preserves the banked
source-module census: leave H210 untouched and postcompose the derivative
principal symbol with a supplied horizontal odd Clifford factor,


source operator. It needs a coherently transforming nowhere-null section
`q_H` (or an explicit line-valued codomain), correct base/stage typing, a
compatible Clifford connection, a forward overlap square, and a reverse
lower-order completion containing the forced `nabla q_H` term.

## Hostile-review repairs adopted

The sixteen-lens hostile review replayed CB-7 and its relevant CB-1, CB-2,
CB-6, source-sign, and v0.140 dependencies. The exact no-go and current
Clifford/source-module classifier survived. Integration adopts these material
repairs:

1. “Summand-blind” means homogeneous source-half primalizers. Arbitrary
   grading-mixing or enlarged-target maps are different grammars.
2. A bare line does not define an untwisted `S+ -> S-` map. The conditional
   horn must supply a coherent section/trivialization, or the entire target
   must retain the line twist. Full rank additionally needs a nowhere-null
   section.
3. `gamma(q_H) d0` is a conditional first-order/principal-symbol type, not a
   completed four-cell operator. Postcomposition has no forward `nabla q_H`
   term, but the reverse formal transpose `d0* gamma(q_H)*` differentiates the
   coefficient and does.
4. A source-`Y` section, a pulled-back `X` section, and a graph-plane `H_J`
   section are different objects. No downstream observer-plane vector repairs
   the upstream source operator without a typed bridge.
5. Boolean/string scope guards protect semantics but are not independent
   covariance, Leibniz, adjoint, or descent proofs.
