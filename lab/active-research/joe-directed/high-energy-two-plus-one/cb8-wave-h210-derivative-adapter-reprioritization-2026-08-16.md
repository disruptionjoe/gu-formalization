# CB-8 integration and path closure

## Result first

The derivative-side repair is mathematically coherent but no longer fertile
as the leading H210 path.

Given a supplied source-`Y`, coherently transforming horizontal covector
section `q_H` and a compatible Clifford connection,

```text
D_q = gamma(q_H) o d0
```

is a natural first-order bundle operator. Its forward symbol and Leibniz rule
are exact, and postcomposition creates no forward `nabla q_H` term.

Its formal density transpose necessarily is

```text
D_q^times alpha
  = gamma(q_H) nabla^a alpha_a
    + gamma(nabla^a q_H) alpha_a.
```

The second term cannot be dropped. On the total one-form bundle it is well
typed, but an explicit current-`Cl(7,7)` first jet makes it nonzero on the
pure-normal H210 Z sector on both halves. Linearity then proves that the jets
which avoid this route form a proper kernel: generic first jets contaminate Z,
although special nonzero jets may not.

Therefore a generic source-`Y` `q_H` is killed only as an **isolated reverse-
H210 completion**, not as a formal transpose. Preserving the clean H210
zero-order story requires the exact additional condition

```text
L_q restricted to Z = 0,
```

with `nabla_N q_H=0` sufficient and full parallelism stronger. No banked source
split or connection implies that condition. It is a newly stipulated horn.
