## 3. Why the word “pullback” was causing a false promotion

Two mathematically different constructions appear next to one another.

### 3.1 Restriction and branching

At the associated-bundle level one restricts the ambient bundle to the image
of the section and then uses a chosen tangent/normal splitting:

```text
s^*(T*Y) ~= T*X + N*,                         fibre rank 14,
RS(TX + N) = Q + Z + F.
```

This is the level at which equation (12.22) identifies F as the revealed
imposter-shaped representation and equation (11.6) identifies Z's internal
`144`. The normal covector representation remains in the restricted fibre;
the operation does not reduce fourteen one-form slots to four.

### 3.2 Pullback of a differential one-form

For `s(x)=(x,g_I(x))` and
`omega=omega_mu dx^mu + omega_I du^I`, literal pullback is

```text
(s*omega)_mu = omega_mu + omega_I partial_mu g_I.
```

It has a rank-four target and contracts the form leg with the graph Jacobian.
A pure normal covector can survive when the section slope is nonzero and can
vanish on a flat graph. B5 and VZ4 therefore prohibit both shortcuts:

- “pullback discards the normal part” is false in general;
- “the associated Z sector exists after restriction, therefore its
  differential-form image survives injectively” does not follow.

For the H210 tensor specifically, contraction changes the typed codomain from
the pre-observation free normal-vector-spinor/`144` presentation to
`T*X tensor s^*S`. A nonzero contracted rank is survival of a spinor-valued
4D one-form map, not preservation of a free Z/internal-`144` codomain.

The two functors can be composed in a detailed construction, but they cannot
be substituted for one another. In particular, the equation-(12.22) F/Q/Z
branching is a representation receipt, not already an observation-rank receipt.
