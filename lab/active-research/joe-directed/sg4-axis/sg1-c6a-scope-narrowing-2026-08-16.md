**This repair could be misread in both directions, so I state the direction
myself before making the argument.**

bigger than two bits." **It is not.** The residual over the cell space the leg

the chirality assignment is **written** — twice, at two loci, at two layers —

coded **more conservatively than its own sibling**, and the direction of the
defect is *against* the leg's headline, not for it. I add no tilt, move no
count, and leave the 3-vs-4 discrepancy exactly where the two legs left it.

The honest statement is that this makes GU's field content **neither more nor
less determined**. It moves one question from *silently answered by a bit that
cannot answer it* to *answered at the source layer, already, by CR-B*.

## 3. NARROWING or EXTENSION — the decision

**It is a NARROWING**, on four independent grounds.

**(a) The cells are right; only the sentence over-reaches.** An extension is the
repair when a coded cell is wrong. None is. §2.4.

**(b) The narrowing is provably inert — shown exhaustively, not by sampling.**
The `note` field is consumed **exactly once in the entire file**, at line 476:

```
src_blob = "".join(c["source"] + c["note"] for c in COMMITMENTS)
```

— the firewall's forbidden-string scan. So *no rewording of any note can move
any bit, whatever it says*, provided it does not introduce `chi(K3)`,
`A-hat=3` or `/8 manufacture`. The proposed narrowing introduces none. A
from-scratch shadow recomputation independently reproduces every structural
output: three field values, two invariance values, two phase values,
`allowed_prov = {GRADED_IG}`, all four corners surviving,
`(ABSENT,MASSIVE) → {B}`, `(PRESENT,CHIRAL) → {A}`, and neither bit alone
forcing a unique carrier.

**(c) An extension would change the leg's headline number.** The result *is*
"the residual is exactly a 2-bit square". Adding a fourth axis changes that
number, and would require re-coding all ten commitments against the new axis
**after the answer is known**. It would also break the cross-leg agreement
receipt, since LEG-B and the referee both code three axes.

**(d) Decisively — the motive for the axis is downstream.** The reason anyone
now wants a chirality axis is that CR-B needed a selector on one. **Adding an
axis to a predeclaration because a downstream result needs a selector on it is
the retro-fit, even when the axis is real.** That is not a slippery-slope
worry; it is the exact shape of the failure the predeclaration was built to
prevent, applied to axes instead of to cells.

## 4. What the receipt protects — and the blind spot that decides the case

`canon/gu-forces-field-space-declaration-RESULTS.md` states the receipt in three
legs, all present verbatim:

1. **Independent agreement.** "Both enumerations constructed independently
   (LEG-A cell-basis, LEG-B constraint-intersection basis); they AGREE on the
   residual and the 4-corner bijection."
2. **Referee verdict.** "refuted=false, p_hacked=false", having explicitly
   constructed and refused the specific p-hack the council feared.
3. **Zero hard eliminations on the cell axes.** "No commitment was allowed to
   silently delete a corner."

Against those three, a narrowing note edit is harmless: it changes no computed
output, so legs 1–3 are untouched.

**The fourth leg is the one that decides it, and it is the file's own
sentence:** *"Predeclaration is frozen: no row is edited after the intersection
is computed."* That is unconditional over **rows**, not merely over coded cells,
and `note=` is a kwarg of `predeclare(...)`, captured by `FROZEN_TABLE`. A note
is part of a row.

**And here is the finding that makes refusal not merely correct but obligatory.**
There is **no hash receipt** anywhere in the campaign — no `sha256`, `hashlib`,
`hexdigest` or `digest` in any of the three `gu-forces` files. The in-file freeze
guard is **same-process only**: `FROZEN_TABLE` is built at line 259 and
`_refreeze` re-compared at line 272, both within a single run. **An edited file
simply re-freezes its edited table and passes.** The probe demonstrates this
mechanically rather than arguing it: a copy of LEG-A with C6a's predeclared note
edited runs to **32/32, exit 0**. The guard cannot see the edit.

So the receipt's only **trust-free** layer — the one a reader can verify without
believing anything about the editor — is that **the file has never been
touched**. That layer is destroyed by *any* edit, including a provably benign
one, and it cannot be restored. A benign edit still costs it, because the proof
of benignity lives outside the file, in a claim about the file, and eliminating
the need for exactly that kind of claim is the entire point.

**Therefore the edit is refused, and the response is the inverse move: the
predeclaration is pinned from outside.** The probe records the byte-length
(30243) and SHA-256 of `leg_a_forcing_enumeration.py`, and of
`referee_leg_a_independent.py`, as of 2026-08-16. **This converts an
immutability that was previously a social convention into a machine check that
turns red on any future edit — at zero cost to the predeclaration, because
pinning a file does not touch it.** The campaign now has the receipt it never
had, and it was added without spending the one it did have.

*Secondary observation, logged not pressed:* the canon receipt's wording "every
GU commitment encoded as ALLOW=all-cells with ZERO hard eliminations" is
unqualified but not literally true — C1 codes `inv_provenance_out={"SUGRA_4D"}`,

claim holds exactly for the three *cell* axes, and no corner is deleted by it, so

## 7. What the three dependent gates inherit, and what the selector is

The routed question splits into two layers that wear the same words.

**Layer 1 — DECLARED CONTENT.** Which of `S-FULL-DIRAC`, `S-HALF-OPPOSITE`,
`S-HALF-SAME` the field-content declaration carries.
**Selector: the source, already read — and it is NOT an SG4 bit.** SG4 is
defined in the leg's own docstring as *"GU's UNWRITTEN source-action field-space
declaration"*. The chirality assignment is **written**: `[00:49:16]` gives the
unsubscripted arena, `[00:32:46]` gives the opposite-half pairing of the printed
corners. Those are not two candidate values of a free bit; they are statements at
two granularities, and CR-B has already resolved the layer — four printed corners
in an unsubscripted `S̸`, carrying classes 3,1,1,3, partitioning into exactly two
class-homogeneous halves, with `S-HALF-SAME` already refuted in canon.

**Layer 2 — OPERATIVE SPLIT.** Whether the VEV is on, whether the class-2
bosonic insertion is switched on, which emergent half is dynamically realized.
**Selector: SG4 bit 2, correctly.**

**7.1 — `CR-B`. Inherits a wording debt, not a result defect.** §4.3 routes
*"which effective split is dynamically realized"* to bit 2. That is a Layer-2
question and bit 2 is its right home; the routing is **sound**. What CR-B must
not carry is §5's table row *"the carrier question is now typed onto bit 2
specifically"* if "carrier question" is read to include Layer 1. CR-B's own
forbidden-summaries already draw the line — *"the DECLARATION is decided; the
OPERATIVE reading is SG4 bit 2"* — so the correction is to CR-B's compression,
not to its result. **This is the honest size, and it is smaller than "CR-B took
an unlicensed step".**

**7.2 — `CS-1`. Inherits nothing.** Its question is which of two horns is
operative — a first-order operator between classes versus a class-2 insertion —
