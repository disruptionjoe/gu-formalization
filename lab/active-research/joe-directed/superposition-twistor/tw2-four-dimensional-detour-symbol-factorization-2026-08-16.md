# TW-2 — four-dimensional detour symbol factorization

## Result first

The open operator-factorization question has a useful but sharply bounded
answer.

On the ordinary four-dimensional twistor-spinor bundles, the third-order
detour middle symbol has an exact normalized cubic representative built from
the first-order Rarita--Schwinger symbol. Let

```text

Tw      = ker Gamma_4 in T*X tensor S, rank 12;
P       = Pi_4;
c(xi)   = Clifford multiplication by xi-sharp;
q(xi)   = g^-1(xi,xi);
T(xi)   = P (xi tensor -);
T#(xi)  = iota_(xi-sharp) P;
Q(xi)   = P (1 tensor c(xi)) P.
```

Here `T#` is the **algebraic metric-dual principal symbol**. It is not a
constructed formal adjoint on a closed analytic domain. Exact Clifford algebra
gives

```text

T# T      = (3/4) q I,
Q^2       = q P - T T#.
```

Consequently the unique projective member of the bounded cubic grammar
`span{Q^3,qQ}` that kills the twistor image on both sides is

```text

  = Q (Q^2 - (1/4)qP)
  = Q^3 - (1/4)qQ
  = (3/4)qQ - (1/2) T c T#,

N_3 T = 0,
T# N_3 = 0.
```

This is an exact normalized cubic complex symbol inside the declared
`span{Q^3,qQ}` grammar, consistent with the standard twistor-spinor detour
leading line. It is not a new full curved formula or an independent
normalization extracted from the standard theorem. Gover--Somberg--Soucek
construct the middle map as

```text

```

and show that its leading term is, up to a nonzero multiple,

```text

```

where `K:Tw^1 -> Tw^2`, `R_1`, and `R_2` are first order. In dimension four
their sequence splits into the two chiral complexes. This probe does not
construct `K`, `Tw^2`, `R_2` or `K*`, and it does not prove that the declared
two-dimensional cubic grammar exhausts every standard equivariant cubic.

The current K77 rolled symbol does **not** equal `N_3`. Under the declared
base-supported `Pi_4` compression, however, its first-order block is exactly


Thus the first-order factors in the cubic are present as compressed subblocks
