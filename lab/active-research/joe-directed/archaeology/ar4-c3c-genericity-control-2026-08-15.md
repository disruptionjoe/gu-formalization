> **Let `V` be a nondegenerate real quadratic space of dimension `N >= 2`, and

>
> 1. **`J` commutes with every split bivector;**
> 2. **`J` anticommutes with every mixed bivector;**
> 3. **`J^2 = (-1)^(a(a-1)/2 + q_A) = +-1`, so `J` is invertible;**
> 4. **hence `[varpi, J] = 2 varpi_mix J`, and `[varpi, J] = 0` if and only if
>    `varpi_mix = 0`, with obstruction kernel exactly `0` and rank exactly
>    `a * b`.**

> parities of `a` and `b`. Nothing in it refers to `Cl(7,7)`, to `(1,3)+(6,4)`,
