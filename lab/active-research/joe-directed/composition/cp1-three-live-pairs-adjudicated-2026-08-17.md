## 1. PREFLIGHT — six lenses, run inline

**Lens 1 — adjudication design (verdicts are claim-indexed).** The extractor's
hit is an allegation; the file fact is the evidence; the verdict must name what
it binds. Consequence: each pair below states its three-way question
(DISCHARGE / CONDITION / NEIGHBOR, or COMPOSE / HOMONYM / SUPERSEDED, or
CONSISTENT / AMEND) before the reading, and the verdict answers exactly that
question — a critique of the wrong target would fail even if its content were
sound.

**Lens 2 — requirements traceability (RTM disposition).** The classic RTM
failure is marking "satisfied" on what is only a dependency. DISCHARGE vs
CONDITION is exactly satisfies vs depends-on, and laundering a condition into
a derivation is the failure mode the channel rule names. Consequence: DELTA-1
touches only the row's `evidence` field, is typed "machinery only" inside its
own text, and the probe asserts the row's `verdict`/`reason_kind` at the base
revision so any co-moved grade would be visible.

**Lens 3 — lexical semantics (same token is not same object).** A join key is
a string; composition needs the object. Consequence: the RA-A6 verification
runs on lineage receipts (source_row -> CB-A:222 -> cycle1 citation), not on
token equality — and it is exactly this lens that surfaced the unregistered
`(10bar,1,3)`-vs-`(4,1,2)` near-collision the register does not yet carry.

**Lens 4 — alarm engineering (a trigger is a baselined predicate).** An alarm
that fires on the baseline state instead of the delta event is a false-positive
generator. RA-A6's trigger watches for "a source-action-selected v_PSB"; the
object's FORMALIZATION already exists (June 2026) and records NOT selected.
Consequence: DELTA-2 writes the baseline into the predicate — the trigger
fires on a selection certificate meeting the June gate's orbit, never on
formalization, and never on the same-token different-object vector.

**Lens 5 — source criticism (evidence grade must survive composition).**
SC-A's reading is reconstruction-grade: the audio check is still owed and
SC-GRP-50 is proposed, unwritten. A composition that cites a reconstruction as
a source determination silently upgrades it. Consequence: the L4 verdict is
stated at reconstruction grade throughout, and the probe pins both caveat
sentences and the register's continued silence on SC-GRP-50.

**Lens 6 — grammatical mood, provider side (FX-1's Lens 4 mirrored).** FX-1
kept two receipts of NEED-side mood-blindness. The same defect exists on the
SUPPLY side: *"until X is built"* carries the provide verb and is a
requirement. Consequence: the rb4 leg of the LT-SM5 pair is re-typed on
exactly this ground, with the quoted line, and the probe carries a
mood-discriminator control (the rb4 line classifies as requirement; the SA-1
provider line "it builds (P_H) from the chimeric spinors" does not).
