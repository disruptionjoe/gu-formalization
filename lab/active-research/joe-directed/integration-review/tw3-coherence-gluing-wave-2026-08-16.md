# TW-3 coherence and gluing

## Result first

The last finite composition of the already-owned TW-1 and TW-2 objects is
coherent, but the coherence has one important type correction.

The base detour maps and the normal Spin lift commute because they act on
separate tensor factors. The base-supported compressed rolled symbol also
intertwines for the same reason. Those results are exact closure, not new
dynamics.

The nontrivial theorem is that the word *dual* names two different current-K77
corner maps.

```text
strict complex-bilinear B14 dual:
  ++ <-> +-
  -- <-> -+

Krein/conjugate dual used by TW2 overline(Tw):
  ++ <-> -+
  +- <-> --
```

Both are bijections of all four corners. The strict bilinear dual keeps the
base Weyl label and exchanges the normal half. The Krein/conjugate dual flips
the base Weyl label and keeps the normal half. Only the latter matches TW-2's
displayed chiral overline target. Therefore algebraic `Tw*` and
`overline(Tw)` may not be used as synonyms.

The current normal spin module owns the needed fibrewise structures. Its
Spin-invariant complex-bilinear form pairs opposite normal halves. The
correctly signed Hermitian forms

```text
h_delta(x,y) = delta i conjugate(x)^T C10 y
             = conjugate(x)^T G10 y,
J10 y = delta i y,
```

have exact signature `(8,8)` on each complex normal half. Raw duals transform
contragrediently. After the Krein/conjugate Riesz identification, the displayed
matrix copy transforms primally and the finite gluing closes.

This does not construct a positive pairing, analytic adjoint, physical
Hilbert space or state. It is indefinite fibrewise K77 kinematics.

## What each problem set established

### TW3-A — tensor intertwining

For every base map `A` among `T,Q,N3,T#`, every selected, central-sign and
opposite-orientation normal lift, every tested null/non-null covector, both
base Weyl halves and all four corners,

```text
(A tensor I)(I tensor S_J)=(I tensor S_J)(A tensor I).
```

The theorem is formal tensor-factor naturality. It also exposed the dual
fence: identity on one fixed normal half is not an equivariant map to its raw
dual because `S_J != S_J^-T`.

The null characteristic-symbol classes tensor by the internal eigenspace
multiplicities `10` and `6`. They remain symbol cohomology, not families,
particles or physical states.

### TW3-B — duality and corner custody

TW3-B constructs the exact current-real-K77 bilinear/Krein classifier. The
normal complex-bilinear Riesz map exchanges half-spin factors, while the
antilinear Krein Riesz map keeps the displayed normal-half label. A rational
noncompact Spin boost proves that raw dual action must be contragredient and
that a primal-looking matrix is licensed only after Riesz identification.

Central lift sign and orientation component remain distinct:

- `S_J -> -S_J` gives the distinct negative endomorphism and preserves its
  square and corner labels;
- `J_N -> -J_N` uses `S_J^-1`, reverses the volume-relative square sign and
  exchanges the fixed-half phase tables.

No corner or ambient half is deleted.

### TW3-C — rolled compression and descent

On TW-2's base-supported carrier, TW-1's rolled lift restricts to

```text
I_(Tw4 direct-sum S4) tensor S_J.
```

It therefore preserves `Pi4`, the base gamma trace and the first-order
compressed rolled matrix `R4`. This matrix is specifically the
Krein/conjugate Riesz-identified target from TW3-B. The strict `B14` dual is a
different contragredient target. `Pi4 != Pi14`, and first-order `R4` is still
not the cubic `N3`.

The exact vector and Spin stabilizer maps have common rank `20` and common
kernel dimension `25=u(3,2)`. Thus the endomorphism descends canonically
relative to a supplied orientation component, lifted `U(3,2)` reduction and
TW-1 exponential-path representative. Central signs on transition lifts
cancel in the quotient relation; replacing the endomorphism by `-S_J` still
gives a distinct negative endomorphism. No global reduction or section was
constructed.
