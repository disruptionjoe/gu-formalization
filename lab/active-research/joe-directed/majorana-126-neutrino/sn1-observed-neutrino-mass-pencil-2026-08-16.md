yet a seesaw hierarchy.

\[

\[

\[

The source-preferred southeast zero does not change this homogeneity. It cannot
by itself produce a parametrically light eigenvalue of order `t^2/M` while a
heavy eigenvalue remains of order `M`.

This is deliberately narrower than “the source-native seesaw is refuted.” A
seesaw remains possible if another construction supplies one of the ingredients

1. a heavy block whose scale remains nonzero while the mixing block is taken to

The result is useful because it removes a recurring shortcut: **a zero corner

\[

### Direct Dirac mass

\[
M_D(t)=\begin{pmatrix}0&tm\\tm&0\end{pmatrix}

\[

zero corner does not **derive** a parametric hierarchy from the common
