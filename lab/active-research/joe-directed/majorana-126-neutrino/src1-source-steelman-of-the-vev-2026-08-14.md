## The steelman, stated as a mechanism

   itself — the perturbation `a` in `F = F_0 + da + a^a`.
2. **The quartic comes for free** from `||a^a||^2` inside `||F||^2`.
3. **The quadratic comes from the cross term** `<F_0, a^a>` — the *unperturbed
   curvature* contracted with `a^a`.
4. **The sign of that cross term is the mass-squared parameter.** When it is
   negative the potential is a Mexican hat and the field breaks symmetry
   spontaneously. So the **background curvature plays the role of `mu^2`**, and
   the breaking scale is set by curvature rather than by a declared parameter.
5. **Fermion mass rides the same term**: "minimal coupling and Yukawa coupling
   are the same thing", so there is no separate Yukawa sector to declare.
6. **The neutrino seesaw is an operator-shape claim**, not a VEV-channel claim:
   the rolled `2x2` has a zero southeast block, and the seesaw is expected from
   that shape.

This is internally coherent and it is *cheaper* than the Standard Model: one
mechanism supplies the quartic, the quadratic, the symmetry breaking, the
fermion mass and the cosmological term, with curvature as the only dial.
