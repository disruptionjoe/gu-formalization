## 2. CARD — Item 2, Spectral flow

**The one-liner, verbatim (parent's compression):**
> "Dirac→two-Weyl is an eigenvalue degeneration; level crossings *through* the
> transition could create or destroy exactly the ±1 the counting lives on. Nobody
> has typed the flow."

**(i) Strongest reconstruction.** The one-liner's mechanism is *level crossings*.
The strong form does not need crossings at all. `VERIFICATION.md:24` records that
"the only unconditionally computable integer is 1" and that the interior net
chiral count is 0 — the whole count story lives on a **graded** quantity. A graded
quantity is conserved only along families that **preserve the grading**. So the
strong question is: *does the source's own Dirac→two-Weyl deformation preserve the
chirality grading the count is graded by?* If it does not, then the count is not
merely at risk of being moved by a crossing — **it is not a topological invariant
along the path at all**, and no crossing is required for it to move.

**(ii) What the repo actually holds (receipts).**

- `CANON.md:136`, verbatim: *"Net chiral spectral flow 0 for self-adjoint,
  chirality-odd, Krein-self-adjoint Fredholm families."* — the repository's only
  conservation law for this quantity, `CONDITIONALLY_RESOLVED`.
- Its faithful model and proof are
  `tests/function-space-ext/dirac_spectral_flow_section.py`, which states the
  mechanism explicitly: hypotheses (ii) Γ-odd and (iii) Krein-self-adjoint force
  `D = sigma_1 (x) B` with `B` Hermitian, whence every eigenvector has chirality
  expectation exactly 0.
- The source's deformation is `SC-CHI-02` (`source-claim-register.yaml:940`),
  verbatim: `dslash_A psi_L(y) = (R(y)/4) psi_R(y)` — a standard Dirac mass
  written in Weyl components, with `m = R(y)/4` (`register:1176`).
- **Nobody has typed the flow across it.** The canon row's scope is stated as
  "interior/closed, spectral-gapped"; no in-repo artifact applies it to, or
  excludes it from, the bit-2 transition.

**(iii) The dig (exact where cheap; probe LEG 3).** Setting: the repository's own
model — `Gamma = sigma_3 (x) I`, cross-chirality Krein `K = sigma_1 (x) I`,
`P` = periodic central-difference `-i d/dx`, `N = 24`, `D_0 = sigma_1 (x) P`.
`D_0` is verified in the canon class (`|{D,Gamma}| = 0`, `|D^dag K - K D| = 0`).

**(a) Exhaustive enumeration of zero-order deformations.** Over the complete
Hermitian basis `{sigma_0, sigma_1, sigma_2, sigma_3} (x) I` — this is *all* of
them, so the enumeration is closed:

| deformation `M` | `|{D,Gamma}|` | `|D^dag K - K D|` | `min|spec|` | in canon class? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sigma_0` | 1.386e+01 | 0.000e+00 | 0.0000 | no |
| `sigma_1` | 0.000e+00 | 0.000e+00 | **0.0000** | **yes** |
| `sigma_2` | 0.000e+00 | 1.386e+01 | 1.0000 | no |
| `sigma_3` (the standard Dirac mass) | 1.386e+01 | 1.386e+01 | 1.0000 | no |

**Exactly one zero-order term survives inside the canon class, and it is the one
that does not gap the operator** (`min|spec| = 0`; `sigma_1 (x) bI` merely shifts
`B`'s spectrum). Every term that actually opens a gap — i.e. every term that is a
*mass* — leaves the class, breaking Γ-oddness or Krein-self-adjointness or both.

**(b) The class cannot contain the decoupled endpoint either.** Γ-odd and Γ-even
are complementary projections on self-adjoint operators, so
canon-class ∩ {`[D,Gamma] = 0`} = {0}. Verified: the Γ-even part of the Γ-odd part
of 200 random self-adjoint operators has norm **0.000e+00** (max over 200 draws);
a generic class member `sigma_1 (x) B` has `|[D,Gamma]| = 92.31`, nowhere near
block-diagonal.

**(c) The measurement that settles the card.** Sweep the standard Dirac mass and
read the graded trace `n_-(m) = tr(Gamma P_{<0})` — the object the ±1 lives on:

| `m` | `n_-(m)` | `|{D,Gamma}|` | `min |spec|` |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | **-0.000000** | 0.000e+00 | 0.0000 |
| 0.15 | **-7.578316** | 2.078e+00 | 0.1500 |
| 0.50 | **-15.423354** | 6.928e+00 | 0.5000 |
| 1.50 | **-21.845865** | 2.078e+01 | 1.5000 |

Read the third and fourth columns together. **`min|spec| = m > 0` at every
nonzero mass: the spectrum is gapped and NOTHING crosses zero.** And yet `n_-`
travels from 0 to -21.85. The graded count moves **by 21.8 units with zero level
crossings**, and it takes non-integer values at every interior point. The
mechanism is the *third* column: `|{D,Gamma}|` grows with `m`, i.e. the
eigenvectors rotate out of the chirality grading. Nothing crosses; the ruler
tilts.

Contrast: the alternative mass `sigma_2 (x) I` keeps `n_- = 0` exactly at every
`m` (it is Γ-odd for all `m`) but has `|D^dag K - K D| = 2.078e+01` at `m=1.5` —
it buys grading-preservation by leaving the Krein class instead.

*Contrary control (planted, must be caught):* a one-sided chiral family
`((sigma_0+sigma_3)/2) (x) P` gives `n_- = +11.0000` with `|{D,Gamma}| = 6.93` and
`|D^dag K - K D| = 4.90` — nonzero flow, and it leaves the class, exactly as the
canon row's own control requires.

**(iv) Verdict: `LIVE-HIGH`.** The one-liner's closing sentence — "Nobody has
typed the flow" — is confirmed, and the reason it matters is stronger than the
one-liner's reason. The repository's only transport theorem for this quantity has

**Scope fence, stated so it cannot be over-read.** This is computed on a 1D
