### Does SC-META-53 let the observed sector inherit it?

**Not by itself.** SC-META-53 is UNCERTAIN: the source proposes maximal-compact
shielding while acknowledging that the unbounded-spectrum issue is unresolved.
The established subgroup chain is not a proof of positive physical dynamics.
Conditional reuse would need all of the following, on the same construction:

1. An actual four-dimensional observed gauge theory with the precise compact
   group/global quotient, bundle sectors and local gauge-invariant observables.
   The pure simple-factor theorem does not automatically cover coupled
   Pati–Salam products, central quotients, extra U(1)s or matter.
2. A source-compatible reduction of the native action, measure, constraints and
   state space to that compact theory. Identify the Wilson-limit dynamics or
   prove the required universality/equivalence; a subgroup inclusion is insufficient.
3. A positive physical inner product or reflection-positive Euclidean state,
   spectrum condition, causal/local observable algebra and appropriate closed
   domains. Retain the actual observation contraction and its analytic burden.
4. Control of coset, dark and fermionic couplings: a consistent invariant pure
   sector or proved decoupling, rather than discarding noncompact/Krein modes.

If independently established, these could identify an observed compact pure
sector with a theory covered by the existence result. They would still supply
no mass gap. No transfer to **Sp(32,32)** occurs, and that added restriction must
not replace the source's full U(64,64) spinor-frame posture. T02/T04/T11/T13/T15
keep real forms, observation, domains, preserved symmetry and positivity distinct.
The native existence/reduction burden remains; the new evidence only changes
what is available on the compact side of a future bridge.

not a new acceptance gate or a retroactive relabeling:
