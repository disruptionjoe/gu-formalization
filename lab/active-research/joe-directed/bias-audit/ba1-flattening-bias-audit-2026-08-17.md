# BA-1 — measuring the flattening-bias claim before anyone cites it

## 0. Verdict first, unsoftened

**The claim is refuted as stated and is retired.** On the documented
corrections of record in its own window, the caught-and-corrected substantive
errors do not "every" run toward the more damning reading of GU: under the
primary rule they split **4 toward-adverse : 4 toward-favorable : 2 ambiguous
: 2 neutral**, and no inclusion rule tried moves the directional split further
than one event from even. Three toward-favorable errors are named below with
their quotes; one of them (`S4`, the `SU(3,2)` chain promoted from
best-supported reconstruction to source determination) was made *and*
corrected inside the session the claim describes, which refutes the universal
on its own stated population. The direction half of the mechanism story
("flattening... has a direction, and the direction is against the source") is
also false in this corpus: layer collapses ran adverse, favorable, ambiguous
and neutral. What survives is narrower and stated in §6: **among the
toward-adverse substantive errors we caught, the mechanism was a layer/type
collapse five times out of five.** That is a fact about mechanism, not about
direction, and it does not license the claim.

Two guards, stated in my own words before any number can be quoted:

- **This result must not be read as "GU is fine" or "the aggregates stand."**
  A measurement of our error *directions* validates nothing about GU; and a
  near-even split on a sample of eight-to-thirteen selected events certifies
  nothing about the error population we did not catch. The adverse aggregate
  picture is exactly as trustworthy after this audit as before it — no more,
  no less.
- **This result must equally not become tone-policing.** The measured split is
  small-sample and rule-unstable in the sense quantified in §5 and §8; the
  honest disposition is to RETIRE the claim, not to install its negation as a
  standing suspicion over adverse results. **Citing the flattening-bias claim
  in either direction — as a discount on adverse verdicts, or as a certified
  absence of tilt — is forbidden. Cite the classification table instead.**

Source facts carried without regression, per the standing correction of
record: ambient Killing/Krein indefiniteness is source-attested;
observed-sector positivity is source-OPEN (not disavowed); the declared

## 1. PREFLIGHT — six problem-matched lenses, run inline, retrieval before work

**L1 — Selection-effect epidemiology (ascertainment bias).** The observable
population is *caught-and-corrected* errors; the population the claim is about
is *errors made*. The catching process here is nonrandom in a direction that
is itself contested: adversarial verification concentrates effort on
load-bearing claims, and in this repository the load-bearing claims are
predominantly adverse findings — which would over-represent toward-adverse
errors among catches even at a symmetric underlying rate. Consequences
applied: (a) every conclusion is scoped to the caught population; (b) the
catching channel is recorded per event and cross-tabulated against direction
(§7), because if different channels catch different directions, the selection
story is testable rather than waved at; (c) no extrapolation to uncaught
errors is made anywhere.

**L2 — Measurement design / operationalization.** "Direction" is
operationalized *at the moment of commission*: compare what a reader would
conclude about GU from the erroneous text versus the corrected text.
`toward-adverse` = the erroneous version licenses a stronger negative (or
weaker positive) conclusion about GU than the corrected version;
`toward-favorable` = the converse; `neutral` = no GU-reading delta (process,
bookkeeping, apparatus); `ambiguous` = the two operative uses of the erroneous
text disagree in sign, or the repairing artifact itself refuses a direction.
Each row carries its counterfactual quoted. Hindsight contamination is
mitigated by typing from the quoted delta, never from the correction's
rhetoric. Where the two signs genuinely coexist (`S1`, `S7`) the trichotomy is
not forced — the task's own instruction.

**L3 — Content analysis with inter-rater concerns.** Single coder (this
audit). No second rater exists, so reliability is bounded honestly instead:
every typing a hostile coder could plausibly flip carries a `flip` flag with
the flip argument quoted, and §5 reports the split under maximal-adverse and
maximal-favorable re-coding as a band. The band, not the point estimate, is
the citable object. This mirrors AR-3's spread discipline exactly.

**L4 — Incident-postmortem practice.** The unit is the documented correction
EVENT: one underlying error, deduplicated across its traces (a withdrawn
clause quoted in five files is one event with five traces). Corrections of
corrections are chain-links: events collapse chains; the item-expanded count
is reported separately (`R-C'`) so neither grain is hidden. Enumeration
preceded classification: the corpus was swept exhaustively from the declared
sources before any row was typed, and exclusions are listed with reasons
(§2.3) rather than silently dropped.

**L5 — Small-sample statistics.** n is 8–13 directional events depending on
rule. All counts are integers; all shares are exact fractions; the symmetric
null is tested with exact binomial tails (Fractions, no floats), reported as
tails, not as significance theater. A pre-registered reading rule: with n
under 15, no tilt short of near-unanimity could be distinguished from noise —
so the decisive test of the claim is its universal quantifier ("every"), which
a single quoted counterexample refutes. That is where the audit's weight
goes: on named, quoted counterexamples, not on the ratio.

**L6 — Decision theory of excuse-generators.** The claim, if adopted,
functions as a standing discount on adverse results; its negation, if
adopted, functions as a certificate the aggregates have not earned. Both are
cheap to reach for and expensive to dislodge, so the adoption bar must be
higher than the retirement bar. Applied: the verdict retires the claim and
forbids citation in both directions; §10 declines to propose a countermeasure
delta because the measured tilt does not clear any bar that would justify one.

**Retrieval before the work** (grep-before-claiming-novelty, whitespace-
normalized and fixed-string per AR-3's two demonstrated burial mechanisms):
the flattening-bias hypothesis appears in NO repository artifact — the string
"more damning" occurs nowhere in `lab/active-research/joe-directed/` — so the
`target_claim` quotes the commissioning brief and is marked unfiled. Prior
art on direction-of-error measurement inside this repository: LA-9 §4.4 ran
