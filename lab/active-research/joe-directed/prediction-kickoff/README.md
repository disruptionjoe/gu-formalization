# Three interfaces for near-term conditional predictions

These results turn three broad prediction ideas into exact conditional interface tests. None yet
produces a GU-derived experimental forecast. They distinguish what an existing
candidate fails, what survives, and the additional physical input needed to
make an observation discriminating.

| Interface | Result | What it can test next |
|---|---|---|
| [Mixed fermion interaction](interaction-transverse-port-2026-10-27.md) | A fixed port descending on every null quotient for unrestricted input spinors must be invisible. But the simple coupled principal equation supplies massless inputs, on which a fixed grade-six port genuinely survives. The broader exclusion cannot be used to discard that case. | Test the surviving coefficient's native cell, real pairing and observation admissibility. The external quotient remains a supplied conditional target, not an established GU physical sector. |
| [Matter motion and light](matter-light-reciprocity-2026-10-27.md) | Eliminating distortion must transform both source and detector and retain a contact term. Reciprocity alone does not fix their response ratio. A singular distortion block can still have a well-defined full coupled response. | Evaluate a fixed source and both readouts on a named coupled realization. Test the response relation itself, rather than identifying a potential or a raw rank with a prediction. |
| [Late-time distances](late-time-finite-node-identifiability-2026-10-27.md) | Under the declared standard-redshift/optics assumptions and increasing-distance branch, unrestricted smooth FLRW histories can reproduce arbitrary increasing finite distance nodes. An independently supplied expansion-variation bound yields a sharp exclusion inequality. Even the full vacuum equations of the existing local scalar horn do not provide a universal such bound from coefficients and positive effective coupling alone. | Derive a bound or test a restricted solution family with shared curvature, explicit initial-data freedom and the actual correlated observational likelihood. An early-universe ruler prediction is not required for every such test. |

## What is preserved

The source's nonchiral total theory, effective observed chirality and
representation-origin `2+1` are not replaced by three ordinary repeated chiral
families. Imposter128, internal partner144 and RS384 remain distinct.
Observation is contraction, not arbitrary projection; ambient gamma kernels
need not survive it. A supplied null radical is not automatically gauge.

The gravity response uses independent displacement from gauge-rotated
Levi-Civita and retains direct metric, density, pairing, coframe and boundary
terms. It does not replace the native coupled first action with a TT-only
second action or import a conventional positive scalar potential.

The cosmology result keeps the local scalar action distinct from the full
ambient theory. It neither selects a dark-energy sign nor restores a forced
no-phantom condition. Point-node interpolation is not a fit to a survey's
finite windows, templates or perturbation data.

These boundaries follow [the source/native routing method](../../../methods/source-native-comparator-routing.md)
and [the object distinctions](../../../../GEOMETER-VS-PHYSICS-OBJECTS.md).
The [Mannheim assessment](../mannheim-compatibility/mannheim-gu-compatibility-and-desi-2026-10-27.md)
remains a separate comparison, not the owner of these three routes.

## Both outcomes remain useful

A failed test excludes its stated realization or assumption class, not the
whole source proposal. A surviving algebraic interface earns its next
source/observation test, not automatic physical credit. An explicit
counterconstruction can show that a prediction remains underdetermined;
that is different from showing it is wrong.

The proofs and executable controls are in the linked notes. Known branching,
spin-LC ranks, scalar-slip results, distance identities and metric f(R)
equations are reused as prior art. New repository derivations make no claim
of external mathematical novelty. The
[pending evidence delta](../../../process/conditional-evidence-deltas/gu-prediction-kickoff-2026-10-27.json)
changes no ledger verdict or canon claim.
