## 1. THE CONSTRUCTION — the predicate

> ### `OWN(Z | S, 𝒲, R)`
>
> Let `𝒲 = 𝒢 ⋉ V` be the declared symmetry object with `V = Ω¹(ad P)` an
> abelian normal subgroup, let `S` be a declared functional, and let `R` be a
> declared reduction (possibly empty). A quantity `Z` is **action-owned by `S`**
> iff all five clauses hold, and the verdict **must be reported with the
> subscript produced by `O4`**.

**`O1` — PRODUCTION.** `Z` occurs as a *named summand* of the first variation
of `S` in the logarithmic trivialization,
`δS = ⟨E(w), η⟩ + dΘ(w, η)`, `η = w⁻¹δw`: that is, `Z` is `E`, `Θ`, a Noether
current of a declared invariance of `S`, or the value of one of these on
declared data.
*Fails when:* `Z` is produced by any other route — an ansatz, a chosen
contraction, a spectral or polar construction, or a declared reduction. This is
RB5's source-provenance gate restated for banked claims.

**`O2` — TRIVIALIZATION-INVARIANCE.** Since `𝒲` is a group and not an affine
space, `η = w⁻¹δw` is a choice. The *load-bearing statement* about `Z` must be
invariant under left↔right trivialization, up to the corresponding `Ad*` on
`Z`'s own index.
*Fails when:* the statement's content is a coordinate of the momentum rather
than a property of the form. Returns `SPLIT` when some sub-statements are
invariant and others are not — which is the common case and must be reported,
not rounded to PASS.

**`O3` — SPLIT-EQUIVARIANCE. This is the non-reductive clause and it has no
Yang–Mills analogue.** `𝒲`-ownership decomposes into two logically independent
obligations:
- **`O3a`, reductive leg:** `Z` transforms in a declared representation of `𝒢`.
- **`O3b`, translation leg:** `Z`'s behaviour under the abelian normal subgroup
  `V = Ω¹(ad P)` is declared, and `∂_V Z` is itself a named summand of `δS`
  restricted to `V`-directions — not an independently posited law.

Because `V` is *normal*, `O3a` implies nothing about `O3b`. A `Z` passing `O3a`
and failing `O3b` is **half-owned**. In Yang–Mills `V` is not a symmetry at all,
so `O3b` is vacuous there — which is exactly why importing the ordinary notion
of "action-owned" into GU imports nothing.

**`O4` — SUBSCRIPTED PAIRING (no invariant raising).** If asserting `S` owns `Z`
requires identifying `Lie(𝒲)*` with `Lie(𝒲)` — i.e. `Z` is a field-type object
asserted to be an Euler expression, or is a metric, positivity, norm-square or
inner-product statement — then the identification must use a pairing invariant
under the group at which ownership is claimed. The verdict is reported as
`OWNED_H` where `H` is that group.
*Fails when:* the subscript is omitted (the claim is **ill-typed**, not false),
or when `H = 𝒲` (see §2: impossible).

**`O5` — RIVAL DISCRIMINATION.** Over the declared rival parent set
`{Spin-native, two-U(32,32)-half, full-U(64,64)}`, let `p` be the number of
rivals producing `Z` under `O1`. Report:
- `p = |rivals|` → **AMBIENT**: `Z` is owned by every live parent, so the
  ownership demand is discharged **without adjudication**. This is the cheapest
  way to move an `A_OWN` row.
- `p = 0` → **NOT PRODUCED**.
- `0 < p < |rivals|` → **CONTINGENT**: ownership is entangled with `A_ID` and the
  row cannot move until the parent is picked.

`O5` is computed, never adjudicated: no rival is preferred anywhere in this
artifact.

## 2. THE OBSTRUCTION — exact, and its threshold

**Theorem (probe §S1, `[E]`).** Let `𝔤` be semisimple, `V = ℝ^d ⊗ 𝔤` with `𝔤`
acting on the second factor by `ad` and trivially on the first, and
`𝔴 = 𝔤 ⋉ V` with `V` an abelian ideal. Then:

1. every `Ad(𝔴)`-invariant symmetric bilinear form has **identically zero
   `V–V` block** — forced, because `B([v,X],u) + B(X,[v,u]) = 0` with `[v,u] = 0`
   gives `B(𝔤·V, V) = 0`, and `𝔤·V = Λ¹ ⊗ [𝔤,𝔤] = V`;
2. the invariant-form space has dimension exactly **`d + 1`**;
3. its maximum rank is exactly **`2 dim 𝔤`**;
4. hence a nondegenerate invariant form exists **iff `d ≤ 1`**.

```text
  d   dim 𝔴   #inv forms   max rank   nondegenerate?
  1       6            2          6   True
  2       9            3          6   False
  3      12            4          6   False
  4      15            5          6   False
 14      45           15          6   False
```

**The four hypotheses are separated by four controls, each violating exactly
one, and each fires:**

| control | hypothesis violated | result |
|---|---|---|
| `d = 1` | `dim V > dim 𝔤` | nondegenerate form EXISTS |
| **`iso(2,1)`** | `dim V > dim 𝔤` (named case) | nondegenerate metric EXISTS while the **Killing form is degenerate** (rank 3 of 6) |
| trivial `V`-module, `dim V = 4 > 3 = dim 𝔤` | `𝔤·V = V` | nondegenerate form EXISTS |
| abelian `𝔤` | `𝔤` semisimple | nondegenerate form EXISTS |

The `iso(2,1)` control is the important one: it is the algebra of 3d
Chern–Simons gravity, whose whole construction rests on the invariant pairing
`⟨J_a, P_b⟩ = η_ab` of a non-reductive algebra with degenerate Killing form. It
proves the obstruction is **not** a general fact about non-reductive algebras.

**GU's instantiation.** `𝔤 = 𝔰𝔬(7,7)` (or the vertical `𝔰𝔬(6,4)`), semisimple,
`dim = 91`. `V = Ω¹(ad P)`, so `d = dim Λ¹ = 4` on `X⁴` and `14` on `Y¹⁴`. Both
hypotheses hold; both are `≥ 2`. **No `𝒲`-invariant nondegenerate pairing on
`Lie(𝒲)` exists.**

**Scope, stated honestly.** `Lie(𝒲) = Ω⁰(ad P) ⋉ Ω¹(ad P)` is
infinite-dimensional. The computation is fibrewise. It reaches the
infinite-dimensional statement through one step: because `𝒢 = Γ(Ad P)` acts
*pointwise* by arbitrary functions, any `𝒢`-invariant pairing of integral type
`∫ B_x(·,·) dvol` requires `B_x` to be `ad P_x`-invariant pointwise. A pairing
of some other type is not excluded and is not claimed to be.

covector functional `c ∈ (Λ¹)*` — i.e. **a choice of a covector on the base**.
Its radical inside `V` is `ker(c) ⊗ ad`, of dimension `(d−1)·dim 𝔤`. Verified
exactly at the small fixture (minimal radical `= 9 = (4−1)·3`, `[E]`). For GU:


## 5. POSTFLIGHT — five lenses, run on this artifact

### Lens Q1 — strongest overclaim available here, and it is refused

The available inflation is: **"the ownership theorem is impossible; GU cannot
own anything."** Refused, and refused on my own numbers.

What is proved is narrow: no `Ad(𝒲)`-invariant *nondegenerate symmetric bilinear
form on `Lie(𝒲)`* exists for `d ≥ 2`. That is one specific structure. It does
**not** show that no ownership theorem exists. It shows that ownership carries a
mandatory group subscript that cannot be `𝒲`. `OWNED_K` remains fully available
and is what `CG-1`'s reduction supplies — at `AUDIT`'s external price, which is
a known, priced, *non-fatal* cost. A second escape I cannot exclude: a pairing
that is not symmetric, not bilinear-on-`Lie(𝒲)`, or not of integral type. My
theorem does not touch those and I do not claim it does.

The second available inflation is **"the 182-parent result is wrong."** Also
refused. Its arithmetic is exact and is not challenged anywhere here; §3.1
re-types it and supplies two missing qualifiers. The packet's own text already
says *"formal preboundary geometry … not a closed analytic phase space,"* which
is consistent with everything above.

### Lens Q2 — strongest contrary construction, which I cannot refute

**A reader can deny that `O3b` is a real clause.** The steelman: `Ω¹(ad P)` is
not a symmetry to be equivariant *under* — it is the space the field *moves in*.
On that reading the honest phase space really is `T*𝒢` (the translations being
configuration directions already accounted for inside `ε` and `ϖ`), the
182-parent is the whole story for the `ε` edge, and my "half-owned" is a
category error dressed as a dimension count.

I cannot refute this from the declared content, and I will not pretend
otherwise. What I can say is that the source itself puts the translations *in
the group*: *"what we're going to be doing is taking a **semi direct
product** … think about the group of gauge transformations as what the Lorentz
group always wanted to be"* (`TII` L20, `SC-GRP-05`), and the whole point of
`Δ2` is that the physical object is the **difference** of two connections,
which is a `V`-valued quantity that transforms under both legs. If the
translations were mere configuration directions there would be no `Δ1` and no
`𝒲`. But the reading is live, and if it is right then `O3b` should be deleted
and the 182-parent's grade stands as filed. **That is the single cheapest way to
kill this artifact and it should be attacked first.**

A second contrary reading, weaker but live: `𝔰𝔬(7,7)` at the fibre is not
literally `Γ(Ad P)`; the vertical group in the reduction story is `𝔰𝔬(6,4)`, and
someone could argue the relevant `d` is not `dim Λ¹`. Any `d ≥ 2` gives the same
verdict, so the theorem is insensitive to this — but the *unpaired-remainder
numbers* (273, 1183) are not, and they should be read as fixture-scoped.

### Lens Q3 — weakest seam of this construction

**The seam is `O5`, and specifically the `AMBIENT` verdict on the 182-parent.**
Unlike `O1`–`O4`, `O5`'s input for that object is a *definitional* judgement —
"the derivation uses only the `Δ2` grammar" — and it is **not computed**. I read
the packet's derivation and it appears to use only that `ε` enters through
`ε⁻¹d₀ε`; I did not re-derive the three parents' boundary terms and check. A
reader who reconstructs any one parent whose `ε`-boundary term is *not* of that
form flips `AMBIENT` to `CONTINGENT`, and then §4's already-small count drops
further. **This is the least-supported load-bearing statement in the artifact
and it is flagged rather than buried.**

Second seam: the substring classifier in §S4 inherits LA-10's evidence policy
and therefore also its weakness. Two token-set decisions are mine and are
reported rather than buried. (i) Bare `domain` and bare `pairing` were **dropped
from the `O4` token set after they fired as false positives** — `domain` matched
inside `full-domain Shiab` on `LT-GR1b` and `LT-SM3b`, and `pairing` matched
inside an evidence *filename* on `LT-GR3`. Restoring them would move three rows
into the "blocked at the datum" bucket and would be wrong. (ii) `LT-GR1`'s and
`LT-GR6`'s `O1` hits rest on `RESTRICTED_ACTION` inside a long `mapping_grade`
string and are marked `w` for exactly that reason; both rows survive as
ownership demands only through their `O4` hits, which are strong prose. A reader
who weights these differently gets a different partition of §4, though not a
different total.

Third seam, structural: **a predicate is not a theorem.** Nothing here proves
any GU quantity is owned. The artifact's product is a definition, one exact
obstruction, and a re-typing of two banked claims.

### Lens Q4 — what a hostile reader should attack next in *this* artifact

In order of expected yield:

1. **Delete `O3b`** (Lens Q2's steelman). If it goes, the headline goes and the
   182-parent's filed grade stands.
2. **Attack `O5 = AMBIENT`** by reconstructing one parent's `ε`-edge (Lens Q3).
3. **Attack the fibrewise-to-global step** in §2's scope paragraph. If a

   I did not prove it.
4. Do **not** attack the invariant-form computation itself. It has four
