## Five provenance tags

| tag | meaning | may it select a construction? |
|---|---|---|
| `ERIC_REQUIRED` | directly required by a checked Weinstein source | only after typing and construction |
| `CURT_CANDIDATE` | proposed by Curt's secondary reconstruction | no; it supplies a rival or search direction |
| `PAIRED_IMPLICATION` | mathematically required for Curt and Eric's claims to compose | yes as an obligation, not as a positive result |
| `REPO_SYNTHESIS` | concrete construction proposed by the repository | only after its stated controls pass |
| `UNSUPPORTED` | stronger source or recovery claim not currently earned | never |

The tags grade provenance. The repository's verified/reconstruction/
speculation grades continue to grade mathematical support.

## Third-lane promotion gate

Curt remains inside the Eric lane unless all three gates pass:

- `TG-1 CARRIER`: a load-bearing non-equivalence survives convention,
  complexification, and source-typing checks;
- `TG-2 DYNAMICS`: the Curt carrier supports a coherent action, variation,
  gauge law, and observation map that cannot be represented as a rival in the
  Eric lane; and
- `TG-3 DISCRIMINATOR`: the rival produces a different no-refit physical or
  mathematical discriminator on the same comparison domain.

The gates are conjunctive. A signature or real-form mismatch alone does not
create a lane.
