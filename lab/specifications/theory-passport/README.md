# Theory passport

The theory passport is GU's machine-checkable contract for turning a unifying
idea into a sequence of physical burdens. It is architecture, not evidence.
Populating a field records what would have to be owned, proved, frozen and
compared; it does not satisfy that burden.

The contract enforces this non-skippable critical path:

1. `action_causal_closure` — own the real coefficient-complete action, its
   fields and constraints, Euler/Noether structure, common causal polynomial,
   Cauchy domain and Green boundary form;
2. `physical_state_space` — construct the interacting gauge/BV/BFV complex,
   closed domain, reduced physical cohomology, conserved positive pairing and
   local-to-global descent; and
3. `observable_export` — derive a controlled reduced process connecting
   preparation, intervention, detector and record to a finite observable and
   a preregistered held-out consequence.

Later burdens cannot become ready while an earlier burden is unsatisfied. The
freeze wall is crossed before scoring, the ordinary-physics null remains live,
and Dynamic Unity export remains `not_ready` until GU can emit a qualified
payload rather than a geometric narrative.

- `theory-passport-v0.1.schema.json` — reusable Draft 2020-12 contract.
- `gu-geometry-first-v0.1.yaml` — JSON-compatible YAML instance for
  `GU-GEOMETRY-FIRST-DYNAMICAL-UNIFICATION`.
- `tests/channel-swings/geometry_first_theory_passport_probe.py` — standalone
  positive and hostile-mutation gate.
