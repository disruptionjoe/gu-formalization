# Finster causal-action selector comparator

## Purpose

Record causal fermion systems as a serious comparison class for GU's
source-action program without importing it as GU evidence or treating an
external-agent synthesis as directive.

The narrow question is:

> What does a mature variational selection program actually have to supply,
> select, and prove—and which of those burdens remain open for GU?

## Native CFS selection problem

For a supplied Hilbert space \(\mathcal H\), spin dimension \(n\), operator
space \(\mathcal F\), admissible measure class \(\mathcal A\), constraints,
and causal Lagrangian \(\mathcal L\), causal fermion systems vary a positive
measure \(\rho\) through

\[
\mathcal S(\rho)
=
\iint_{\mathcal F\times\mathcal F}
\mathcal L(x,y)\,d\rho(x)d\rho(y).
\]

The selected object is a minimizer or minimizer set

\[
\rho\in\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{\mathcal A}\mathcal S.
\]

Abstract spacetime is \(M=\operatorname{supp}\rho\).

This is a genuine selection architecture. It is not just a coordinate change
or a unitary dilation. It also makes its antecedents visible:

```text
Hilbert space
spin dimension and operator domain
constraint class
regularization / microscopic structure
matter or state sector
boundary and continuum ansatz
```

Primary sources:

- [Causal Variational Principles on Measure Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.2666);
- [The Continuum Limit of Causal Fermion Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04742);
- [Causal Fermion Systems: An Introduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06450);
- [Continuum Limit Analysis for Curved Spacetimes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30199); and
- [A Geometric Derivation of the Einstein Equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13871).

## What it teaches the GU source-action lane

### 1. An action may select without self-sourcing

CFS can select \(\rho\) while taking \(\mathcal H\), \(n\), constraints,
regularization, and sector as the definition of the problem. Likewise, a GU
action can be mathematically well formed and have critical points without
thereby deriving its own domain, physical sector, regulator, boundary
conditions, or observer interface.

This is not a defect. It is the typed boundary between:

```text
law definition
configuration selection
unique physical selection
observer-facing readout selection
```

### 2. Euler--Lagrange closure is not minimizer uniqueness

CFS existence results are scoped, and explicit nonunique examples exist.
Linearized causal-variational equations also admit many homogeneous and other
solutions; see
[Solving the Linearized Field Equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.00965).

GU should therefore preserve separate receipts for:

1. action existence and covariance;
2. admissible domain and boundary definition;
3. existence of critical points;
4. existence of minimizers;
5. uniqueness or classified degeneracy modulo gauge;
6. robustness under the allowed regulator/sector family; and
7. a held-out physical consequence.

Passing an Euler--Lagrange or finite coefficient test cannot silently discharge
the later items.

### 3. Recovery of known physics can still be conditional

The 2026 curved-spacetime continuum result obtains trace-free Einstein and
Einstein--Dirac equations inside a supplied globally hyperbolic spin-manifold,
Dirac/Hadamard-state, and regularization construction. The 2026 geometric
derivation starts from a minimizing measure with smooth support and
osculating-vacuum structure.

These are substantial results. Their strength comes partly from stating the
conditional bridge exactly. The GU analogue should likewise distinguish:

- compatibility with a supplied physical arena;
- reconstruction from program-native objects;
- unique selection by the source action; and
- parameter- or regulator-robust physical prediction.

### 4. Record/interface selection remains a separate burden

CFS local algebras, future algebras, Fock states, and effective-collapse
constructions are relevant neighbors, but they do not automatically show that
the causal action selects one observer, instrument, pointer/archive algebra,
or finality rule.

For GU, a source action or state is not yet an observer-facing measurement
postulate. The existing geometer/physics-object boundary remains controlling.

## Comparator card for future GU work

Before treating a source-action result as physical selection, answer:

| field | required answer |
|---|---|
| `action_domain` | What exact fields, measures, operators, or sections vary? |
| `supplied_structure` | Which Hilbert, bundle, metric, spin, constraint, regulator, sector, and boundary data are antecedent? |
| `selected_object` | Critical point, minimizer, orbit, measure, section, operator, or interface? |
| `existence_grade` | Is existence proved in the actual infinite/physical class or only a finite/regularized arena? |
| `degeneracy` | What remains nonunique modulo the declared gauge? |
| `regulator_robustness` | Which result survives the admitted regulator family without refitting? |
| `known_physics_bridge` | Is known physics supplied, recovered conditionally, or derived? |
| `held_out_delta` | What target not used to choose the action/domain is predicted? |
| `record_interface` | If an observer claim is made, what selects the instrument/archive/access map? |

## Barandes cross-check

Barandes supplies the opposite comparison. His revised stochastic-quantum
proof starts from \(\Gamma(t\leftarrow0)\), chooses phases/Kraus data, and
uses Stinespring dilation to build an enlarged unitary representation whose
conditioning-time set is \(\{0\}\). It is a representation/null model, not a
source-action selector or complete multi-time process.

Together:

```text
CFS: supplied variational problem -> selected configuration(s)
Barandes: supplied base-time statistics -> nonunique representation
GU burden: program-native action/domain -> physically selected object
           -> held-out physical consequence
```

## Verdict

`USEFUL_SELECTOR_COMPARATOR / NO GU STATUS CHANGE`.

CFS demonstrates what a serious action-based selection program can look like
and how much conditional structure it may still require. It neither supplies
GU's missing physical source operator nor solves GU's source-action,
chirality, generation, record, or prediction burdens.
