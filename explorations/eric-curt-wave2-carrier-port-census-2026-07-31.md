## Exact portable core

If two connections share the same affine gauge law,

\[
A^g=gAg^{-1}-dg\,g^{-1},\qquad
B^g=gBg^{-1}-dg\,g^{-1},
\]

then

\[
T^g=A^g-B^g=gTg^{-1}.
\]

The derivative term cancels exactly. This is why the IG/cocycle/distortion
grammar survives even when its representations differ.

Trace reversal also survives an overall base-sign convention:

\[
\tau_{-h}(k)
=k-\frac12\operatorname{tr}_{-h}(k)(-h)
=\tau_h(k).
\]

But the placement of `tau_h` in the vertical bilinear and adjoint changes when
the vertical block is sign-reversed. A shared involution is not a shared
field-space metric.

For fourteen-dimensional Hodge stars,

\[
\star^2|_{\Lambda^k}=(-1)^{k(14-k)+q}.
\]

The parity is the same for `q=5` and `q=7`, since they differ by two. That does
not identify the Hodge operators: their metrics, real Clifford modules, and
adjoints remain different.

Finally, if `H_1,H_2` are self-adjoint for any nondegenerate branch metric
`G`, the exact algebraic implication

\[
Q=[H_1,H_2],\qquad Q^{\dagger_G}=-Q
\]

holds. This transfers RB6's **commutator grammar**, not its evaluated W177
operators, nonzero value, polar branch, or physical interpretation.
