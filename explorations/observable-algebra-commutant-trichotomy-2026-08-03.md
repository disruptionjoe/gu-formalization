## 2. Method: two independent certificates, no blind 1664^2 solve

**(i) Spectral-graph certificate on `V` (the actual record-sector computation).**
A generic Hermitian element `H1 = sum_i c_i K_i + h.c.` (complex Gaussian `c_i`)
lies in the complexified algebra because the generator family is adjoint-closed
(verified). `H1` has simple spectrum (min gap `1.3e-3` against spread `80.1`),
so any commutant element is diagonal in its eigenbasis; each further generator
`K` forces `d_i = d_j` wherever `(W^dag K W)_{ij}` is genuinely nonzero. The
number of connected components of that graph equals `dim_C` of the commutant.
Computed: **1 component**, stable across edge thresholds `1e-4 / 1e-6 / 1e-8`
(relative, per generator) and replicated by an independent seed on an
independently drawn 22-generator subfamily (also 1, min gap `9.2e-4`). Because
the component count is identical at the very conservative `1e-4` threshold
(strong edges only), the conclusion does not depend on any near-threshold
matrix entry. Since commutants only shrink when generators are added,
`dim_C = 1` for this sub-list certifies `dim_C = 1` for the full generating
family stated in the task. The antilinear leg: every generator is H-linear
(`J`-commuting, defects `< 1e-11`) and `J` preserves `V`, so the real commutant
is `C·I + C·I·J = span_R{I, iI, J, iJ}` with `J^2 = -1`: `H·I`, `dim_R = 4`.

**(ii) Ambient tensor-shortcut cross-check.** Anything commuting with the full
`I (x) Cl(9,5) = I (x) M(64,H)` part is of the form `Y (x) (H-linear scalar)`:
the spinor-factor commutant of `Cl(9,5)` on `C^128` is certified `C·I` (same
graph method at the 128 level, 1 component at all thresholds), and over `R` the
H-linear scalars are `span{1, i, J, iJ}`. Imposing the frame part kills
`Y`: the nullspace of `Y -> [Y, so(9,5)]` on 14x14 matrices, computed over all
91 frame generators, is exactly 1-dimensional (`Y = cI`). Imposing the
projector and `M_D` is then automatic: both are H-linear (defects `3.2e-12`,
`8.8e-12`). Ambient commutant = `H·I`, matching the record-sector result.

The two certificates are logically independent: (ii) alone would not settle the
compressed (record-sector) algebra, because compression is not an algebra
homomorphism; (i) settles it directly.

## 3. The trichotomy read-out (computed, not assumed)

**Classification leg (Theorem 2 machinery): residual family dimension 0.**
`dim_C(commutant) = 1` ⇒ `V` is irreducible over `H` under `A` ⇒ trivial
multiplicity structure ⇒ `sum_lambda dim_R(D_lambda) a_lambda b_lambda = 0`.
The only complex-linear involutions commuting with `A` are `+/-I`; the
antilinear commutant elements `cJ` all square to `-|c|^2 <= 0`, so allowing

**Existence leg (Proposition 1): EMPTY on this kinematic carrier for the
constructed pair.** Admissibility demands `eta_V(., C.) > 0` with `C` in the
commutant; the candidates are `+/-I`, and `eta_V` has computed signature

what Proposition 1's necessity leg predicts: the probe verifies that the
diagonal boost `D = M_{09} (x) I + I (x) sigma_{09}` (a) preserves `V`, (b) is
`eta_V`-skew (its one-parameter subgroup lies in `O(eta_V)`), (c) is literally

rotation contrast direction shows rate `3e-17`, bounded). Any invariance

**What the finite computation does and does not establish on the compactness
question.** Deciding `F = ∅` versus singleton in general is decided by the
relative compactness of the closure of the generated group — and that closure
property is NOT a finite matrix check: no finite list of matrix identities

**Branch landed: NON-COMPACT / EMPTY on the kinematic carrier, with
