evidentiary credit only if its result is uniform across the S3 family or a

charged as model selection. The S9 20-observer-slot centrality/domain
return/P2 identification and enlarged-BV compatibility, plus P3, remain held
out unless an earlier interface explicitly imposes them. If a genuinely
unselected consequence then lands, its information comes from constraint
