## Plain English

We first verified that there are many mathematically valid ways to extend the
Einstein-like contraction from ordinary Riemann curvature to the much larger
GU curvature space. So the problem is not that no extension can be written.

The problem is that the same extension cannot play two roles at once on the
full field space:

1. reproduce the nonzero fourteen-dimensional Einstein contraction on true
   Riemann curvature; and
2. be exactly the equation obtained by varying the cubic source term in which
   that contraction is inserted.

The counterexample uses fields for which one ordering produces genuine
constant-curvature Riemann data, but the cyclically exchanged ordering is
exactly zero. A derivative of one cubic action must treat those orderings
cyclically. The proposed endpoint does not: its direct derivative is only one
third of the claimed value. This happens independently in the low and high
Clifford copies, so tuning their mixture cannot fix it.

This tells us what to build next. The missing ingredient is not another
zero-order contraction formula. It must be a moving/derivative contribution,
an auxiliary parent field, the actual symmetrized Euler equation, or a
geometrically derived restriction of the field domain.
