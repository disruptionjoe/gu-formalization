K976--K977 show that four jointly declared parent assumptions cannot realize
the exact positive-rate dephasing semigroup on an interval containing zero: a
fixed product assignment for every input, a bounded time-independent
self-adjoint Hamiltonian, a fixed normalized environment state, and
differentiability of the reduced unitary dynamics at zero.

Therefore any exact microscopic parent must fail at least one of those
assumptions. Candidate ways to leave the class include an unbounded or singular
generator/domain, fresh resets or time-dependent driving, an assignment
outside the fixed-product theorem, or a non-Hamiltonian/nondifferentiable
limit. This list is explanatory, not an exhaustive classification theorem.

K978 supplies one explicit contrary route: fresh collisions converge uniformly
on every fixed finite horizon, while importing ancilla supply and a diverging
coupling rate. Neither the theorem nor that construction selects which horn a
GU-owned action should take.
