## 2. Universal monotone-path estimate

A nonzero j-th term visits distinct increasing indices
0<=k0<...<kj<=d-1. Their weights satisfy

    sum_(ell=0..j)(kell+1) <= (j+1)(2d-j)/2 <= d(d+1)/2.

The largest sum is attained by the last j+1 indices. Normalize each diagonal
block by C^[-(k+1)]. Every normalized block has norm at most d. Expand block
paths, take absolute coefficient matrices, and use the induced l1 norm:
the sum of normalized contributions is at most d^(j+1)(B d^2)^j. This
already sums branches; do not multiply again by the number of paths. Restore
the maximum C-weight to get

    ||R_d|| <= sum_(j=0..d-1) B^j d^(3j+1)
                             C^[(j+1)(2d-j)/2].          (1)

There are d summands, each bounded by
b^(d-1)d^(3d-2)C^[d(d+1)/2], proving the result. No cancellation or
commutation of different diagonal inverses was assumed.
