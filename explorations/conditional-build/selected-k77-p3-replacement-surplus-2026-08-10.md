# Selected K77 P3 replacement: globality and constraint surplus

## Result in plain English

The two replacements opened by v0.148 are not equally economical.

The **tangential same-bundle route** is the only zero-continuous-cost route:
identify the proposed selector connection with the actual positive-chiral
spin/source connection on the observer base. This is globally meaningful as a
rank-two complex `SL(2,C)` bundle, and its `c2` is integral. But its integer is
then the characteristic number of the actual observation background; it is not
universally `+1`. A round spin `S4` supplies the planted `+1` control while a
flat spin `T4` supplies `0`. Moreover, on a Lorentzian four-plane the Hodge
operator satisfies `*^2=-1`: the chiral three-planes are complex `+/- i`
eigenspaces, not real compact `SU(2)+/-` eigenspaces. Thus the topological
bundle survives, but the compact BPST/self-dual connection and a real selected
action pairing do not follow automatically.

The **horizontal-normal soldering route** has the right signature only after
it is given extra structure. Exact Lorentz representation theory gives

```text
Hom_SO(1,3)(H, Sym^2 H*) = 0.
```

So there is no natural zero-order map from the observer tangent four-plane
into the ten-dimensional normal metric fibre. A general signature-compatible
normal four-plane costs 24 pointwise functions after quotienting the source
frame. The smallest explicit repair uses a unit timelike vector `u` and
`j_u(v)=u_flat sym v_flat`; its induced DeWitt metric has inertia `(1,3)`, but
choosing `u` costs three pointwise functions. P1 can orient an already-existing
line; it cannot manufacture that timelike line.

Therefore the current ranking is decisive but conditional:

1. prefer the tangential same-bundle route because it adds no independent
   connection or continuous selector;
2. test it on an admissible observation background, including its actual
   `c2`, Lorentzian reality and action pairing;
3. keep H-N soldering dormant unless the source action itself derives the
   reduction or timelike field with more independent equations than new
   functions;
4. do not restrict the action yet.

This does not assign P3. It says where a P3-like selector could live at lowest
cost and what must be true before the conditional build may use it.

## Exact calculations

### No natural zero-order H-N map

The executable probe constructs the six infinitesimal Lorentz generators on
`H` and their induced action on `Sym^2 H*`. Solving all intertwiner equations
for a `10 x 4` matrix returns dimension zero. The planted invariant metric line
in `Sym^2 H*` survives, proving the solver is not simply rejecting every
invariant.

For a general isometric framed injection of `(1,3)` into `(6,4)`, the family is

```text
O(6,4) / O(5,1),   dimension 45 - 15 = 30.
```

Modding out by the six-dimensional source `O(1,3)` frame leaves 24 pointwise
subspace functions.

### Minimal one-vector repair

At trace-reversal coefficient `1/2`, the DeWitt pairing on
`j_u(H) subset Sym^2 H*` has exact inertia

```text
u timelike:  (1,3,0)
u spacelike: (3,1,0)
u null:      (0,0,4).
```

This shows that a timelike `u` can produce the desired Lorentz carrier. It also
isolates the missing selection: a unit timelike field is a three-function
choice. The statement is not a no-go against an action-owned `u`; it is a
constraint-surplus fence against calling an unowned `u` free.

### Tangential topology and real form

The same-bundle identification costs zero new continuous coordinates, but
`c2(S_X^+)` is background-dependent. The exact controls `S4 -> 1` and `T4 -> 0`
refute a universal `+1` claim. In Lorentz signature the exact Hodge matrices
give `*^2=-1`, zero real `+1` eigenspace and complex `+i` eigenspace dimension
three. Hence the complex chiral bundle and its integral `c2` remain available,
while the compact real self-dual connection and the reality of its action
coupling require a separate analytic construction.
