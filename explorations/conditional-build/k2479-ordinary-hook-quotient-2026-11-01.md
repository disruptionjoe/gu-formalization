## 1. An original degree12 compatibility row

Write t_ij=h_iij for i!=j, with h_iji=-t_ij. The tracefree condition is
sum_i eta_i t_ij=0. In the original raw symmetric convention,

    F_ij=eta_i xi_i t_ij+eta_j xi_j t_ji, i<j,
    F_ii=-2 sum_j eta_j xi_j t_ij.

For full-support xi, set c_ii=0 and c_ij=1/(xi_i xi_j). Directly,

    sum_(i<j) c_ij F_ij
      =sum_j (1/xi_j) sum_i eta_i t_ij =0.

Clearing the product of all fourteen covector coordinates gives a polynomial
row with no division:

    C_ii(xi)=0, C_ij(xi)=product_(l != i,j) xi_l.          (1)

Thus C A_xi h=0 for every tracefree repeated hook, including the original161
polar generators. Since their coefficient functions are unrestricted before
imposing the additional boundary, every repeated-only field V satisfies

    C(D) Q=sum_(i<j) [product_(l != i,j) partial_l]Q_ij=0,
    Q=D V.                                              (2)

The derivatives and coordinates in(1)--(2) are original ambient coordinates,
not the adapted y/z coordinates. Equation(2) remains necessary when each
coefficient is in I_E. It is not a compatibility condition on the full
linear-generator field family.

At xi=(1,...,1), C is zero on the diagonal and one off diagonal. It is
independent of tau and b_full: the diagonal of alpha*tau+beta*b_full is
alpha*eta_i+beta, and both signs occur. Hence alpha=beta=0. The actual
original-bank modular minor gives rank102 at this covector, and also at
xi=(1,2,...,14). The three independent exact rational rows supply the upper
bound, so these are exact ranks, not only modular guesses. A nonzero minor
and the universal rows establish generic rank102. The reused transverse
rank88 and e0 rank25 are separately reproduced; none implies a norm bound.

For the larger full tracefree repeated carrier there is a useful all-support
real statement. Put r_ij=eta_i t_ij/xi_j and lambda_i=eta_i xi_i^2. The
symbol kernel is exactly

    r^T=-r, r*1=0, r*lambda=0.

For a real full-support covector, lambda and1 are independent because the
metric has both signs. Skew forms annihilating their span have dimension
binom(12,2)=66. The tracefree repeated carrier has dimension182-14=168,
so its rank is168-66=102. This does not prove the original seven-polar

## 4. What is closed and what is next
