# VEV amplitude-selector route triage

## Why preserve this

Ledger v0.142 establishes an exact one-amplitude local source-Euler family and
exhausts the named **local classical** selectors already built. This note does
not add a result. It preserves the most useful mathematical route ordering so
future work does not fall back into another local symmetry, finite-Hessian or
classical-BFV test that is already known to be amplitude-blind.

The conditional build still fits at its present grade if one admissible route
selects the amplitude without introducing equivalent unexplained freedom and
without breaking the other GU/physics legs.

## Ten specialist lenses

| lens | best question | expected discriminatory power |
|---|---|---|
| variational bicomplex | Does variation of a genuinely global normalized observer/source functional add a third independent Euler condition? | highest classical priority |
| global/Fredholm analysis | Does solvability of the global Euler problem impose a compatibility condition on the amplitude? | potentially decisive without a fitted parameter |
| Krein operator theory | Is a common closed, stable or self-adjoint domain admissible only at selected amplitudes? | strong after the operator and domain are typed |
| symplectic/BV--BFV geometry | Does the coupled global bulk--boundary reduction or its boundary charge distinguish amplitudes? | necessary composition; local classical BFV is already blind |
| principal-bundle/holonomy geometry | Does the local family descend globally for every amplitude, or only for a restricted subset? | cheap early global obstruction test |
| geometric invariant theory | Does an action-owned global moment map or stability condition intersect the family discretely? | live only if the functional is derived, not invented |
| index/K-theory | Does spectral flow, eta or an index wall change along the family? | good for sectors/signs; weak for a continuous magnitude by itself |
| microlocal/hyperbolic PDE | Do constraint propagation and admissible boundary data survive for every amplitude? | strong physical filter, not automatically a scale derivation |
| Picard--Lefschetz/path integral | Does the correctly defined effective action, determinant or thimble isolate an amplitude? | best quantum selector; premature before domain construction |
| RG/dimensional transmutation | Can running generate a preferred scale with improved vacuum-shift response? | capable of selecting scale but expensive and vulnerable to relocated tuning |

An explicitly typed external normalizer remains a separate live construction
route. It is informative when its free choices are preregistered and the
independent constraints it satisfies give positive constraint surplus. It is
not downgraded merely because it is supplied rather than derived.

## Efficient ordering

1. Construct the global normalized observer/source functional and name its
   variational owner.
2. On the whole one-amplitude family, test global descent/holonomy and the
   Fredholm compatibility condition before doing quantum work.
3. Construct the common Green/Krein and coupled BV--BFV domain; check whether
   admissibility or boundary charge supplies an independent condition.
4. If the classical/global gates remain blind, compute the effective action or
   spectral determinant on that established domain.
5. In parallel or as the explicit conditional horn, type an external
   normalizer, count its freedom and constraint surplus, and test preservation
   of all other mapped physics rows.
6. Only after a selector survives derive observation-slice Hilbert stress,
   vacuum-shift susceptibility, FLRW perturbations and held-out cosmology.

## Stopping and promotion rules

- Do not repeat a local symmetry, pointwise Hessian, classical symplectic or
  classical BFV selector unless it introduces a genuinely new action-owned
  equation.
- A global compatibility condition selects the amplitude only if it reduces
  the family rank rather than moving the free parameter into boundary data,
  normalization, scheme or contour choice.
- Index and topology can select a component without selecting its continuous
  scale; report those outcomes separately.
- A quantum minimum is not sufficient until contour, measure, determinant and
  renormalization choices are counted.
- An external datum is a successful conditional fit only when its type is
  explicit, it preserves the other legs, and its constraint surplus is
  positive.

No ledger migration is warranted by this planning synthesis. The current
v0.142 Build frontier already names the correct first step.
