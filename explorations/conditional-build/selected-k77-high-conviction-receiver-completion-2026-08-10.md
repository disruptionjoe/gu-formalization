   requires 128 additional independent barred/left field directions. The
   source's current four-field graph does not own that extension. It is a
   precise construction target, not a quotient and not a zero-cost repair.
3. **Homological/BV gauge-image route.** The natural spinor gauge symbol has
   rank 128 modulo the original receiver. At timelike and spacelike covectors

   (`rank sigma(D)d=128`). At the null covector it is a genuine symbol complex,
