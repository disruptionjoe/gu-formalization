# A finite atom defeats the fluid candidate beyond an explicit curve

## Result and scope

Let H_s be the positive scalar flow generated by

    q(v)=2sqrt(1+2v)-2-v for 0<v<=3/2; q(v)=1/2 for v>=3/2.

For fixed 0<S<=1/2 define

    v_c=sqrt(3)/4, theta_c(S)=H_S(v_c),
    lambda_loc(S)=1/theta_c(S).                                    (1)

Whenever lambda>lambda_loc(S), the feasible pure-fluid limit lambda H_-S(1/lambda) is STRICTLY below the original stopping supremum. An initial finite atom followed by a sufficiently fine finite tail proves it. At S=1/2 the necessary boundary is approximately1.66781386861025267; at S=1/4 it is approximately1.95280689462448788. Rigorous rational brackets are in the report.

Equality is deliberately different: at u=v_c, all sufficiently small optimized initial atoms LOSE at cubic order. This is local stability only; neither equality nor any smaller multiplier is thereby globally optimal. K2612's sufficient region, approximately lambda<=0.86043755 at half score, remains the certified lower side. The gap between sufficient and necessary conditions remains open.

## 1. Expand the original finite action, including its optimizer

Reuse the original leakage

    w(a,b)=log[1+(b-a)²/(2(a+b)(2-a-b))],
    F_b(u)=min_(0<=a<=b) [(1+a)u+2w(a,b)].

On a compact positive input interval near v_c, put r=a/b and write

    alpha=(1-r)²/[4(1+r)], beta=(1+r)/2,
    2w(rb,b)=b g(r)+b² B(r)+b³ T(r)+O(b4),
    g=2alpha, B=2alpha beta-alpha²,
    T=2alpha beta²-2alpha² beta+2alpha³/3.                         (2)

The original log argument is alpha b/(1-beta b); denominators stay away from zero, so these are analytic uniform remainders with their needed derivatives. The unique leading optimizer is

    r(u)=2/sqrt(1+2u)-1, g''=4/(1+r)³>0.

It stays strictly inside(0,1) near v_c. The implicit function theorem gives

    a_b(u)/b=r(u)+b rho(r(u))+O(b²),
    rho=-B'/g''=-(r-1)(3r³+11r²+17r+1)/32.                       (3)

The derivative here is with respect to r. Strict original convexity and interior margins identify this branch with the actual constrained minimizer for small b. Substitution yields

    F_b(u)=u+b q(u)+b² B(r)+b³[T(r)-B'(r)²/(2g''(r))]+O(b4).

Subtract the true flow Taylor expansion

    H_b(u)=u+b q+b² q'q/2+b³[q''q²+(q')²q]/6+O(b4).

Thus

    F_b(u)-H_b(u)=D(u)b²+E(u)b³+O(b4),
    D=B-qr/2=q(3-6r-r²)/8,
    E=T-B'²/(2g'')-[q''q²+r²q]/6.                              (4)

The action correction is essential at cubic order. A frozen ratio gives a strictly larger cubic coefficient and cannot determine the optimized boundary.

## 2. Fixed-score exclusion by an actual initial atom

For any positive theta=1/lambda, the backward trajectory u=H_-S(theta) exists and stays positive: q(v) is positive for v>0 and behaves as v near zero. For lambda>lambda_loc(S), u<v_c. The analytic expansion and optimizer argument of section1 also apply on a small compact neighborhood of each such fixed positive u, since its leading optimizer remains strictly interior and all denominators are nonzero. Its r(u) lies above r_c=2sqrt(3)-3, so D(u)<0. The leading minimizer is interior for each such fixed u, and (4) proves F_b(u)<H_b(u) for sufficiently small b<S.

Choose that b and its actual minimizing action a_b(u). Remaining score is S-b and its fluid continuation is lambda H_b(u), since H_(S-b)(H_b(u))=theta. The repeated first stage has original survival1/(1+a) and cost2w/(1+a). Therefore its atom-plus-fluid-tail improvement over lambda u is exactly

    lambda [H_b(u)-F_b(u)]/(1+a_b(u)) >0.                       (5)

This keeps the original prefix survival. It is not an unweighted cost comparison.

The tail limit can be replaced by a FINITE policy. On the compact positive trajectory, use equal score steps h and actions h r(v(s)) in the original recurrence. Consistency gives local O(h²) error, and bounded derivatives plus discrete Gronwall give total O(h). This argument proves convergence to the fluid candidate even outside any globally optimal fluid region; it invokes no unproved Bellman upper bound there. For a fixed positive gap in(5), choose the tail error smaller than half that gap. The resulting finite policy strictly beats the candidate. Abort, infinite-time hitting, and a fictional continuum policy are not needed for the witness.

Hence fluid optimality at fixed S NECESSARILY requires lambda<=lambda_loc(S). It is not sufficient. The curve decreases strictly with S because

    lambda_loc'(S)=-q(theta_c)/theta_c²<0,
    lim_(S->0) lambda_loc(S)=4/sqrt(3).                          (6)

This recovers the old small-score boundary as a limit, rather than claiming it anew.

## 3. Critical equality, cubic opening and local neutral curve

Exact algebra at r_c gives

    D(v_c)=0,
    A=D'(v_c)=(47sqrt(3)-81)/2>0,
    E_c=E(v_c)=(10309-5949sqrt(3))/192>0.                        (7)

The signs can be checked without decimals:3*47²-81²=66 and10309²-3*5949²=103678. Thus at u=v_c the residual is positive E_c b³+O(b4), and sufficiently small optimized atoms lose.

Let delta=v_c-u>0. Uniform analyticity gives D(u)=-A delta+O(delta²), E(u)=E_c+O(delta). Choose

    b=t_* delta, t_*=2A/(3E_c).

For fixed S and small delta, b<S and the original atom gain(5) is

    4lambda A³/(27E_c²) delta³+O(delta4).                       (8)

The survival denominator is1+O(delta), so it affects only the displayed remainder. Choosing a finite tail with h=O(delta4) preserves(8) as a realizable lower-bound construction; its number of tail stages is finite for every delta>0. Alternatively any tail error o(delta³) preserves the leading coefficient. This is NOT an asymptotic equality for the globally optimal value minus fluid: some other policy may already win by more.

For fixed small b, divide (4) by b² and extend analytically through b=0. Its u derivative at(v_c,0) is A>0. The implicit function theorem supplies a unique local atom-neutral input

    u_b=v_c-(E_c/A)b+O(b²).                                    (9)

Near this curve the atom wins below u_b and loses above it. This decides the local finite-atom choice, not the globally best atom or whole phase.

At fixed S, differentiation of H_-S(theta) gives

    delta=[q(v_c)/(lambda_loc² q(theta_c))]
                       (lambda-lambda_loc)+O((lambda-lambda_loc)²).

Substitute into(8) for an explicit cubic lower witness in multiplier distance. The exact neutral equation and this first derivative have different roles from a claim that the global frontier begins there.
