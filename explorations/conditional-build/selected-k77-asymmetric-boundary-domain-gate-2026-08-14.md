## Exact proof outline

For each canonical conormal `n`, restrict

```text
G_n = P_action sigma_D(n)
```

to exact Gaussian-rational bases of W and M. Both action horns give the same
rank pattern. On a base conormal, the diagonal restrictions vanish and both
cross restrictions have rank 192, so W and M are complementary maximal
isotropic inside their rank-384 sum. On a normal conormal, the diagonal
restrictions have rank 192 and the cross restrictions vanish, so W and M fail
isotropy.

For gauge stability, use the total one-form Spin action. Every one of the 51
split generators commutes with the W projector, while every one of the 40
mixed generators has a nonzero commutator. Reconstructing the selected
ordinary-gauge map on the exact 196-cell coefficient bank reproduces rank 25,
with split/mixed ranks 17 and 8. Its eight active mixed generator columns are
therefore explicit obstructions to preserving either half.

## Hostile boundary

The strongest overclaim is “no asymmetric physical boundary can exist.” The
result is narrower. It classifies the two smallest current W/mirror relations
under the current action Green symbols and ordinary-gauge image. A new
action-owned split-stabilizer mechanism, a nontrivial boundary moment map with
BFV edge fields, a different source-derived boundary carrier or a broken
vacuum could define another problem.

The strongest positive control is the base-conormal result itself: W and
mirror really are exact maximal-isotropic action-Green candidates, so the
calculation is not a blanket rejection of boundary asymmetry. The strongest
negative control is a normal conormal, where the same halves are provably
non-isotropic.

The weakest seam is analytic realization. Even after gauge closure, one must
construct an actual codimension-one boundary or interface, a variable-
coefficient global domain, closedness and the relevant well-posedness or
self-adjointness estimate.
