## K1351: no full-group U(1) character

Write the split metric as

```text
eta = diag(+1,...,+1,-1,...,-1),   seven signs of each kind.
```

The standard matrix basis of `so(7,7)` has forty-two same-sign rotations and
forty-nine opposite-sign boosts. Every basis generator is an explicit
commutator. With `i,j` of positive sign and `a,b` of negative sign,

```text
B_ia = E_ia+E_ai,
R_ij = E_ij-E_ji,
R_ab = E_ab-E_ba,

[B_ia,B_ja] = R_ij,
[B_ia,B_ib] = R_ab,
[R_ij,B_ja] = B_ia.
```

Hence `[g,g]=g`. If `chi:G->U(1)` is a continuous character, its differential
maps `g` to the abelian algebra `iR` and therefore annihilates `[g,g]`. It is
zero, and connectedness forces `chi` to be the identity character. Thus the
full connected split group has no direct nontrivial U(1) abelianization.

This does not exclude a U(1) subgroup in a separately selected stabilizer or
maximal compact subgroup. A subgroup inclusion is not a full-group character,
and identifying it with physical hypercharge still requires source-owned
reduction and representation data.

## K1352: the uniform phase is not an internal charge sector

Let `pi` be the nontrivial irreducible unitary principal series on `H_ps`.
Suppose an internal one-parameter subgroup generated by `X` acted by one
uniform phase on the entire Hilbert space:

```text
pi(exp(tX)) = exp(i q t) I.
```

Then `d pi(X)=i q I`, so for every `Y`,

```text
d pi([X,Y]) = [d pi(X),d pi(Y)] = 0.
```

The kernel of a nontrivial representation of the simple Lie algebra `g` is a
proper ideal, hence zero. Therefore `[X,Y]=0` for all `Y`; the center of `g` is
zero, so `X=0` and `q=0`. No nontrivial internal one-parameter subgroup acts
as K1346's common scalar phase.

The same argument fences a fixed nonzero charge sector. If a nonzero closed
`q`-eigenspace for an internal U(1) were invariant under all of `G`,
irreducibility would make it all of `H_ps`, returning the impossible uniform-
phase case. A nonzero fixed-charge sector can instead be preserved by the
subgroup centralizer or normalizer. That is a genuine symmetry reduction, not
full-G equivariance.

## K1353: no pointwise finite-fibre linear bridge

Let `V` be any finite-dimensional pointwise source fibre, including a finite
direct sum of released `epsilon`, `varpi`, `nu`, or `zeta` fibres. For a
continuous linear equivariant map

```text
T: V -> H_ps,
```

the image is finite-dimensional, closed, and `G`-invariant. Irreducibility of
`H_ps` makes that image either zero or all of `H_ps`; dimension excludes the
second case. Therefore `T=0`.

The quantifiers are load-bearing. This is a pointwise finite-fibre theorem.
It does not cover infinite-dimensional spaces of source sections,
differential or integral transforms, distributional intertwiners, nonlinear
maps, or maps formed after explicit symmetry reduction. Equal dimensions of a
selected finite subspace, a common group label, or a matching field name does
not supply such a bridge.

## K1354: full split gauging has no positive invariant quadratic kinetic form

The Cartan decomposition is

```text
so(7,7) = k + p,
k = so(7)+so(7),   dim k = 21+21 = 42,
dim p = 7*7 = 49.
```

Up to a positive scalar, the Killing form is the matrix trace form. On the
explicit generators,

```text
Tr(R_ij^2) = -2,    Tr(B_ia^2) = +2.
```

Its signature is therefore `(49,42)`, up to overall reversal. Since the split
real algebra is simple, every invariant symmetric bilinear form is a scalar
multiple of the Killing form. Every nonzero choice is indefinite and the zero
choice is degenerate. A conventional quadratic gauge kinetic term on the full
split algebra cannot be both positive definite and invariant under the full
local adjoint action.

There are exact positive controls. `-B` restricted to the maximal compact
algebra is positive, and a Cartan involution gives the positive majorant
`B_theta(X,Y)=-B(X,theta Y)`. But selecting `k` or `theta` is extra structure.
It must be source/action owned, compatible with the local gauge symmetry, and
tied to the observed-state map. This is the SC-META-53 boundary rather than
its resolution.

## Postflight hostile review

The strongest overclaim is that no U(1) can occur in GU. False: the theorem
excludes a full-group character and a uniform scalar action on the entire
irreducible principal series, not a U(1) inside a selected stabilizer or its
nonuniform weight spaces. The strongest representation objection is that
source fields are spaces of sections, not only pointwise fibres. K1353 is
explicitly pointwise and leaves section-space transforms open.

The strongest positivity objection is that a Cartan majorant is already
positive. It is, after choosing a Cartan involution; the theorem says that
full split Ad invariance alone does not make that choice physical or locally
gauge invariant. The strongest source objection also survives: the source's
maximal-compact shielding language does not construct the reduction, positive
quotient, charge sector, or observation map.

The useful result is therefore narrow and durable. K1346's U(1) cannot acquire
source ownership by the most direct symmetry relabelings, and full split
gauging cannot inherit its positive quadratic energy without additional
reduction data.
