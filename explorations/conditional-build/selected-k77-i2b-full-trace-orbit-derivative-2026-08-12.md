For the unit negative trace vector `q`, the exact matrix classifier gives

```text

dimensions 91 = 78 + 13.
```

All 78 stabilizer generators commute with `P_+` on the complete 392-real
carrier.  Twelve of them nevertheless rotate the chosen residual target.
This is not a contradiction: the moved target remains exactly `P_+`-fixed in
every case.  The stabilizer preserves the selected class, not each vector
inside it pointwise.

For every orbit generator `L_i`, exact arithmetic verifies

```text
dot P_i P + P dot P_i = dot P_i,
P dot P_i P = 0,
(1-P) dot P_i (1-P) = 0.
```

Every `dot P_i` has rank 56, is self-adjoint for the selected real action
pairing, and comes from an action-skew generator.  The joint column space of
the 13 maps has rank 392.  This rank describes coverage of the target by a
family of derivatives; it is not a count of physical modes.
