## Result and scope

and target mass of the original verifier, even with retained quantum memory.
Its exact condition is preservation of both test effects on the joint carrier.
Preserving the target alone, covariance, and isolated reset-memory calibration

## 1. Exact full-memory condition and converse

Let Q,T be the original rank10/rank12 projectors on H=C^108, and P their
rank-one intersection. Let E be arbitrary finite-dimensional retained storage
memory, initially allowed to be correlated with H and an untouched reference.
For each reached gap and record history h, supply a CPTP map Phi_h on H E,
with Kraus operators K_h,alpha. Require

    [K_h,alpha,Q tensor I_E]=[K_h,alpha,T tensor I_E]=0.       (1)

Equivalently, Phi_h^*(Q tensor I_E)=Q tensor I_E and the analogous equation
for T. This equivalence is universal in the input state, not a calibration on
one prepared state. To prove necessity for a projection R, expand

    sum_alpha [K_alpha,R]^dagger [K_alpha,R]
      =Phi^*(R)-Phi^*(R)R-R Phi^*(R)+R.

If Phi^*(R)=R, the right side is zero. Each summand is positive, hence every
commutator vanishes. The converse follows by completeness sum K^dagger K=I.
No assumption that Phi is unital in the Schroedinger picture is needed.
Universal preservation of both one-test probabilities is therefore necessary
and sufficient for (1), and for universal nondisturbance of all Q/T words.
Preservation of only one prescribed all-pass experiment need not imply (1).

For a fixed ideal-outcome word, write its system Kraus product M. Each gap
K commutes with Q,T and their complements, so it commutes through all later
ideal tests. Products of the gap Kraus operators still form a trace-preserving
channel for that fixed history. The total probability is consequently
Tr(M rho M^dagger), exactly the no-storage probability. P belongs to the
algebra generated by Q,T, so the same proof gives terminal target mass
Tr(P M rho M^dagger). It also preserves the reduced reference state when
paired with any reference effect. The entire system output need not be fixed.
The argument allows different gaps, adaptive history-dependent maps, returning
storage environments and initial system-memory entanglement, provided (1)
holds on the complete retained carrier at each gap.

## 2. Flags and controls are an independent boundary

The preceding theorem is wordwise. Average it over any exogenous classical
flag sequence independent of the initial system/storage-memory/reference, with its original
law unchanged by storage. It then preserves the entire noisy record law,
all stopping-prefix probabilities and terminal target mass. Markov flag
independence across time is unnecessary; independence from the stored system
and absence of storage feedback into the flag generator are essential for
reusing the original averaged formula.

If the storage memory includes the flag generator, (1) on Q,T alone does not
preserve its transition law. A sufficient enlarged condition is commutation
with the complete baseline flag/instrument dynamics as well. For example,
a memory-only flip of an initially correct flag commutes with Q,T but can
turn certain acceptance of P into certain recorded rejection. Such a process
is outside the exogenous-flag theorem.

## 3. The exact original commutant

K2488's reducing sectors are P, the two-plane Z+Y, A, B, N, of dimensions
1,2,8,10,87. On the plane, Q=diag(1,0), while T has diagonal r,1-r and
nonzero off-diagonal sqrt(r(1-r)), for r=27/127 or15/67. Commuting with Q
forces a plane operator diagonal; commuting with T makes its two entries
equal. The other four sectors carry the distinct characters (1,1),(1,0),
(0,1),(0,0), so no inter-sector block survives. Thus

    {Q,T}' = C_P direct-sum C I_plane direct-sum M_8
             direct-sum M_10 direct-sum M_87,
    complex dimension =1+1+64+100+7569=7735.

The generated test algebra has dimension1+4+1+1+1=8. With memory E,
tensor each commutant block with B(E). This allows arbitrary noise within
multiplicity sectors and arbitrary storage-memory evolution on the plane,
but forces identity on the plane's system factor. Sector dephasing and
within-sector random unitaries are explicit nontrivial examples. They preserve
all sector populations, including P: this class cannot pump bad weight into
the target. Cross-sector coherence may change, which is irrelevant to the
stated record/target observables. Commuting with only P is much weaker.

## 4. Two contrary constructions

First, the CPTP map

    Phi(X)=PXP+Tr((I-P)X)(I-P)/107

fixes P, preserves its population, and is covariant under every system unitary
that preserves P. Nevertheless, a Q-only A input passes Q, then storage, then
T with probability11/107; without storage that probability is zero.
Covariance means intertwining an action, not fixing each measured effect.
K2492's target-fixing A/B swap remains another counterexample.

Second, let M be a two-state clock and let V fix P and all common sectors,
while on the principal plane

    V=[[sqrt(r),sqrt(1-r)],[-sqrt(1-r),sqrt(r)]].

Then V maps the T-accepted vector to the Q-accepted vector. Define the unitary

    W=I tensor |1><0|+V tensor |0><1|.

Every isolated application with the clock reset to0 induces the identity
system channel. With the clock retained, the first gap applies I and the
second V. For input F P+(1-F)Z, the ideal Q,T,Q word now has acceptance
F+(1-F)r instead of F+(1-F)r^2; target mass remains F. Thus reset-memory
single-gap tomography can certify identity while the actual process fails
(1). This example does not claim identical actual conditional gap maps.
A convex mixture of genuinely projection-fixing channels, conversely, stays
projection-fixing and cannot supply this counterexample.

## 5. Robust finite-cap and resource consumer

Suppose each actual joint gap map, uniformly over reached histories and all
memory/reference inputs, is within epsilon_j in half diamond norm of a
specified transparent joint gap map. Baseline tests and exogenous flag law
are identical. Pad stopped histories by absorbing identities. Telescoping
CPTP compositions gives full record-state trace distance at cap n at most

    E_n=min(1,sum_{j=1}^{n-1} epsilon_j).

For ideal all-pass probability s0 and unnormalized bad mass b0, actual s and b
obey |s-s0|<=E_n and |b-b0|<=E_n. If s0>E_n,

    conditional error <= min(1,(b0+E_n)/(s0-E_n)).          (2)

No guarantee is inferred when the denominator is nonpositive. For initial
fidelity F>=F0, K2488 gives s0>=F0 t_n and sharp ideal error e_n. A conservative
uniform version is min(1,(e_n+eta_n)/(1-eta_n)), with
eta_n=E_n/(F0 t_n)<1. Hence the original Markov phase boundary remains exact
for transparent storage, but a fixed per-gap approximation error does not
prove asymptotic purification: t_n may decay exponentially.

Let one capped attempt charge nonnegative test prices c_j on reaching test j,
gap prices d_j on reaching gap j, and initial preparation c0. Under exact
transparency, reach probabilities q_j are the original ones, so

    A=c0+sum_{j=1}^n c_j q_{j-1}+sum_{j=1}^{n-1} d_j q_j,
    q_0=1.

For approximate storage, an absolute attempt-cost error bound is
D=sum c_j E_{j-1}+sum d_j E_j, using the prefix total-variation bounds
E_0=E_1=0. Gap j is paid after test j and before its noise. With genuinely
independent fresh restarts, expected renewal cost is A/s, even when attempt
cost and success are correlated within each attempt. It is at most
(A0+D)/(s0-E_n) when s0>E_n. Reusing memory across attempts does not establish
the renewal formula. Add actual storage/reset prices; algebraic transparency
alone does not make them zero or prove spatial implementability.

An exact rational consumer with s0=1/1000,b0=1/1000000,E=1/100000 gives error
at most1/90, whereas omitting the acceptance denominator would suggest
an unjustified tiny bound. This is an illustration of (2), not a calibrated
original-verifier parameter point.
