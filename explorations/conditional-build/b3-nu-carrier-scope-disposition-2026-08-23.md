## Disposition

`B3-NU-05` is disposed
`DISPOSED_NO_LEDGER_MOVEMENT__MULTI_CARRIER_SCOPE`.

There is no single measured object called “the neutrino mass.” Four current
primary carriers constrain four different functions:

1. The joint T2K/NOvA long-baseline spectra constrain mass-squared splittings,
   mixing and `delta_CP` within the three-flavor oscillation model. They report
   no strong mass-ordering preference and cannot determine the absolute mass
   scale or Dirac-versus-Majorana reality.
2. KATRIN's tritium endpoint constrains the effective beta-decay mass `m_beta`.
   Its 259-day result is consistent with zero and gives `m_beta < 0.45 eV` at
   90 percent confidence; it does not determine ordering or Majorana status.
3. KamLAND-Zen finds no neutrinoless-double-beta signal. Its half-life limit
   maps to `m_beta_beta = 28--122 meV` only under a light-Majorana exchange
   mechanism and a range of nuclear matrix elements. A null result does not
   prove neutrinos are Dirac.
4. DESI+CMB constrains the cosmological sum. The same DR2 analysis gives
   `sum m_nu < 0.064 eV` in Lambda-CDM and `< 0.16 eV` in `w0-wa` CDM, directly
   displaying the background-model dependence.

The source-faithful target is therefore not a slogan or one number. GU owes
one neutral operator and observation map that produces absolute masses,
splittings, mixing, charge/reality semantics and any lepton-number-violating
channel, followed by four separate likelihood evaluations.
