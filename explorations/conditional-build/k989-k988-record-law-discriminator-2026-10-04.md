# K989 record-law discriminator

Classification: `INTERNAL_PREREGISTERED_HOLDOUT`.

```gu-typed-objects
result: exact record-law separation of operationally identical independent-increment horns
carrier: K986/K987 system plus an independently accessible microscopic path record LAYER=observed CHIRALITY=N/A
pairing: imported positive system pairing and classical path readout ON=repository_stochastic_models
real_structure: real Brownian and signed-jump paths
grading: path-space events and quadratic-variation decomposition
action_owner: UNTYPED -- no GU action owns either record or makes it observable
target: inequivalent empirical resolution required after K988 MAP-TYPE=evaluation
```

The Brownian phase has continuous paths almost surely, terminal law
`Normal(0,gamma T)`, and quadratic variation `gamma T`. The symmetric
compound-Poisson phase is piecewise constant, has an atomic terminal law, and
has jump quadratic variation `theta^2 N_T` with zero continuous quadratic
variation. Its path is continuous only when no event occurs, with probability
`exp(-lambda T)`.

Freeze `gamma` from prior system calibration, `T=2`, and `theta=pi/4`, hence
`lambda=2 gamma`. The primary record test is resolved jump occurrence/path
continuity; the secondary test is the continuous-versus-jump quadratic-
variation decomposition. No record data may refit `gamma` or `theta`.

No empirical score is assigned. Exact path access and resolution are idealized
imports. A GU-owned action or microscopic theory must first make the record an
operational observable and connect it to the system trials.
