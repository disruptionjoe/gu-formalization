## 1. Conditional experiment and inherited exact confidence

At each calibration round t, given past F_(t-1), a common CPTP map Phi_t on the full declared carrier is fixed before the private setting draw J_t. It may depend on all prior data. The fresh trusted preparation is decoupled from device memory conditional on the past. All reused quantum memory is included in E. A marginal-state promise does not imply these premises. Map responses to different inputs may differ, naturally, but a side channel that changes the selected map with J_t is excluded.

Put d=108k, sector ranks r=(10k,98k,12k,96k). Freeze positive design allowances a,b and c=(r_1/a^2,...,r_4/a^2,d/b), W=sum c=2d/a^2+d/b. Choose q_i=c_i/W. For ideal wrong means p_ti and observed wrong bit X_t, mu_t=E[X_t|F_(t-1)]. In the unbiased case mu_t=sum q_i p_ti. Let A_n=sum mu_t, S_n=sum X_t.

K2430's process M_n=1{S_n=0}/prod(1-mu_t), killed when mu_t=1, is nonnegative supermartingale. On a zero path, AM-GM gives M_n >= (1-A_n/n)^(-n). Ville therefore gives, simultaneously at every n with S_n=0,

  A_n/n <= U_n=1-beta^(1/n).

No iid or fixed stopping time is needed. Counts cannot be silently restarted after a failure. For nonzero counts, fixed lambda>0 gives

  exp[(1-e^-lambda) A_n-lambda S_n],
  A_n/n <= [lambda S_n+log(1/beta)]/[n(1-e^-lambda)].

This follows from E exp(-lambda X_t)=1-(1-e^-lambda)mu_t <= exp[-(1-e^-lambda)mu_t]. To select among zero/nonzero protocols or lambda values, preallocate beta among them. G predictably selected classes can each use its own clock with predeclared beta_g and sum beta_g<=alpha. No conditional confidence on a postselected calibration transcript is claimed.

Known setting probability bias eta_ti implies ideal p_ti <= observed p_ti+eta_ti. Thus on the event above,

  B_n := W U_n + (1/n)sum_t sum_i c_i eta_ti

bounds the arithmetic mean of L_t=sum_i c_i p_ti. For uniform time-independent biases, B_n=W(U_n+eta_eff), eta_eff=sum q_i eta_i. Conditional/joint preparation and readout error must be against the same fresh decoupled ideal experiment.

## 2. Deterministic operator transfer

Let A_R(Phi)=S Phi*(R)S on S and L(Phi)=S Phi*(I-S)S. K2504's B-D^2=A-A^2>=0 and trace domination give, for each t,

  eps_t^2/a^2 + ell_t/b <= L_t,

where eps_t=max_R ||A_R(Phi_t)-R_S|| and ell_t=||L(Phi_t)||. (Each eps_t^2 is bounded by the maximum of four sector trace errors, hence by their sum; leakage is bounded by its fifth trace contribution.) Consequently

  (1/n)sum eps_t^2 <= a^2 B_n,
  (1/n)sum ell_t <= b B_n.

These control average squared operator error and average leakage, not all individual maps. Markov gives fraction{eps_t>x} <= a^2 B_n/x^2 and fraction{ell_t>y} <= b B_n/y.

For any weights w_t>=0, sum w_t=1, w_t<=rho/n (rho>=1), define Phi_w=sum w_t Phi_t. Linearity of the five means and positivity give

  ||A_R(Phi_w)-R_S|| <= min(1,a sqrt(rho B_n)),
  ||L(Phi_w)|| <= min(1,b rho B_n).

This is pathwise and simultaneous over all such weights. Weights can be chosen after observing calibration data; no statistical union over weights is needed. The weighted mean of eps_t and ell_t has the same respective bounds by Cauchy-Schwarz. Do not infer a uniform individual-map result from these statements.

## 3. Actual deployment premise and theorem

For each deployment gap j and *every* declared classical history h, including histories reachable only in proof hybrids, suppose there exist weights w_jh,t with cap rho/n and effect-specific drift bounds

  ||A_R(Psi_jh)-A_R(Phi_wjh)|| <= delta_R,j, R=Q,T,
  ||L(Psi_jh)-L(Phi_wjh)|| <= delta_L,j.

All channels act on the same complete retained carrier and support. These are map/operator promises uniform over supported inputs and untouched references, not average training-state errors or empirical mean drift. Neither the counts nor choice of weights verifies the promise. The same calibration event then implies simultaneously across all deployment histories

  epsilon_j <= min(1,a sqrt(rho B_n)+max_R delta_R,j),
  ell_j <= min(1,b rho B_n+delta_L,j).

This is the endpoint that can be plugged into the original record theorem. It is weaker than diamond-distance coverage: only the three relevant Heisenberg effects are transported. A confidence-qualified coverage premise with failure probability gamma costs alpha+gamma once by union bound, without conditioning inference on its event or assuming independence.

## 4. Explicit record, postselection and sample costs

Last-gap leakage is never charged. Take the minimum with K2502's applicable bounds; do not claim the newer formula always dominates. In particular, uniform epsilon_D,ell_D at m=2 gives

## 5. Adaptive setting allocation

The labels may be adaptive without claiming uniform optimality. Suppose q_ti>0 is predictable and a predeclared deterministic C bounds max_i c_i/q_ti for every allowed past. Then L_t<=C mu_t+sum_i c_i eta_ti, so replace B_n by C U_n+mean sum c_i eta_ti. This certifies the same arithmetic-mean channel. If q_ti>=gamma c_i/W then C=W/gamma suffices. The fixed optimal weights attain C=W because max_i c_i/q_i>=sum c_i=W. Adaptive allocation may serve other goals but cannot improve this scalar worst-coordinate domination coefficient. Deterministic quotas have zero probabilities and cannot use this common-map pooled argument; separate setting clocks need their own map-transfer argument because their tested map ensembles may differ.

## 6. Exact contrary constructions

A. Effect-perfect but diamond-far: choose a supported original joint-sector vector a and an orthogonal vector b; define U=I-2|a><a| with a a joint Q/T eigenvector. U commutes with Q,T,S; its channel has epsilon=ell=0. It maps (a+b)/sqrt2 to (-a+b)/sqrt2, orthogonal to the input. Thus half-diamond distance to identity is exactly1. It leaves every original record invariant by the inherited effect theorem. This proves requiring diamond-small drift would discard valid effect-perfect storage.

B. Test/deployment indistinguishability: device I throughout calibration, then swap original A-sector vector a (Q1,T0) with B-sector b (Q0,T1) during deployment. Calibration labels/outcomes have exactly the identity-device law; all five wrong bits are zero, while the deployment compressed Q/T defect is1. Choose initial a and a Q test after storage: ideal outcome Q=1, actual Q=0 with certainty. No calibration-only certificate can exclude this absent coverage. This defeats a uniform guarantee for any number of tests, not merely a rare finite transcript.

C. Uncapped reweighting: among n conditional calibration maps, n-1 are identity and one is that swap. Its average effect error vanishes as n grows, but deployment selecting the single bad map has error1. A density cap rho independent of n forbids this selection. It is not enough to say that deployment picks a previously tested map.

D. Marginal channels do not determine a process: set U to the A/B swap. Compare a fair shared hidden bit Z reused across two gaps, applying U^Z both times, versus two independent fair bits. Every isolated single-use marginal channel is (Id+Ad_U)/2. Start at a, measure Q after each gap. Shared-bit record law is P(1,1)=P(0,1)=1/2. Independent-bit law is uniform on the four records. Their TV distance is1/2, despite identical single-use marginals. The first measurement changes the conditional law of the shared memory; historywise maps are not the marginal mixture. Include that memory in E and demand coverage on the full retained carrier, or the K2500/K2502 premises have not been met.

E. Missing bias/label floor: a positive wrong-effect on a starved sector can have large c_i p_i and negligible pooled mean. Unknown preparation/readout bias can hide a defect regardless of n. Counts are neither self-calibration nor evidence of route secrecy.
