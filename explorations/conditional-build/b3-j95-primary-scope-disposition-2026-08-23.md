## The three primary objects

1. Ted Jacobson, “Thermodynamics of Spacetime: The Einstein Equation of
   State,” *Physical Review Letters* **75** (1995) 1260–1263,
   [arXiv:gr-qc/9504004v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9504004),
   [doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.75.1260](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.75.1260).
   The Einstein equation follows after assuming entropy proportional to local
   causal-horizon area, Unruh temperature, boost-energy flux and the
   equilibrium Clausius relation for all local Rindler horizons. This is a
   conditional derivation, not an empirical measurement of those premises.
2. Christopher Eling, Raf Guedens and Ted Jacobson, “Non-equilibrium
   Thermodynamics of Spacetime,” *Physical Review Letters* **96** (2006)
   121301, [arXiv:gr-qc/0602001](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0602001),
   [doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.121301](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.121301).
   The paper proves that an entropy correction polynomial in the Ricci scalar
   requires an entropy-balance law with an internal production term. Its
   stated result is not a theorem for every curvature correction, and it does
   not mention a GU Weyl/Bach action.
3. Raf Guedens, Ted Jacobson and Sudipta Sarkar, “Horizon entropy and higher
   curvature equations of state,” *Physical Review D* **85** (2012) 064017,
   [arXiv:1112.6215v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.6215),
   [doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.85.064017](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.85.064017).
   This later paper constructs a restricted Clausius route for Lagrangians
   algebraic in the metric and Riemann tensor. It requires special horizon
   slices, a controlled approximate Killing vector and an integrability
   condition on the entropy density; it appears not to extend to Lagrangians
   containing derivatives of curvature.

The 2006 and 2012 results are complementary, not interchangeable. The first
establishes a nonequilibrium requirement in the `f(R)` setting. The second
keeps an equilibrium higher-curvature route mathematically available under a
different entropy construction and explicit restrictions.
