## Exact coordinate theorem

Let `y` collect the reference, metric, section, Hodge, Shiab, trace-frame and
observer coordinates. For any derivative `K=D_y B`, the tangent coordinate
change is

```text
(delta varpi, delta y) -> (delta T, delta y)
                        = (delta varpi-K delta y, delta y).
```

Its Jacobian and cotangent pullback are

```text
J = [ I  -K ]       J^T (E_T,E_y) = (E_T, E_y-K^T E_T).
    [ 0   I ]
```

other Euler component. The executable probe verifies this on the actual
196-cell covector with an unrelated exact rational `196 x 7` control matrix;
it does not fit `K` to the target.

The same conclusion survives eliminating an auxiliary observer or frame by


could manufacture a chain-term cancellation, but it would not solve the
