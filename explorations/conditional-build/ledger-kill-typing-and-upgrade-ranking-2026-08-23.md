## Part 1 — Why `AC-F3` reads as a falsification, and why that is probably wrong

`AC-F3` ("bulk anomaly inflow carries the 3-primary generation count") is
typed `OVER_DETERMINED / GENUINE_FALSIFICATION`. Four facts, none of them
new physics, say that typing is inconsistent with the repository's own
records:

1. **Its own mapping grade says otherwise.** The row's `mapping_grade` is
   `EXACT_ROUTE_KILL`, and its `distance` and `revival_trigger` both speak of
   constructing *a different bridge* ("a non-inflow framed/String/boundary
   bridge", "a nonzero 3-primary bridge not sourced by the locally vanishing
   bulk anomaly"). A result with a named live alternative is a route kill.
2. **The ledger's own taxonomy has the right kind, and its sibling uses it.**
   `ROUTE_KILLED` exists under `DIFFERS`. `AC-F4` — same axis, same 3-primary
   target object, same shape of result ("spin-bordism torsion can carry a
   3-primary count") — is typed `DIFFERS / ROUTE_KILLED`. Two route kills
   against the same target carrying different verdict kinds is an internal
   inconsistency, independent of any view about GU.
3. **The target is a framing the source disavows.** `SC-GEN-01` records the
   draft *disavowing* the belief that nature repeated a generation three
   times; `SC-GEN-02` asserts the `2+1` replacement with two true families
   and one effective imposter; `SC-GEN-04` states the imposter is explicitly
   not a true generation. A row whose target is "the 3-primary generation
   count carried by bulk anomaly inflow" is testing a conventional
   comparator, not the source's stated object — exactly fork 1 and fork 4 of
   the comparator-routing method.
4. **The source's anomaly stance rules the mechanism out in advance.**
   `SC-CHI-50` records the claim that the world is not chiral and GU is not
   chiral, "so the chiral-anomaly critique of the theory misses, because the
   theory has no chirality for the anomaly to attach to." An anomaly-inflow
   carrier for the count is therefore not the source's proposed mechanism at
   all.

**This is not a new discovery about the ledger; the repository already
recorded the cause.** The kill-target gate's own docstring states that a
2026-08-13 sweep found **0 of 84 rows naming any register ID**, that this
left 8 untyped kill-bearing rows "including all three
`GENUINE_FALSIFICATION` verdicts (RA-D2, LT-GR1b, AC-F3)", and — verbatim —
"**That is how RA-D2, our strongest negative verdict type, came to target a
claim the source never made.**" The gate then installed a ratchet
(`LEDGER_BASELINE = 8`) whose retirement condition is the baseline reaching
zero. That work was never done, so the mis-typing has stood for ten days.

**Disposition.** Verdict re-typing is a claim-status change and belongs to
canonical Progress, so this artifact proposes rather than applies:
`AC-F3` → `DIFFERS / ROUTE_KILLED` (matching `AC-F4`), with `LT-GR1b` and
`RA-D2` proposed for the same review. `RA-D2` is the one of the three that
plausibly *does* engage a registered source claim — `SC-CHI-51` asserts GU
must produce an effectively chiral world via a VEV in a
Dirac-Rarita-Schwinger-like operator — but even there the mapping grade ends
`REPLACEMENT_SHIAB_OR_FUNCTIONAL_BUILD_OPEN`, i.e. a bounded kill of
specific realizations with replacements open, not a kill of the claim. If
Progress accepts, the ledger's genuine-falsification count moves from three
toward zero and the same evidence is preserved intact one column to the left.

**One regression repaired here, not proposed.** The gate had gone RED at 9
against its baseline of 8: the ninth row was `LT-GR8`, added in this
session, whose `kill_scope` field states what a *future* packet failure
would bound rather than any kill the row performs. It is now typed
`NONE-NOT-A-KILL` with that reason recorded, and the gate is back to `ok`.
The defect was mine and is fixed at source.

## Part 2 — Where the fastest ledger upgrades are

A seven-seat council ran inline over the v0.260 distribution (85 targets:
33 `SAME`, 19 `DIFFERS`, 28 `NEEDS`, 5 `OVER_DETERMINED`). Ranked by
ledger movement per unit effort, with non-competition against the scheduled
CBRS-1 candidate lane as a hard filter:

**1. Finish the kill-typing ratchet (8 rows).** Cheapest and already
mechanically gated. Type each remaining kill-bearing row against the
register or the hatch, drive `LEDGER_BASELINE` to zero, and let Progress
disposition the three verdict re-typings. Moves up to three rows out of the
most alarming column in the ledger and retires a standing gate. Pure
R4-level typing work; touches no CBRS object.

**2. Audit the 15 `DERIVED_CONDITIONAL` rows for promotable conditions.**
This is the real reconstruction score: `SAME` splits 15 `DERIVED` / 15
`DERIVED_CONDITIONAL` / 3 `DERIVED_PARTIAL`. Nobody has asked whether those
15 are conditional on *the same* handful of items, nor whether any condition
has since been discharged or narrowed — and this session alone found two
surfaces stale by six weeks. Even two promotions materially change the
program's headline. Medium cost, highest score movement.

**3. Preregister the 9 `DIFFERS / PREDICTION` rows.** The upside inventory:
an extra `U(1)`, a forced right-handed neutrino, at least two Higgs
doublets, the Higgs sharing an irrep with color triplets, no physical
spin-3/2 sector, no mirror sector, rank/dimension counts. These are where GU
says something *different* and testable, and none is frozen under the PP
preregistration framework the way PP1/PP3 are. This is R6-side work in your
lane, and it converts standing structure into falsifiable content without
needing the source action.

**4. Re-check the 2 `STALE_PREMISE` rows.** By construction these are rows
whose premise went stale. Staleness has been this session's most productive
defect class, and the class is named in the taxonomy but unaudited. Cheap.

**5. Sweep the 7 small `NEEDS`.** Of 28 `NEEDS`, 21 are
`MISSING_CONSTRUCTION` and genuinely wait on the source action. The other
seven do not: 3 `REAL_PARAMETER`, 2 `EXTERNAL_DATUM`, 2 `FINITE_CHOICE`. A
finite choice or a one-bit datum can sometimes be discharged by an existing
result rather than a new construction. Low cost, and the only `NEEDS`
movement available without OWNER-A.

**What the council explicitly did not rank.** Anything requiring a
background, an action owner, or a positivity construction — those are the
21 `MISSING_CONSTRUCTION` rows and the seven-owner ledger, and they are
where the scheduled lane already works. The five routes above are chosen
precisely because they move the ledger without touching that lane.

## Hostile review

The strongest attack is that re-typing falsifications to route kills is
grade inflation dressed as bookkeeping. It is not, and the guard is that
**no evidence changes and no row moves toward `SAME`**: a re-typed row stays
a negative result, keeps its exact computation, its distance and its revival
trigger, and moves only from "the theory is over-determined here" to "this
route is dead here" — which is what its own mapping grade already says, and
what its sibling row already carries. The second attack is that this
artifact is itself a kill aimed at the ledger; it is not, and it is typed
`NONE-NOT-A-KILL` accordingly. The third is that the council ranking is
self-serving toward the author's lane; the ranking is stated in ledger
movement per unit effort with the competition filter explicit, so it can be
checked against those criteria rather than taken on authority.

No scientific ledger verdict, canon, source ownership, prediction credit, or
public posture changes. All verdict re-typings are proposals filed as a
conditional evidence delta for canonical Progress disposition.
