## Result

every K386-reachable proper face. Across all `4,480` ordered descriptors and
`695` face instances, `12,320,960` determinant-matrix uses reduce to a finite
bank of `60` singular templates and `75` confluent templates. The singular
rank histogram is `{1: 2, 2: 5, 3: 10, 4: 17, 5: 26}`; every assignment dual
matches its primal optimum, and the largest row or column divided-difference

K389 replays the complete second-derivative product rule on all `3,113,600`
ordered descriptor-face pairs. Every reachable face is locally integrable,
the global minimum face-normal power is zero, and the all-twenty-axis `s1`
face replays the degree-eight all-zero radial ledger. The all-zero singular
powers are `[11, 12, 13]`, with minimum second-derivative power eight.

K390 turns those results into `695` executable approach programs, resolving
all `3,113,600` descriptor-face programs and all `12,320,960` matrix uses to
the K388 template bank. Eighteen hybrids have reachable faces; `v9` and `v10`
correctly retain positive-interior fallbacks. Each proper-face program fixes
three positive normal levels and avoids raw Bessel evaluation at zero.
