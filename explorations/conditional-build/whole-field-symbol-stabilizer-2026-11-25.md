## 2. Distinguish the three questions before solving

Write symbols `sigma_theta(x)=sum theta_k S_k(x)`.

1. **Universal same-parameter stabilizer:** the same pair `(N,M)` satisfies `N S_k(x)=S_k(x) M` for every k and every x. Equivalently `N sigma_theta=sigma_theta M` for every independent theta, with the **same** maps. This is what the theorem decides.
2. **One fixed operator:** `N sigma_theta0(x)=sigma_theta0(x) M` at one coefficient point. Cancellations between arrows are permitted. The universal answer is only a subgroup of this answer.
3. **Parameter-changing equivalence/normalizer:** `N sigma_theta(x)=sigma_f(theta)(x) M`, possibly with theta-dependent maps. This is a different question; transformations may mix the coefficient basis. Even a fixed pair that preserves the space need not preserve each generator.

For the horizon's convention `L sigma R=sigma`, set `R=M`, `L=N^-1`. Independent barred-row transport is `bar_chi' = bar_chi N^-1`. There is no `M†` constraint. If a separate density factor moves, absorb its specified scalar into the row law before comparing this formula.

## 3. Theorem: exact universal independent left/right stabilizer

Let `Omega²=I`, `Omega c_x=-c_x Omega`, and

`G=diag(I_(V*)⊗Omega,-Omega)`, `Pi_±=(I±G)/2`.

For either complete18 or complete14, all complex-linear pairs `(N,M)` satisfying the coefficientwise equations are

`M=a I+b G = r_+ Pi_+ + r_- Pi_-`,

`N=a I-b G = r_- Pi_+ + r_+ Pi_-`,

with arbitrary complex a,b. Both are invertible exactly when `r_+ r_- != 0`. Thus the invertible universal stabilizer is `(C*)²`. In the requested left/right convention:

`R=r_+ Pi_+ + r_- Pi_-`,

`L=r_-^-1 Pi_+ + r_+^-1 Pi_-`.

If one additionally insists on ordinary same-frame conjugation, `N=M`, then `b=0` and only common scalars remain. That extra requirement is not imposed by independent classical bars.

### Proof, including arbitrary provenance mixing

Write unrestricted blocks

`M=[[A,B],[C,D]]`, `N=[[E,F],[H,K]]`.

The P equations are

`N P_x = [[0,E j_x],[0,H j_x]]`,

`P_x M = [[j_x C,j_x D],[0,0]]`.

For all x they force `C=0`, `H=0`, and `E=I_(V*)⊗D`: the images of the insertions span the complete one-form carrier. The Q equations similarly force `F=0`, `B=0`, and `A=I_(V*)⊗K` because evaluations separate one-form components. This removes not merely a presupposed scalar block shear but every initially allowed one-form/scalar mixing map. The arbitrary one-form block has also become a common spinor map on all form slots; trace/RS mixing has not been prohibited by fiat.

Choose a nonnull orthogonal frame and evaluate W at `x=e_i`. In metric-raised one-form coordinates its block from form component a to the same component a, for `a != i`, is `c_i`. The `j_x Gamma` contribution is zero in that output component. Hence

`D c_i = c_i K` for every i.

This is valid for all nondegenerate signatures and uses only `n>=2`. Eliminating K implies that D commutes with every even Clifford product `c_i c_j`. The commutant is exactly `span_C{I,Omega}`. An elementary proof avoids an unexamined Schur hypothesis: under `End_C(S)=Cl_C(V)`, conjugation by `c_i c_j` acts on each Clifford monomial by a sign which is negative exactly when its index set contains one of i,j. Commuting with every pair leaves only the empty and full index sets. Thus `D=alpha I+beta Omega`, `K=alpha I-beta Omega`. Renaming beta gives the stated M,N.

Conversely every one of the complete18 generators anticommutes with G. Its input-side Omega partner does too, because the input Omega operator commutes with G. Therefore `(aI-bG) S_k=S_k(aI+bG)` for every generator. This proves sufficiency for complete18 and its complete14 submodule, and establishes that the three-arrow argument has not overlooked further restrictions. Neither timelike-symbol inversion nor a nondegenerate particular coefficient choice enters the proof.

**Degenerate alternatives, correctly quantified:** omitting an independently weighted P or Q, retaining only a tied combination, evaluating at a single parameter point, restricting x to a smaller subspace, or passing to a constrained/quotient carrier changes the equations and may enlarge their solution space. A zero symbol has all independent GL pairs. In particular, no pointwise full-support condition can replace coefficientwise independence.

## 5. Differential compatibility: the exact first-jet equation

Use a local frame and write supplied first-order operators

`D_theta=sum_mu sigma_theta^mu partial_mu+B_theta`,

`D'_theta=sum_mu sigma_theta^mu partial_mu+B'_theta`.

For a pair from the pointwise stabilizer, full intertwining `D'_theta M=N D_theta` is equivalent to

`sum_mu sigma_theta^mu (partial_mu M)+B'_theta M-N B_theta=0`.   (J1)

This follows by applying both sides to an arbitrary test section and separately equating its value and first jet. It includes derivatives of the **column** map. There is no derivative of the row map in this direct operator identity; introducing one without integration by parts is an error. Formal-adjoint or integrated-action questions would have their own density and boundary terms.

In a G-parallel reference frame, `M=aI+bG` gives

`sigma_theta(da)+sigma_theta(db)G+B'_theta M-N B_theta=0`.       (J2)

If G moves in the chosen connection, add `b sum_mu sigma_theta^mu nabla_mu G`; do not freeze it. If a known coherent geometric/frame transport has already identified the two principal families, factor that transport out first, apply the theorem to the residual maps, and include its derivatives when restoring (J1). This residual classification does not prohibit ordinary simultaneous gauge covariance of a fully transported tensor tuple.

**Exact special case.** Suppose the same lower coefficients are retained and every `B_theta` anticommutes with G. Then `B_theta M=N B_theta`. If the transformation must work coefficientwise for the complete family, the NE arrow `P` alone forces `da=db=0`: its residual on scalar input is the full one-form `d(a-b Omega)`. Both half-spinor projectors are nonzero, so both scalar derivatives vanish. Conversely constant a,b work under these hypotheses. Hence the universal pointwise freedom does not become arbitrary local functions without specifically compensating lower-order terms.

If lower terms are allowed to change, the exact required change is

`B'_theta=N B_theta M^-1 - sum_mu sigma_theta^mu(partial_mu M)M^-1`.  (J3)

This is a compatibility equation, not permission to invent those terms. Whether a source transformation supplies (J3) is the missing custody question.

## 6. Concrete next calculation on the owned printed NE arrow

The already-owned source wrapped NE connection is `Dhat=D0+U`, and a left tilt at fixed reference gives `U'=h U h^-1`. Once a **coefficientwise whole-family** law is assumed, Section 3 forces its NE input map to

`Q=q_+ P_+ + q_- P_-`, `P_±=(I±Omega)/2`,

and its one-form output map to `I_(V*)⊗Q`. Thus a genuinely full-field unknown, initially including scalar/trace/RS mixing, reduces to the following two-function necessary equation:

`D0 Q + U' Q - Q U = 0`.                                   (NE1)

Assume the supplied reference preserves Omega. Block decomposition relative to `P_±` makes (NE1) exactly

`U'_(ss)-U_(ss) = -(dq_s/q_s) I_(S_s)` for `s=+,-`,          (NE2)

`U'_(st)=(q_s/q_t) U_(st)` for `s != t`.                    (NE3)

These are equations of one-forms; q_s are nowhere zero. The notation `dq_s/q_s` is local and does not presume a global logarithm.

Executable discrimination for a supplied h,U:

1. Compute `U'=hUh^-1`, and the four Omega blocks of U and U'.
2. Check whether the two diagonal differences are scalar multiples of identity. Any traceless remainder fails (NE2).
3. Name the resulting scalar one-forms beta_s with diagonal difference `-beta_s I`; local solvability requires `d beta_s=0` on a simply connected patch, and then `dq_s/q_s=beta_s`.
4. Check both off-diagonal equations with the **same** ratio `q_+/q_-`; they cannot be rescaled independently. Equivalently the beta difference controls the derivative of that ratio. If one direction vanishes, preserve that degenerate branch rather than divide by a zero block.
5. Substitute the surviving pair into the remaining supplied NW, SW and SE zero-order equations in (J1). Passing NE is necessary, not sufficient for the whole operator.
6. For global nonzero q_s, also check multiplicative holonomy/periods of beta_s; a merely local exact solution supplies no global descent or domain.
