# Selected K77 operative pairing-symmetry closure

## Plain-English result

The two `C^(32,32)` Weyl halves and full `U(64,64)` are both real pieces of the
source story, but they do not mean the same action on the residual used in the
current build.

The current residual keeps Clifford grades `1`, `2` and `5`, a
2,107-dimensional coefficient carrier.  That carrier is exactly closed under
`Spin(7,7)`.  It is **not** closed under either:

- the block-preserving `U(32,32) x U(32,32)` action on the two Weyl halves; or
- full `U(64,64)`, which also mixes the halves.

Under the block product, the smallest complex invariant closure has dimension
`16,382`.  Under full `U(64,64)`, it has dimension `16,383`.  Thus the large
group cannot be used as a symmetry selector on the 2,107-dimensional
truncation without doing one of three explicit things: enlarge the residual,
construct an equivariant reduction after symmetry breaking/observation, or
state that the operative observed action has reduced to Spin.

This is useful rather than fatal.  It removes the false choice between “one
full-group weight” and “unknown block weights.”  The actual choice is between
different **action parents**.  For the existing selected action, the canonical
scalar-Clifford-trace pairing remains a zero-tuning construction point and the
next lower-order `D_g Upsilon` build can proceed conditionally.  It is not a
source derivation: without a full-group parent or an added chirality-exchange
symmetry, two relative pairing ratios remain unselected.

## Exact theorem

Represent a Clifford monomial by a 14-bit mask `A`.  For two masks,

`[e_A,e_B] != 0` iff `|A||B|-|A intersect B|` is odd,

and the resulting support is `A xor B`.  The metric signs change the nonzero
coefficient, not this support theorem.

|---|---|---:|---:|

| `gl(S+) + gl(S-)` | `sl(S+) + sl(S-) + Hom(S-,S+) + Hom(S+,S-)` | 16,382 | 3 |

Three explicit grade-four block generators send grades `1`, `2`, `5` to

summands pair dually, giving three invariant bilinear coordinates.  Full
