### 1.2 Verdict: COMPATIBLE. No tension. And the claim is now quantified.

The two statements are not about the same object — Weinstein's is about the
Einstein setting, DC-H2's is about GU's own gimmel block ratio — so they could
not contradict each other even in principle. What makes the pairing worth
filing is stronger than non-contradiction: **they are the same theorem twice**,
run at two levels, and the source runs the argument by hand exactly as far as
the repo proves it can go, then stops at the same place and says so.

**A1 — the source's own derivation terminates one step short of a scale, and
he says so.** The derivation at `:44` produces `partial_mu Lambda = 0`. That
is a statement that `Lambda` is constant. It contains no information about
WHICH constant. The very next clause — "and once it's constant, it has no
explanation" — is Weinstein stating the scale half of the split himself. He is
not claiming to have derived a magnitude; he is complaining that nothing does.

**A2 — the uniqueness claim is Lovelock, and Lovelock is explicitly
form-up-to-coefficients.** This repo already names the theorem, at
`lab/process/perspective-passes/01-foundational-math-lenses/05-general-relativist.md:32`:

> "in 4D the unique 2nd-order metric theory with diffeo invariance is
> Einstein-Hilbert + cosmological constant. Anything pulled back from 14D that
> is 2nd-order and diffeo-invariant on X must equal this, **up to
> coefficients**."

The qualifier is the whole finding. Lovelock forces `a G_munu + b g_munu`; it
leaves exactly two constants, both dimensionful. "Essentially no choice" is
true of the FORM and vacuous about `b`.

**A3 — why no condition of that kind could ever fix the scale. One line,
exact.** The constraint is `nabla^mu X_munu = 0`. It is **linear and
homogeneous** in `X`. Its solution set is therefore a linear subspace, hence
closed under `X -> cX` for every real `c`, hence never a single nonzero point.
A divergence-freeness demand can force at most a **ray** — which is precisely
what "the candidate space is one-dimensional" means, i.e. what "unique" means
here — and is structurally incapable of fixing the point on that ray. In the
algebraic sector the candidate space `span{g}` is already entirely
divergence-free, so the condition cuts nothing at all and the leftover is the
full line.

**A4 — DC-H2 is the general version of A3, computed instead of argued.** Where
A3 uses linearity, DC-H2 uses invariance under the blockwise congruence group
and shows (`SA4`/`SA5`) that the group **moves the ratio** while the condition
does not see the move — so the condition cannot fix a coordinate on its own
orbits. Broader class, same conclusion, exact rational certificate, 35/35.

**The quantification.** "There is essentially no choice" is exactly true, and
its precise content is now statable: *the candidate space is one-dimensional
(two-dimensional in the Lovelock sector), so the form is forced and exactly one
dimensionful real number is left free; and the class of conditions that
provably cannot supply that number contains divergence-freeness itself, gauge
equivariance, symmetry-of-the-pairing, self-adjointness/reciprocity, and every
condition invariant under blockwise congruence.* **Corollary, and it is the
useful part: no strengthening of the divergence-free demand will ever produce
`Lambda`'s magnitude.** That is why the 120-orders problem is not a hard
version of the divergence-free problem — it is a different type of problem, and
the repo can now say why in one sentence.

### 2.2 The adjacent HOMONYM, which is real

There is a SECOND written form of "the dark-energy replacement" in this repo,
and it has the **wrong form degree**.

- `explorations/cycle-gates-and-audits/weinstein-ucsd-2025-04-analysis-2026-06-22.md:50-56`

- The transcript's own degree statement is unambiguous, `:20` `[00:03:06]`:
  "this whole thing is gonna **live in add valued one forms**, and it's gonna
  replace the cosmological constant times the metric."

**Reconciliation, which works — and is why this is a homonym and not an error
in canon.** Canon's own `tau^+` homomorphism (`:32`) is
`g |-> (g, d_{nabla_aleph}(g) . g^{-1})`. On that diagonal
`B = d_A(epsilon) . epsilon^{-1}`, so `Ad(epsilon^{-1}) B = epsilon^{-1} d_A epsilon`,
a Maurer-Cartan **1-form**. Then

```
theta  =  pi  -  epsilon^{-1} d_A epsilon          (degree 1)
```

which contains exactly the three ingredients Weinstein names at `:17`

degree right**. So `epsilon_omega o d_A` is a lossy abbreviation of
`epsilon_omega^{-1} d_A epsilon_omega`, in which the gauge transformation is
the **argument** of `d_A`, not a post-composition. Under that reading both repo
shorthands agree with canon and with the source.

Under the literal reading actually written at
`weinstein-ucsd-2025-04-analysis-2026-06-22.md:52` — `d_A pi` — the degree
disagrees with canon, with the transcript, and with that file's own next
