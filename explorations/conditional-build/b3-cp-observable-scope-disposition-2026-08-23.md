## Disposition

`B3-CP-04` is disposed
`DISPOSED_NO_LEDGER_MOVEMENT__OBSERVABLE_TO_THETA_SCOPE`.

The PSI experiment measures an electric-field-correlated neutron-precession
carrier and reports
`d_n = (0.0 +/- 1.1_stat +/- 0.2_sys) x 10^-26 e cm`. It does not directly
measure a QCD theta angle. The conversion from a microscopic CP-odd term to
`d_n` requires a hadronic matrix element, phase convention and control of
excited-state, continuum and chiral systematics. Bhattacharya et al.'s
2+1+1-flavor lattice calculation makes that dependence explicit and concludes
that its present statistics do not provide a reliable precision estimate of
the theta contribution.

This is two-sided and supplies no escape. Hadronic uncertainty does not erase
the neutron-EDM constraint. Conversely, a null EDM estimate cannot be promoted
to a direct observation that theta equals zero or to evidence for any one
microscopic suppression mechanism.

1. **Observed carrier:** the neutron EDM, plus other separately declared

2. **Theory map:** the physical angle combination, including topological
