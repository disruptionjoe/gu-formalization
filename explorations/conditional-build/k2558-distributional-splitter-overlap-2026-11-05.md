## 2. A right-inverse construction that does not commute chosen inverses

Reuse the local distributional factor inverses justified in K2556. Each

the solution affine space in the Hilbert Sobolev space has a unique

The full matrix identities already established in K2554 are

    M(-7K,Y)v=c(Y+mu)v, v=(K,0,1,0,0,0)^T,
    d(-7K,Y)=c(Y+mu)R4(Y),
    adj(M(-7K,Y))v=R4(Y)v, deg R4=4.                    (2)

Keep the actual mixed determinant. First choose q solving

    R4(Y)U=q, d(-7K,Y)U=rho,

## 3. Exact defect when the two inverses are not synchronized

Let q_s=R4(Y)U_s and q_t both solve c(Y+mu)q=rho. Let W_s,W_t both solve
(Y+5K)W=rho. Define eta=q_s-q_t and delta=W_s-W_t. Then

    (Y+mu)eta=0, (Y+5K)delta=0,
    h_s-h_t=-(rE/2)Lie_(W_s grad_A psi)G
              +(rE delta+rE² eta)Tpsi,               (5)
    E(h_s-h_t)=(rE²/2)(mu-5K)eta Tpsi.                (6)

Here E is the full Einstein linearization on the stationary background,
which annihilates diffeomorphisms; on f(B)Tpsi it is
-(Y+5K)f Tpsi/2. The coefficient is strictly nonzero:

    mu-5K=(67736495/4439232512)kappa²>0.

Thus an arbitrary independent displacement-inverse mismatch need not be
gauge. Formula (5) also displays the separate Einstein homogeneous freedom;
E failing to detect delta is not a proof that delta Tpsi is gauge. Setting
eta=delta=0 is a sufficient synchronized prescription, not a classification
of all observationally equivalent inverses.
