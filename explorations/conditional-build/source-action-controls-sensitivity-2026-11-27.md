### GR: quotient before physical energy

Use linearized ADM variables h_ij and pi^ij at a nonzero Fourier covector along
axis3. Independent off-diagonal canonical momenta are twice pi^ij. The kinetic
Hamiltonian is pi_ij*pi_ij minus one half of the squared trace. Momentum
constraints impose pi_i3=0; the scalar constraint imposes h11+h22=0. Gauge
vectors come from the canonical Poisson matrix applied to constraint gradients.
A negative raw trace direction is therefore not automatically a physical mode.

Primary formulation: [ADM](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0405109); harmonic formulation context: [Choquet-Bruhat](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.3490).

The harmonic equations are wave equations. Their natural estimate controls
(h, dt h) in H^(s+1) x H^s, or a balanced first-order reduction. It is not an
estimate in an arbitrary equal-order norm for unbalanced variables. The test
is linear and flat; no nonlinear global GR theorem is claimed.

### Stelle: a ghost can have a well-posed linear evolution

Primary quadratic-gravity reference: [Stelle](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00760427).

For one TT polarization, let a=|k|^2, b=a+m2^2 with m2^2>0. The ordinary real
classical prescription has L=(-h_tt^2+(a+b)h_t^2-ab*h^2)/(2*m2^2), hence
(dt^2+a)(dt^2+b)h=0. The finite-jet fields
x=(h_tt+b*h)/m2^2 and y=-(h_tt+a*h)/m2^2 satisfy h=x+y. Their canonical
energy has opposite signs, while a positive sum of oscillator energies is a
conserved **estimate norm**. It is not a replacement physical Hamiltonian.
The finite-order change of variables gives polynomial frequency costs; the
negative-energy oscillator alone does not imply failure of every finite loss.
No nonlinear runaway or alternate PT/fakeon quantization is inferred.

### Dirac: signed energy is not negative norm

For the standard field and pairing see [Tong’s author-written Dirac derivation](https://www.damtp.cam.ac.uk/user/tong/qft/qfthtml/S4.html).

The ordinary free Dirac Hamiltonian H=alpha.k+beta*m obeys H*=H and
H^2=(|k|^2+m^2)I. Thus its energy has both signs, but exp(-itH) preserves the
positive spinor norm in each H^s. Calling a negative first-quantized eigenvalue
a physical ghost would be a false alarm. The ordinary particle/antiparticle
QFT interpretation additionally uses CAR and a vacuum.

### Mutations and conservation

A balanced oscillator has imaginary eigenvalues. The elliptic Cauchy control
has real eigenvalue |k| and exact exponential growth, which outruns every fixed
polynomial derivative allowance. A square-zero generator kN instead has
exp(tkN)=I+tkN: one derivative loss can suffice. A Jordan defect by itself
cannot establish the stronger all-finite-loss obstruction.

Conservation uses the actual field type. For the flat linearized Ricci symbol,
writing H=eta^{ab}h_ab, v_nu=xi^mu h_mu_nu and w=xi^mu v_mu gives
R_munu=(xi^2 h_munu+xi_mu xi_nu H-xi_mu v_nu-xi_nu v_mu)/2.
Then R=xi^2 H-w and xi^mu R_munu=xi_nu R/2, so xi^mu G_munu=0
off shell. The exact adapter checks the full ten-dimensional symmetric tensor
basis at the declared all-component non-null covector, with trace-sign and
index-raising mutants. The generic identity follows from this displayed
contraction argument, not an extrapolation from that one covector.

Linearized Einstein Bianchi, ordinary Dirac current conservation and covariant
divergence of an adjoint-valued theta are different statements. Density/connection contraction
controls can expose an omitted term; they cannot manufacture the missing
GU theta-to-stress dictionary.

### Why six entire families can be handled without recomputation

Write S_total=S_B(b)+Re integral(bar(psi) D_b(lambda) psi), with independent
matter fields and barred fields. At identically zero matter fields and all their jets, psi=bar(psi)=0, the matter term and its
first and second **bosonic** derivatives vanish; mixed boson/matter Hessian
terms vanish too. The matter Hessian need not vanish, but it is decoupled from
the bosonic block at linear order. Zero matter solves its own linearized
equations for every D in this grammar. Therefore every lambda changing only
this matter grammar/completion leaves the held backgrounds, bosonic Hessian,
constraints, energy and local coderivative unchanged. Functional parameters
need not be enumerated. Local tube containment supplies the remaining domain
qualification. Constants and the frequency threshold may depend on each fixed tube; no uniform shrinking-tube estimate or bosonic-domain change is asserted. The matter–matter Hessian and cubic interactions remain. This is algebraic autonomy, not physical-state autonomy.

The proof does not certify that every imaginable member of the named source
family is matter-only. Its quantifier is the recorded family definition. It
also does not transfer the held positive-matter-current receiver to all
fermion recipes; that receiver is a separate theorem and is not E, F or C.
