## Result first

Neither apparent CBRS-1Y successor is yet an admissible target-blind local
owner in its smallest advertised form.

For one mirror vector `Psi`, every smooth `O(1,3)`-equivariant symmetric
two-tensor has the form

```text

```

The scalar invariant algebra of one orthogonal vector is generated by `s`
alone. The second tensor structure is not a second invariant. Its radial and
tangential eigenvalues are `a+bs` and `a`; nondegeneracy requires both to be
nonzero. Equivariance therefore licenses two arbitrary functions but supplies
no independent pre-density datum that fixes `b`. Choosing its jet after the
CBRS-1Y equation reads `J=(9/4)I_base` repeats the same fit with another
function. No source or action principle currently selects a constant-curvature
relation, Hessian potential, second vector, cross invariant or other owner.

The tempting alternative is also smaller only in coordinates. The base-J4
momentum `M0` has eighteen nonzero cells, but it has nonzero components in both
Clifford grades one and three. For `V=R^(9,5)`, the smallest real
`Spin(9,5)`-stable carrier containing it is

```text

     =Lambda^1(V) direct-sum Lambda^3(V),
```

retains both summands. The eighteen-cell support is not invariant: the existing
contracted Spin orbit already reaches a rank-78 receiver. Selecting those


target. Thus a standalone quadratic odd field can contribute Hilbert stress
