## 1. Complete law and exact transfer

Use K2484/K2488's original carrier, with r=27/127 or15/67, Q rank10 and T
rank12. The reducing categories P,Z,Y,A,B,N have dimensions

    d=(1,1,1,8,10,87), sum d=108.

P is the target. Z,Y denote the principal-plane eigenvectors in the CURRENT
measurement basis, so they rotate between Q and T; A is Q-only and B T-only.
Start the flag independently of the input/reference with stationary
pi=(v,u)/(u+v), H=[[1-u,u],[v,1-v]], 0<u<v<1. Consecutive used flags are m
transitions apart, m a positive integer. Write J=H^m. After every accepted test
except the terminal one, apply to the retained system, independently of the
full flag memory and reference,

    D_delta(X)=(1-delta)X+delta Tr(X) I_108/108,
    0<=delta<=1,

then the next recorded-accept projection (R for bit0, I-R for bit1).
No storage precedes the first test; no recovery or noise follows the final
test. On a joint state the replacement is I_108/108 tensor the partial trace,
not a reset of the external reference. Classical transitions and storage
commute under this declared independence.

Let x_(j,e) be six unnormalized sector masses after accepted test j with hidden
bit e. Let M_(Q,0)=diag(1,1,0,1,0,0),
M_(T,0)=diag(1,1,0,0,1,0), and bit1 masks be their complements. For initial
sector weights w, set x_(1,e)=pi_e M_(Q,e) w. Before test j+1 form

    y_c=sum_e J_ec x_(j,e),
    x_(j+1,c)=M_(R_(j+1),c)[(1-delta) O y_c
                                    +delta d (sum y_c)/108],

where O is identity off Z,Y and its Z,Y block is
[[r,1-r],[1-r,r]]. Thus

    q_j=sum_e sum x_(j,e),  t_j=sum_e x_(j,e)[P],
    F_out(j)=t_j/q_j.

Proof: first Q measurement kills Z/Y coherence, conditional on either bit.
Every later accepted principal-plane state has rank one in the current basis,
and consecutive squared overlaps are r or1-r. Cross-category coherences and
within-multiplicity coherences never feed trace or P mass under these reducing
projections and isotropic replacement. The reset contribution is exactly d/108.
This justifies the scalar reduction for fidelity, including initially entangled
references after marginalization; it does not replace the full channel on all
observables by a six-dimensional quantum system. In particular I_6/6 is an
incorrect replacement law.

## 2. Sharp finite fidelity and the unavoidable last-noise floor

For each fixed cap, q and t are affine in initial w and q>0. For F>=F0>0,
the allowed weight polytope has vertices P and
F0 P+(1-F0)V for V=Z,Y,A,B,N. A ratio of affine functions at a convex mixture
is a denominator-weighted average of the vertex ratios. Hence these SIX
vertices give the exact worst conditional fidelity. Retain pure P: storage
can degrade it, and no theorem here drops that vertex.

Let k=10 or12 be the final-test rank and let L be the conditional probability
of hidden bit0 immediately before the final test, given the accepted prefix.
Writing prefix mass q, fresh replacement alone injects bad accepted mass at
least q B, whereas total target mass is at most q G, with

    B=delta[(k-1)L+(108-k)(1-L)]/108,
    G=(1-delta+delta/108)L.

Other surviving bad mass only increases the error. Thus for n>=2,

    1-F_out >= B/(B+G)
             >= delta(k-1)/[108(1-delta)+delta k]
             >= delta/12.

The first ratio decreases in L, so the fixed Markov model strengthens the
bound by substituting L_max=max(J_00,J_10). Prefix conditioning changes the
flag distribution but cannot exceed that next-transition bound. These lower
bounds need not be attained by this fixed protocol. The middle bound is sharp
over arbitrary pre-final states/flags: take pure P and bit0 certain. The
last inequality uses k>=10 and delta<=1.

The floor is specific to this final Q/T instrument and output time. A final
ideal rank-one P projection after storage outputs P exactly whenever accepted;
it is an explicit changed-instrument counterexample to a universal
purification impossibility. Cap n=1 has no storage. Delta0 recovers K2488.

## 3. Parity limit and survival cost

For delta>0 every live category has exactly one accepting hidden bit at each
parity. Its six-by-six transfer has entries

    J_(e_old,e_new)[(1-delta)O_kl+delta d_k/108] > 0.

Both J entries and all reset masses are positive. The two-step transfer is
strictly positive, so Perron--Frobenius gives a unique normalized limiting
sector vector for each parity, independent of initial state. The fidelity
therefore tends to its corresponding positive-floor parity limit. This is a
sector/fidelity assertion, not an unproved full density-matrix convergence.
Every step after the first rejects at least 10delta/108 of prefix mass, hence

    q_n <= q_1(1-10delta/108)^(n-1).

For preparation price c_p, test prices c_j>=0 and per-transition price d>=0,

    C_n=c_p+sum_(j=1)^n c_j q_(j-1)
               +m d sum_(j=1)^(n-1) q_j,  q_0=1.

Independent restart of BOTH input and memory gives renewal cost C_n/q_n;
without that assumption no such renewal claim is made. C_n is nondecreasing
and q_n nonincreasing, so the first feasible deterministic cap minimizes
renewal cost for each fixed input and also its worst-input supremum, even when
fidelity is nonmonotone. Exact worst renewal cost likewise occurs at the six
vertices. A finite scan can establish the globally cheapest cap for a fixed
gap when it finds the first feasible cap; absence in the scan is not absence
at all caps.
