## 2. The conditional build: the 14D local system, solved exactly in U1

### 2.1 What was computed

For a chiral fermion in the slot `Omega^p (x) /S` on `Y^14`, the local anomaly is
carried by the degree-16 index density

```
    D_p  =  [ A-hat(TY^14) . ch(Lambda^p T*Y_C) . ch(S_gauge) ]_16 ,
```

and the theory's total local anomaly is `I_16(x) = sum_p x_p D_p`. Local anomaly
cancellation is the requirement that `I_16(x)` vanish **as a polynomial identity** in
the characteristic classes, i.e. that *every* degree-16 monomial coefficient vanish
separately. That is a linear system `M x = 0` on the 15-dimensional content lattice,
and it was built and solved with exact rational arithmetic
(`fractions.Fraction`, no floats anywhere; script reproduced in Appendix A).

The degree-16 monomial basis has twelve entries: the five purely gravitational
monomials `{p1^4, p1^2 p2, p2^2, p1 p3, p4}` at gauge-weight 0, and seven mixed
monomials carrying `1..4` powers of the gauge Casimir.

### 2.3 The answer

```
  content lattice                       dim  Z^15   =  15
  independent local anomaly conditions  rank M      =   5
  admissible contents                   dim ker M   =  10
```

Five consequences, each new:

**(A) The gauge-group fork `U2` is IRRELEVANT to the entire local row.** The
gravitational-only system (5 conditions) and the full gravity-plus-gauge system (12
conditions) have the **same rank, 5**. The reason is structural and holds for any
gauge reading: the gauge factor `ch(S_gauge)` multiplies every slot identically, so
the condition splits by gauge weight into the gravitational conditions at each weight
`0..4`, and those are already implied by the top-weight five. Therefore the local
14D anomaly row does **not** require deciding whether the source gauges `Sp(64)`,
`Sp(1) = right-H`, or a `Cl(7,7)` real-form analogue. **MOVE1-01's conclusion
survives the real-form fork even though its premise does not** (row G2).

**(B) `W = 0` is DERIVED, not assumed.** The net-chirality functional
`W(x) = sum_p x_p C(14,p)` lies **inside the rank-5 row space**: appending `W` as a
thirteenth row leaves the rank at 5. Local 14D anomaly cancellation therefore
*forces* `W = 0`. The repo's standing statement that the `-13` is "EXPLICITLY
CONDITIONAL on the assumed truncated fermion content" (CANON.md:170) is correct as
written, but the conditionality is now **one-sided**: any content that cancels the
local anomaly necessarily has `W = 0`, so the vanishing branch is not a lucky choice
among branches, it is the only branch consistency permits.

**(C) `W = 0` is NECESSARY but NOT SUFFICIENT.** This corrects a convention that has
been load-bearing since MOVE-1. The MOVE-1 multiplicity convention makes every
channel proportional to `W`, which would make the system rank **1**. The honest
tangent-twisted system has rank **5**. Explicit witness, computed:

```
   x  =  91 * [Omega^0 (x) S^+]  -  1 * [Omega^2 (x) S^-]
   W(x) = 91*C(14,0) - 1*C(14,2) = 91 - 91 = 0
   nonzero degree-16 coefficients: 11 of 12   (pure-gravitational p4 = -304/105)
```

So a content can be exactly chirality-balanced in the `W` sense and still be locally
anomalous. Four independent conditions beyond `W = 0` must also be imposed on the
source action's content. **This is a strictly stronger constraint on the unknown than
anything previously filed.**

**(D) The admissible set has a clean structure.** `D_p = D_{14-p}` exactly (Hodge
symmetry, verified for all `p`). Hence the seven antisymmetric directions
`e_p - e_{14-p}`, `p = 0..6`, are automatically admissible and carry no anomaly at
all; the system sees only the eight Hodge-symmetric combinations
`s_p = x_p + x_{14-p}`, on which its rank is 5. `7 + (8 - 5) = 10 = dim ker M`,
cross-checked.

**(E) The chiral-truncation branch fails in every channel, not one.** The `C0`
content `Omega^0 (x) S^+ + Omega^1 (x) S^-` (`W = -13`) has **12 of 12** degree-16
coefficients nonzero. It is not a theory with one bad term that a counterterm might
absorb.

### 2.4 Layer-0 fence on the two "13"s

`|W(C0)| = |1 - 14| = 13` and `rank(ker Gamma) = 14 - 1 = 13` (from
`1664 = 13 x 128`) are the same arithmetic `C(14,1) - C(14,0)` read with opposite
sign, and they are **different types**: the first is the value of a signed linear
functional on the content lattice; the second is the unsigned rank of a bundle. The
pack's `13`-homonym fence (register M-M28) applies. Do not identify them. For the
record, the `ker Gamma`-refined content (`U6` resolved toward gamma-tracelessness)
has `W = -12`, not `-13`, and is anomalous in 12/12 coefficients: a third,
numerically distinct object.
