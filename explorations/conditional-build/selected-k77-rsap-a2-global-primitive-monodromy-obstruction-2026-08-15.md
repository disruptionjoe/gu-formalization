# Selected-K77 RSAP compact-A2 global primitive-monodromy obstruction

## Result first

The selected group/cotangent-section route for the adjacent compact-`A2`
common refinement does not globalize over its full connected regular
semisimple overlap as one section chart with a globally exact relative
primitive transition.

For the principal factor

```text
T*(SU(3)/SO(3)) -> su(3)*,
```

restrict a regular coadjoint orbit to either simple-root subgroup. The orbit
contains

```text
SU(2)/U(1) = S2.
```

The north and south diagonalizing group sections have equatorial transition

```text
h(phi)=exp(i phi H_alpha).
```

Its root-circle winding is one. Hence a global group section would trivialize
the Hopf bundle on this `S2`, contradicting its first Chern number. Passing
from group representatives to `SU(3)/SO(3)` only divides the fibre circle by
its finite `Z2` intersection with `SO(3)`; the clutching class remains
nonzero. The quotient therefore does not repair the obstruction.

The same transition decides the relative primitive question for this route.
If the regular diagonal covector is

```text
xi=i diag(lambda1,lambda2,lambda3),
```

then, in the normalization
`<xi,H_alpha>=mu_alpha=lambda1-lambda2`, the two lifted cotangent primitives
differ on the equator by

```text
theta_N-theta_S = mu_alpha dphi,
integral theta_N-theta_S = 2 pi mu_alpha.
```

Regularity requires `lambda1 != lambda2`, so this period is nonzero throughout
the selected simple-root regular overlap. A globally exact primitive gauge
would pull back to an exact one-form on the lifted equator and have zero
period. It cannot exist.

This does not kill the symplectic or moment-map atlas, and it does not remove
the globally defined tautological one-form on `T*(SU(3)/SO(3))` itself. The
local cotangent charts still glue by Hamiltonian torus transitions and retain
the passing regular-germ moment and symplectic identities. What fails is the
stronger relative exact-global presentation generated by one diagonalizing
section and one single-valued transition function. The honest global
comparison must retain the nontrivial torus bundle
and its Cech--de Rham primitive class, or add a separately owned prequantum or
twisted correction that cancels that class.

## Explicit two-chart restriction

Write the simple-root orbit as the Riemann sphere with north coordinate `z`
and south coordinate `w=1/z`. Standard normalized `SU(2)` diagonalizers on
the two discs differ on `|z|=1`, `z=exp(i phi)`, by the diagonal root circle

```text
diag(exp(i phi),exp(-i phi)).
```

The first eigenline therefore has clutching monomial `z` and degree one; the
second has `z^-1`. Their determinant degree is zero, as required for `SU(2)`,
but neither eigenline is trivial. Equivalently, choose north/south connection
potentials

```text
A_N=(1-cos(theta))/2 dphi,
A_S=-(1+cos(theta))/2 dphi.
```

They are regular at their respective poles, satisfy `A_N-A_S=dphi` on the
equator, and have curvature with normalized sphere integral one. These facts
are the exact clutching certificate used by the probe.

If a section over the complete regular compact overlap existed, its
restriction to this simple-root sphere would give a section of the restricted
bundle. The unit Chern number rules that out. This restriction argument is
why no global `SU(3)` formula or higher-dimensional search is required.

## Primitive period and the quotient subtlety

Changing a cotangent group section by `h` adds the canonical Maurer--Cartan
term

```text
<xi,h^-1 dh> = mu_alpha dphi.
```

The lifted equatorial loop has period `2 pi mu_alpha`. This is the cleanest
normalization-independent obstruction: rescaling the root generator rescales
`mu_alpha` inversely, leaving the pairing fixed.

The intersection of the root circle with `SO(3)` is finite. A loop in the
quotient fibre may therefore close after a half-turn, and one may describe
the quotient clutching class with a different integer convention. That does
not weaken the result. If the quotient primitive difference were exact, its
pullback to the group-section cover would be exact; the displayed nonzero
period disproves that possibility.

The exceptional algebraic condition `mu_alpha=0` is exactly an eigenvalue
collision for this root. It lies on the discriminant wall and is excluded
from the regular overlap. Thus the obstruction is not merely generic on the
chosen regular component: it holds at every one of its values.

## What survives and the minimal repair

The common-refinement germ remains correct. On each contractible patch,
relative Darboux normalization supplies an exact primitive gauge and the
complete moment square commutes. Globally:

- the symplectic two-forms glue;
- the moment maps glue equivariantly;
- the local primitives differ by closed one-forms whose Cech--de Rham class
  pairs the torus Chern class with the charge;
- no single-valued generating function exists for this section-generated
  transition at nonzero regular simple-root charge.

The minimal honest continuation is therefore an atlas-valued RSAP candidate
with its torus bundle and primitive cocycle retained. A prequantum line or
other twisting datum could cancel the class only if it is independently
constructed and its integrality and sign match are proved; it cannot be
inserted as a gauge convention.
