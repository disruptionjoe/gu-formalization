This is uniform over all bounded amplitude pairs, including k-dependent pairs. Any fixed polynomial growth in their norms, fixed finite derivative loss, finite time integral, or known polynomial packet-transport factor can also be absorbed. It does not assert a bound on an unbuilt full propagator.

### Inverse phase-charge cost

Suppose the same family supplied |F_k|>=c0 k^-m, c0>0, at large frequencies. Equation(5), for every N>m, forces

    ||a_k|| ||b_k|| >= (c0/C_N) k^(N-m).

For the positive diagonal a=b, the left side is precisely the selected phase charge Q(a). Thus polynomial-size weak coupling requires **superpolynomial phase-charge growth** in this fixed family. Energy cost additionally needs a positive lower bound on omega, which is not assumed. Nonnegative energy alone is not such a gap.

More generally, a polynomial-cost family providing such a source must fail the uniform normal regularity or another stated premise. This is a necessary condition, not construction or physical admission of a replacement family.

## 5. What survives the barrier

- **Rapidly small but nonzero couplings:** O(k^-N) for every N is not exact zero. Local output and its derivatives can be nonzero while their oscillations cancel in bulk integration.
- **Fixed positive physical time:** exp(rho k T) is not polynomial in k. Rapid decay alone cannot decide its competition with a source tail. Even the scalar control `a'_k=rho k a_k+epsilon_k` has opposite fixed-time outcomes for tails exp(-alpha k) with alpha above or below rho T. No such scalar propagator or tail is attributed to the candidate.
- **Boundary and trace sources:** a sharp finite-tube boundary can produce polynomial Fourier tails even from a constant bulk function. Slice/delta sources likewise are not smooth bulk currents or finite-order interior pseudodifferential pullbacks. The theorem keeps supports a fixed distance from the boundary and says nothing about these channels.
- **Nonuniform families:** enlarging the band, varying the tube/normalization with k, admitting genuinely rapid normal matter structure, approaching a singular coefficient, or nonlinear feedback can invalidate the uniform bounds. None is installed or declared source-required here.
- **Source-selected physics:** SC-OP-04/05 supplies field/operator grammar; SC-META-52/53 leaves upstairs boundary handling and positive-state shielding unresolved. Neither requires this fixed lift or robust unrestricted Cauchy evolution. SC-CHI-51/52 does not supply the missing bosonic source-domain bridge. The result does not validate maximal-compact shielding or refute GU.

No further pointwise current census or growing-eigenvector fit can, by itself, repair the failed polynomial-source inference in this fixed family. A fixed-time forced-response claim needs an actual admitted boundary/Green prescription and quantitative source-tail control. Projecting the CK current onto the triangular scalar ansatz would introduce an unowned source restriction and is not performed.
