Lean now proves two related kernels without new axioms.

For any associative ring and element `theta` satisfying `theta * theta = 1`,
the map `x ↦ theta * x * theta` is involutive and multiplicative. It preserves
the associative commutator, so commutators of even/even and odd/odd inputs are
even while an even/odd commutator is odd. This is the algebraic eigenspace core
behind F; it does not prove that the involution is Cartan, that a Killing form
becomes positive, or that the fixed algebra is maximal compact.

For any linear involution `p` over a characteristic-zero field, Lean constructs
the half-sum and half-difference maps `(id+p)/2` and `(id-p)/2`. They sum to the
identity, transform under `p` with signs `+1` and `-1`, are idempotent, and
annihilate each other in both orders. This is the abstract projector core behind
G; it does not identify a GU mirror carrier, choose which half is physical, or
derive any spectral gap.
