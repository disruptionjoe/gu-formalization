Nominal station separation is not the relevant statement. Each trial needs
two distinct relations:

```text
A_setting is spacelike from B_outcome,
B_setting is spacelike from A_outcome.
```

K1026's robust margin is evaluated separately in both directions. The trial
is locality-certified only when both margins are strictly positive. An exact
control passes the first direction and fails the second, showing why a
one-direction audit cannot be silently promoted.

The required record retains, for every trial, the setting-choice and outcome-
registration event intervals plus their calibration provenance on both wings.
This excludes subluminal or luminal setting-to-remote-outcome influence only
inside the supplied Minkowski uncertainty model. It does not establish those
records or a GU-native causal domain.
