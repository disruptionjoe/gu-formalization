## Part 1 — the grade did its job by refusing everything

construction wave were each reviewed against FC-1..FC-7 and each refused.

**The finding that matters: FC-7 was scored MET on first pass in all four
packets and overturned to NOT MET in all four.** That is the direct evidence
that the criterion earned its place — it was added the same day the grade was
installed, before anything had been banked, precisely because FC-1 through
FC-6 test whether a construction is *consistent* and none tests whether it is
*contentful*. Four reviewers, working the other six criteria, passed
prohibitions that do not hold.

FC-1 and FC-2 also failed 4/4: not one object was built with a complete
structure fingerprint, and not one sat at a named descent rung.

### The FC-7 refusal taxonomy

The refusals fall into reusable patterns, and this is the wave's most
transferable output:

- **CIRCULAR** — the prohibition restates the construction's own definition.
  "It forbids objects that do not satisfy it" is not content.
- **INHERITED** — the prohibition is a property of the *carrier*, true with or
  without GU, so the construction adds nothing. (The vertical-density line is
  a line bundle fact for any 4-manifold; what the construction supplies is one
  constant, and that constant is basis-convention dependent.)
- **EMPTY EXTENSION** — no possible violator. Every candidate anyone has
  proposed already satisfies it.
- **ANALYTIC** — a rewording no measurement could falsify. The "perfect fluid
  is thermodynamically visible iff `w != -1`" sharpening is exactly
  `T_ab k^a k^b = (rho+p)(u·k)^2` vanishing on all null `k` iff `rho + p = 0`.
- **DOUBLE-COUNTED** — the same fact booked as the FC-6 demotion condition and
  again as the FC-7 content. A scope boundary cannot also be the content.
- **PRIOR-ART INSTRUMENT** — the prohibition's witness is already the
  repository's own standing control.

### The structural distinction that separates a pass from a fail

The grade already had **one** banked entry when this wave ran, filed by a
concurrent lane about an hour after the method was installed:
`FC-GR-ROTATION-EINSTEIN-WEYL-STATIC-1`, rung R5, row LT-GR11, anchor
EXT-GR-ROTATION. (The wave brief's claim that these would be the program's
first entries was wrong, and the correction is recorded here.)

That entry carries `construction_created_before_anchor: true` — its
construction evidence is dated 2026-07-11 against anchor acceptance on
2026-08-23. **It was built six weeks before the target it was later measured
against.** All four of this wave's objects were assembled against targets
already in hand. The grade's own text makes that decisive: *"every choice must
be frozen before evaluation against a target, inheriting the CBRS
owner-before-evaluation discipline."*

That is the cleanest available statement of what admission requires, and it is
not a criterion anyone can satisfy retroactively.

### A marking irregularity, and the method fix it forces

One packet's FC-4 was substantively clean — no `ASSERTS` claim violated, no
`DISAVOWS` reading relied on — but the packet named **zero** `SC-` ids. The
reviewer supplied the search and scored the criterion met.

That destroys the criterion. FC-3 and FC-4 exist so that *an unchecked claim is
visibly unchecked*; a reviewer who performs the missing search converts an
unchecked claim into a checked one and erases the signal. **Rule, now added to
the grade: a reviewer who supplies a missing FC-3 or FC-4 search records it as
reviewer-supplied and scores the packet as failed.**

## Part 2 — the first B3 register

Bucket B3 has never been used. Six entries now exist, **all surviving the
citation standard, none softening an obligation without relocating it**: two
enlarge GU's obligation, two are two-sided, one withdraws a target GU could
accidentally hit while keeping the one it already fails, one narrows a
comparator-side premise while adding a joint-evaluation constraint. **Zero are
net-softening.**

The two that enlarge are worth stating plainly, because they run against the
direction anyone would expect from a "tension" register:

- **B3-J95-03 (LT-GR8, EXT-J95).** What is measured: **nothing.** No Hawking
  radiation, no Unruh effect, no measured horizon entropy, no tested first
  law. Jacobson 1995 is a conditional theorem and each premise is a
  hypothesis. This changes the *type* of the obligation, not its size — and it
  **tightens** it, because Eling-Guedens-Jacobson establish that
  curvature-corrected entropy needs a nonequilibrium treatment, while LT-GR3
  books a Weyl/Bach sector, so the equilibrium branch is presumptively
  unavailable. Stated as *presumptively* closed pending an area-exactness
  demonstration: the entry that enlarges an obligation must itself be exact.
- **B3-H0-06 (EXT-COSMO-BACKGROUND, LT-GR2e).** No published `H_0` is
  measured; every one is derived, on both sides. The obligation is therefore
  the measured angles and ratios plus GU's *own* sound horizon — compute
  `theta*`, `r_d` and `D(z)/r_d` from an action-owned background. **The Hubble
  tension supplies GU no relief.**

**B3-DE-01 discloses that it cuts both ways**, which is the discipline working.
Withdrawing the CPL central pair as a target also withdraws the CPL-based
argument *against* GU recorded in the comparative-tensions ledger. Undisclosed
that reads as one-sided relief; disclosed, it is exactly the relocation B3 is
for — the lean-against moves onto the pointwise object where the recorded kill
already lives.

### The maintenance rule the register must carry

`sigma_8`/`S_8` was refused as an entry, and it is the cautionary case:
KiDS-Legacy now agrees with Planck at `0.73 sigma`. **A register built a year
ago would today be holding `S_8` as a live excuse.** Entries are therefore
re-checked **at citation time, not filing time** — a rule this deliverable
demonstrated on itself.
