## The information-preserving taxonomy

Every canonical row has `verdict`, `reason_kind`, `distance`, and
`revival_trigger`. If a new row does not fit, `NEW_KIND_REQUIRED` fires; a
forced fit is forbidden.

| verdict | current kinds | why the split matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SAME` | `DERIVED`, `DERIVED_CONDITIONAL`, `DERIVED_PARTIAL`, `IMPORTED`, `PUT_IN` | derived agreement is evidence; imported agreement is compatibility; put-in agreement is construction bookkeeping |
| `DIFFERS` | `PREDICTION`, `CONVENTION_ARTIFACT`, `STATED_INERT`, `STRUCTURAL_DIFFERENCE`, `ROUTE_KILLED` | a prediction should be tested; a convention should be repaired; an inert statement should not consume Build effort |
| `NEEDS` | `ONE_BIT`, `FINITE_CHOICE`, `REAL_PARAMETER`, `INTEGER_DATUM`, `EXTERNAL_DATUM`, `MISSING_CONSTRUCTION`, `PROVEN_UNSUPPLYABLE` | one-bit adjudication and a missing BV complex are opposite-sized jobs; an object proven unavailable from current machinery is a no-go on that supply route |
| `OVER_DETERMINED` | `GENUINE_FALSIFICATION`, `FORK_ARTIFACT`, `SCOPE_ERROR`, `STALE_PREMISE` | only the first is direct adverse evidence; the other three redirect work without being explained away |
