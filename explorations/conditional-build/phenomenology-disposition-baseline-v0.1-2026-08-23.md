## Result

The exhaustion rule is now executable and it evaluates **false**.

The prior method named four buckets and said B1, B3 and B4 must be exhausted
before B2/source-action work. It did not name the complete denominator, define
how mixed rows split, distinguish a B3 subdisposition from a row terminal, or
bind the result to the current ledger digest. No program could prove or refute
exhaustion from that surface.

The live register now covers every row of conditional-physics ledger v0.263
exactly once. It recognizes only three evidence-backed terminal row outcomes:

| row | bucket | terminal outcome | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LT-GR9 | B2 | `B2_NAMED_REQUIREMENT` | SF-1 through SF-5 |
| LT-GR10 | B2 | `B2_NAMED_REQUIREMENT` | PPN-1 through PPN-4 |
| LT-GR11 | B1 | `FITTING_CONSTRUCTION` | `FC-GR-ROTATION-EINSTEIN-WEYL-STATIC-1` |

That leaves **88 open rows**. The open denominator is not a claim that those
rows have never been researched. It is the narrower and checkable fact that
they have not received a terminal outcome under the active disposition method.

## Why the ledger verdict cannot supply the missing disposition

- The 34 `SAME` rows are not automatically B1. B1 requires FC-1 through FC-7,
  including chronology, source/prior-art search, pathway and demotion evidence.
  Agreement at a ledger grade is not retroactive fitting-construction
  admission.
- The 29 open `NEEDS` rows are not automatically B2. A named source owner,
  coefficient or external datum must be proved, and a mixed row must be split
  before its parts can take different buckets.
- The 22 `DIFFERS` rows are not automatically B4 or precise impossibilities.
  Each needs the source-native locus or a typed class-relative impossibility.
- The three `OVER_DETERMINED` rows still require a stale-premise, fork or
  precise-impossibility disposition. Their verdict does not bypass the method.

## Executable rule

B2/source-action selection is eligible only when a current live register:

1. matches the canonical ledger digest;
2. covers every row exactly once and preserves every required split parent;
3. gives every row, or all of its subclaims, one of
   `FITTING_CONSTRUCTION`, `PRECISE_IMPOSSIBILITY` or
   `B2_NAMED_REQUIREMENT`; and
4. derives `exhausted` and `b2_selectable` from those facts.

The live v0.1 evaluation is:

```text
denominator rows                 91
terminal rows                     3
open rows                        88
completed B3 subdispositions      3
pending B3 subdispositions        3
exhausted                     false
B2 selectable                 false
```

A new ledger version makes this register stale until explicit rebase. Missing,
duplicate, uncovered, falsely terminal or manually inconsistent state fails
closed.

## Hostile review

- **Strongest overclaim:** “88 open” means 88 unattempted scientific problems.
  It does not. It means 88 rows lack this method's terminal evidence; many
  contain substantial prior computation and bounded negative results.
- **Strongest contrary reading:** `SAME` already means a fit. It means
  agreement at the row's stated grade, but FC exists specifically to prevent
  that agreement from being laundered into a frozen fitting construction.
- **Weakest seam:** the denominator is version-bound. The gate is trustworthy
  only while the v0.263 digest matches; the probe therefore treats successor
  ledger state as a hard currency failure, not as silent inheritance.

## Consequence

The exhaustion rule can now govern selection because its answer is computed,
not presumed. Its current answer is **not exhausted**. Phenomenology
disposition remains the work; B2/source-action work is not yet eligible. No
ledger row, scientific verdict, source owner, prediction, confirmation, canon
or public posture changes.
