## What the added approaches reveal

### 1. Schuller changes the order of work

The phrase "direct action energy" could not be verified as the standard name
of Frederic Schuller's programme. The primary-source programme is
**constructive gravity**: prescribe the matter dynamics, then calculate or
constrain the compatible gravitational Lagrangian. Schuller states the basic
programme directly; the covariant extension explicitly adds common causal
initial-data compatibility. That makes H2 a selection gate for H1, not a rival
ontology. See [Schuller, *Constructive Gravity: Foundations and Applications*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.09726)
and [Reinhart and Alex, *Covariant Constructive Gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.00168).

For GU the concrete transfer is:

```text
owned matter principal polynomial
    + diffeomorphism covariance
    + shared causal initial surfaces
    => admissible coefficients/operators for S[A,R]
```

That calculation has not been performed.

### 2. Indivisible processes relocate superposition again

Barandes's current formulation represents suitable indivisible,
non-Markovian stochastic laws in Hilbert-space language and treats wave
functions as secondary representation data. The reported dictionary is exact
for the systems satisfying its hypotheses; GU has supplied none of its needed
transition matrices. See [Barandes, *Quantum Systems as Indivisible Stochastic Processes*](https://philsci-archive.pitt.edu/26042/1/Quantum_Systems_as_Indivisible_Stochastic_Processes06.pdf).

This gives a precise alternative to "the complex structure is superposition":

```text
normal/reduction dynamics
    -> observed indivisible transition law Gamma(t <- t0)
    -> Hilbert dilation/representation
    -> amplitudes and superposition
```

No arrow in this GU-specific chain is constructed. The idea is nevertheless
valuable because it makes the amplitude question an explicit transition-law
question.

### 3. Non-Markovian projection gives the first honest rate route

Mori--Zwanzig and related projection methods show how eliminating unresolved
variables can yield exact drift, memory and fluctuating terms. Modern work also
warns that well-posedness of the orthogonal dynamics is not automatic. This is
a much stronger route than declaring `decoherence proportional to |II|^2`.
The GU task is to derive the memory kernel from the action-owned normal sector,
not fit it. See [Widder and Schilling, *Generalised Langevin Dynamics*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20453)
and the open-quantum review [Breuer et al., *Non-Markovian dynamics in open quantum systems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01385).

### 4. Mannheim supplies a comparator, not an identity

The exact contact is Bach-flatness of the untwisted observed-base twistor
detour. Mannheim's programme instead treats conformal gravity and its
fourth-order dynamics as fundamental and advocates a PT-symmetric resolution
of the associated state-space problem. GU's full-`II` candidate and coupled
reduction current are not the same action. The comparison suggests a precise
test of the Bach/PT horn; it does not let GU inherit Mannheim's claims. See
[Mannheim, *Making the Case for Conformal Gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.2186).

### 5. Port-Hamiltonian PDEs connect the neglected boundary

Stokes--Dirac/port-Hamiltonian PDE theory organizes a formally skew bulk
operator, boundary trace spaces and an exact power balance. That is unusually
well matched to GU's simultaneous bulk current and unresolved charged endpoint.
It suggests that the coupled complex should be built with its Green/BFV ports
from the start. The transfer remains conditional until the GU trace spaces and
power balance are proved. See [Brugnoli, Haine and Matignon, *Stokes-Dirac structures for distributed parameter port-Hamiltonian systems*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08816).

### 6. Woit clarifies the Euclidean horn and the name

No relevant twistor-physics work by **Peter Voigt** was located. The context
matches **Peter Woit**. His 2026 work emphasizes that Wick rotating Weyl
spinors is structurally nontrivial, strengthening the requirement that H6
supply an explicit real-time reconstruction rather than treating Euclidean
continuation as innocent. See [Woit's 2026 Euclidean-twistor sequence](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~woit/wordpress/?cat=31)
and [*Euclidean Twistor Unification*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.05099).

### 7. Factorization/network language tests the pointwise mistake

Factorization algebras provide a genuine local-to-global mathematical model of
observables in QFT; network/MMO language is only its rough engineering rhyme.
Both insist that a local point or plane is not the global state. A GU
factorization algebra remains unbuilt. See [Costello and Gwilliam,
*Factorization algebra*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06137).

## Strongest synthesis

The best current steelman is therefore:

> GU superposition, if GU derives it at all, is not the existence of a
> pointwise `J`. It is the linear structure on a positive physical cohomology
> of the action-selected coupled connection/reduction complex, expressed
> twistorially on the observed Lorentzian carrier. Eliminating normal modes may
> then represent that cohomology operationally as an indivisible or
> non-Markovian observed process.

The sentence contains three separable burdens:

1. **action burden — H2:** construct the causally compatible action owner;
2. **state-space burden — H1:** construct the coupled physical complex and
   positive pairing;
3. **observable burden — H3:** derive the reduced process, memory kernel,
   channel and coherence functional.

Failure at any burden returns to H0 rather than being repaired by analogy.
