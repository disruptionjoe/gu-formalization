## The carrier-neutral core

A finite-dimensional generalized probabilistic theory can be stated without
choosing quantum mechanics. Subnormalized states live in a generating positive
cone of a real ordered vector space. A strictly positive order unit gives
normalization and deterministic discard. Effects are positive dual
functionals between zero and that unit. Interventions are positive
subnormalizing maps grouped into instruments whose nonselective sum preserves
normalization. A composite rule supplies local embeddings and marginal maps.

That syntax captures convex mixing, normalized nonnegative events,
interventions and the statement of no-signalling. It does **not** determine:

- a complex Hilbert carrier or positive-semidefinite cone;
- the Born trace pairing;
- tensor-product composition or complete positivity;
- which nonclassical composites exist;
- the quantum rather than superquantum correlation set;
- unitary phase transport or CPTP decoherence; or
- a spacetime causal domain and common-future record comparison.

QD-R1-2 therefore contains a genuine GPT core—positive cone, deterministic
unit and effect interval—but its physical quotient and pairing remain
theory-selecting. QD-R1-1, QD-R1-3 and QD-R1-4 require additional composite,
causal and dynamical structure. QD-R1-5 is a quotient-compatibility schema: all
effects and admissible transformations must descend, but GPT syntax cannot
name the GU gauge relation.
