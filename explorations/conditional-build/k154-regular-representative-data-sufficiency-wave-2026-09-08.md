## 1. Chart and domain data do not determine the regular matrix

Fix one charge projection `Q`, a boundedly invertible chart `U` commuting with
`Q`, and a self-adjoint semibounded regular representative `R` on domain `D`.
For a normalized `phi in D cap Ran(Q)` and real `t`, put

```text
K_t=t |phi><phi|,       R_t=R+K_t,       H_t=U* R_t U.       (1)
```

`K_t` is bounded, self-adjoint and charge preserving. Kato--Rellich therefore
leaves `D`, self-adjointness and semiboundedness intact; `U`, its contraction
bound, the conforming domain `U^-1 D`, the charge decomposition, inverse tails
and Gram conditioning are unchanged. But

```text
r_t(phi,phi)=r(phi,phi)+t,
R_t phi=R phi+t phi.                                      (2)
```

Thus the *serialized chart/domain/charge facts* do not determine even the
first regular form entry or action column. Equation (1) changes the physical
operator, so it does not prove physical extension ambiguity. It proves that a
consumer cannot recover the fixed operator's omitted coefficients from data
that are equally compatible with both models.

## 2. One exact seed and an HVZ edge do not determine the next floor

The obstruction remains after a seed form and action are supplied. On a
reducing charge carrier with orthonormal vectors `e_0,e_1,...`, compare

```text
R_delta e_0=0,
R_delta e_1=delta e_1,       0<delta<tau_ess,
sigma_ess(R_delta)=[tau_ess,infinity).                    (3)
```

Every member of (3) has the same exact ground seed form, action and zero
residual, and the same essential edge. Its next distinct spectral point is
`delta`, which can be arbitrarily close to zero. A bounded rank-one change on
`e_1` moves `delta` without touching the seed. Therefore

```text

sigma(R)\sigma_ground subset [tau_ess,infinity).          (4)
```

certified spectral count below `b`, or an equivalent lower bound for `R` (and
after chart transport, `H`) on the orthogonal complement of the complete
ground eigenspace.
