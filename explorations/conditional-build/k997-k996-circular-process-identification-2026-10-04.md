# K997 circular process identification

Classification: `INTERNAL_CONDITIONAL_MATHEMATICS`.

```gu-typed-objects
result: identification of a supplied circle-valued independent-increment process from all-time integer harmonics
carrier: circle-valued stochastic paths starting at the identity LAYER=observed CHIRALITY=N/A
pairing: expectation against circular increment laws ON=repository_stochastic_model
real_structure: conjugate Fourier coefficients at harmonics n and -n
grading: time increment and integer Fourier harmonic
action_owner: UNTYPED -- no GU action owns the generator clock or process
target: finite-dimensional circular process laws MAP-TYPE=evaluation
```

For a stationary independent-increment process on the circle, all integer
harmonics at each time determine each increment law by K996. Independent
increments then determine every finite-dimensional law by iterating those
increment kernels. Thus all-time harmonic semigroup data identify the complete
circular process.

One-time data are insufficient. At `T=2`, deterministic drifts `a=0` and
`a=pi` have identical terminal circle laws because the second winds once, but
they differ at `t=1`. Equivalently, taking a logarithm of one terminal Fourier
table does not select the generator branch. The positive theorem therefore
requires the all-time semigroup or an independently owned generator. It does
not select a real-valued lift.
