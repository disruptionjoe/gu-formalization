# Quantum-anchor reverse-scaffold interface wave

Massive matter-wave interference and spacelike Bell/no-signalling jointly
force a sharper conditional interface than either anchor alone. Matter-wave
interference requires a phase-bearing coherent-alternative carrier that is not
equivalent to a classical mixture, together with recombination and an
environmental decoherence map. Bell violation requires a nonfactorizable joint
state and context-indexed local effects. No-signalling simultaneously requires
every nonselective local instrument to preserve the remote marginal, even
though outcome-conditioned joint records remain correlated.

An exact two-qubit control realizes all of those demands at once. The coherent
plus/minus states are perfectly distinguished by recombination; their classical
mixture gives probability `1/2`. A dephasing channel multiplies fringe
visibility by the exact parameter `eta` while preserving populations and
normalization. The Bell state with `X/Z` and rotated `(Z +/- X)/sqrt(2)`
contexts gives CHSH `2 sqrt(2)`, while exhaustive deterministic local response
assignments are bounded by `2`. Nonselective local dephasing changes the joint
state but leaves the remote reduced state exactly `I/2`.

This is a conditional standard-quantum witness, not a GU construction. Complex
linear state space, density operators, the Born pairing, tensor-product
composition, completely positive instruments, partial trace, the dephasing
model, spacelike factorization, classical records and the causal common future
are all explicitly imported or assumed. The two calibration anchors earn zero
prediction or confirmation credit. Delayed-choice entanglement swapping is
reserved as a distinct multipartite held-out family and is not scored here.

## R6 and R5: frozen observations and operational demands

The anchor facts are frozen only at their admitted empirical scope. For the
matter-wave anchor, the demanded operations are preparation of coherent
alternatives, controllable phase transport, recombination, centre-of-mass
composition and environmental record coupling. For the Bell anchor, they are
distributed preparation, independent context choice, local outcome recording,
joint-probability comparison, contextual correlation and invariant remote
nonselective marginals.

Three demands are shared: normalized nonnegative event probabilities,
composable preparations/interventions, and outcome instruments whose
nonselective sum preserves normalization. The joint constraint is important:
no-signalling alone admits a classical local simplex, while Bell violation
excludes every factorized local-response model on that simplex. Interference
alone distinguishes coherent alternatives from classical mixtures but does not
force spatial nonfactorizability. The two anchors therefore close different
loopholes in one interface.

## R4 and R3: conditional state, event, composition and record interface

The exact witness uses positive trace-one states, effects between zero and the
identity, the trace pairing, tensor composition, and completely positive
outcome maps whose sum is trace preserving. A record consists of an outcome
label and a post-intervention state. Nonselective remote-marginal invariance is
an instrument-level law; it is stronger and more reusable than checking one
fixed pair of measurements.

The decoherence interface is a trace-preserving dephasing channel. It suppresses
the off-diagonal phase carrier continuously through `eta` and leaves the
diagonal populations fixed. Nothing in the anchor licenses a fundamental
mass/size cutoff or identifies `eta` with a GU coefficient. A future model must
construct its environment, coupling, reduced state and visibility functional
rather than borrow this control's parameter.

The GU gauge quotient is sharply obstructed at the present carrier. A valid
quotient would need one equivalence relation, effects constant on classes,
representative-independent positivity/normalization, and descent of every
intervention and dynamics map. GU currently supplies no action-owned gauge
generator, common analytic domain or physical-effect algebra that can own
those conditions. The interface records the burden without pretending to have
built the quotient.

## R2: causal and dynamical descent

The constructed interface propagates six requirements backward:

1. reversible phase-preserving evolution before recombination;
2. state-preserving open evolution with a controlled decoherence limit;
3. nonfactorizable composite dynamics compatible with local instruments;
4. remote-marginal invariance for every nonselective local instrument;
5. local record creation with comparison only in a named common causal future;
6. preservation of normalization, positivity and composition under evolution.

The causal statement is operational. It does not choose a tensor factorization
for GU, prove microcausality, build a local net or identify a physical light
cone. If a future GU carrier is non-factorizing, the fallback route is an
algebraic-locality formulation in terms of commuting operation algebras and
state restrictions. The switch condition is a typed failure of subsystem
factorization, not aesthetic preference.

## R1: demand-derived action requirements

No action candidate is selected. Any later candidate must cite the frozen
demands that require all of the following: coherent and nonfactorizable state
carriers; a variational/constraint descent to a normalized positive event
pairing; a domain and principal dynamics supporting local interventions and
remote-marginal invariance; one dynamics compatible with reversible phase
transport and open-system decoherence; and gauge identifications through which
effects, interventions and evolution descend.

This demand lineage prevents an action-shaped expression from entering the
queue merely because it resembles a familiar quantum action. Source
authentication may later decide attribution; it does not supply these
constructions.

## Preflight bookend

The route census compared operator-algebraic, generalized-probabilistic,
algebraic-QFT, causal-inference, quantum-information, open-system,
compositional and exact-computation routes. A minimal density-operator witness
won because it decides all current anchor constraints with exact arithmetic
and exposes every import. A generalized-probabilistic formulation is the
strongest next abstraction because it can test which demands survive without
complex Hilbert space. Broad numerical fitting was rejected: these anchors
admit exact finite discriminators, and fitting calibration data would carry no
credit.

The first switch is failure of tensor-factor subsystem semantics on a typed GU
carrier, which moves the locality packet to commuting local algebras. The first
kill is any candidate whose nonselective local operations change a spacelike
remote marginal or whose coherent-alternative state collapses to the classical
mixture before an environment is introduced.

## Postflight bookend

- Strongest overclaim: the exact witness may be mistaken for recovered quantum
  mechanics. It is only an imported standard-quantum control and requirement
  generator.
- Strongest contrary construction: a local hidden-variable simplex preserves
  no-signalling, but exhaustive deterministic assignments satisfy CHSH at most
  `2`; it cannot carry the second anchor.
- Weakest reproducibility seam: the exact probe certifies a finite tensor
  product, not arbitrary dimensions, quantum fields, curved spacetime or a GU
  quotient. Those extensions remain named burdens.

The exact probe passes its positive controls before foreground assertions and
catches planted loss of coherence, factorized Bell state, remote signalling,
unphysical decoherence amplification, reversed search direction, calibration
credit, holdout scoring, missing instrument no-signalling, premature action
selection and false GU ownership of an imported postulate. No target claim,
canon surface, paper, prediction, confirmation or public posture is promoted.
