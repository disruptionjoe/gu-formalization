The reason is representation-theoretic, not numerical.  The connected Spin
parent is semisimple, and the derivative of the determinant character is the
trace of the infinitesimal representation.  Every spin generator is a
Clifford commutator and has trace zero.  Therefore

```text
det rho(g) = 1
```

on the connected Spin parent, for the full and both half-spin
representations.  The exact real `Cl(7,7)` bank verifies all `91` generators,
including zero trace on each `64`-dimensional chirality eigenspace.

change the topology of the vector bundle being framed.  It permits many more
frames and connections, but its associated determinant line is still
`det(S)`.  Consequently a unitary connection may have pointwise nonzero trace
curvature, but on this trivial line that curvature is globally exact and its
integral over every closed two-cycle is zero.  Observation pullback preserves
the trivial line and `c1=0`.

## Exact character theorem on the actual K77 carrier

Let `rho` be the complex full-spin or half-spin representation of the connected
`Spin(7,7)` reduction.  For `X` in `spin(7,7)`,

```text
d(det rho)_e(X) = trace(d rho(X)).
```

The Lie algebra is semisimple, so every finite-dimensional representation is
trace-free.  Concretely, the spin representation is generated by
`gamma_a gamma_b` for `a<b`.  On the exact real `128 x 128` K77 bank:

- all `91` generators have trace zero;
- all commute with the real volume grading;
- their traces on both `64`-dimensional grading eigenspaces are zero.

Hence the determinant character has zero differential.  It equals one at the
identity, so it is identically one on the connected group.  The associated
determinant lines of `S+`, `S-`, and `S+ direct-sum S-` are trivial.  This is a
global associated-bundle theorem, not a statement that every possible
`U(64,64)` principal bundle is trivial.

If the source intended a genuinely independent `U(64,64)` bundle rather than
the unitary spinor-frame bundle of `S`, its determinant topology would be new
bundle data and this theorem would not decide it.  The checked source does not
provide that independent twist.

## The 128th-root qualification

The scalar center and determinant line are related but not identical.  If an
independent scalar line `L` twists the spinor bundle, then

```text
det(S tensor L) = det(S) tensor L^128,
c1(det(S tensor L)) = 128 c1(L).
```

Thus determinant triviality does not detect an `L` whose class is killed by
128.  In particular the `Z/2` class associated with the Lorentz-fibre spine
could be invisible after taking the 128th power.  Installing such an `L`, or
lifting the unitary frame bundle to a chosen scalar root, is extra global data;
it is not supplied by the connected Spin representation.

A separate finite possibility also exists on a topologically trivial vector
bundle: a flat nonabelian connection can have holonomy in the discrete center
`mu_128` even though the determinant holonomy is trivial.  This is not smooth
central curvature flux.  It is a representation of the fundamental group and
must be tested as holonomy/domain data.
