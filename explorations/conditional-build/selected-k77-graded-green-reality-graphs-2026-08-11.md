## Exact graded theorem

Let `A` be the normal coefficient and order the independent odd boundary
variables as `(psi,bar)`. Their field-space differentials commute, so the
fermionic sector of the graded-even Green form is represented by

```text
G_A = [[0,A^T],[A,0]],       G_A^T=G_A.
```

The graph `bar=P conjugate(psi)` has column matrix `L_P=[I;P]`. Its restriction
is

```text
L_P^T G_A L_P = P^T A + A^T P.                  (1)
```

For the symmetric/anti-adjoint horn, `(1)` vanishes by
`P A + A^T P=0`. For the skew/self-adjoint horn, it vanishes because
`P^T=-P` and `P A-A^T P=0`. When `A` is invertible, `G_A` is nondegenerate and
the graph has half dimension, hence it is graded Lagrangian.

The anti-linear exchange

```text
R_P(psi,bar) = (P^-1 conjugate(bar), P conjugate(psi))
```

squares to one, fixes exactly this graph and satisfies
`R_P^T G_A R_P=-G_A` in both horns. A mismatched pairing fails `(1)`, its graph
is non-isotropic, and a singular characteristic `A` degenerates `G_A`; all
three controls fire. A fourth control confirms that the valid graded skew
graph still fails the old even comparator, proving that the two tests are not
being conflated.

## Moving frame and overlap

For a frame transition `T`, use

```text
psi -> T psi,       bar -> T^-T bar,
A -> T A T^-1,      P -> T^-T P T^-1.
```

Then `G_A`, equation `(1)`, the graph carrier and `R_P` all transport
tensorially. An exact noncommuting three-patch rational cocycle passes for both
horns. Differentiating the tensor identity also transports a moving family of
isotropic graphs.

This is not the complete moving-field theorem: variations of the bosonic
geometry can contribute mixed terms to the total preboundary form. The result
is exact for fixed/Dirichlet normal geometry and for frame transport of the
family; unrestricted mixed variations remain a named successor.
