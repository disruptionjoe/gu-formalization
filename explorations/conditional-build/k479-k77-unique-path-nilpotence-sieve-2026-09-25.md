## Theorem

two factors are declared nonzero. Exactly one such path leaves a single
nonzero product, so that entry cannot vanish over a field. The support is
therefore incompatible with nilpotence. Zero paths are already zero; two or
more paths merely allow cancellation and do not prove it.

The hostile control contains one unique path and is rejected. A dense
two-middle support gives two paths for all four target-source pairs and passes
only as `nilpotence possible`; it does not solve coefficients.
