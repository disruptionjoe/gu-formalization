## What moved

The physical coefficient in the fermion Green form is now typed as the normal
principal symbol of the complete first-order four-field operator. It is not
the bosonic `E_B-E_T` bank, the residual Riesz map, or v0.165's scalar finite
comparator.

The symbol is a global real-K77 associated-bundle morphism. On
noncharacteristic conormals it is invertible, and the v0.166 Darboux atlas
glues with the transition-owned cotangent momentum shift. On null conormals
the actual complete symbol has rank `1024` and kernel `896`; the requirement
that one global coefficient be invertible on every causal stratum is killed.

This closes two construction conditions and opens a more precise analytic
split. The noncharacteristic route needs a Cauchy/maximal-dissipative closed
domain unless an elliptic Calderon problem is separately owned. The null route
needs a characteristic relation and BFV/edge completion. Neither selects among
at least 120 graph coordinates.
