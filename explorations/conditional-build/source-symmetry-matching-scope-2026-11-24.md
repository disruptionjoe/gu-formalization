# Source total symmetries and the anomaly prerequisite: finite audit

**None of the audited source assertions currently establishes all prerequisites for a nonzero observed-sector anomaly-matching target.** The SM gauge test is well posed as an empirical target and gives zero for either 15 or 16 Weyl components per family. ## 2. Global group and transformation distinctions

For the standard orthogonal splitting, the embedded maximal compact subgroup of Spin(6,4) is the image

    [Spin(6) × Spin(4)] / diagonal Z2
      = [SU(4) × SU(2)L × SU(2)R] / <(−I4,−I2,−I2)>.

The covering product is often used in representation notation. Anomaly tests must retain whether only lifted bundles are admitted or the full quotient background category is allowed. Similarly the SM can have quotients by Z_q, q=1,2,3,6; source local branching does not alone settle global observation/quantum bundle access.

The 16 restricts to `(4,2,1) + (bar4,1,2)` in all-left-handed notation, and the diagonal kernel acts trivially. Deleting only ν^c leaves 15 SM components but **not** a representation of unbroken PS: the missing state shares an SU(2)R doublet and an SU(4) multiplet. ### SM only: zero either way

Per family, `SU(3)^3`, `SU(3)^2Y`, `SU(2)^2Y`, `Y^3` and mixed gravitational-Y sums vanish. There are four weak doublets (three colors plus lepton), so the familiar SU(2) mod2 anomaly vanishes. ν^c is an SM singlet and changes none of these statements. Ordinary four-dimensional spin backgrounds add no pure-gravity chiral anomaly here. Hence both 15 and 16 are anomaly-free for the ordinary SM gauge target. Global SM forms and their bordism categories are treated explicitly by [Davighi–Gripaios–Lohitsiri, §§4,6](https://arxiv.org/html/1910.11277v3).

## 4. What matching would prove if its inputs were supplied

In a fixed four-dimensional quantum theory with exact symmetry H and a defined background category, the anomaly is RG invariant. If the low-energy theory separates into observed and hidden sectors, plus possible Wess–Zumino/inflow/topological terms,

    alpha_UV restricted to H
       = alpha_observed + alpha_hidden + alpha_remaining_terms.

An observed nonzero **global** 't Hooft anomaly is not an inconsistency by itself. It constrains the rest of the theory and excludes a symmetry-preserving, fully trivial gapped completion under the relevant hypotheses. Gauge anomalies must cancel in a consistent stand-alone gauge theory; inflow and extra sectors alter the full system to which cancellation is applied. Merely calling the hidden matter dark or decoupled does not erase its anomaly. Nor may a heavy charged fermion be removed without its residual effective terms or the symmetry breaking that permits its mass.

SC-CHI-01/53 explicitly retain a hidden half, so that is a source-compatible matching channel, not an added rescue by default. To falsify the claim through matching one must prove that every source-admitted completion lacks the required response. The present source does not require that half to be a trivial symmetric gapped vacuum.

Also, the source's total fields live on Y14. A conventional fourteen-dimensional chiral anomaly would use I16 and a fifteen-dimensional anomaly theory; a four-dimensional chiral anomaly uses I6 and a five-dimensional theory. Section observation is not ordinary same-dimensional RG flow. A physical reduction/pushforward/defect construction with common domains and its anomaly map is required before equating these. We have not constructed it, and are not permitted to build a missing source operator to do so.
