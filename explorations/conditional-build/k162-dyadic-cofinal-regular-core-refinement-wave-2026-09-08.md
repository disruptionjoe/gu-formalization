## 1. A literal nested K141 momentum-cell family

For `j>=0`, put

```text

I_(j,k)=[k delta_j,(k+1)delta_j),
-2^(2j) <= k < 2^(2j).                                  (1)
```

Let `V_j` be the span of the **unnormalized** indicators `chi_(j,k)` of these
cells. The next physical window contains the current one and every coarse cell
is the disjoint union of two fine cells:

```text
chi_(j,k)=chi_(j+1,2k)+chi_(j+1,2k+1).                 (2)
```

Therefore `V_j subset V_(j+1)` literally inside `L2(R)`. The mesh tends to
zero and the cutoff tends to infinity, so the union is dense in `L2(R)` and,
after graph projection of compactly supported smooth functions, in the
`sqrt(1+p^2)` multiplication graph core used by K141.

Using unnormalized cells avoids algebraic square roots. Their Gram matrices
are

```text

```

If `J_j` is the zero-one refinement matrix from (2), each column has its two
disjoint children and

```text

```
