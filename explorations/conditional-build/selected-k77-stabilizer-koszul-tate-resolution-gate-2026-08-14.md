# Selected K77 stabilizer-aware Koszul--Tate resolution gate

## Result first

The frozen selected distortion has a mathematically proper reducible
constraint model on its **single homogeneous gauge orbit**.  Its exact
infinitesimal sequence is

```text
0 -> h_21 -> so(7,7)_91 -> T(Spin(7,7)/H)_70 -> 0,
H = SO(3,4).
```

The 21 stabilizer generators are precisely the first-stage relations among
the 91 labelled moment-map components.  There is no higher linear
reducibility.  In a local complement tangent basis the Koszul--Tate
differential has the normal form

```text
delta b_m = p_m,
delta b_h = 0,
delta beta_h = b_h.
```

The 70 momenta are a regular coordinate sequence, while the 21
`(beta_h,b_h)` pairs are contractible.  Hence the complex is acyclic above
degree zero and resolves the cotangent zero section.  Globally, the correct
minimal ghosts live in the tangent bundle of the homogeneous orbit, not in a
fixed 70-dimensional Lie algebra.

This resolves the predecessor's **Koszul--Tate properness problem on that
orbit**, but it does not admit the actual selected-action endpoint.  That
endpoint annihilates all 21 distortion-stabilizer directions and therefore is
a legitimate covector on the orbit, yet it has 30 nonzero components on the
70 independent orbit directions.  Because the action is transitive, the full
moment-map zero level in `T*(G/H)` is exactly the cotangent zero section.  The
selected endpoint is not on it.

The physical frontier therefore changes.  One must now derive either:

1. a source/action-owned boundary condition that makes the endpoint charge
   vanish; or
2. a compensating edge momentum whose moment map cancels it.

Without one of those owners, imposing zero would discard the action-derived
endpoint rather than reduce it.

## The exact homogeneous-orbit sequence

Let `T` be the frozen selected distortion and let

```text
O_T = Spin(7,7) . T.
```

The exact action bank has rank 70.  Its kernel is the 21-dimensional odd-axis
`so(3,4)` algebra `h`.  All non-stabilizer bivectors form a 70-dimensional
`h`-invariant complement `m`, giving

```text

```

Thus `O_T=G/H`, the moving stabilizers form `G times_H h`, and
`TO_T=G times_H m`.  This globalization costs no new configuration field:

## Why 70 fixed ghosts are not enough

The natural complement is reductive but not a Lie subalgebra.  Exact bracket
projection gives

```text

```

with image spanning all 21 directions of `h`.  This is the curvature of the
natural homogeneous connection.  Therefore one cannot replace the 91-ghost
system by a fixed 70-generator Lie algebra.

There are two honest presentations:

- the global minimal presentation with ghosts valued in `TO_T`; or
- a fixed-labelled reducible `91+21` presentation carrying the connection and
  curvature terms generated by the moving stabilizer bundle.


## Endpoint admission

For the exact action endpoint `P=E_B-E_T`,

```text
J_X(T,P)=P([X,T]).
```

All 21 `h` components vanish because `[h,T]=0`.  Thirty of the 70 independent
orbit components are nonzero.  Thus `P` descends to `T*O_T`, but


Since the `G` action on `O_T` is transitive, `J(T,P)=0` iff `P=0`.  A
zero-charge boundary condition would therefore remove the current endpoint.
A compensating edge phase space could instead carry `J_edge=-J_endpoint`, but
no such source/action-owned object has been constructed.
