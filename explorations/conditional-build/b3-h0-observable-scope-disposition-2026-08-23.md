## Disposition

`B3-H0-06` is **disposed with no ledger movement**. Its conclusion survives:
the Hubble tension supplies GU no relief from constructing its own cosmology.
Its filing slogan does not survive source review. “No published `H_0` is
measured; every one is derived” collapses three materially different
evidential maps into one word and understates the empirical content of the
local distance ladder.

The source-faithful statement is:

```text
H0 is an inferred parameter in every route,
but the measured inputs and inference maps are not interchangeable:
  local ladder = geometric anchors + calibrated Cepheids/SNe + redshifts;
  CMB = acoustic spectra/angle + a cosmological model;
  BAO = peak positions -> distances in units of the sound horizon.
```

This is a precise impossibility result about the proposed binary slogan. The
fact that all routes contain inference cannot turn calibrated observations
into “nothing measured,” and the empirical status of the distance ladder
cannot turn Planck or BAO into direct, model-free observations of `H_0`.

## The three primary objects

### 1. Local distance ladder: an empirical calibrated determination

Adam G. Riess et al., “A Comprehensive Measurement of the Local Value of the
Hubble Constant with 1 km/s/Mpc Uncertainty from the Hubble Space Telescope
and the SH0ES Team,” *The Astrophysical Journal Letters* **934** (2022) L7,
[arXiv:2112.04510v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04510),
[doi:10.3847/2041-8213/ac5c5b](https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/ac5c5b).

The measured inputs include geometric calibration from Gaia parallaxes, the
NGC 4258 maser and LMC detached eclipsing binaries; Cepheid periods and fluxes
in anchors and supernova hosts; Type Ia supernova fluxes; and Hubble-flow
redshifts. The ladder calibrates the Cepheid relation, then the supernova
absolute magnitude, then the magnitude--redshift relation. Its baseline fit is
`H_0 = 73.04 +/- 1.04 km s^-1 Mpc^-1`, including systematics.

`H_0` is a fitted parameter, not a raw detector output. The fit carries
calibration, population, dust, metallicity, peculiar-flow and low-redshift
kinematic assumptions, all of which the paper tests through analysis
variants. But calling this route “nothing measured” is false: it is a
geometrically anchored empirical distance chain, deliberately designed to
avoid importing an early-Universe sound horizon or base-`Lambda`CDM fit.

### 2. CMB: the acoustic scale is measured; H0 is model-inferred

Planck Collaboration, “Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters,”
*Astronomy & Astrophysics* **641** (2020) A6,
[arXiv:1807.06209v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209),
[doi:10.1051/0004-6361/201833910](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201833910).

Planck measures the CMB temperature, polarization and lensing spectra. The
paper reports the angular acoustic scale at `0.03%` precision,
`100 theta_* = 1.0411 +/- 0.0003`. It then explicitly says that **assuming the
base-`Lambda`CDM cosmology** the inferred late-Universe Hubble constant is
`67.4 +/- 0.5 km s^-1 Mpc^-1`.

The semantic owner is therefore the forward map from early-time physical
densities and perturbations through recombination to `r_s(z_*)`, and from the
late-time background to `D_M(z_*)`, with `theta_* = r_s/D_M`. A GU comparison
may not import the Planck `H_0` number while changing that map. It must compute
its own acoustic scale or evaluate the primary spectra with its own
action-owned background and perturbations.

### 3. BAO: distance ratios, not H0 alone

DESI Collaboration, “DESI DR2 Results II: Measurements of Baryon Acoustic
Oscillations and Cosmological Constraints,” *Physical Review D* **112**
(2025) 083515,
[arXiv:2503.14738v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14738),
[doi:10.1103/tr6y-kpc6](https://doi.org/10.1103/tr6y-kpc6).

DESI fits the BAO peak through the scaling parameters and reports
`D_V/r_d`, `D_M/r_d`, `D_H/r_d` and `D_M/D_H` at effective redshifts. The
paper states that, in flat `Lambda`CDM, BAO constrains `Omega_m` and the
combination `H_0 r_d`. It does not measure `H_0` alone. An `H_0` number from
BAO requires a sound-horizon calibration or another early-Universe prior.

The repository's existing DR2 likelihood assets already pin all 13 official
distance-ratio means and the full covariance. This disposition changes none
of those numbers. It only makes their semantic ceiling explicit: GU must
compute its own `r_d` and its own `D_V`, `D_M` and `D_H`, then confront the
ratio likelihood. Importing Planck's `r_d` while changing the early-time
model would test a hybrid, not GU.
