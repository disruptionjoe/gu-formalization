## 1. Original support and exact fixed-slice theorem

Let m calibration probabilities be p, science probability q, and 1<=t<=m the observed number of ones. Write

    P_j(p)=[z^j] product_i (1-p_i+p_i z),
    A=q P_(t-1), B=(1-q)P_t, Z=A+B, w=A/Z only if Z>0.

Here w is the conditional probability that the science role lies in the group of t ones; each one-position has probability w/t. It is not the odds at a single position. The previous odds r satisfy w=t r/(m-t+1+t r).

For h(p)=#{p_i=1} and k(p)=#{p_i>0}, P_j>0 exactly when h<=j<=k: forced ones contribute h and each remaining genuinely random Bernoulli variable gives positive probability at every count up to k.

Use K2409's simultaneously feasible least/greatest metric extensions a(q),b(q), including all original closed interval bounds. A slice is supported precisely when:

| q | Necessary and sufficient condition |
|---|---|
| 0<q<1 | h(a)<=t and k(b)>=t-1 |
| q=0 | h(a)<=t<=k(b) |
| q=1 | h(a)<=t-1<=k(b) |

A strict convex combination of a,b has exactly their forced ones and potentially positive coordinates, proving sufficiency. Coordinate order proves necessity.

For an interior supported q, the maximum is w(a,q) if a is supported. Otherwise the supremum is 1. The minimum is w(b,q) if b is supported; otherwise the infimum is 0. Coordinate monotonicity follows from the interior theorem by simultaneously regularizing ordered vectors toward (1/2,...,1/2) and taking limits at supported points. If a is unsupported, then k(a)<t-1; along (1-epsilon)a+epsilon b the ratio P_t/P_(t-1) tends to zero by its lowest nonzero power in epsilon, so w tends to one. Complementation proves the lower case.

An unsupported envelope does not by itself imply nonattainment. At interior q, w=1 is attained exactly when a feasible p has k(p)=t-1; w=0 exactly when h(p)=t. The finite face search fixes coordinates outside a chosen (t-1)-set to zero, or fixes a chosen t-set to one, intersects those restrictions with the ORIGINAL bounds, and checks the resulting simultaneous greatest/least extension. It returns a supported witness or proves no such face exists. At q=0 or1 every supported point instead has deterministic w=q, regardless of whether the extremal envelope itself is supported.

## 2. Complete global certificate

The solver uses the same rational pseudometric feasibility and breakpoints as K2412, then classifies each isolated breakpoint and open cell. On each open cell every coordinate of a,b stays zero, strictly between zero and one, or one. An affine x-d lower branch can meet zero only at its max-switch when the constant branch is zero; an upper x+d branch can meet one only at its min-switch when its constant branch is one. Other hits lie outside the science interval. Thus each original event polynomial is identically zero or strictly positive throughout the cell. Midpoint evaluation uses this structural fact; sampling alone is not a support proof.

For a supported cell, each sharp envelope is a rational function, or constant 0/1 when its extremal calibration envelope is null. Candidate values are all supported breakpoint bounds, both one-sided cell limits, constant-cell values, and every stationary root of N'D-ND'. Exact squarefree root isolation is complete. Rational interval evaluation refines each interior root until the value width meets the rational tolerance. Denominators cannot vanish inside a supported cell, so refinement terminates. The bounded conditional probabilities have finite one-sided limits even when the original endpoint event is null.

The minimum/maximum of the candidate intervals encloses the sharp infimum/supremum. Attainment is recorded **per candidate**: supported envelopes and stationary roots have witnesses; endpoint limits do not assert attainment. The solver does not decide global attainment by comparing possibly equal algebraic values. Outward endpoints remain conservative even if unattained. An entirely unsupported parameter family returns zero-event-support, not an evidence value.

## Verification, corrections and continuation

Exact support is checked against243 direct Bernoulli count enumerations, boundary face witnesses, nonconstant endpoint regressions, complete root-cell certificates and original-permutation consumers.
