## Result and scope

A channel can fix the target perfectly and still destroy the alternating
verifier's discrimination. A specified fresh reset-to-target channel instead
has a sharp purification threshold, including its equality case. At equality
purification is algebraic, not exponential. A full-memory counterexample has
the same single-gap channel but fails completely.

The positive Hilbert space, original Q/T instruments, flag law and pump are
supplied assumptions. SC-META-53 remains UNCERTAIN; ledger0.263 LT-SM8 and

## 1. Two target-preserving channels with different consequences

Reuse K2484/K2488's rank10 Q, rank12 T, target P, principal-plane squared
overlap r=27/127 or15/67, and orthogonal sectors P,Z,Y,A,B,N of dimensions
1,1,1,8,10,87. A is Q-only, B T-only. Choose unit vectors in A and B and a
unitary U swapping them while fixing P and their orthogonal complement.
After every accepted test apply U. Starting in A, the bad vector alternates
A,B,A,B and is accepted precisely on the same all-zero flag word as P.
Thus input F P+(1-F)|A><A| retains fidelity F at every cap. This holds for any
flag law independent of input, including even-spaced Markov flags. U fixes P;
that fact alone is not a filtering theorem.

Conversely, target dephasing Delta_P(X)=PXP+(I-P)X(I-P) commutes with both
projector instruments. Off-diagonal target/complement blocks never contribute
to trace or target mass. Inserting this dephasing leaves every K2488 survival
and fidelity expression unchanged. It avoids K2490's depolarizing injection
floor but does not repair K2488's antipersistent failure phase. Neither U nor
Delta_P is presumed free or locally implementable.

## 2. Fresh reset-to-target family

Let 0<u<v<1, a=1-u, b=1-v and

    H=[[a,u],[v,b]], pi=(v,u)/(u+v).
    Lambda_gamma(X)=h X+gamma Tr(X)P, h=1-gamma, 0<=gamma<=1.

Start the flag independently of the input/reference. Apply this channel after
each accepted test except the terminal test, followed by one H transition and
the next alternating Q/T instrument. At every reached gap the channel acts as
Lambda_gamma tensor identity on all retained memory/reference. Its replacement
term on SR is P tensor Tr_S(rho_SR). Discarded environments never return;
flags are unaffected by pumping. These complete-process assumptions are
stronger than isolated-gap tomography.

For conditional bad masses B_(j,0), B_(j,1), target mass T_j, exactly

    T_(j+1)=a T_j+gamma[a B_(j,0)+v B_(j,1)].

Every bad sector mass is h^(j-1) times its no-pump K2488 value. Reset sends bad
mass to P and creates no bad mass; P itself is fixed. The original moving-basis
six-sector recursion therefore remains exact, replacing K2490's d/108 reset
vector by the target basis vector. Coherences cannot change these two scalar
observables; this does not reduce the entire quantum channel to six classical
states. The probe compares against a fixed-basis selective CP oracle.

## 3. Sharp threshold and equality resonance

K2488's bad rates are the common-null rate b, the alternating rate sqrt(uv),
and the principal-plane Perron root lambda of

    K=[[a*r,u*(1-r)],[v*(1-r),b*r]].

If sqrt(uv)<a, all three rates are below a. If sqrt(uv)>=a, the principal
rate is strictly below sqrt(uv), and b<a. For a direct proof, K is diagonally similar to the symmetric matrix with
diagonal a*r,b*r and off-diagonal (1-r)*sqrt(uv). If sqrt(uv)<a, both row sums
are below a. If sqrt(uv)>=a, both are at most sqrt(uv), at least one strictly
less; irreducibility makes the Perron root strictly smaller. Consequently for gamma>0 and fixed 0<F0<1, uniformly over inputs F>=F0,

    error tends to zero  iff  h*sqrt(uv)<=a.

For gamma=0 the strict original condition uv<a^2 remains necessary and
sufficient; equality then has a nonzero parity floor. For gamma=1 every
accepted output from cap2 onward is P. If the original chain is supercritical,
the attained positive threshold is gamma_c=1-a/sqrt(uv).

Proof below the bad-rate threshold: target mass is at least pi0 F0 a^(n-1),
while every bad sector decays at a strictly smaller exponential rate. At the
positive equality h sqrt(uv)=a, only A/B can attain the target rate. Each
surviving such component injects a positive amount into target at every step,
producing a linear factor n in target mass. Other bad sectors decay relatively
exponentially. The exact finite six-vertex maximum in section4 gives uniform convergence
over the input-weight simplex. The exact recurrences below prove failure on the other side.

Put D=h^2 uv. For an initial target/A mixture, the odd-cap target-to-bad ratio
x_k=T_(2k+1)/B_(2k+1) obeys

    x_(k+1)=(a^2/D)x_k+gamma(a^2+h uv)/D.

At D=a^2, x_k=x_0+k gamma(1+1/h). The B-sector slope is
that quantity times sqrt(v/u), with roles interchanged at even caps. Thus for
fixed 0<F0<1 the sharp worst-input error has the parity-independent asymptotic

    error_n ~ 2h/[gamma(1+h)n].

The prefactor is not uniform as gamma tends to zero or F0 tends to one. For
F0=1 the output is already perfect. This resonance is why replacing <= by <
in the positive-pump criterion would be incorrect.

## 4. Full-carrier limiting floor below the pump threshold

For fixed 0<F0<1, 0<gamma<gamma_c and D>a^2, the A/B target-to-bad limiting ratios are

    R1=gamma(a^2+h uv)/(D-a^2),
    R2=gamma a v(1+h)/(D-a^2).

They swap with parity and sector. To obtain the *full-carrier* worst limit,
one must also retain the principal plane. In column-mass convention let

    z=(lambda-a*r)/(v*(1-r)), ell=h*lambda.

The principal eigenvector has bad bit masses proportional to (1,z). If ell>a,
its target/bad limiting ratio is

    Rp=gamma(a+v*z)/[(ell-a)(1+z)].

Its limiting error is 1/(1+Rp). If ell<=a that error tends to zero (equality
again injects a linear target factor). Common-null error tends to zero because
h b<a. The exact sharp worst-input limiting error on either parity is therefore

    max(1/(1+R1), 1/(1+R2), principal-plane limiting error).

Proof: the initial-fidelity polytope has the six vertices P and
F0 P+(1-F0)V, V=Z,Y,A,B,N. Output error is a ratio of affine functions, hence a
denominator-weighted average of vertex errors. The maximum is at a vertex.
Positive Perron eigenvectors give the displayed plane limit for either Z/Y;
A/B recurrences give the other two. A slower bad rate alone does not justify
dropping its vertex from this maximum. These formulas are analytic results;
the finite rational fixture does not itself prove their limits.

## 5. A real memory failure with the same isolated-gap channel

Choose one frozen independent Bernoulli(gamma) at launch. In one branch reset
to P at every gap; in the other apply identity at every gap. Every isolated
gap has Lambda_gamma as its marginal. On a supercritical target/A mixture,
the identity branch contributes bad mass at rate sqrt(uv)>a, while target
mass in either branch grows only at rate a. For 0<gamma<1 the accepted error
tends to one, even when the fresh-channel threshold is met. The probe checks
identical cap2 and different cap3 output. Generic adaptive-reset freshness and
resource requirements already appear in K2256; this is their exact original-
verifier consequence, not a new generic channel theorem.

## 6. Charged finite consumer and released resource consequence

Freeze r=27/127,u=1/2,v=8/9,F0=.9. Then gamma_c=1/4. Charge preparation,
each test and each reached pump gap one unit each. For acceptance q_j and q_0=1,

    C_n=1+sum_(j=1)^n q_(j-1)+sum_(j=1)^(n-1)q_j.

Independent restart of input, flag law and pump environment gives C_n/q_n.
