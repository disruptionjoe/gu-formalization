## 1. Universal rank-two normal form

Use h_ijk=-h_ikj and cyclic sum zero. With an ordinary covector raised by
the nondegenerate metric, let B(v)_ij=h_ivj+h_jvi. K2458 proves
B(v)(v,v)=0 and injectivity of h->B on cyclic hooks. If rank B=2, write

    B(v)=alpha(v) Q1+beta(v) Q2,

with independent alpha,beta. The cubic identity is
alpha(v)q1(v)+beta(v)q2(v)=0, q_i(v)=Q_i(v,v).
Independent linear polynomials are coprime; hence q1=beta*l and
q2=-alpha*l for a linear l. Put m=l/2 and define
(u odot v)_ij=u_i v_j+v_i u_j. Polarization gives

    Q1=beta odot m, Q2=-alpha odot m,
    Omega=alpha wedge beta,
    h_ijk=[2m_i Omega_jk-Omega_ij m_k+Omega_ik m_j]/3.    (1)

The inverse follows directly from h_ijk=(B(e_j)_ik-B(e_k)_ij)/3.
Conversely independent alpha,beta and m!=0 yield rank2, since multiplying
a nonzero linear polynomial by m is injective. Contracting the first two
slots shows tracefree iff <m,alpha>=<m,beta>=0. This proof is
characteristic-zero and signature independent. No positivity of the metric
is used in this normal form.

## 2. Real tracefree repeated classification is finite

Now impose the actual repeated carrier: h_ijk=0 when i,j,k are distinct,
in the original diagonal eta_i=+/-1 basis. Equation (1) for the three
cyclic entries says

    m_i Omega_jk=m_j Omega_ki=m_k Omega_ij               (2)

for every distinct triple. If m has at least two nonzero coordinates,
(2) forces Omega to be supported inside supp(m). For support size at
least4, set r_ij=Omega_ij/(m_i m_j) there. On each triple
r_ij=r_jk=r_ki. On four indices, triples123 and124 give
r12=r23=r31=r24=r41=t; triple134 forces r13=r41, so -t=t.
Characteristic zero makes t=0. Every pair lies in a four-subset, hence
Omega=0, impossible.

For support size1, (2) kills edges off that index and tracefreeness kills
all remaining edges. For support size2, (2) confines Omega to that pair
and tracefreeness kills its one edge. Therefore support is exactly a triple
(a,b,c), with nonzero common m_a Omega_bc=m_b Omega_ca=m_c Omega_ab.
Tracefreeness gives

    eta_a m_a^2=eta_b m_b^2=eta_c m_c^2.                (3)

Over the reals these are three same-sign axes and equal absolute m_i.
There are7 positive and7 negative axes. Up to overall hook scale, normalize
m_a=1 and choose m_b,m_c independently +/-1. Take
Omega_ab=1/m_c, Omega_bc=1/m_a, Omega_ca=1/m_b. Then
h_iij=m_i Omega_ij and h_iji=-h_iij are the only nonzero entries.
Thus exactly2*binom(7,3)*4=280 projective candidates exhaust this class.
This does not classify non-repeated, higher-rank or complex hooks.

The probe checks every candidate against the original Gram and full polar
conditions, with no changed background coefficients. Exactly16 survive:
all four relative-sign patterns on each of

    (2,3,10), (4,5,6), (7,8,9), (11,12,13).

All16 have rank2 and12-dimensional ordinary-null coordinate rings. The
report lists their complete original first-order scalar coefficients. Every
original second-order scalar coefficient vanishes on each such ring; the
probe evaluates all null-direction pairs against every original background
hook. This finite exhaustive calculation, together with the classification,
covers the original16 lines, not arbitrary changed background models.

## 4. Actual twelve-variable analytic primitive

    Jstar(h f)=(-partial_x5+partial_x6)f/16              (4)

    Sg(t,u,z)=-16 t integral_0^1 g(st,u+(1-s)t,z) ds.    (5)

Change variable r=st: Sg=-16 integral_0^t g(r,u+t-r,z)dr.

## 5. Released complete scalar image and analytic inverse

The complete original first-order coefficients in the report show that the

rings: both first- and second-order scalar terms vanish. The four lines on
(2,3,10) are active. Write a=x2,b=x3,c=x10 and Z for the11 other ambient
coordinates. For sign pattern(s,t), their ring is R[Z,ell_st],
ell_st=a+s b+t c, and their scalar derivative is
s(partial_x5-partial_x6)/16. All four have the same boundary coordinate
x5=y2, so the sign-adjusted primitive above is onto their respective ring.

It follows that the full scalar image of arbitrary individually
ordinary-null original real repeated rank-two banks is exactly

    V = sum_(s,t=+/-1) R[Z,a+s b+t c]
      = ker(partial_a^2-partial_b^2)
          intersect ker(partial_a^2-partial_c^2),        (7)

where the sum is LINEAR, not a generated algebra. The inclusion into the
PDE kernel follows by differentiating each ridge function. For the reverse,
fix a spectator monomial and homogeneous triple degree k. The differential
equations give the coefficient recurrences

    (j+2)(j+1)p_(i,j+2,l)=(i+2)(i+1)p_(i+2,j,l),
    (l+2)(l+1)p_(i,j,l+2)=(i+2)(i+1)p_(i+2,j,l).

Every coefficient is determined by the at most four representatives with
j,l in{0,1}. For k>=2 an explicit reconstruction is

    p_k=sum c_st (a+s b+t c)^k,
    c_st=[p_(k,0,0)+(s/k)p_(k-1,1,0)+(t/k)p_(k-1,0,1)

For k=1 omit the last term; for k=0 use c_st=p0/4. These ridge powers
satisfy the equations and match all representative coefficients, proving
(7). The four sign characters have rank4 for k>=2, rank3 for k=1 and
rank1 for k=0. Thus the exact homogeneous total-degree n image dimension is

    binom(n+11,11)+2 binom(n+10,11)+binom(n+9,11),      (9)

The explicit target a^2 fails (7), as does a^2*x0^(n-2) for every n>=2.
The actual4/d top-jet interpretation is inherited for d=n+1>=4; use a^3

## 6. Released cancellation rigidity in the entire rank-two span

Allow polynomial coefficients on all16 original constant lines and require
only the SUM to have ordinary response zero. This does not enlarge (7).
The four triples are disjoint, so their ordinary symmetric output blocks
cannot cancel one another. On one same-sign triple(a,b,c), the four lines
span the full three-dimensional repeated tracefree space. Write

    h_aab=u, h_ccb=-u, h_aac=v, h_bbc=-v,
    h_bba=w, h_cca=-w,

with antisymmetric last slots. The ordinary response vanishes exactly when

    u_b+v_c=0, w_a-v_c=0, u_a+w_b=0,
    v_a-w_c=0, v_b+u_c=0.                             (11)

Commuting derivatives gives u_aa=u_bb=u_cc, and the same for v,w:
for example u_aa=-w_ba=-v_cb=u_cc and u_bb=-v_cb;
v_aa=w_ca=v_cc, v_bb=-u_cb=v_cc;
w_aa=v_ca=w_cc, w_bb=-u_ab=v_ca. Thus each coefficient has the
four-ridge decomposition (8), coefficientwise in the eleven spectators.

For homogeneous triple degree k>=3, derivative ridge powers have degree
at least2 and are independent. Each signed coefficient vector must therefore
lie in ker A_ell, so each term is individually ordinary-null. Degree2
needs care: the four derivative ridges have sole relation weighted by st.
Hence their residual symbols have the form A_ell(q_st)=st R for one
symmetric R. Every ordinary symbol is tracefree and annihilates ell⊗ell
by the cyclic identity. All four ell impose R_ab=R_ac=R_bc=0 and
R_aa+R_bb+R_cc=0. But a diagonal A_ell(q) must be zero: its offdiagonal
equations force q=(u,v,w)=(-s,t,1)w, the ordinary-null vector itself.
Thus R=0 and the decomposition still holds. At k=1, the five independent
conditions (11) on nine coefficients leave dimension4; the four h_st ell_st
are independent (four sign characters), so span it. At k=0 the constants
form dimension3 and the four h_st span them.

Therefore every ordinary-null polynomial field in this full12-dimensional
constant span is a sum of individually ordinary-null fields. This statement
is on the larger space WITHOUT boundary restrictions, so no boundary-splitting

scalar-dark; the four active lines give (7). Existing boundary-preserving
