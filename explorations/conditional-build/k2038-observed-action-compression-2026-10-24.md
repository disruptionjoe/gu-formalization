## 4. A nonlinear action with a positive local current

Set M=I11 tensor(g0 g1 g2 g10). It is skew, M^2=-I, [M,T]=0, and
{M,Cj}=0. Put S=T M, which is symmetric and invertible. For real commuting
fields on a coordinate patch, with compactly supported variations, set

    s(u)=u^T S u/2,
    V(u)=mu*s(u)+(g/2)*s(u)^2,
    L= (1/2)u^T T(∂t+sum Cj ∂j)u + V(u).          (3)

Here mu and g are arbitrary real constants. Integration by parts and the
skewness of T and T Cj give the exact Euler equation

    ∂t u+sum Cj ∂j u+(mu+g*s(u))*M*u=0.            (4)

The quartic term is nonzero when g!=0. Since M is skew, every smooth solution
satisfies

    ∂t |u|^2 + sum ∂j(u^T Cj u)=0.                 (5)

The norm is conserved on periodic domains, or with the appropriate vanishing
flux/decay conditions. The directional flux is bounded by |u|^2 because
C(n)^2=|n|^2 I. Integrating (5) on exterior light cones gives speed at most1
for smooth solutions with compactly supported initial data. This conditional
support statement is not a global existence theorem.

More generally, the action with any differentiable V produces
Du+T^-1 grad V=0. Its positive-norm source term is
2*dV_u(T^-1 u). Thus the necessary and sufficient pointwise condition for
(5) is dV_u(T^-1 u)=0. It is invariance of V under that linear rotation,
not an automatic consequence of having an action.

The one-field action (3) is not a Grassmann-odd action. For odd u, u^T S u
