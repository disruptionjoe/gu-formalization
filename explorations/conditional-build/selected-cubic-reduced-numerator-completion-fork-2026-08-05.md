## 4. Why the two-massless numerator vanishes

Let the inherited constant-background `hh` response be

\[
 \delta J_{hh}(z)=d z
 \begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}. \tag{3}
\]

The moving trilinear polarization of `theta h Box h` between legs `i,j` is

\[
 N_{hh}(i,j)={d\over2}(z_i+z_j)
  (u_i)_h(u_j)_h. \tag{4}
\]

Therefore

\[
 N_{hh}(0,0)=0,
 \quad N_{hh}(0,m)={dM^2\over2},
 \quad N_{hh}(m,m)=dM^2. \tag{5}
\]

Equation (4) is the finite-pencil form of the standard equivalence-theorem
fact: a scalar-dependent kinetic normalization generates an interaction
proportional to the external inverse propagators. A nonzero value of (3) at an
off-shell TT symbol is completely compatible with (5)'s first zero.
