# Conditional Physics Ledger v0.260

Ledger v0.260 preserves every v0.259 row and appends one target-centered
requirement, `LT-GR8`.

## Result

The repository now treats the appropriate semiclassical relation among
causal horizons, leading area entropy, first-law or generalized-entropy
behavior, and Einstein-like dynamics as an **external scoped benchmark** for
the source-aligned K77 `(7,7)` reverse scaffold. The separate B5 `(9,5)`
calculation remains a comparator, not the primary carrier for this target.

The benchmark and its GU realization are different records:

```text
external benchmark       accepted at scoped semiclassical grade
reverse-scaffold target  active
GU/K77 realization       missing construction
mechanism commitment     none
confirmation credit      none
```

`NEEDS` applies to the missing GU/K77 bridge, not to the external benchmark.
Jacobson 1995 remains a conditional theorem: given local Rindler horizons,
Unruh temperature, area entropy and equilibrium Clausius behavior for all
local horizons, Einstein dynamics follow. The 2022--2025 boundary, ensemble,
contour and partition-function papers remain model-scoped candidate
mechanisms and tests.

## Council disposition

The adversarial ten-seat vote was `8-1-1`:

- eight accepted the benchmark while withholding mechanism commitment;
- one favored direct use as a reverse premise;
- one favored later validation only.

The selected two-track rule is:

```text
reverse track: freeze target -> infer required roles -> construct backward
forward track: K77 action -> observation -> boundary/reduction -> consequences
adjudication: independent convergence = candidate explanation
              reverse-only = compatibility
              forward held-out output = prediction candidate
              disagreement = negative evidence
```

## New row

`LT-GR8` asks whether the selected observed gravitational theory reproduces
the appropriate scoped semiclassical horizon-entropy and first-law benchmark.
Its missing construction must separately own:

1. a K77-to-observed-`3+1` Lorentzian bridge;
2. one named physical causal region and boundary;
3. action-derived constraints and canonical/BFV reduction;
4. entropy, temperature or KMS state, and energy-flux functionals;
5. frozen normalization and equilibrium or nonequilibrium law;
6. field equations and higher-curvature corrections; and
7. one held-out consequence frozen before comparison.

The row's v0.260 context is `layer: L2`, `carrier: C2`, and
`grant: UNTYPED`. The current Grant poset has no object for the full
Jacobson assumption set, so the ledger declares that gap rather than forcing
the benchmark into the existing in-row-conditional bucket.

## Boundary and mechanism fences

Do not identify without a constructed map:

- local Rindler horizon;
- cosmological horizon;
- York ensemble boundary;
- analytic operator-domain boundary;
- BV--BFV boundary; or
- Observerse capability boundary.

Do not import an ADM lapse, Brown--York energy, York wall, below-zero lapse
contour, edge-mode interpretation or simplicial saddle as the K77 mechanism.
They remain candidates to test after the native objects exist.

## Confirmation ceiling

A target-driven reconstruction cannot later be counted as an independent
prediction. Reverse-only success establishes compatibility. Failure kills
only the scoped GU--Jacobson thermodynamic-emergence bridge unless a separate
necessity theorem proves that the benchmark is mandatory for a wider GU
claim.
