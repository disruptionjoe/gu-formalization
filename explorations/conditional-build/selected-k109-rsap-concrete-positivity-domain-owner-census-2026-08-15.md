## Entry criterion

implicit carrier check explicit. A candidate enters another positivity swing
only if one typed packet supplies:

1. the conditional `98D` phase/BFV carrier, or an explicit proved map into it;
2. a source/action-owned non-invariant selector or constraint;

polarization is not quantum cohomology; and a kinematic source split is not a

### Why the two strongest partial results do not close the gap

The TT `C`-operator is not dismissed. It proves that action-owned field
mixing can repair a fixed indefinite two-mode Hessian without a fitted sign.
But its background is not proved stationary for the complete action, its
positive metric is finite-dimensional, and its own artifact leaves the
common interacting BV/Green/Fock domain open.

The observed defect Green system is also not dismissed. It proves a common
normally hyperbolic domain for a coupled two-field operator on the inherited
globally hyperbolic observed defect. Its field pairing has determinant `-1`,
the two poles have opposite residues, and the artifact explicitly says the
domain is Krein rather than positive. It is not the ambient `Y14` domain and
not the balanced RSAP/BFV carrier.

The nonlocal ultrahyperbolic route is weaker at the required grade. The
strict-center support plus `ker N(k)` removes the flat principal Jordan chains,
but the construction labels itself a domain ingredient rather than a physical
closed domain. Its Green-adjoint successor also finds radicals on the direct
dual-kernel domain. Neither supplies positivity or a map to the `98D` BFV
carrier.
