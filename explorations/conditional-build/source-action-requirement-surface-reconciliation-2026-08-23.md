## Why this audit ran

The 2026-08-23 descents produced a seven-owner demand ledger. Before treating
that ledger as the program's scorecard, it had to be checked against the
surface that already existed: the consolidated source-action requirements
spec (2026-07-13, 27 rows) and the constraint-intersection map (2026-07-11).
The question was deliberately hostile to the day's own work — *did the
descents add constraint, or re-describe what was known?* The answer is
mixed and worth recording exactly: mostly re-description, plus one genuine
coverage gap the descents surfaced because they demanded the same
coefficient from an independent direction.

## Finding A — coverage gap, now closed (SA-G10)

**Exact statement.** The consolidated requirements spec contained no row for
`alpha_W`, the gravity ambient-curvature (`R^Y.B`) coefficient, under that
name or the aliases `c_W`, `R^Y`, `OQ2-A` or `Willmore` — while claiming to
consolidate "everything the unbuilt source action must supply … from every
research leg." Its declared `depends_on` set contains no path4-line artifact
and not the constraint-intersection map, which is the mechanical reason the
row was absent: the spec was assembled from a source set that excluded the
gravity-coefficient line.

**Why the omission matters.** The same coefficient is:
- named by the constraint-intersection as one of **exactly two** genuinely
  remaining free scalars ("`alpha_W` … fixed by the gravity leg alone");
- identified by path4 wave 2 as the **OQ2-A / `c_W`** coefficient, with
  verdict `GATED` (not parameter-free);
- the **one term W225 could not settle** without the branch-fixed source
  action; and
- demanded independently by today's ledger as `ROT-1` (the
  Einstein-versus-conformal ratio) and reached through `SF-1`.

A candidate action audited against the spec alone would therefore have passed
while leaving that coefficient unowned.

**Repair.** `SA-G10` added at class **FIT (gated)** with its correct typing
(welded to `f_0` by leading-order Schwarzschild consistency, but that weld IS
the unbuilt `c_W`, carries beta/alpha, and terminates in an action
coefficient; no shared-theta Ward identity pins it). Spec tallies moved
27→28 rows and 10→11 FIT; the companion consistency test's table and tally
checks were updated in the same change and pass. **No new freedom is
created**: the row records freedom already computed and classified in the
cited artifacts.
