## 4. Selector-independent principal symbol

Write the derivative-containing part of the bosonic action as

\[
 I_1^B=\int_Y
 \left\langle T,
 \mathscr S_\epsilon\left(
 F_B+\frac12d_BT+\frac13[T,T]
 \right)+\frac{\kappa_1}{2}\,*T
 \right\rangle . \tag{1}
\]

At fixed `epsilon`, suppose `mathscr S_epsilon` is zero-order in the `B,T`
jets, as in the displayed source family. For independent local variations
`beta=delta B` and `alpha=delta T`, the derivative-only parts are

\[
 \delta_\beta F_B\simeq d\beta,
 \qquad
 \delta_\alpha(d_BT)\simeq d\alpha. \tag{2}
\]

Let

\[
 \nu^a=dy^a-s_i^a dx^i
\]

be a graph conormal. Equations (1)--(2) give the two conormal Legendre
symbols

\[
 \boxed{
 \sigma_B^a(\beta)
 =\mu_s\langle T,\mathscr S_\epsilon(\nu^a\wedge\beta)\rangle,
 \qquad
 \sigma_T^a(\alpha)
 =\frac{\mu_s}{2}\langle T,\mathscr S_\epsilon(\nu^a\wedge\alpha)\rangle .
 } \tag{3}
\]

For the same one-form direction, `sigma_B=2 sigma_T`. The `1/3[T,T]` and
mass terms are zero-order and contribute nothing to (3). Direct symbolic
differentiation of an arbitrary Shiab matrix reproduces (3) exactly. A
rational live matrix verifies nonvacuity and graph-slope mixing, but is not
identified with K77's preferred representative.

The `T` row in (3) is source-owned because the draft explicitly varies

## 5. Fixed-section and all-section factorization

For a supplied four-plane `H` in a fourteen-dimensional cotangent space,

\[
 \Lambda^2T^*Y
 =\Lambda^2H^*
 \oplus(H^*\wedge N^*)
 \oplus\Lambda^2N^* . \tag{4}
\]

The dimensions are `6+40+45=91`. Conormal wedges span the last two summands,
dimension `85`. Assuming arbitrary `T` and a nondegenerate adjoint/top-form
pairing, zero-jet factorization at this section is exactly

\[
 \mathscr S_\epsilon(\nu\wedge\alpha)=0
 \quad\text{for every }\nu\in N^*,\ \alpha\in T^*Y. \tag{5}
\]

Per paired adjoint coefficient block, (5) kills `14*85=1190` matrix entries
and permits `14*6=84` purely tangential entries. This does **not** imply that
the whole Shiab vanishes.

arbitrary-`T` hypotheses, all `14*91=1274` entries per paired block vanish.
Therefore a nonzero Shiab cannot factor through only zero jets for every
section. The live K77 construction must either retain ambient normal jets or
select a special fixed-section annihilator and prove it covariant. We retain
the already-owned ambient first jet; no extension law or datum is added.
