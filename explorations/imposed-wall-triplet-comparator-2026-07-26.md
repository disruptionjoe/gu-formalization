# Target-Blind Imposed-Wall Triplet Comparator

## Question

At the first physical-toy rung of `SRC-TOY-01`, what does a conventional,
imposed domain wall actually establish when the global carrier is vectorlike
and the triplet factor is supplied independently?

## Construction fork and scope

This is the **standard-field** side of the construction fork: a
positive-Hilbert domain-wall index ledger. It is used because `SRC-TOY-01`
explicitly permits that first comparator to expose necessary physical-selection
structure. It is not silently transferred to GU's program-native `(9,5)`
Krein, gimmel, shiab, or `ker Gamma` carrier; a later transport must prove the
real action, physical quotient, operator, and domain.

For a supplied vectorlike multiplicity (N\ge1) and imposed signed wall index
(q\), the local accessible sector has index (qN). The remote wall/boundary
completion carries (-qN), so the global ledger stays index zero. The finite
certificate tests (N=1,2,3,4), (q=0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm3), orientation reversal,
direct-sum stabilization, and a deliberately target-coded positive control.

## Result

For a unit wall, the accessible rank is exactly the independently supplied
multiplicity:

| supplied (N) | unit-wall accessible rank |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 |
| 4 | 4 |

Thus the imposed wall conditionally **hosts** a local chiral triplet when
(N=3), but it does not select that multiplicity. Reversing the wall reverses
chirality while preserving rank; zero, opposite, and non-target wall sectors
remain explicit returns. The partner is not discarded: its opposite index
keeps the global finite ledger vectorlike.

The precise return is `IMPOSED_BOUNDARY_HOSTING / EFFECTIVE_ACCESS_N`, not
`DYNAMIC_BOUNDARY_SELECTED`, `GLOBAL_INDEX_THREE_CONDITIONAL`, or a generation
derivation. A construction choosing (N=3,q=1) merely because it produces
three is detected as `THREE_TARGET_IMPORTED`.

## Consequence

This passes the lowest physical hosting control and narrows the next work: the
finite-selector/dynamical-wall branch must specify a target-blind source field
and topological sector that select a wall and its orientation while retaining
the paired mirror, anomaly/inflow, and GU-transport obligations. A further
imposed-wall recount would be duplicate work.

## Validation

`tests/source-action/imposed_wall_triplet_comparator.py` is a stdlib-only
certificate. It verifies the full declared control grid and does not perform a
continuum anomaly calculation, solve a dynamical wall equation, or establish a
program-native operator.
