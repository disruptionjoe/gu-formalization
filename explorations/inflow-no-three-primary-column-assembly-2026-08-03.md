# The two sentences, put next to each other

**(A)** Canon (CRT split of the count problem): the 2-primary selector arena and the
3-primary count arena are CRT-disjoint summands; "they cannot interact directly;
**anomaly inflow is the sole bridge**."

**(B)** Computed (global anomaly leg, 2026-07-20): the spin Dai–Freed ledger — the
wall through which a bulk-SPT inflow argument would run — has **no 3-primary column
in any dimension**: spin-bordism torsion is all 2-primary (Wall 1960 + ABP), the
`BSp(64)` factor adds none (Borel), every 15d torsion receptacle is empty, and the
free part vanishes exactly on the balanced contents.

## Consequence (a syllogism, not a new result)

If the sole bridge between the arenas is anomaly inflow, and the spin-structure
inflow wall has no 3-primary component to carry, then **no spin-wall inflow argument
can move information into or out of the Z/3 count arena**. In particular the
single-decider program's route that would extract an integer count from a bulk
`-p_1/24`-type SPT class through spin Dai–Freed inflow is closed as stated: whatever
that route computes is 2-primary on arrival, and `Hom(Z/3, anything-2-primary) = 0`.

## Scope — what this does NOT close

- It does not touch **framed** bordism (`pi_13^s = Z/3` — purely 3-primary) or
  **String**-type refinements (tmf-capable of odd torsion), or any other
  explicitly odd-primary-capable tangential structure: those walls are different
  structures, and the dim-13 boundary program (audit/panel item U3) lives exactly
  there. This assembly *redirects* the bridge search onto those structures; it
  does not end it. [Revised 2026-08-03 per the interior-agent review: **Pin** is
  removed from the candidate list — Pin bordism phenomena are presumptively
  2-primary and a Pin wall should not be assumed odd-capable without proof.]
- It does not move the generation-count verdict (OPEN), bar(b), or H59.
- It is conditional on (A)'s "sole bridge" framing at its canon scope; if a
  non-inflow bridge mechanism were ever exhibited, (A) itself would be the thing
  superseded.
