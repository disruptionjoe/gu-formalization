# Wave 3D-B2C1 — prior source/action and external-datum collision

## Result

The previous source-action build does **not** supply the constraint/gauge
quotient left open by B2B.

The exact earlier finite-fibre construction was

\[
G(k)=\Pi_{\ker\Gamma}(k\otimes -).
\]

It came from a minimum-distance KKT/Schur solve enforcing
\(\Gamma G=0\).  Its own packet says why that is not yet a physical source
map: the Noether equation leaves every tangent correction
\(Z:\mathbb C^{128}\to\ker\Gamma\) free, a space of complex dimension
\(1664\cdot128=212{,}992\).  The orthogonal solution selects \(Z=0\) by
minimization, not by an independently derived GU current or tangent
differential.

B2C1 collides that exact map with B2B's actual W131 generators.  For a spatial
covector \(\xi\), let

\[
C(\xi)=A_t^{-1}A(\xi),\qquad
N_\xi=C(\xi)^2-|\xi|^2I.
\]

For \(\xi=e_y,e_x,e_z,(1,2,3)\), \(\operatorname{rank}N_\xi=128\) and
\(N_\xi^2=0\), reproducing B2B.  At each characteristic root
\(\mu=\pm|\xi|\):

- \(G(\xi-\mu,dt)\) has rank 128;
- \((A(\xi)-\mu A_t)G(\xi-\mu,dt)\) has rank 64, not zero;
- its parameter nullspace therefore gives one rank-64 on-characteristic
  residual half;
- that half intersects \(\operatorname{im}N_\xi\) in rank 64; and
- the two opposite-root halves together equal
  \(\operatorname{im}N_\xi\), with two-sided subspace residual below
  \(2\times10^{-9}\).

This is a useful identification, but not a quotient derivation.  The full
projected map is neither characteristic-symbol-null nor invariant under the
evolution generator.  Selecting only its residual half depends on the target
principal symbol and characteristic root.  The previous source/tau solve
does not select that half or prove a nilpotent BV differential whose image it
is.

## The fixed-quotient fork also fails physically

One could try to avoid root-dependent selection by taking a single invariant
subspace across all spatial directions.  The three coordinate Jordan images
span rank 384, and the tested generic \((1,2,3)\) image lies in that span.  But
the rank-384 span is not invariant.  Its smallest tested common invariant
closure has rank 512.

That rank-512 closure is not an innocuous gauge tail.  It equals, by two-sided
subspace comparison, the entire projected vector-spinor carrier generated by
the admitted observer section's \(y,x,z,t\) vector slots.  It is right-
\(\mathbb H\) invariant.  Quotienting it leaves dimension 1152, where the
three compressed generators are Hermitian Clifford involutions and the
identity is a positive simultaneous symmetrizer.

But the observer-section extraction map is nonzero of full rank 512 on the
proposed quotient kernel.  Therefore it does not descend to that quotient.
The algebraic hyperbolicity repair succeeds only by deleting the whole
projected carrier through which Wave 3 observation was defined.  That fork is
killed as target erasure, not accepted as a physical reduction.
