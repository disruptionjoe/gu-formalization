# The moduli-negative vs time-negative hypothesis

## Statement (two halves — keep them separate)

**HALF A — PROVENANCE (computed, solid).** `Y14 = Met(X4)` has signature `(9,5) = (3,1)_base + (6,4)_fibre`.
The base contributes **one** negative direction — ordinary time. **All four extra negative directions come
from the fibre**, i.e. from the DeWitt/Frobenius metric on the *space of metrics*
(Frobenius `(7,3)` -> trace-reversal -> `(6,4)`). They are **configuration-space directions, not physical
times.** The indefiniteness of the DeWitt supermetric is standard canonical gravity, not a GU artifact.

**HALF B — DISSOLUTION (hypothesis, CONDITIONAL).** *If* the moduli directions can be separated, reduced,
constrained, or gauge-fixed, *then* the residual operator on the reduced space may be hyperbolic (one time)
rather than ultrahyperbolic, and index-type arguments may return.

## The hole that must not be allowed to open

**Provenance does not move a symbol.** The Dirac/RS operator on `Y14` is built from the *ambient* Clifford
algebra `Cl(9,5)`, and its principal symbol is `sigma(D)(xi)^2 = g_Y(xi,xi) Id` where `g_Y` is the **full
ambient metric**. That metric does not know, and cannot be told, that four of its negative directions have
moduli provenance. The null cone of `g_Y` includes directions mixing base and fibre.

**So the operator is ultrahyperbolic regardless of where the signs came from.** Half A is a statement about
*interpretation*; the obstruction is a statement about *the symbol*. Interpretation does not move symbols.

The drift to guard against, stated so it can be caught: **"they are moduli, therefore the ultrahyperbolic
problem dissolves."** That inference is invalid as stated. The valid form is Half B, and its antecedent —
an actual separation, reduction or gauge-fixing that removes those directions from the symbol — **is
unperformed work, not a given.**

## What makes this MORE valuable than stated: it is the GHP conformal lead

The indefiniteness of the DeWitt supermetric is the **conformal-factor problem** of Euclidean quantum
gravity. It has been open since Gibbons-Hawking-Perry (1978), and the standard treatment is **contour
rotation of exactly the conformal mode** — you rotate that direction in the complex plane.

Two consequences, and both matter:

1. **A precedent about difficulty.** "It is only a configuration-space direction" has been known for ~45
   years and did not by itself solve the conformal-factor problem. That is evidence about how hard Half B's
   antecedent is, and it should temper "the difficulties become precise, attackable technical problems."
   They become **well-posed**; they do not become small.
2. **A convergence with the repository's own top unexplored lead.** The 25-lens signature council recorded,
   as *"the single most promising unexplored lead"*: the conformal mode carries negative norm (computed:
   spatial block `(5,1)`), the standard treatment is the GHP contour rotation of that mode, and **Curt's
   "the trace contributes either `(1,0)` or `(0,1)` depending on a choice" may be a CONTOUR choice rather
   than a SIGNATURE choice — which would reframe the K95/K77 fork entirely.**

**This hypothesis and that lead are the same object.** If contour rotation of the moduli/conformal directions
applies, it could simultaneously (a) restore one effective time, (b) restore ellipticity post-rotation, and
(c) dissolve `SIGNATURE-AMBIENT` into a contour choice rather than a fork. That is a far stronger claim than
"the difficulties become attackable", and it is the reason to keep this hypothesis alive.

## Kill conditions (declared in advance)

This hypothesis is **dead**, not merely weakened, if:

1. No separation/reduction/gauge-fixing exists that removes the four fibre-negative directions from the
   principal symbol — i.e. the ambient symbol is irreducibly ultrahyperbolic on any admissible reduction.
2. A reduction exists but the residual operator is still not hyperbolic (the base time does not survive as
   the unique time of the reduced system).
3. The GHP-style contour rotation is shown not to apply to the DeWitt directions in this construction
   (e.g. the rotation required is not a single-mode rotation, or it changes `p-q` in a way that lands off
   both horns).
4. Index-type arguments fail to return even after a successful reduction — i.e. the reduction is achieved
   and the count is still not an index.

## What this hypothesis does NOT license

- It does **not** establish that ordinary hyperbolic physics is recovered. Recovery is Half B's consequent,
  and Half B is conditional.
- It does **not** make `X4` emergent. `X4` is the BASE of `Met(X4)` and is presupposed by the construction.
  The object that could plausibly be observer-selected is the **section `sigma`**, not the manifold.
- It does **not** repair the missing families pushforward, which is blocked for four independent reasons.
- It says nothing about the generation count. The standing verdict is *located, not forced*, tilting toward
  **one**.
