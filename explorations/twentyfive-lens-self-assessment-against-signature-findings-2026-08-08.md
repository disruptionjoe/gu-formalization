## A — reality and parity

## 1. Clifford algebraist

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: very high.** The two real Clifford
forms are not interchangeable even though their complexifications coincide;
any result involving right-`H`, a real pairing, Hodge star, adjoint or Krein
structure must declare the horn on which it was constructed. Recent work that
ported such a result from `(9,5)` without rebuilding the real structure was
under-scoped, whereas explicitly tensor-natural work may still transfer.

## 2. Majorana–Weyl specialist

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: very high.** With the fibre fixed at
`(6,4)`, the certificate correctly shows that the Majorana–Weyl condition leaves
only the reachable `(7,7)` horn. The dissent is logical rather than algebraic:
the need for a Majorana–Weyl carrier is a GU construction requirement still to
be justified, not a theorem extracted from the `4+10` split alone.

## 3. Kramers and antiunitary specialist

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: high.** A commuting antiunitary with
square `-1` forces even spectral multiplicity on the quaternionic horn, so R3's
universal balance may indeed contain a Kramers contribution. Removing that
forced doubling on `(7,7)` only removes an obstruction: it does not itself prove
simple spectrum, a unique `C`, or a dynamics-selected ghost parity.

## 4. Parity arithmetic specialist

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: high.** Odd versus even is the right
cheap discriminator if the relevant generation observable is the multiplicity
of the same antiunitary-stable spectral object. Layer 0 remains load-bearing:
three generations, three blocks, and a three-dimensional eigenspace are
different counts, so the parity result cannot migrate to the generation ledger
until the counted object is identified.

## 5. KO-theory and Bott-periodicity specialist

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: high.** Bott periodicity explains why
the real-versus-quaternionic distinction is discrete and why complexification
forgets exactly the datum at issue. The next calculation should therefore type

“even index” risks being transferred between different carriers or gradings.

## 6. Random-matrix and Dyson specialist

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: medium.** The GOE/GSE contrast gives
a clean negative control: a matched quaternionic ensemble should exhibit

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: medium.** The tenfold-way class cannot

## B — complex-analytic lenses

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: high.** Wick rotation can connect

## 9. Signature-blindness specialist

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: very high.** The complex Clifford

rule is fail-closed: if the proposed output changes between `(9,5)` and `(7,7)`,
the computation must retain a real structure, involution, pairing or contour
that can see that difference.

## 10. Steepest-descent and Picard–Lefschetz specialist

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: medium.** A contour choice can select

**Classification: ACTUAL MATH. Confidence: low.** Changing real form can change
