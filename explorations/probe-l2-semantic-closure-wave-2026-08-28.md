# Probe L2 semantic-closure wave

The residual probe-authorship L2 category is closed at zero after semantic
review of all seventy entering reports. Fifty-one are immutable historical or
wave-snapshot pins: forty-five versioned conditional-ledger probes, one frozen
v0.17 donor crosswalk, and five exact source-action-spec wave tallies. Nineteen
are exact scientific or certificate-local invariants, including dimensions,

None is a moving shared-state pin, so none is weakened or rewritten.

The detector defect was in label attribution. It searched every quoted token
on a `check(...)` line, so expression keys such as `ledger["total"]` were
mistaken for check-label semantics. It also treated the domain word `total` as
sufficient evidence of shared-state currency, sweeping exact phrases such as
“total-current bridge” and “total trace.” The repaired rule parses only direct
string arguments of the `check(...)` call and requires both explicit moving-
state language and count language. A current global or repository-wide count
pinned by literal equality remains red; a non-regression inequality remains
green.

The authorship ratchet therefore moves from 70 findings across 1,013 probes to
zero findings across 1,014 probes. The coupled certificate reproduces the
entering 51/19 partition and passes ten semantic controls, including moving-pin,
expression-key, exact-invariant and condition-first call forms.
