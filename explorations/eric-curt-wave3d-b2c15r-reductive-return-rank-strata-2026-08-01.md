existed, it could mix the reduction and metric blocks and invalidate the
apparently clean direct sum.

construction. It was a Layer-0 mix-up between four different objects: a local

\[

\[

This restores the fixed-Shiab principal direct-sum result that B2C15 had
correctly withheld. It does **not** close the moving-Shiab, full nonlinear,

\[
