## H1 — The external datum is a HOLONOMY of boundary framing data, not a stored value

**Statement.** GU's external ledger pieces (P1 the ℤ/2 orientation; by
extension the P1/P2 weld) are not values supplied from outside but the
**holonomy class of a framing/connection choice on the boundary link**.
Their externality is the externality of a *gauge choice with monodromy*,
not of a datum handed in.

**What suggested it (atlas C2).** In the one engineered case where two
regions overlap and *either* may legitimately hold authority, the
resolving datum is a **connection**: published rules make the assignment a
function of the trajectory, not the position (SpatialOS `hex_grid`: an
overlap is "valid for either" worker; UnrealGDK ships
`MigrationBackoffTimeInSeconds` plus a migration diagnostic for actors
stuck at a boundary). Hysteresis at a boundary IS holonomy. The atlas
graded that row RHYME with a paper-executable deciding check.

**Why it lands on GU's objects.** The boundary link's deformation-retract
spine is RP³ ≅ SO(3), π₁ = ℤ/2 (`canon/boundary-einvariant-and-the-
tangential-fork.md` §7). M-H7's dim-13 restatement independently reports
that the surviving open freedom is **reframings** (general [L¹³, SO]), and
that the ℤ/3 slot specifically requires a **non-product framing**
(`explorations/mh7-dim13-restatement-2026-08-03.md`). So the framing is
already the locus of the freedom on both the P1-side and the count-side.

**Evidence class, stated before the appeal.** Atlas cluster A separately
found that the ONE place netcode meets genuine monodromy is
π₁(SO(3)) = ℤ/2 (the quaternion double-cover in rotation compression),
which both fields diagnose and repair identically. That the same group
appears in both places is **number-matching — the weakest class in the
program's own taxonomy (N13)** and is NOT offered as evidence. What is
offered is the **mechanism match**: a binary choice on an overlap resolved
by path-continuation. If the mechanism match fails, H1 fails; the group
coincidence rescues nothing.

**Deciding check (DC-H1).** Are the six B5 chirality-orbit relative signs
the ℤ/2 monodromy of the spine's double cover — i.e. is the orbit-sign
assignment a function of a path/framing class rather than of a point? Run
on existing objects (the B5 observer-symbol matrix and the spine's
covering data). Outcomes, preregistered: (a) signs = monodromy ⇒ P1 is
determined by the framing, NOT independently free, and the external ledger
REDUCES; (b) signs independent of the framing class ⇒ H1 dead, P1 stays a
value-type datum; (c) partial ⇒ report the split, no promotion.

**What H1 does NOT claim.** No physical exterior; no simulation framing;
no movement of the external-by-structure result. The exterior-admissibility
line applies and is satisfied: what the datum must satisfy is writable and
could come back empty.

## H2 — Z_U's blocked block ratio is fixed by reciprocity, not chosen

**Statement.** The `(c_b : c_f)` block ratio of the native kinetic term
`Z_U = |D_A U|²` — named as the decisive unbuilt object at the A4
(normalization) arrow — is **fixed by requiring the source pairing to be
reciprocal / self-adjoint**, rather than being independent data.

**What suggested it (atlas C1).** "Relevance" is an adjoint function:
rendering's importance is the solution of the adjoint transport equation,
and the graphics reciprocity relation IS Bell–Glasstone's neutron
reciprocity, with page-precise citation inheritance. The invariant object
in that whole family is a **reciprocal pairing** ⟨W, L⟩, and its
normalization is fixed by self-adjointness rather than by a separate
choice. The atlas's payload was that netcode occupies the identical slot
with a hand-tuned score where the derived version already exists.

**Why it lands on GU's blocker.** `explorations/de-certification-redo-
2026-08-03.md` establishes the composition ledger: A1 defined, A2
defined-conditional, A3 reconstruction-grade, **A4 UNBUILDABLE** pending
Z_U (W203 coefficient ledger, machine-tied), A5 mechanical thereafter. The
source action's job is precisely to pair a source with a field; if the
pairing must be reciprocal, the normalization is not free.

**Evidence class.** Structural analogy at the level of *what kind of
object fixes a normalization*. This is weaker than a transported theorem
and is labelled as such. Its value is cost: it is checkable against
existing GU objects without building new geometry.

**Deciding check (DC-H2).** Write the candidate source pairing on the A3
configuration and impose reciprocity (self-adjointness of the pairing
under the Krein/C structure GU already carries). Does that condition fix
`(c_b : c_f)` uniquely, fix it up to a discrete choice, or leave it free?
Preregistered: (a) uniquely fixed ⇒ A4 unblocks by derivation and M-H13's
item (a) changes status; (b) fixed up to discrete choice ⇒ the residue is
a NEW small external datum, typed and counted; (c) free ⇒ H2 dead, A4
stays BLOCKED-ON-A4 and the blocker statement is confirmed rather than
weakened.

## H3 — NEGATIVE: no fixed count can come from the algebra of a pairwise gluing

**Statement.** The "exactly one extra irreducible piece" structure (the
pullback defect, of which GU's 2+1 imposter is an instance) holds **iff
the joint constraint has arity exactly two**. Therefore no *fixed count
greater than one* can be produced by the internal algebra of a pairwise
gluing; whatever supplies a count must supply the ARITY.

**Provenance.** Atlas cluster A found it empirically (five independently
designed mechanism families produce the shape; the count always tracks the
composite's arity, never the constraint's algebra). Atlas cluster C proved
the reason: the one-extra-term result requires the cover's nerve to have
dimension ≤ 1 (no triple overlaps; Čech truncation / Mayer–Vietoris).
Real spatial partitions RAISE the arity — a 2-D grid corner sits in four
cells — and the excess becomes the full alternating sum.

**Consequence for GU, stated as a redirect, not a result.** Consistent
with the count already being killed five ways in-repo. It forbids a family
of approaches (deriving "three" from the internal structure of a two-part
base+fibre gluing) and predicts a structural property of any pullback-type
family reading: the extra piece would be **jointly determined by the two
sectors' border data**, not independently free.

**Deciding check (DC-H3).** Test the prediction against the actual
imposter block: is the (10,−4) relative gamma-trace mode a function of the
two sectors' shared/border data, or does it carry data independent of
both? Preregistered: (a) jointly determined ⇒ the pullback reading is
structurally consistent and the arity statement is the binding constraint;
(b) carries independent data ⇒ the imposter is NOT a pullback defect in
the atlas's sense and the transported intuition is retired.
