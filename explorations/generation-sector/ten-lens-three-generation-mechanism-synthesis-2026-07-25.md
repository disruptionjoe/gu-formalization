# Ten-lens synthesis: what could cause three generations?

## Decision boundary

This note preserves a divergent search over possible causes of the three
observed matter generations. It does **not** derive three, select a physical
source action, identify a flavor model, or change the generation-count
verdict.

The strongest integrated guess produced by the pass is promoted only to an
active-research conjecture:

> **Triplet--Boundary--Flavor Conjecture.** Four-dimensional self-dual
> geometry supplies a canonical three-dimensional carrier; a separately
> derived boundary/source-action spectral mechanism selects one chiral,
> physically accessible sector from each mirror pair; and symmetry breaking
> plus renormalization-group dynamics differentiates their masses and
> mixings.

The controlling active-research gate is
`lab/active-research/triplet-boundary-flavor-conjecture-2026-07-25.md`.

## What the question actually contains

“Why three generations?” combines three logically different problems:

1. **Carrier multiplicity:** why does the available representation space
   contain a natural triplet rather than an arbitrary fitted three-copy
   factor?
2. **Physical selection:** why do three chiral, light, observer-accessible
   families survive rather than three vectorlike mirror pairs, another
   integer index, or an unrestricted completion?
3. **Flavor differentiation:** why do the surviving families have their
   observed mass hierarchy and mixing matrices?

A proposed mechanism that answers only one of these questions has not answered
the other two. In particular, finding a three-dimensional representation is
not the same as deriving three chiral generations.

## Frozen GU facts

The pass starts from the following repository-controlled facts rather than
from the desired answer:

- GU has a canonical odd carrier in four-dimensional self-dual geometry: the
  `SU(2)_+` triplet / `Lambda^2_+` sector. The current observer carrier can be
  written schematically as `3E_+ + 3E_- + X`.
- That carrier is vectorlike. The current admissible Krein/chirality
  structure gives net chiral count zero; it does not turn representation
  multiplicity into three physical Standard Model generations.
- A bare order-three torsion class is not an integer generation count.
  In particular, `Hom(Z/3,Z)=0`; an independently constructed integer
  observable and a lawful bridge are required.
- The only still-serious fine topological bridge currently located in the
  repository is an order-three equivariant spectral `rho`/`eta` object for a
  source-owned action. The required physical source-action operator has not
  been constructed.
- K3 arithmetic, flux choices, fitted ranks, threshold values chosen to return
  three, and post hoc division by a generation unit are controls or imports,
  not derivations.

These facts preserve the standing verdict: **the number three is located but
not forced**.

## Relative assessment

The lenses do not have equal evidentiary standing:

1. **Best-supported component:** the geometric triplet carrier. It is a real
   structural fact, but only answers the carrier question.
2. **Most promising missing bridge:** an order-three equivariant
   source-action `rho`/`eta` or APS index. It is the narrowest route that could
   convert the located triplet into a protected physical count without simply
   choosing a flux of three.
3. **Most conventional completion:** symmetry breaking and RG flow for masses
   and mixings. This is credible machinery, but it becomes explanatory only
   if it is more constrained than a general Yukawa fit.
4. **Most distinct Dynamic Unity extension:** typed regional
   certification/access and holonomy. It can discipline what “physically
   survives” means, but cannot replace the missing GU operator or index.
5. **Strongest skeptical alternative:** more modes are fundamental and only
   three are light or accessible. This should remain an explicit completion
   class because current low-energy evidence does not by itself exclude every
   heavy or sterile family-like sector.

`TBF-A` is structurally located. `TBF-B` is open and load-bearing. `TBF-C` is

## Global kill conditions

The integrated conjecture is killed, rather than merely weakened, if any of
the following survives the complete admitted construction class:

1. no source-owned admissible operator can evade the vectorlike/net-zero
   chirality result;
2. every order-three spectral refinement has zero or nonintegral pairing with
   every independently justified physical count;
3. every occurrence of integer three enters through a selected flux, rank,
   boundary condition, threshold, K3 normalization, or completion chosen
   after the target is known;
4. the triplet carrier can be removed or replaced without changing any
   physical prediction or capability; or
5. the flavor stage has no held-out consequence beyond a general
   Standard-Model Yukawa refit.

A failure of one particular operator, threshold, code, or regional model is a
local formalization failure. It becomes a conjecture kill only when the
relevant construction family is exhausted or a no-go reaches the gate
invariant.
