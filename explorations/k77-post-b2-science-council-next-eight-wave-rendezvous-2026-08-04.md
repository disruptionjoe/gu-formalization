## Ten inline specialist lenses

### 1. Differential geometer

The pointwise Riemann restriction is real progress, but the scientific object
is a natural associated-bundle map on `Y^14`. Classify full-domain extensions,
soldering/atlas descent, and active-`epsilon` covariance in one gate. Do not
call a fixed-frame map global.

### 2. Representation and invariant theorist

The efficient search order is irreducible decomposition, equivariant Hom
basis, real-form/reality intersection, then expression representatives.
Canonical DAG enumeration should represent a computed finite Hom space, not
serve as a brute-force substitute for it. Sage and FLINT are appropriate for
characters, exact ranks, ideals, and exceptional loci.

### 3. Variational-bicomplex and BV specialist

An eligible Shiab must define a cyclic trilinear/action density whose Euler
operator satisfies Helmholtz. Only after that should the mixed super-IG action
be asked to generate a Noether/BV differential. A bracket, projector, or
characteristic kernel cannot substitute for an off-shell Ward identity.

### 4. Hyperbolic PDE and operator theorist

Principal-symbol adjointness may be tested pointwise, but Green data and a
physical domain require the descended, moving coupled operator. Keep the
Lorentz-section Cauchy route distinct from a genuine ambient ultrahyperbolic
boundary problem. Do not import a positive Hilbert metric into the native
Krein problem.

### 5. Symplectic and covariant-phase-space geometer

The tilted inhomogeneous gauge group, action-derived presymplectic current,
and BFV boundary reduction are the natural meeting point of Eric's guidance
and the independent construction. Derive the quotient from degeneracies of
the same action; never guess the Jordan projector and call it gauge.

### 6. Standard-Model and fermion phenomenologist

Carrier support is only the first rung. The next useful physics check is
whether one observed Euler system produces the correct chiral multiplet
couplings, gauge currents, photon kernel, Goldstone count, and mirror-mode
decoupling. Update the atomic ledger after every physics-emitting wave.

### 7. Topology, index, and anomaly specialist

D1 is a time-orientation-cover problem; P3 is a separate real-KO/count
candidate; the odd-primary generation bridge cannot travel through a
spin-only 2-primary wall. Apply each only after the physical operator and
domain exist, and require a natural map into the actual receiving slot.

### 8. Gravity and cosmology specialist

The trace-reversed Frobenius fibre, gauge-rotated Levi-Civita/contorsion, and
olive/cosmological VEV must be varied in the same action that emits the
Einstein equation. Run the black-hole residual and PP3/DESI shape as sealed
tests; do not tune the vacuum after seeing them.

### 9. Statistical ML and computational-algebra engineer

No standalone ML model is needed now. If the exact invariant basis or
candidate strata become large, use active fixture selection, sensitivity,
and surrogate ranking only to choose the next exact computation. Admission is
always rational reconstruction plus exact held-out identities. Constraint
surplus is scored only after gauge, field-redefinition, normalization,
boundary, and redundant-parameter quotients.

### 10. Distributed-systems and proof-engineering specialist

Use the evidence ledger like a monotone provenance DAG, not as a model of the
field equations. Each wave must move one named gate, declare exact inputs and
outputs, propagate superseding results, and carry two hostile charges:
summary outruns artifact; rigor defends a superseded object.

**Held out:** analytic domain, stationary vacuum, index/count value.

## Scheduling and efficiency rules

- Waves are sequential at their named gates. Parallelize only independent
  candidate construction, source collision, and held-out fixture preparation
  inside a wave.
- Every wave uses inline divergent pre-assessment and at least two hostile
  post-review charges: summary outruns artifact; artifact answers a superseded
  object. Field-specific reviewers remain required for verdict changes.
- Activate ML ranking only when the exact symmetry-reduced candidate set is
  large enough to justify it; all acceptances remain exact.
- A wave with no possible named-gate movement is prerequisite work and must be
  batched into its parent rather than receiving a suffix.
- Construction outputs trigger their own hardening. Hardening never leads the
  queue merely because it is easier to finish.

## Final council judgment

The most effective path is not “keep doing B3 forever” and not “leave K77 for
the Eric lane.” It is one bounded B3 construction, followed by a forced
rendezvous at the source action, then observation/datum, early local physics,
domain, vacuum/cosmology, fermion/anomaly/count, and a frozen integrated test.

That sequence preserves what has actually worked, absorbs the newest datum
result, reaches physics sooner, and makes every remaining external datum pay
rent through a natural arrow into a constructed object.
