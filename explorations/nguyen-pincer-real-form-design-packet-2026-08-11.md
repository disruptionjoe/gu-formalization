source's own `U(64,64)` presentation, but its strongest surviving form is

chiral branch is fatally anomalous with no Green-Schwarz escape
(`cb-c-anomaly-conditions-2026-08-05.md` 2.3(B), 4.2 H1). The real-form pincer
