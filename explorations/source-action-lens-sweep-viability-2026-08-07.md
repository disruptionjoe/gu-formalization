# Source-action lens sweep: what survived a viability check

## Framing, stated honestly

A 25-lens sweep proposed what H41 **is**, why it **exists**, and what the
**external datum** is. Most lenses produced analogies. An analogy is not a
hypothesis and cannot be tested; treating one as a research target would produce
prose, not results. Four lenses produced falsifiable propositions. Three cheap
checks were run against them. This file records those three outcomes and the two
propositions that earned a run plan.

**Twenty-one of the twenty-five lenses are recorded as framing only and are
deliberately not carried forward.** That is a result, not an omission.

## Check 1 — the principles / parameters / lexicon mapping

**Proposition (generative-grammar lens).** The requirement spec's
`8 FORCED / 9 DECLARATION / 10 FIT` split is a principles / parameters / lexicon
split, and therefore effort spent deriving FIT rows is misdirected by
construction.

**Executed.** All 27 rows parsed and classified from
`explorations/source-action-requirements-spec-2026-07-13.md`:

```text
FORCED       ( 8): SA-Y1, SA-Y7a, SA-G9, SA-C2, SA-C4, SA-U1, SA-U3, SA-U4
DECLARATION  ( 9): SA-Y2, SA-Y5, SA-Y6, SA-Y8, SA-G1, SA-C1, SA-C3, SA-U2, SA-U5
FIT          (10): SA-Y3, SA-Y4, SA-Y7b, SA-G2, SA-G3, SA-G4, SA-G5, SA-G6, SA-G7, SA-G8
```

**Outcome: two terms hold, the third is refuted, and the refutation is the
useful part.**

- `FORCED` ↔ principles **holds**. The legend defines FORCED as "a theorem or
  exact computation says the source action MUST contain or satisfy this item;
  failing it is an inconsistency, not a modeling choice."
- `DECLARATION` ↔ parameters **holds strongly**. The legend defines it as "a
  choice the source action makes; **the built structure provably cannot decide
  it**."
- `FIT` ↔ lexicon **is refuted by the repository's own definition**. FIT is "a
  number the source action **must output** (or leave explicitly free)." The lens
  claimed FIT is irreducibly imported. These are opposite claims.

**Why the refutation matters.** Neither side is proved. The spec's FIT definition
records the program's *intent* that H41 emits these numbers; it is not a theorem
that H41 can. So the lens survives as a **live rival hypothesis to the program's
own stated position**, which is a sharper object than the analogy it came from.
If the lens is right, the falsifiability-keystone claim (H53: every empirical
channel gates on the scale H41 would force) weakens, because ten of the numbers
would not be H41's to force.

**The correction relocates the payload.** The set that is irreducibly external is
not FIT. It is `DECLARATION`, because DECLARATION is *defined* by a proof that
the built structure cannot decide it. That is the strongest formalization of
"external datum" available in this repository, and it is nine named rows. This
converts the sweep's vaguest cluster — six independent lenses asserting the
external datum is structurally irreducible rather than merely unfound — into one
well-posed question, carried to the investigation plan as Test A.

## Check 2 — a coincidence, killed before it became a finding

The ledger residue carries `9 forks`. The requirement spec has `9 DECLARATION`
rows. The match invites the reading that they are the same nine.

**They are not.** `lab/process/layer0-fork-registry.yaml` holds **12 entries, 10
open**. Three surfaces use the word "fork" and report 9, 10 and 9 over three
different sets:

| surface | count | what it counts |
|---|---|---|
| conditional physics ledger, residue | 9 | discrete forks/strata in the residue |
| layer0 fork registry | 12 (10 open) | named Layer-0 construction forks |
| source-action requirements spec | 9 | DECLARATION rows |

Recorded here because this is a homonym forming in real time, of exactly the
class `GEOMETER-VS-PHYSICS-OBJECTS.md` exists to catch. **No inference may be
drawn from the numerical agreement.** If a future artifact wants to relate these
sets it must construct the map explicitly.

## Check 3 — interface ownership of the imported scales

**Proposition (network-layering and coding lenses).** The imported scale belongs
to the *interface* between the ambient 14D and observed 4D layers, not to either
layer — which would explain why no purely-ambient or purely-observed derivation
can reach it.

**Partially confirmed.**

- DC-H2's `(c_b : c_f)` is defined as "the horizontal:vertical relative scale of
  that same `G`" (`dc-h2-reciprocity-and-the-zu-block-ratio-2026-08-04.md:223`).
  That is literally a ratio across the layer boundary. **Confirmed for this
  scale.**
- `ell^2 = Z_U*kappa` (`pred-norm-rank-2026-07-15.md:35, 80`) is recorded as
  invariant under local field redefinition but not numerically fixed. Its layer
  ownership is **not stated**, so the proposition is untested for it.

**The stronger signal is the recorded circularity.** DC-H2 states that "to state
a reciprocity condition sharp enough to constrain the ratio, one must first fix
the pairing with respect to which" the ratio is defined (`:225`). A self-
reference obstruction is what the interface reading *predicts*, rather than
merely notes. If that obstruction can be shown to be structural rather than
incidental, a recorded blocker becomes a theorem about where imported data must
live. Carried to the investigation plan as a secondary objective under Test A.

## What was promoted, and what was not

**Promoted to `historical-investigation`:**

- **Test A — the DECLARATION floor.** Of the 9 DECLARATION rows, how many admit
  derivation by a new mechanism and how many are permanently undecidable from
  within? This answers whether P1/P2/P3 sitting unconsumed is a failure state or
  the answer.
- **Test B — rank the quotients.** CB-D states that **zero** quotients have been
  ranked and that 83 is an upper bound *before* quotients. Three lenses
  independently proposed that H41 is a selector rather than a thing — that the
  real object is the dependency structure collapsing the 83. Test B is that
  claim made computational, and it is work the repository already says is owed.

**Not promoted.** The remaining 21 lenses produced framing without a test. They
are not recorded as work items and should not be revived as such. If one later
yields a falsifiable proposition, that proposition — not the lens — is the
artifact.
