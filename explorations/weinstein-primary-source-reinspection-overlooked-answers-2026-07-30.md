rival branches**.

## Plain English

Weinstein's documentation does not tell us the missing answer outright, but
it does tell us we may have assembled the candidate in the wrong order.

Our current action contains a matter action and then a second term that
couples the distortion to a matter current. When we vary the connection, the
same current can cancel itself. Weinstein's draft instead says: vary the
bosonic and fermionic pieces, put both results in one equation, and treat
that combined result as the object to solve. If that source-native
organization survives exact typing, it removes the most suspicious
same-current cancellation without fitting a new coefficient.

His draft also says the curvature contraction by itself is not the
derivative of an action. It needs a specific nonlinear completion. That gives
us a concrete test: add the source-shaped completion, vary it, and see
whether the gauge identity works. If it does not, we learn exactly which
source-to-repo map fails.

Finally, the draft says the mass-like scalar should be a scalar component of
the gauge potential whose vacuum value also participates in the
cosmological sector. That is the kind of overdetermined common carrier we
want: one geometric object has to satisfy the Standard Model/Yukawa,
gravity/dark-energy, Krein/reality, and covariance constraints at once.
