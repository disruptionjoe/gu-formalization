## The finding that matters most for stewardship

**Absorption here is CONTENT-addressed, not PATH-addressed.** Of the
2026-08-10 batch, only one note is cited by filename anywhere; four
others are cited ZERO times yet are fully dispositioned by content in two
files. **A filename-keyed drain would have re-opened four already-decided
notes.** Conversely one note IS cited by mailbox path
(`papers/candidates/located-not-forced/STAGING-NOTES.md:228-237`), so the
channel is read sometimes and cited rarely.

Consequence: the queue was **unarchived**, not uniformly unread. Those are
different diseases. The archive discipline failed; absorption partly did
not.

**But the failure is concentrated, not diffuse.** Six of the eight live
notes are from a single window, 2026-07-27 to 07-29. The 08-10 batch is
almost entirely absorbed. Whatever went wrong went wrong in one week.

## Process consequence

cited or absorbed. Two mechanisms would have prevented most of this: a
periodic drain with receipts (this pass), and a pointer discipline — the

treat it as expected rather than optional.
