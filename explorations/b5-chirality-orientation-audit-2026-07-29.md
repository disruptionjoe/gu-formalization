## The distinction this investigation turns on

The phase-sum audit found chirality grading is not blind to six of the ten
special orbits, and claimed only **capability**, never that a forced rule fires.
That gap is the whole question:

- **Distinguishing** — the two cells carry different labels. Fixes nothing.
- **Orienting** — something canonically says *which* member takes the `+` phase.
  Only an orientation fixes a sign, hence the signed sum.

A label difference with no canonical direction is a **convention**, not a datum.
