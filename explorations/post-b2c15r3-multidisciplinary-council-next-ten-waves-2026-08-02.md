## How statistics, ML, and counterfactuals enter

The allowed pipeline is:

1. construct the symmetry-reduced legal basis;
2. use a heuristic to rank candidates or fixtures;
3. reconstruct rational or algebraic expressions;
4. test held-out exact fixtures; and
5. produce a symbolic, cohomological, or analytic certificate.

Useful methods include:

- automatic differentiation to expose missing action owners;
- leverage-score or D-optimal fixture selection;
- sparse interpolation of candidate polynomial factors and exceptional loci;
- equivariant neural ranking of already legal contraction graphs;
- sensitivity/Jacobian analysis to locate invisible parameter directions;
- Bayesian expected-information scheduling; and
- one-variable-at-a-time counterfactual interventions across lanes.

Not allowed as scientific closure:

- numerical rank without exact minors or a justified analytic bound;
- a learned formula without exact covariance and Ward/Green checks;
- a posterior probability that GU is true;
- consensus confidence as a selector; or
- a causal story without a constructed intervention and invariance map.

After the physical quotient exists, define the independent constraint rank as
the generic rank of the held-out observed response. Define effective parameter
dimension after removing gauge, field redefinitions, normalization, boundary
terms, and response-invisible directions. Only then compute

\[
\text{constraint surplus}
=\operatorname{rank}(D R_{\rm heldout})
-\dim(\Theta_{\rm effective}).
\]

This is how a reverse-engineered fit can become informative rather than
circular.
