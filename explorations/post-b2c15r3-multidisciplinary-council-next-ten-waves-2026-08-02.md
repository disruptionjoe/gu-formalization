# Post-B2C15R3 multidisciplinary council

## Outcome first

The campaign is making real construction progress, and it is still pursuing
the original goal: build the source action and the smallest external datum
that make the GU geometry close while preserving the Standard Model, GR,
quantum, and cosmological legs.

The smartest change is not a new theory lane. It is a stricter construction
order:

1. construct or obstruct the **actual public-source-to-native real-form
   bundle port**;
2. assemble the **complete native first jet, action variation, and effective
   operator**;
3. let that action derive its own **Noether/BV quotient and covariant phase
   space**;
4. only then determine whether observation leaves a unique choice, a finite
   torsor, or continuous external data;
5. solve the physical domain/evolution problem on that quotient before
   proceeding to the full matter, Higgs, generation, quantum, and cosmology
   legs.

This reorders work that was already scaffolded. It does not abandon the guided
construction or erase the independent-native work.

Two first-wave branches should rendezvous instead of competing silently:

- the recorded alternative gate `B2C15R4`, which must build the actual native
  port and `Alt(T)` owner; and
- the guided gate `B2C2C`, which must build the mixed-carrier super-IG action
  representation and Ward owner.

They can run in parallel against one shared interface contract. They may merge
only if the actual bundle, action, and variation maps agree. Otherwise the
result is an exact branch-separation theorem, not a blended construction.

## Ten inline engineering personas

These personas ask what would be missing if the mathematics had to become a
working, testable system rather than a collection of local arguments.

### 1. ML platform and inverse-design engineer

“Compile the legal language before optimizing it.” The missing artifact is one
executable construction graph:

`T -> Alt(T) -> K -> connection -> curvature -> Shiab -> action -> Euler -> quotient -> observation`.

They would wrap this graph with automatic differentiation, active fixture
selection, rational reconstruction, and exact verifier hooks. A neural system
may rank expressions, but it may not cross bundle, real-form, right-H, Krein,
or ownership boundaries.

### 2. Industrial statistician and reliability engineer

“Separate design tests, qualification tests, and acceptance tests.” The repo
needs sealed discovery and confirmation banks, gauge-quotiented
identifiability, and explicit power against rival lanes. They would use
D-optimal or leverage-based exact fixture selection, freeze coefficients, and
compute rank-based constraint surplus only afterward. A constraint used to
tune the action cannot be counted again as a prediction.

“A solver is only as correct as its assembled residual, Jacobian, boundary
operators, and convergence study.” They see the unassembled `A1 Z1` and moving
coefficients as a stop, not a nuisance. They would compare symbolic and
automatic Jacobians, create exact manufactured solutions, and test patch/mesh
convergence before any characteristic or spectral claim.

### 4. Pressure-vessel and structural-integrity engineer

“Trace the complete load path; local stress does not certify the vessel.” They
would build an energy/flux balance sheet from the trace-reversed fibre through
the Krein pairing, Green boundary flux, observation support, and physical
stress. Null loading, sign reversal, and coupled worst-case modes become
mandatory tests. Installing a positive block metric is an unpriced structural
reinforcement if it changes the native geometry.

### 5. Flight-control and safety-critical systems engineer

“Prove observability and hidden-mode safety before tuning a controller.” They
would compute whether the physical quotient is observable from the section,
whether the no-leakage manifold is invariant, and whether action-derived
evolution can stabilize it. A section that hides a nongauge unstable mode is a
failed design even if its visible equations look right.

### 6. Distributed-systems and site-reliability engineer

“Name the state, validity rule, identity, fault model, recovery rule, and
source of truth.” They would keep deterministic Čech checks in the critical
path, use a provenance DAG for calculations and receipts, and inject patch,
gauge, and update-order failures. Eventual agreement is not evidence that the
agreed object is geometrically valid.

### 7. ZK protocol and proof-circuit engineer

“Write the statement and public inputs before building the witness.” They
would emit proof-carrying construction packets bound to the actual bundle
transitions, invariant forms, jets, and action variation. Their primary

the active native system.

### 8. Computational-geometry and mesh-infrastructure engineer

“Coordinate-free intent still needs actual charts and transitions.” They
would build a small representative `Y14` atlas, observation-section mesh, and
induced-bundle testbed, then require invariance under recharting, refinement,
and non-unimodular fibre transformations. The `SL2 -> Sp4` fixture remains a
unit test, not the implementation.

### 9. Scientific-instrumentation and data-acquisition engineer

“An observable needs a calibrated chain from state to record.” They interpret
the section/current/domain packet as an instrument specification: support,
normalization, response, bandwidth, null channels, and injection-recovery all
have to close. Any calibration constant or projection matrix that carries the
desired Standard Model answer is a hidden datum.

### 10. Systems-integration and verification engineer

“Maintain a thin vertical slice while deepening the components.” They would
freeze one explicitly nonphysical smoke fixture and propagate it through every
wave so interface gaps surface early. A separate sealed physical bank remains
untouched until acceptance. This avoids reaching Wave 10 with ten individually
impressive components that require new adapters to connect.

complete effective symbol before phase-space reduction; the symplectic lens
wants the native action port before phase-space language; the engineering
lenses want an end-to-end fixture immediately. The ten-wave scaffold satisfies
all three: port first, full operator second, derived quotient third, with a
nonphysical integration fixture running throughout.

## How statistics, ML, and counterfactuals enter

The allowed pipeline is:

1. construct the symmetry-reduced legal basis;
2. use a heuristic to rank candidates or fixtures;
3. reconstruct rational or algebraic expressions;
4. test held-out exact fixtures; and
5. produce a symbolic, cohomological, or analytic certificate.

Useful methods include:

- automatic differentiation to expose missing action owners;
- leverage-score or D-optimal fixture selection;
- sparse interpolation of candidate polynomial factors and exceptional loci;
- equivariant neural ranking of already legal contraction graphs;
- sensitivity/Jacobian analysis to locate invisible parameter directions;
- Bayesian expected-information scheduling; and
- one-variable-at-a-time counterfactual interventions across lanes.

Not allowed as scientific closure:

- numerical rank without exact minors or a justified analytic bound;
- a learned formula without exact covariance and Ward/Green checks;
- a posterior probability that GU is true;
- consensus confidence as a selector; or
- a causal story without a constructed intervention and invariance map.

After the physical quotient exists, define the independent constraint rank as
the generic rank of the held-out observed response. Define effective parameter
dimension after removing gauge, field redefinitions, normalization, boundary
terms, and response-invisible directions. Only then compute

\[
\text{constraint surplus}
=\operatorname{rank}(D R_{\rm heldout})
-\dim(\Theta_{\rm effective}).
\]

This is how a reverse-engineered fit can become informative rather than
circular.
