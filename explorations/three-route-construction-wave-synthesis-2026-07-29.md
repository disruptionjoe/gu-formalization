## Route A: what died and what remains

The proposed repair

```text

```

does not produce the desired native Bott deck grading. The exact algebraic
fork is:

```text

i S_nat commutes with J_H, but (i S_nat)^2 = -1.
```

Thus no allowed phase makes this product both a deck involution and
right-quaternionic-linear. Separately, the standard doubled `H`-line Bott
mass passes its right-`H`, deck, and unit-gap controls, while the natural
diagonal lift to the native `H^64` carrier scales the local clutching density
by exactly 64. Selecting one coordinate restores one copy only by failing a
carrier-mixing naturality control.

This kills the direct `S_nat` admission and any unpriced unit-class/P3 use of
it. It does not prove that no native mirror pair exists. The lawful reopener
is an already-owned GU mirror pair with a different right-`H`-linear
involution, an RS-preserving mass embedding, and a functorial
Clifford--Morita pushforward. A Callias-domain campaign before those maps
would harden the wrong object.

## Route C: the actual fibre carries the bit

The earlier abstract mixed-plane loop now has an actual gimmel realization.
For

```text

```

```text

E -> B_t^T E B_t  on Sym^2T*X.
```

At the generator endpoint:

```text

Sym^2T*X:   2 positive + 2 negative reversals
total:       3 positive + 3 negative reversals.
```

Therefore the actual positive-frame orientation character is `-1`, and the
doubled loop is `+1`. A reference Clifford lift returns the distinct

gives the central irreducible-spinor mismatch `-I`.

noncentral assignment passes a permissive support matcher and fails strict
centrality. The P1/P2 one-bit merger therefore remains conditional on a
source-owned associated-bundle transport, not on another sign computation.
