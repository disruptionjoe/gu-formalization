# Wave 1A: absorb Batch 2 without choosing Batch 3's answer

## Outcome

Batch 2 is now absorbed into the source-action/external-datum campaign. The
result is deliberately **not** a lane selection. It moves the named
`POST-B3-LANE-RESELECTION` gate from `OPEN` to
`BATCH2_ABSORBED_AWAITING_BATCH3_FORK_RESOLVERS`: all surviving routes now
inherit the corrected object types. The named Batch 3 results are the minimum
currently known fork resolvers for the final route decision; new source or
mathematical evidence may add or retype a blocker.

decide the winning route until the listed fork resolvers land.” It does not
mean stop construction. The present interior, Eric-guided, independent-native,
and boundary work remain live as construction routes, not four new repository
Lanes. No third Lane is promoted.

## What this does to the old build

The 2026-07-29 term table is valuable construction provenance, not the current
jointly typed action. Its `T1`--`T9` labels record where terms were first placed,
but several receiving maps were not constructed. In particular:

- `P1/P2/P3` may select among already admissible objects only after natural
  arrows into receiving slots exist. They cannot manufacture a current,
  Riesz map, BV differential, projector, domain, or missing bundle morphism.
- P2 is typed at **algebraic-candidate grade** as the phase/orientation of the
  canonical vertical projected RS symbol on X. Its identification with the
  independently constructed fibre-loop coflip, the differential, and P1
  remains conditional. J settles none of those identifications.
- P3 remains the distinct integer-valued count/index datum. The candidate
  order-3 homotopy carrier is a separate object; the map from it to P3 is open
  and possibly a category error.
- The vertical--Krein weld supplies a candidate Yukawa carrier, not the
  background/VEV, texture, stationary orbit, or Majorana mass construction.
- The current interior action campaign has built substantial finite and
  variational comparators, but has not yet assembled one actual native
  `Y^14` action, its action-derived quotient, common domain, observation
  pushdown, and physical equations.

The correct next question is therefore not “do we need an external datum and a
source action?” It is: **which minimal action constructs the receiving
structures, and what is the smallest datum left after admissibility and gauge
equivalence have been derived?**
