# Wave 3D-B2C5 — frozen-principal action candidate, Green control, and curved Ward gate

## Plain-English result

The already-filtered B2C4 repair admits a typed quadratic fermion-action
class. That is the central constructive result of this swing. An independent
Krein-lowered dual pairs with the complete repaired rolled operator in the
right top degree, while the coefficient symbol passes the active
Krein/right-`H` and principal charge-reality gates. The executable variation
is deliberately narrower: it is a frozen finite algebraic emission control,
not yet the full covariant Hodge/density/integration-by-parts derivation.

The action does **not** explain everything about that equation. It does not
force the reciprocal `11/12` coefficient, it does not tie the two chiral
weights, and it does not reduce the surviving family to a discrete datum.
Those reciprocal equations arise only when the separately declared physical
section dynamics is required to be Dirac-like and semisimple. After the one
explicitly verified frozen-principal chiral-boost field equivalence is
removed, two continuous principal-action parameters remain: an overall
source coupling and one dimensionless shape. Extension of that equivalence
through `M0`, moving Hodge/density, the connection graph, boundary action, and
domain is unproved. Only if one deliberately identifies actions on the same Euler-equation
ray does the shape remain alone. Neither parameter is P1, P2, or P3.

The PDE result also strengthens. B2C4 had direction-dependent positive
majorants. Here one positive right-`H` matrix symmetrizes all three spatial
evolution matrices at once. Thus the repaired section system has a concrete
candidate energy.

Two closures nevertheless fail to follow automatically:

1. the maximally incoming energy boundary space is not isotropic for the
   action's Green form, so a dissipative evolution domain does not by itself
   make the variational boundary term vanish; and
2. the flat spinorial sequence is not yet a curved Noether complex. Exact
   Levi-Civita right-composition fixtures match one-half Einstein-gamma. A
   commuting scalar endomorphism fixture and hand-built torsion/moving-weight
   arrays expose possible independent remainder types; admissible GU-adjoint
   curvature and derived first-jet formulas remain untested. A coupled Ward
   identity therefore still needs reconstructed fermion-to-boson maps and the
   graph-complete G2/G3 differential Euler module.

The result is a genuine forward construction with a precise split verdict:
the principal action class and ordinary one-time dynamics survive, but the
complete covariant variation, overall coupling/shape selection, curved
differential Euler-module closure, and a common energy/variational boundary
domain remain to be built.
