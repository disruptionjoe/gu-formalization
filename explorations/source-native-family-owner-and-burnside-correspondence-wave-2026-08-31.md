# Family/owner identifiability and Burnside correspondence Big Wave

This wave closes two independent proof-stable interfaces that were explicitly
open at the current frontier.

## Source-native identifiability result

The held representation packet contains two equivalent family copies and two
surviving Pati--Salam-preserving symmetric-quadratic owners, `54` and `210`.
Lean now proves:

- invariance under exchange of the family copies is exactly equality of the
  two family coefficients;
- such an invariant packet cannot select exactly one family copy;
- invariant `54`-only and `210`-only packets both exist and are distinct.

Thus family symmetry removes a one-family selector but does not determine the
quadratic-owner axis. A source-owned action term, family covector, and
independent `54`/`210` coefficients remain required.

## Burnside categorical result

For a supplied homomorphism `phi : H -> K`, categorical restriction defines
the additive correspondence

`P(A,B) = Hom_H(A, Res_phi B)`.

Precomposition by an `H`-span and postcomposition by the restriction of a
`K`-span are additive and commute. For subgroup inclusion, the ordinary
set-action induction/restriction adjunction sends induced equivariant maps to
graph elements in this correspondence, with both adjunction roundtrips exact.

This is the correctly typed induction direction at the present ceiling. It is
not an induction functor on Burnside span categories: ordinary induction need
not preserve the pullbacks that define span composition. Full biset
composition or Mackey 2-functor coherence remains open.

## Claim ceiling

No physical action, observed family, mass, selector, quotient, prediction, or
Geometric Unity verdict changes. The results are exact deductions from
explicit supplied algebraic and categorical premises.
