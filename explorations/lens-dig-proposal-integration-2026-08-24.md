## Question and method

The parent register item compressed the live residues into eight labels, while
the four source artifacts each carried proposal text in their card sections.
The integration therefore re-read every proposed-register section, treated the
compressed title as a pointer rather than an exhaustive count, and compared
each proposal against current owner truth through the 2026-08-24 B1P1 close.

The comparison is intentionally three-way:

1. `DONE_LATER` when a current owner receipt actually implements the proposal;
2. `QUEUED_NARROWED` when the proposal remains live but later work shrinks its
   residue or adds a dependency; and
3. `QUEUED_AS_FILED` when its original owner, activation and ceiling remain
   current.

No scientific proposal is accepted by being registered. The register prevents
loss; the named owner and activation still govern execution and acceptance.
