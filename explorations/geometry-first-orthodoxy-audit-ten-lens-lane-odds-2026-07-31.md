# Geometry-first orthodoxy audit

## Result first

The concern is real, especially in the independent N1/N3 construction.
Several familiar physical structures were placed into that candidate action
before the fourteen-dimensional geometry had a chance to emit them:

- an ambient Yang--Mills curvature square;
- a coefficient-bearing Dirac/Rarita--Schwinger operator and curvature vertex;
- a four-dimensional Seiberg--Witten defect square;
- explicit Krein- and charge-conjugation Yukawa terms;
- free `Y_K/Y_C` flavour matrices, a selected `P_0`, and optional spurions;
- a bulk-plus-defect action; and
- induced-gravity and bare-vacuum terms in the broader family.

That does not make the construction illegitimate. N1 was explicitly written
as a constraint-satisfaction candidate. It asks whether familiar physical
pieces can coexist on the GU carrier. But success against those same pieces
cannot later be counted as independent evidence that they emerged.

The Eric-guided G1--G3 lane is a cleaner geometry-first experiment. It has
not yet inserted an independent Yang--Mills term, Dirac matter action,
Yukawa matrix, Higgs potential, defect action, Standard Model representation,
or cosmological model. More importantly, it allowed exact variation to reject
the expected compressed Eric equation and emit a less familiar
slot-symmetrized Euler covector. G3 then derived the coupled Ward identity and
preboundary geometry from that action.

But the guided lane is not a derivation from “four” alone. It still starts
after substantial structure is supplied: a Lorentzian/spin sector, the GU
trace-reversed metric normalization, a principal `H`-bundle, the decision to
gauge an ambient connection, the IG/tilted grammar, a particular first-order
action family, its invariant pairing and `kappa_1`, and the ordinary gauge
algebra used in the partial BV completion.

The honest result is therefore:

> Only the `4+10=14` metric-bundle carrier is close to automatic. The present
> program has not yet shown that gauge theory, the physical Dirac operator,
> photons, forces, the Standard Model, or cosmology are uniquely forced by it.
> The guided lane currently gives those structures more room to emerge; the
> control lane currently supplies more of them as test architecture.

## Plain English

Starting with a four-dimensional manifold gives a ten-dimensional space of
symmetric metric components at every point. That is why the metric bundle has
fourteen dimensions. It does **not** automatically tell us which connection
is a physical force, which automorphisms should be gauged, what action those
fields obey, which spinor modes are particles, or which vertical scalar is a
Higgs.

The independent lane has been saying, roughly, “put the best versions of
Yang--Mills, Dirac, Yukawa, gravity, and an observer defect on this geometry,
then see whether they close.” That builds quickly, but some of the desired
answer is already present in the question.

The guided lane now says, roughly, “write the source-shaped bosonic geometry,
vary exactly what was written, and see what equations and boundary structures
it actually produces before adding familiar particle physics.” That is the
cleaner emergence experiment, though it may fail when asked to produce matter
and observation physics.

Standard Model and cosmological knowledge should not be discarded. It should
be kept behind a sealed wall and used afterward as a hostile test. An
unfamiliar emergent theory does not get to contradict experiment, but it does
get to exist mathematically before being rejected for failing to look
term-for-term like a familiar Lagrangian.

The starting ladder must be kept explicit.

| layer | object | status |

The normalization point matters. The repository's own normalization ledger
uses

\[
G_\lambda(S,T)
=\operatorname{tr}(g^{-1}Sg^{-1}T)
-\lambda\operatorname{tr}_g(S)\operatorname{tr}_g(T),
\]

with source-native `lambda=1/2`. Four-dimensionality supplies the trace
decomposition, but not the selection of that coefficient from the whole
natural family. RB7 shows that trace reversal is load-bearing once selected;
that is different from proving it is uniquely forced at the start.

> “The carrier admits this familiar object” is not “the source action selects

## 3. Where the control lane is template-shaped

The independent lane's N1/N3 action contains real mathematical objects, and
many are well typed. The concern is epistemic ownership.

| inserted or selected object | what it legitimately tests | what it cannot evidence by itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `int <F_A,*F_A>` | compatibility of ambient curvature-square dynamics | emergence of Yang--Mills from the metric bundle |

N3's `J_D/J_F` discovery illustrates the cost. The action-derived current is
valuable, but `J_D` exists because a Dirac-type term was already installed.
The bridge then has to decide whether it cancels `J_D`, `J_D+J_F`, or a
different soldered current. This is an internal-coherence test of inserted
matter structure, not yet its derivation.

## 4. Where the guided lane still assumes too much

The cleaner guided lane has five important debits.

1. **Physical gauging is supplied.** `Cl(9,5)` makes a spin/Krein
   automorphism group available, but no theorem yet shows that the full
   `Sp(32,32;H)` group—or a particular stabilizer—is the physical local gauge
   symmetry with a freely varied connection.
2. **The trace normalization is program-native, not dimension-forced.** Its
   exact consequences are real, but a naturality/uniqueness argument remains.
3. **The G2 action is a guided candidate.** `IG`, `tau_+`, the moving
   reference, the transgression-shaped 14-form, `1/2`, `1/3`, and `kappa_1`
   are not all consequences of the metric-bundle functor.
4. **Ordinary minimal BV is conditional.** The ghost-antifield term is forced
   once the ordinary gauge algebra is selected. That establishes consistency
   of the chosen algebra through the tested order, not emergence of the full
   GU/IG/diffeomorphism/matter quantum complex.
5. **G4 could hide the fit.** A retract, projector, polarization, or boundary
   domain chosen because it keeps the Standard Model, three generations, or
   PP3 would simply relocate the orthodox insertion from the action to the
   observation map.

The positive control is that exact geometry has already been allowed to say
“no.” The native Shiab failed the cyclic/Helmholtz identities needed for the
expected compressed Euler formula. The action survived, but its actual Euler
covector changed. This is the strongest current evidence that the guided lane
is not merely forcing Eric's words into equations.

## 6. What should remain orthodox—and where

The correction is not “ignore known physics.” Some orthodox structures are
general mathematical infrastructure, and some physical facts are unavoidable
acceptance tests.

### Keep upstream as mathematical infrastructure

- differential geometry of bundles and connections;
- variational bicomplex and Green identities;
- representation theory and Clifford algebra;
- equivariance, BRST/BV once the actual symmetry complex is known;
- well-posedness, domains, locality, and causal propagation tests; and
- anomaly and consistency calculations after the physical representation is
  emitted.

### Move downstream behind a sealed test wall

- Yang--Mills curvature-square dynamics;
- the physical Dirac/RS coefficient matrix;
- Higgs representation and potential;
- Yukawa/flavour matrices;
- Pati--Salam/Spin(10)/hypercharge selection;
- a defect action;
- FLRW scalar truncations, bare vacuum offsets, and PP3; and
- the observed number of generations.

These may reappear as outputs. They may also be introduced as explicit
comparators. They simply cannot receive “emergence” credit if they helped
select the candidate.

## 7. The highest-information next construction

Insert a bounded **G3.5 target-blind naturality census and template ablation**
before, and partly in parallel with, G4.

### 7.1 Freeze the input alphabet

Allow only:

```text
X^4 and its declared sectoral structures;
Y=Met_(3,1)(X), tautological/vertical geometry;
source-native trace/Krein/right-H data, charged explicitly;
moving reduction and its LC graph;
a declared maximum differential and polynomial order.
```

Do not use the words `Yang--Mills`, `Dirac particle`, `Higgs`, `generation`,
`Standard Model`, `dark energy`, or `PP3` while generating the basis.

### 7.2 Enumerate before interpreting

Enumerate:

- natural principal/associated bundles and possible gauged automorphism
  groups;
- equivariant maps between the native form/spinor/vertical carriers;
- invariant local fourteen-forms at bounded order;
- their exact Euler, Hessian, Green, and symmetry complexes; and
- the finite coefficient space and natural equivalences.

Only after the family is frozen should familiar low-energy names be assigned.

### 7.3 Ablate the control action

Remove, one family at a time:

```text
F^2 Yang--Mills;
SW defect;
K/C Yukawa and free flavour matrices;
bare Lambda/vacuum offset;
independent masses and selected P0;
bulk-plus-defect weld.
```

Then ask whether each structure reappears from:

- the G2/G3 action and its Hessian;
- auxiliary-field elimination or a proved replacement theorem;
- the observation pullback and normal operator;
- integration of non-observed modes; or
- physical cohomology.

If deleting a dedicated term deletes the entire leg, that leg was supplied.
If it reappears uniquely with frozen parameters and survives independent
constraints, the lane has learned something.

### 7.4 Preserve G4

G3.5 does not replace the observation/domain swing. It prevents G4 from
silently selecting the desired answer. G4 must still build

\[
R_sL_s=1,
\qquad R_sD_YL_s=D_X,
\qquad (1-L_sR_s)D_YL_s=0,
\]

the Euler-covector dual, positive majorant, closed Krein domain, and boundary
polarization without SM, chirality, count, or PP3 labels.

## 8. Odds-changing experiments shared by the council

1. **Natural gauging theorem.** Derive or sharply classify `P,H,A` from the
   automorphisms of the metric-bundle spin/Krein construction.
2. **G4 target-blind retract/domain.** A finite, covariant, leakage-free
   observation packet is major guided evidence. Arbitrary continuous
   polarization is a new datum.
3. **Fermionic Hessian emission.** Obtain the Dirac/RS complex from the source
   action, retract, and domain rather than installing its coefficient matrix.
4. **G2/N1 replacement theorem.** Prove which N1 terms are expansions,
   auxiliary forms, reductions, or genuine additions to G2.
5. **Unique held-out hypercharge.** Freeze the stabilizer first; enumerate all
   allowed `U(1)` lines; then ask whether one SM line and anomaly cancellation
   survive with positive surplus.
6. **Same-parent Hessian/stress.** One action and stationary orbit must produce
   the heavy doublet and light cosmological mode without independent
   normalizations or a free vacuum offset.
7. **Ablation survival.** The control lane gains strongly if its familiar
   pieces re-emerge after their explicit terms are erased. It loses strongly
   if every physical leg disappears with its dedicated term.
8. **Constraint-rank audit.** Freeze action, domain, state, projection, and
   datum first; then rank truly independent constraints against all freedoms.

## Boundary

This council does not say the guided lane is right, the control lane is
wrong, or GU is likely true. The unconditional research possibility that
both architectures fail or require substantial additional datum remains
dominant and deliberately unquantified. Nor does geometry-first methodology
permit contradiction with established experiments. It changes the order:
let the candidate emerge, freeze its freedoms, and then let known physics try
to kill it.
