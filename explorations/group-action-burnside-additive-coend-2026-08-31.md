# Additive balanced coend for Burnside restriction correspondences

## Result

The preceding all-middle quotient imposed dinatural balance only at the level
of sets. Lean now starts from the free abelian group on every composable
all-middle pair and quotients by the subgroup generated by three exact relation
families:

```text
[x + x', y] = [x, y] + [x', y],
[x, y + y'] = [x, y] + [x, y'],
[Res(k) x, y] = [x, y k].
```

Every evaluator into an additive commutative group that is additive in each
correspondence variable and balanced across every intermediate morphism
extends to a unique additive homomorphism from this quotient. This is not the
mere free additive envelope of the set quotient: the two bilinearity relation
families are imposed explicitly. The existing restriction-correspondence
composition laws instantiate such an evaluator, so Lean also constructs its
descended additive homomorphism from the coend to the iterated-restriction hom
group.

The exact GF(2) control begins with eight pair generators over two intermediate
objects. Balance alone leaves four set classes. Left and right additivity plus
balance reduce the additive quotient to one dimension, with exactly two
GF(2)-valued evaluators. Omitting balance leaves dimension two; omitting either
additivity family leaves extra dimensions.
