# Science council: the categorical reading, reviewed

Eleven seats, inline, each self-declaring basis (DIRECT / PRINCIPLE /
ANALOGY) and confidence, reviewing
`explorations/categorical-reading-of-gu-geometry-2026-08-12.md`.

**Seat 1 — Research-programme methodology (PRINCIPLE, high).** The
debt-as-scoreboard claim is the strongest item: it converts the residue
ledger from bookkeeping into a progressiveness measure. But
zero-free-choices is too strong; canonical constructions may carry
discrete forks. **Delta: split the count into continuous moduli versus
discrete forks.** ADOPTED into the reading.

**Seat 2 — Source criticism (DIRECT, high).** Universality is the
program's ambition as READ BY THIS REPOSITORY, not an authorial claim;
the source explicitly admits a connection choice ("semi-canonically
related to `TY` after a connection choice"). **Delta: do not add
universality/naturality rows to the source-claim register; type as
repo-side reading.** ADOPTED.

**Seat 3 — Archival/typing (DIRECT, high).** Section 5 restates the
existing Rung-1 multiplicity-not-index fence in better vocabulary. Given
this session's false-novelty near-miss, that must be said in the
artifact itself. **Delta: label it a vocabulary upgrade explicitly.**
ADOPTED.

**Seat 4 — Checklist design (PRINCIPLE, high).** The reading's real
operational value is that it predicts WHERE problems appear. Convert
prediction into a forcing function: **any construction that ascends for
canonicity declares its descent obligation at admission** — a
`descent_obligation:` frontmatter field naming which pullback,
restriction, or real-form passage it will owe. PROPOSED (wave/owner).

**Seat 5 — Multi-agent context (DIRECT, high).** Categorical vocabulary
is compressive: "naturality defect" survives summarization where a
paragraph does not — directly relevant to this session's measured
frame-regression failures. **Delta: adopt a small controlled vocabulary
(naturality defect; descent obligation; groupoid `pi_0`; universal-
property gap; comparison-map loss) for relays and ledger blocks.**
PROPOSED.

**Seat 6 — Red team (PRINCIPLE, high on the warning).** Danger: a
categorical framing can rename problems instead of solving them, and
"it's only a derived-functor correction" could excuse permanent
openness. Teeth: **every categorical typing must name the concrete
computation it implies, and must make a falsifiable prediction about
which squares can commute.** ADOPTED as the reading's Section 7.

**Seat 7 — Process engineering (DIRECT, medium-high).** The one-diagram
proposal is actionable but will rot as prose. If built, it must be a
machine-readable object (nodes, arrows, per-square status) with a gate
requiring new construction artifacts to name the arrow they touch.
PROPOSED, conditional on the standing gate-retirement discipline.

**Seat 8 — Metrology (PRINCIPLE, medium-high).** The reading supplies
two progress metrics the program lacks — free-choice count
(continuous/discrete) and the fraction of squares whose commutation
status is known. The program currently measures ledger versions, which
is activity, not progress. **Delta: baseline both at the next
stewardship pass.** PROPOSED.

**Seat 9 — Ontology/schema (PRINCIPLE, medium).** If the diagram is
built, cap it (<= 12 nodes to start) and type each arrow
(linear/antilinear, equivariant or not). An over-formalized 30-node
diagram nobody maintains is worse than none.

**Seat 10 — Physics insider (PRINCIPLE, medium; literature-flagged).**
Two guards: monoidality supplies slots, NOT the count (the quantum
numbers are separate work); and "the mirror isomorphism is not natural"
must not obscure that a MASS TERM is required — breaking the isomorphism
is dynamical, not merely structural. Both ADOPTED into the reading.

**Seat 11 — In-house skeptic (DIRECT, high on the risk).** This is a
reframing, not a result, and it adds a surface on a day that already
added several. It earns its place only if it predicts, is
enforcement-backed where it makes demands, and REPLACES ad hoc
route-typing prose rather than accreting beside it. **Delta: all claims
graded PROPOSED; the council review must not launder them into
CONFIRMED.** ADOPTED.

## Convergence

Unanimous: the reading is diagnostic, not productive — valuable for
locating difficulty and for compressive vocabulary, worthless if it
becomes a way to rename open problems. Its two concrete process
outputs are the descent-obligation declaration (Seat 4) and the two
progress metrics (Seat 8).

## Dissents recorded

- Seat 6: renaming risk; conditions of adoption are the
  named-computation and falsifiable-prediction requirements.
- Seat 11: standing objection to surface growth; adoption conditional on
  replacement rather than accretion.
