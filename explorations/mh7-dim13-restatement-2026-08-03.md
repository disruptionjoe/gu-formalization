## 4. The restatement

**Located-not-forced, dim-13 form.** For the net chiral generation count
`chi` of the Clifford RS sector, `chi = chi_interior + chi_external`
(external-by-structure synthesis, cited with its own grade and its RS
function-space residual). Carried on the true 13-dim link object:

**(i) Interior-even — unchanged.** `chi_interior` is even/2-primary over the
complete delimited class C (proof/computed grade, modulo the RS
function-space residual). This clause never referenced the surrogate and
restates without loss.

**(ii) Receptacle purity — the upgrade.** In dim 3 the no-go was a
DISJOINTNESS statement: obstructions live in the `Z/8` arena of
`pi_3^s = Z/8 (+) Z/3` and are blind to the `Z/3` arena — a CRT bookkeeping
fact needing the two-primary lemma to police the border. In dim 13 the
receptacle is `Z/3` outright: there is no 2-primary arena to be blind to.
The statement becomes a group identity, certified exhaustively in the probe
(Part 1):

> For every finite abelian 2-group `A`: `Hom(A, Z/3) = 0` and
> `Hom(Z/3, A) = 0`. Equivalently: 2 is invertible mod 3, so the only
> element of the receptacle any 2-primary datum can name is 0.

"The boundary group cannot express the interior obstruction's type" — and
symmetrically, the interior cannot populate the boundary receptacle. The
Firewall-Boundary Hypothesis's negative half, as arithmetic identity rather
than evidence. All four 2-adic convention subtleties of the surrogate
computation (spin structure `+/-1/8`, lens factor, `e_C` vs `e_R`, the `3/8`
shift) become VACUOUS here: there is no 2-part for them to contaminate.

**(iii) The located slot — sharpened and empty.** Any homotopy-theoretic
count datum on the true link lives in `Omega_13^fr = Z/3 = <alpha_1 beta_1>`
and can ONLY be reached by: a closed `L^13`, a stable parallelization, and a
genuinely NON-PRODUCT framing. The probe's product-framing kill (Part 3,
exact) closes every product route the link's own geometry offers:

- any `S^6` product factor forces the class into 2-torsion
  (`exp pi_6^s = 2`), hence 0 in `Z/3`;
- any closed framed `X^4` (or 5-dim) factor is itself null-bordant
  (`pi_4^s = pi_5^s = 0`), hence kills the product outright — the base
  CANNOT enter as a product factor;
- the only stem split of 13 that can carry 3-torsion at all is `3 + 10`,
  which is NOT one of the link's geometric factorizations (`9 + 4`,
  `6 + 7`, `6 + 3 + 4`).

So a nonzero occupant, if it ever exists, must come from a framing that
mixes base and fiber non-trivially (Wave C: "non-product framings remain
open") — and `Im J_13 = 0` guarantees the dim-3 mechanism (reframe a fixed
standard class through the classical `J`, i.e. twists factoring through the
top-cell collapse `L -> S^13`) contributes nothing. Scope fence: general
reframings act through the full `[L^13, SO]`, which `Im J_13 = 0` does NOT
kill — that residual freedom IS the open non-product-framing item (gap O4),
not a closed door. **Nothing currently occupies the slot.**

**(iv) Not forced — carried over verbatim, sharpened.** The
order-3-class-to-integer-3 identification remains a theorem of nothing. In
dim 13 the deflation is sharper: in dim 3 there was at least a constructed
nonzero order-3 object (the spine class, tangential reading); in dim 13
even that is absent. The receptacle's order 3 is a fact about spheres,
identical in a one-generation universe.

## 5. What the spine reduction does and does not preserve

The deformation-retract argument's exact scope:

**What it is.** `F -> RP^3` is a homotopy equivalence of the OPEN metric
fiber onto its spine (fiberwise over `X^4`: `Y^14`'s fiber bundle retracts
onto `P(TX^4)`). It exists only over the indefinite-signature fiber — the
second place the indefinite-base requirement enters: not only must the end
exist (Section 2), the retract target `RP^3` and its `Z_2` (time
reflection) are artifacts of the Lorentzian stabilizer `O(3,1) ~ O(3) x O(1)`.

**Preserved (and certified where cheap).** Homotopy-invariant data of the
fiber: (co)homology, K-theory, bundle classifications pulled back from the
spine. At the link level the probe adds an exact transfer statement (Part
4): the mod-3 homology of the 9-dim fiber-link is spine-controlled in BOTH
orientation branches — `H_3(L^9; F_3) = H_3(RP^3; F_3) = F_3`, everything
in degrees <= 5 is the spine's, and the only unknown is one orientation bit
`w` on the `S^6` row (untwisted: mod-3 homology `S^3 x S^6`, matching the
model; twisted: no mod-3 fundamental class at all — `H_9 = 0` — and the
Euler-characteristic control excludes neither branch). That bit is Wave C's
normal-bundle item, not a homotopy consequence.

**NOT preserved — the three losses that make the surrogate a surrogate.**

1. **The link is not the spine.** The retract is a statement about `F`; the
   link is the boundary-at-infinity of `F` — a different, closed manifold
   that merely maps to the spine. No homotopy equivalence of open manifolds
   controls its end structure; that control comes (if at all) from the `nu`
   decomposition, which is M-H5's referee target.
2. **Framed-bordism classes do not ride homotopy equivalences.** The
   surrogate's class `[RP^3, twisted framing] in pi_3^s` is a datum of a
   CLOSED framed 3-manifold. There is no map `pi_3^s -> pi_13^s` induced by
   the retract; the only candidate transports are external products, and
   the probe kills every product route exactly (Section 4(iii)). The
   surrogate's positive result — class 2, `e_R = 1/12`, tangential fork
   resolved at reconstruction grade — is a fact about the spine and DOES
   NOT TRANSFER. It does not become false in dim 13; it becomes
   inapplicable: its mechanism (`Im J_3 = Z/24` saturating the receptacle)
   has image 0 in dim 13.
3. **The receptacle changes group.** `Z/24 -> Z/3`: the e-invariant dies,
   the CRT two-arena structure disappears, and closedness/compactness —
   free for `RP^3` — becomes an open hypothesis on `L^13` (the base `X^4`).

## 7. What the dim-13 statement gains and loses vs the RP^3 surrogate

**Gains.**

1. Blindness becomes impossibility: the negative half of located-not-forced
   is a finite group identity (P3), with no CRT bookkeeping, no two-primary
   lemma policing, and no 2-adic convention exposure — the four flagged
   subtleties of the surrogate computation are vacuous in dim 13.
2. The structure forcing is sharper: spin provably cannot carry the datum
   (`Omega_13^Spin = 0` + no 3-primary Dai–Freed column), String is
   automatic rather than an extra hypothesis, and the slot's address is
   unique (`Z/3 = <alpha_1 beta_1>`, stem site `3 + 10` — the only
   arithmetic site that works, P4).
3. The construction target is now exactly specified: closed + stably
   parallelizable + non-product-framed `L^13` with nonzero PT class — with
   the product alternatives eliminated exactly, not heuristically.

**Losses.**

1. The entire constructed positive half. The surrogate has a nonzero
   order-3 occupant with a resolved fork and a value (`e_R = 1/12`, class
   2); the dim-13 slot has NO occupant, no construction, and no mechanism —
   `Im J_13 = 0` closes the route that produced the surrogate's class.
2. The detector. `e` is identically zero on `pi_13^s`; the f-invariant is
   not yet an instrument (even-stem; corner object unbuilt). A constructed
   class could not currently be evaluated.
3. Free closedness. `RP^3` is closed; `L^13` is closed only if `X^4`
   cooperates — a physics-facing hypothesis the surrogate never needed.
4. Rhetorical safety. `|Z/3| = 3` sits one careless sentence away from
   "three generations." The fence (Section 1) exists because the dim-13
   form makes the order-vs-count conflation MORE tempting, not less, while
   supplying strictly less of an occupant to point at.

**Net honest headline.** In dimension 13, located-not-forced reads: *the
slot is located exactly (a purely 3-primary framed receptacle no interior
or spin-structure datum can touch), the interior provably cannot fill it,
and nothing currently fills it.* The surrogate's nonzero class is a fact
about the spine that the deformation retract cannot carry to the link; what
survives the trip is the address of the slot, not its contents.
