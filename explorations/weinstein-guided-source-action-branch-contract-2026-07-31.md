## North Star

> construct an external datum and source action that jointly retain the
> Standard Model/Yukawa, quantum/Krein/BV, induced-gravity/trace-reversed,

## What guidance is allowed to do

Guidance may:

1. select which candidate family to write first;
2. supply a field grammar such as `(epsilon,varpi) in IG` and `T_omega`;
3. supply an explicit conditional coefficient or representation assignment;
4. reduce a symmetry group or choose a real-form fork when the source actually
   does so; and
5. suggest a physical interpretation after the mathematical object is typed.

Guidance may not:

1. count as a proof;
2. convert a decomposition into the observed generation count;
3. identify a field with an Euler covector without the Krein/Hodge
   pseudo-musical and coordinate transport;
4. turn a VEV slogan into a state or vacuum-selection prescription;
5. turn an ad-valued one-form into the physical Higgs without its Standard
   Model representation and fermion bilinear; or
6. import a positive Hilbert/U(128) model over the native
   `Sp(32,32;H)`-Krein fork silently.

## Shared scoring surface

A candidate does not win by resembling the transcript. Both lanes use the same
comparison record:

| leg | minimum passing evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Standard Model | typed Higgs/Yukawa incidence, zero-order `P0/rho/Y_K/Y_C/C` placement, anomaly-compatible physical channel |
| quantum | Krein bilinear, charge conjugation, domain, BV/Noether, and physical-state policy |
| gravity | moving soldering/section response, trace-reversed fibre, coupled connection/distortion/metric equations |
| cosmology | observation-slice stress, state/VEV prescription, amplitude ownership, and frozen PP1/PP3 confrontation |
| datum/count | P1/P2/P3 types explicit, right-`H` compatibility, no multiplicity-to-index/count leakage |

For each candidate:

\[
\mathrm{surplus}
=\operatorname{rank}(\text{independent passed constraints})
-\operatorname{rank}(\text{supplied freedoms and data}).
\]

The supplied guidance is a debit, not an automatic disqualification. A
positive-surplus fit is informative.

## Transfer rule

Only a result that remains true after deleting its guidance premise may be
proposed for transfer to the independent branch. Examples include an exact
semidirect-product identity, a representation-theoretic obstruction, a
planted-control harness, or a variational type correction. A transcript-chosen
field ansatz, coefficient, real form, or physical interpretation stays guided.

Any later cherry-pick must name:

1. the guided discovery commit;
2. the minimal branch-independent statement;
3. its independent proof or executable receipt; and
4. the guidance-dependent content deliberately excluded.

No automatic merge is authorized.
