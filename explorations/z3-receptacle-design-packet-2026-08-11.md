object: a Z/3-valued count invariant that could actually exist in the
program's 13-dimensional boundary setting. The standing anchor fact

   post-mortem section cites the register and re-litigates nothing; no step
   below uses `pi_!`, the `A-hat`-times-rank form, or the `+8` boundary term.

   every mathematical fact not opened in the repo this session is flagged
   `[for independent citation-check]`; the wave must verify each before any
   disposition rests on it. In-repo facts carry file citations at their own
   recorded grades.

   tangential fork) do not transfer to dim 13 — framed classes do not ride

   (`mh7-dim13-restatement` Section 5). Mitigation: no dim-3 positive fact is
   used as a dim-13 fact anywhere below; dim-3 appears only as a planted

   parenthetical "(framed/String or replace the sole-bridge premise)"
   predates 2026-08-05: the spin-inflow bridge is now dead in BOTH columns
   (torsion empty AND free column identically zero by local consistency),
   making the framed/String redirect "the *only* survivor rather than the
   preferred one"

   Section 4.2). Mitigation: the bridge audit below states the current state.

   would just be fitting" without counting surplus (AGENTS.md operating
