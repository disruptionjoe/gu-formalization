- Domain object: the filed `(832,832)` section trace has exact deck-fixed
  extension moduli `disjoint_union_k Gr(k,832)`, maximal dimension `346112`.
  It is not the domain classification of the ambient ultrahyperbolic operator.
- Spectral-metric object: pseudo-Hermitian `C` is fixed only up to the relevant
  commutant. A CSCO can be additional selection data; no GU CSCO is supplied.
- Eta object: H37 computes a zero eta sum on a finite Hermitian proxy. It does
  not own a global 13-dimensional boundary operator, domain or eta form.
- End object: OC2 lists partial fibre roots while leaving the horizontal
  tau-shifted spectrum and projected sector unowned. A partial list cannot
  establish a complete half-infinite window.
- Claim ceiling: correct the owner surfaces and register rows only. Preserve
  all still-valid finite calculations and every missing owner.

Routes considered were direct recalculation, wholesale historical rewrite and
forward-only typed correction. Recalculation cannot supply the absent owners;
wholesale rewrite destroys provenance. Forward-only correction is selected.
