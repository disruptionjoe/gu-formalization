# Jacobson, K77, and reverse scaffolding

## Decision

Joe proposed treating Jacobson's successful gravity/thermodynamics relation
as a phenomenon to build backward from, rather than demanding that GU first
discover it unaided. An adversarial ten-seat science council returned:

| disposition | votes |
| --- | ---: |
| accept the benchmark while withholding mechanism commitment | 8 |
| use Jacobson directly as a reverse-scaffold premise | 1 |
| reserve Jacobson entirely for later validation | 1 |

The 8-1-1 selected disposition is the first. It accepts a target without
installing an explanation. Reverse-only success is compatibility, not a
prediction or confirmation.

## The split object

“Jacobson works” collapses three different objects:

1. **External benchmark behavior.** In its appropriate low-energy
   semiclassical regime, a viable observed gravitational theory should recover
   the relevant horizon entropy, first-law or generalized-entropy behavior and
   tested Einstein-like dynamics.
2. **Conditional theorem or candidate mechanism.** Jacobson 1995 derives the
   Einstein equation from local Rindler horizons, Unruh temperature, universal
   area entropy and equilibrium Clausius behavior. The newer papers add scoped
   ensemble, energy, constraint, contour and state-count constructions.
3. **GU/K77 realization.** The source-aligned `(7,7)` construction still has
   to supply a typed observation map, physical causal region, constraints,
   boundary algebra, state space, entropy functional and field equation.

The first is admitted as a target. The second remains uncommitted. The third
is the missing construction.

## Why neither extreme was selected

Treating the complete Jacobson mechanism as a premise risks circularity and
overfitting. Area entropy density may define Newton's constant rather than
predict it; a boundary, contour or entropy-production term chosen after the
desired answer can manufacture agreement. The leading Einstein term is also
an effective-field-theory universality result with low diagnosticity among
microscopic theories.

Reserving all Jacobson work for the end wastes genuine information. The
external benchmark tells the construction which physical roles must
eventually exist and supplies discriminating questions about boundaries,
constraints, entropy accounting and higher-curvature corrections.

## Two-track protocol

### Reverse track

Freeze the benchmark and work backward:

```text
scoped horizon/first-law/Einstein benchmark
  <- entropy and energy-flux accounting
  <- physical causal boundary and observable algebra
  <- canonical constraints and reduced state space
  <- observed 3+1 Lorentzian system
  <- K77 observation map and source action
```

Every dependency chosen because of the target is labeled comparator-driven.
Boundary identity, entropy normalization, equilibrium law and comparison
observable are frozen before reading the target-facing result.

### Forward track

Independently propagate the source-aligned K77 construction:

```text
K77 action
  -> observation
  -> native constraints and boundary structure
  -> reduced physical state space
  -> entropy and gravitational consequences
```

The forward track does not choose among otherwise legal constructions because
one happens to match Jacobson.

### Adjudication

| result | evidential meaning |
| --- | --- |
| both tracks reach the same owned structure | candidate explanation |
| reverse track succeeds alone | compatibility or target fit |
| forward track produces a frozen held-out result | prediction candidate |
| tracks disagree | negative evidence or scoped bridge kill |

## Jacobson 1995 versus the newer papers

Jacobson 1995 is a conditional theoretical derivation, not itself an
empirical observation that gravity is fundamentally entropic. Its theorem may
be used exactly at that grade.

The 2022--2025 papers are narrower:

- the de Sitter first-law paper resolves a sign after an ensemble and energy
  are specified;
- the causal-diamond paper studies a York-boundary ensemble and warns that an
  imposed boundary defines a different system;
- the lapse-contour paper depends on a gravitational Hamiltonian constraint,
  lapse and integration order;
- the simplicial paper is a controlled `2+1` minisuperspace model; and
- the partition-function paper is explicitly foundational and exploratory.

They extend the test library. They are not universal K77 premises.

## K77 and B5 placement

K77 `(7,7)` remains the primary source-aligned reconstruction. The strict B5
`(9,5)` fixed-background calculation is a separate comparator fork. Its odd
spinor normal multiplier is not an ADM lapse, so the direct Jacobson contour
is `WRONG_TYPE` there. That negative result does not dispose of the K77
reverse target and must not be transferred to `(7,7)`.

The Jacobson benchmark primarily belongs after the K77 observation map in the
physical observed `3+1` system, not on the ambient multiple-time carrier by
default.

## W151 correction

W151 correctly labels Jacobson 1995 as `PORTED`, reports only sign agreement,
leaves normalization uncomputed, and identifies the equilibrium `R^2`
mismatch. Its sentence identifying the measurement-gated capability boundary
with a local causal horizon outruns the evidence. That identity is now a
proposed typed bridge. A capability boundary, local Rindler horizon,
cosmological horizon, York boundary, analytic domain boundary and BV--BFV
boundary remain distinct until a map is constructed.

## Claim ceiling

This decision adds one reverse-scaffold target and no GU result. It does not
establish an entropic ontology, a K77 horizon, an entropy law, a physical
Hilbert space, an edge mode, an Einstein-equation derivation, a prediction or
a GU verdict.
