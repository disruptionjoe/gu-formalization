## 1. `RA-D2` — the cluster, and the only place the sweep found real new ground

`RA-D2` is typed `GENUINE_FALSIFICATION`. Its recorded distance and revival
trigger are both about **satisfying** the row — build `h_omega`, exhibit a chiral
carrier not obtained by equivariant mass splitting. Every item below instead
questions whether the row is **correctly typed**. That is orthogonal work with no
current ledger entry, and it should be settled before more is spent satisfying a
row whose type is in doubt.

**1.1 The source may never have asserted a mass mechanism.**
D2 reads *"in regions of low scalar curvature the non-chiral theory decouples
into chiral sectors"* as a statement about **mass**. "Decouples" can equally name
a **spectral** splitting — which is precisely the survivor D2 leaves alive. If
the spectral reading is the source's, D2 kills a reading nobody advanced and
should retype from `GENUINE_FALSIFICATION` toward `SCOPE_ERROR`.
*Check:* re-read the primary source at the cited locus and grade which reading it
supports, under `SOURCE-CONFIRMS` / `SOURCE-CORRECTS` / `SOURCE-SILENT`. Cheap.
This is the single highest-impact item in the file.

**1.2 The survivor column names one mechanism where there are at least four.**
Chirality from a vectorlike parent is solved in the literature by four
structurally distinct routes: index of a twisted Dirac operator, Wilson lines /
discrete holonomy, flux quantization, and orbifold projection. D2 kills exactly
one (mass/VEV) and names exactly one survivor (index). **Wilson lines, flux and
orbifold projection are neither killed nor named.**
*Check:* type each of the three against the GU setting — structurally available,
or excluded, and by what. Half a day, and it changes what "next" means for the
whole chirality axis.

**1.3 The one named survivor may already be dead.**
D2's surviving index/zero-mode route assumes an operator's index is a free
choice. Rung 1 records the index as **grading-determined and unmoved by any
coefficient**. If that result covers D2's carrier, the survivor is not free and
D2 is closer to a class kill than a mechanism kill — a *strengthening*, not a
weakening.
*Check:* does the grading-determined index result apply to D2's carrier? One
read, and it cuts both ways.

**1.4 The no-Majorana step may collide with the repo's own `126`.**
D2 argues every mass term is a cross-half Dirac pairing because the invariant
pairing is zero *within* a half, and cites `16 ⊗ 16` zero-weight multiplicity 0.
In SO(10) the `ν_R` Majorana mass comes from the **126**, and this repository
separately records `Λ⁵ ⊃ 126` as a Lorentz singlet.
*Check:* does the program's own `126` supply a channel D2 assumes absent? If yes,
the six-parameter Schur count is incomplete and the kill does not close.

**1.5 `RA-D2` has no firing negative control.**
`LT-GR1b` has a strong one (exact counterexample family, nonzero survivors = 0)
and CB-C reports controls with real power. D2 has none stated. An argument with
no firing control has not been shown to have teeth.

verify the Schur argument fails there. Cheap, and it is the control the row
should always have carried.
