## Preflight: route and overclaim controls

The live failure was not safely fixable by raising a ratchet, adding a broad
stopword pattern, or marking every new pair `UNTYPED` without reading it.
Those routes were rejected because they could erase a real construction edge.

The selected route applied four ordered checks:

1. classify only explicit status/control vocabulary as extraction noise;
2. read both sides of every remaining scientific-token candidate;
3. preserve superseded lifecycle evidence outside the live exact-key map; and
4. activate CT-3 context typing only from the ledger's existing CT-2 fields,
   with mixed or absent carriers remaining declared markers.

The strongest contrary risk was false composition: a generic group or carrier
token can occur in dozens of provider files without any one file supplying the
need. The strongest overclaim was treating `ALREADY_COMPOSED` as `DISCHARGED`.
Every affected note therefore states whether the match is merely citation,
conditioning machinery, carrier equality, or still untyped.
