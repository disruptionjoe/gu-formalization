## The four highest-value discoveries

### 1. E0 is a zero-jet shadow, not the global cocycle

E0 uses

\[
c_0(g)=A_0-\operatorname{Ad}_gA_0.
\]

That is an exact finite coboundary. The actual gauge transformation of a
connection contains `dg`; infinitesimally its affine cocycle contains

\[
\xi\longmapsto-D_{A_0}\xi.
\]

In the pointwise adjoint module
`Omega^1(Y,ad P)`, a coboundary is zeroth-order in `xi`, while
`D_A xi` has a nonzero first-order symbol. Choose `xi(y0)=0` but
`d xi(y0) != 0`: every pointwise inner coboundary vanishes at `y0`, while
`D_A xi(y0)` need not. The zero-jet computation therefore cannot prove the
global class trivial.

This is not a contradiction. The cocycle can become tautologically
compensated after enlarging the object to the affine space of connections,
covariant differential operators, or a moving-reference/Stueckelberg field.
That enlargement is exactly the sort of job `epsilon` might perform, but it
must be constructed and charged as data.

The branch-independent algebra survives for any genuine cocycle `q`:

\[
\tau_q(g)=(g,q(g)),
\qquad
\Theta_q(g,a)=\operatorname{Ad}_{g^{-1}}(a-q(g)),
\]

with left tilted invariance and right adjoint covariance. E0 proved the
coboundary subclass; the next swing must prove the derivative/global case.

### 2. The full Noether identity is coupled

The actual variational objects on fourteen dimensions are density-dual:

\[
E_B,E_T\in\Omega^{13}(Y,\operatorname{ad}^*P),
\qquad
E_\epsilon\in\Omega^{14}(Y,\operatorname{ad}^*P).
\]

The distortion `T` is a primal one-form. A Hodge/DeWitt/Krein/adjoint
pseudo-musical is required before these can be compared. For a coupled gauge
symmetry, Noether II has the schematic form

\[
D_B^{\mathrm{coad}}E_B
+\operatorname{ad}_T^!E_T
+R_\epsilon^!E_\epsilon
+R_Z^!E_Z
+R_s^!E_s+\cdots=0.
\]

It is not `D_B^*T=0`. Isolating a divergence law for `T` requires the typed
Euler-to-field map, on-shell control of the other Euler terms, and vanishing
boundary flux.

The same correction applies to the existing Seiberg--Witten comparator. Gauge
invariance of `||F_A^+-mu(Psi)||^2` yields

\[
D_A^{\mathrm{coad}}E_A+R_\Psi^!E_\Psi=0,
\]

not isolated off-shell `D_A^*E_A=0`. This preserves the finite equivariance
result but removes an overstrong Noether reading.

### 3. The full `(9,5)` system has no ordinary Cauchy hypersurface

Any codimension-one subspace of a quadratic space with negative index five
has negative index at least four. Hence a 13-dimensional hypersurface in
`Y^14` cannot be spacelike. The old conditional four-dimensional
section-reduced hyperbolic estimate is not a proof of an ambient Cauchy
problem.

Nor can data on `s(Sigma^3)` determine a generic 14-dimensional first-order
field: it is codimension eleven. The next construction needs an explicit
packet

\[
\mathcal O=(s,\mathcal L_s,\mathcal R_s,\Theta,
\mathcal D_\partial,\Pi_{\rm adm}),
\]

where `L_s` lifts reduced fields, `R_s` restricts them, `Theta` is a positive
majorant/Cartan polarization, `D_partial` is a closed Krein domain, and
`Pi_adm` imposes any required ultrahyperbolic mode constraint. The load-bearing
equations are

\[
\mathcal R_s\mathcal L_s=1,
\quad
\mathcal R_sD_Y\mathcal L_s=D_s,
\quad
(1-\mathcal L_s\mathcal R_s)D_Y\mathcal L_s=0.
\]

The last is the off-slice leakage test.

This packet is not `P2`. A domain or polarization could emit the existing
`P1/P2` orientation only through a constructed determinant/orientation-line
map.

### 4. One parent field must have two protected modes, not one impossible mode

The strongest economical construction still uses one global distortion
parent `T_omega`. Its vertical observation component may project to a moving
Pati--Salam bidoublet and enter the full ordered Krein/charge-conjugation
Yukawa kernels. A gauge-invariant radial or trace-relative mode of the same
field may source cosmological stress.

But a single canonically normalized two-derivative eigenmode cannot
simultaneously have Standard Model Higgs-scale potential curvature and the
PP3 cosmological curvature `M^2 ~ 3--8 H0^2`. The viable one-parent route needs
at least two protected Hessian modes:

- a heavy gauge-doublet fluctuation; and
- a light gauge-singlet radial/trace-relative fluctuation,

or a genuinely constrained/degenerate kinetic structure. The splitting is a
datum debit unless the native geometry fixes it. An arbitrary additive vacuum
counterterm would also give back the unowned cosmological amplitude and must
be rejected as a fake closure.

## Cross-lens adjudications

### Affine geometry x group cohomology

The exact E0 algebra is retained, but its global interpretation moves from
“the cocycle is a coboundary” to “the general cocycle law is enough; the
moving-reference enlargement must absorb the derivative class.” Standard
contorsion is already tensorial when both connections transform. The actual GU
question is whether the moving reference connection is constructed from the
native geometry and descends under the full action.

### Symplectic x variational/BV

The canonical objects are not an assumed symplectic form on `Conn(Y)` and a
guessed moment map. They are:

- the covariant presymplectic current
  `omega_L in Omega^(13,2)(J^infinity F)` produced by the written 14-form
  Lagrangian; and
- the canonical `(-1)`-shifted BV cotangent form on `T*[-1]F`.

If a 13-chain is later selected, its presymplectic form is the integral of
`omega_L`, subject to boundary and four-dimensional defect corner flux.

### Krein x PDE

The same `K` and DeWitt structures that rescue the physical bilinear also make
domain and symmetrizer choices nontrivial. On a positive quaternionic Hilbert
space, a self-adjoint operator cannot square to `-I`; inserting a chosen
complex `i` is a polarization choice that must pass right-`H`. The action must
own the majorant/domain, or their full continuous freedom is an external datum
debit.

### Representation x complex/index

The precise durable version of Eric's higher-group statement is:

```text
ambient:  S and im Gamma are spinor-type; ker Gamma is RS-type
observer: each supplies one E+ and one E-; ker Gamma also supplies X_1536
```

A physical differential must make `3E_- + X` contractible while retaining one
`E_+` from each provenance. That would construct three surviving low-energy
lines **conditional on P3/domain data**. It would not derive the observed count
from the three algebraic blocks.

### Standard Model x cosmology

One parent field is still the best positive-surplus attempt, but the physical
Higgs and cosmological source are two projections/eigenmodes of that parent.
The construction wins only if one action, one stationary orbit, one
orientation, and one normalization pass both legs. Independent Higgs and
dark-energy normalizations kill the advertised economy.
