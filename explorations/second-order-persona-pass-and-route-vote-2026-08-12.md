`novelty-check.py` on "Hilbert 90" and "Galois descent" and got zero
exact hits — and nearly filed a novelty claim anyway. The prior art was

  finite parameter list; the live space is still function-valued, so the

  seed already exists: the pairing determinants in the v0.173/v0.174
  banks.
