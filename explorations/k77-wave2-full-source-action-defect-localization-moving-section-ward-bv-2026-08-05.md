## 4. Canonical induced-density localization

Let `G` be the ambient K77 pseudo-metric density owner and write the complete
source-shaped ambient density as

\[
\mathcal L_Y(j^1\Phi)=L_Y(j^1\Phi)\,\mu_G.
\]

For a supplied nondegenerate observation section `s:X->Y`, define

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Loc}_s(\mathcal L_Y)[\Phi]
=\int_X L_Y\!\left(j^1\Phi|_{s(x)}\right)\,\mu_s .
} \tag{1}
\]

Here `mu_s` is the density induced on the graph by the relevant section
geometry. Equation (1) is not `s^*mathcal L_Y`; the latter is zero for degree
reasons. It is a scalar evaluation followed by a new density pairing.

This construction is coordinate invariant because `L_Y` is a scalar and
`mu_s` is a density. The exact probe reverses and rescales the normal
coordinate and verifies the localized integral unchanged. No **new** oriented
vertical volume is chosen, so P1 is not consumed. This does not remove any
ambient orientation/Hodge assumption already present in the source action.

The current notation is equivalent. Define the section delta current relative
to `mu_G` by

\[

=\int_X f\circ s\,\mu_s.

Then (1) is `\langle\delta_s^G,L_Y\rangle`. This is compatible with N1's
`mu_Y+s_*mu_X`; it does not pretend the current is a smooth ambient density.

## 5. Exact first-order field variation

Use local base coordinates `x^i` and normal coordinates `y^a`, with graph
`y^a=s^a(x)`. For one field component `Phi`, write

\[
P^i=\frac{\partial L}{\partial(\partial_i\Phi)},
\qquad
P^a=\frac{\partial L}{\partial(\partial_a\Phi)}.
\]

At fixed section,

\[
\delta S_{\rm loc}
=\int_X\mu_s
\left(
L_\Phi\eta+P^i\partial_i\eta+P^a\partial_a\eta
\right)_{s}.
\]

The derivative along the graph is

\[
D_i(\eta|_s)
=(\partial_i\eta+s_i^a\partial_a\eta)|_s.
\]

Tangential integration by parts therefore gives

\[
\delta S_{\rm loc}
=\int_X\left[
E_0\,\eta|_s+E_a\,(\partial_a\eta)|_s
\right]+\text{boundary}, \tag{2}
\]

where the density-valued coefficients are

\[
\boxed{
E_0=\mu_sL_\Phi-D_i(\mu_sP^i),
\qquad
E_a=\mu_s(P^a-s_i^aP^i).
} \tag{3}
\]

As an ambient distribution this is schematically

\[
\mathcal E_{\rm loc}
=\delta_s E_0-\partial_a(\delta_sE_a). \tag{4}
\]

The second term is a normal dipole. It is not optional bookkeeping: the exact
polynomial fixture differentiates the localized action directly and verifies
(2)--(4). Dropping `E_a` or the graph-mixing term `-s_i^aP^i` changes the
variation.

For connections and bundle-valued fields, ordinary derivatives in (2)--(4)
are replaced by the corresponding covariant horizontal/normal jet operators,
and the invariant pairing carries the adjoint index. The current theorem is
the local variational architecture for the first-order ambient field sector;
expanding the actual moving Shiab and all primitive `epsilon/B` chains remains
part of the next coefficient build. Existing defect or section terms with
