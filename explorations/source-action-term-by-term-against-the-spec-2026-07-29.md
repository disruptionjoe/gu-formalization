## The four demands the pass generated

These were **not** on my prior hardening list. The writing produced them.

**D1 — the Yukawa texture must be re-derived under 2+1.** `SA-Y4`'s three
couplings come from a `9 -> 3` reduction *"by the derived Z/3"*, and `SA-Y7a`'s
spurion is typed as a **Z/3 doublet**. Both assume a symmetric triplet. The
product rule does not supply one: it supplies two Leibniz partners plus a
structurally different correction term. A `2+1` texture need not have the same
reduction, the same count of surviving couplings, or a `Z/3` doublet spurion.
**Computation that settles it:** branch all three terms on the `4+10` split and
redo the Hom-space count with `2+1` rather than `Z/3` as the flavour structure.

**D2 — `SA-Y6` may move from DECLARATION to FORCED.** The row exists because
which grading sector is which physical generation is a free assignment, and
identifying sector 0 with the top quark is the answer-as-premise trap. But under
`2+1` the third family is **identifiable** — it is the `S(V)(x)S(W)` term and it
is not of Leibniz form. So the assignment is no longer free among three
symmetric sectors: one of the three is distinguishable *before* any physics
input. That does not fix which of the remaining two is which, so the row would
narrow rather than close.

**D3 — `SA-C3` may be discharged.** The realized chiral rank is a DECLARATION
because *"the count is `{1, 3}`, not pinned."* If `2+1` is forced by the split
plus gamma-tracelessness, the rank is not a free declaration at all. **This is
the highest-value item in the pass.** Settling it needs the split's rigidity —
if the split deforms, `2+1` is itself declared and `SA-C3` survives.

**D4 — `SA-C1`'s conditional forcing changes role.** Carrier B is forced *only
if* the source action is to count-select. If the count comes from the product
rule instead, the carrier bit is no longer doing that job and its `B`-forcing
premise lapses. The bit may still be forced for another reason; it would need a
new one.

## What the pass shows about the state

Two rows are **satisfied by construction** as written — `SA-C2` via T5 and
`SA-U4` via T6 — because today's runs established both as forced and both are
now explicit terms. Four rows are **unbuilt** (`SA-G9`, `SA-C4`, `SA-U1`,
`SA-U3`). One term (**T10**, the `Lambda^0` Yukawa) is **missing and must be
written** — `SA-Y1` is FORCED and nothing currently carries it. One term
(**T9**) awaits `P1`.

The single most load-bearing unverified claim is `SA-Y8`'s: that `mu(Psi)`
supplies the chirality-preserving Majorana block. The whole Seiberg-Witten route
rests on it and it has not been checked.
