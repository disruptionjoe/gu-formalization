## Findings

**F1 — route-ordering lag, measured.** The family-completion route that
v0.173 executed was available in-repo since 2026-08-01: the B2C4 wave built
the wedge-Shiab/nonzero-southeast family on active Cl(9,5), showed the
Jordan defect is member-specific rather than family-universal, and exhibited
the `11/12` reciprocal relations, semisimplicity and symmetrizability
(`explorations/eric-curt-wave3d-b2c4-shiab-family-southeast-completion-2026-08-01.md`).
v0.163 then killed every southeast-only repair *and explicitly left
Shiab-family changes open*. Nine further waves (v0.164-v0.172) attacked the
old operator's defect with constraint families, symmetrizer searches,
polarization, quotients and ownership analyses before the port landed as
v0.173. Some of those waves have independent value (v0.163's no-go is
load-bearing for the family selection; v0.164-v0.167 built Noether,
preboundary, anti-dualizer and descent structure that survives operator
change); the honest counterfactual cost is roughly four to six waves, not
nine. The mechanism is structural, not a one-off: the queue is re-authored
every wave from the latest gate, so a locally-greedy chain never asks the
altitude question. **Proposed standing rule for the queue (posture-level,
for the cadence to adopt or reject): when a wave's target is repairing or
living with an obstruction, the wave's pre-flight must answer "is this
obstruction family-universal or member-specific, and does a sibling
construction already remove it?" with a citation either way.** The 08-01 /
08-11 pair is the worked example of the cost of not asking.

**F2 — the foundation fork has no resolver and the queue does not carry
that fact.** The whole campaign is conditional on real K77;
`SIGNATURE-AMBIENT` is the deepest live fork (fork-depth audit today:
depth 10, over threshold, reported every run). On 2026-08-08 the named
resolver M-H9 was falsified at Tier 1 (the endpoint mechanism does not
discriminate; register entry updated) — so the fork is over threshold
**with no named resolver**, and no queue rank since has either constructed
a replacement discriminator or filed a deliberate fork posture. The same
day produced real progress that should be weighed: the source-notation
mirroring certificate (`tests/source_signature_notation_is_mirrored.py`)
strengthens the author-asserted (7,7) reading, and the 25-lens council
converged on (7,7) — but the council is planning evidence by standing rule,
and the mirroring certificate types the source's notation, not the fork.
**Proposed queue addition (rank 4, non-displacing): either (a) a bounded
resolver-construction wave — new discriminator design for
`SIGNATURE-AMBIENT`, entered only through the six-axis/Layer-0 protocol, or
(b) a filed fork-posture decision recording that the campaign proceeds
K77-conditional-by-source-assertion with resolver search parked behind an
explicit wake condition.** Either move prices the liability; the one wrong
response is continuing to deepen the conditional stack silently while the
depth audit fires every run.
