## Result first

The package fails its hardening gate. Its current headline and abstract are
not citable as repository results. The failure is entailment, not merely a
missing bibliography:

1. W44 computes a flat-momentum projector and a synthetic superficial-degree
   ledger. It does not derive the curved interacting projected-Rarita--
   Schwinger action, its gauge fixing and ghosts, or its divergent effective
   action. Its finite counterterm list is enumerated from assumed dimensions;
   closure under renormalization is not computed. The further identification
   of the gamma-trace complement with “exactly the Velo--Zwanziger modes” is
   asserted, not established by the projector calculation. The paper may cite
   this as a conditional power-counting model, not a renormalizability theorem
   for the advertised class.
2. W45 itself labels the projected-spin-3/2 Weyl shift `ESTIMATED` and its
   `R^2` beta shift `GUESS` with anchor zero. It also explicitly proves that
   positive `f_0^2` is **not** asymptotically free. Salvio and Strumia's primary
   agravity follow-up likewise states that the conformal-mode coupling `f_0`
   is not asymptotically free and instead studies its large-`f_0` conformal
   limit ([arXiv:1705.03896](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.03896)). The candidate
   cannot promote the signed negative-ratio continuation in W80 into “the
   dimensionless couplings are asymptotically free” without first proving the
   physical coupling domain and the missing projected-spin-3/2 heat kernel.
3. Homogeneous quadratic beta functions do prove that any nonzero zero comes
   with a scale ray; they do not prove that the repository's guessed enlarged
   beta system is the theory's beta system. A Gaussian ultraviolet value
   `f_2=0` also does not predict the finite running coupling: the RG trajectory
   still carries its scale/integration datum. The candidate's “Weyl coupling
   is predicted rather than tuned” claim is withdrawn.
4. W48 explicitly finds that Krein pseudo-unitarity does not imply physical
   positivity and that the positive branch needs an added prescription with a
   microcausality cost. W54 proves only the absence of a polynomial/entire-
   symbol positive metric in the free Pais--Uhlenbeck model and labels the
   “any local field operator” extension unproved. W53 and W79 propagate an
   imported beta trajectory and finite/conditional scalar model. They do not
   construct the interacting physical state space, BRST/BV quotient, positive
   pairing, scattering operator or optical theorem for the advertised field
   theory. The paper's tree/algebraic unitarity headline is therefore too
   strong even though the pseudo-unitary and conditional finite-model
   identities remain reproducible.
5. The algebraic formula `M_0^2=gamma/(6 beta)` for a declared quadratic
   `f(R)` model survives conditionally. The claim that asymptotic freedom
   **forces** `beta<0` does not: it depends on the guessed projected-RS
   `R^2` coefficient, a signed continuation whose physical domain is not
   proved, and a native-action identification that has not been derived.

The candidate is therefore reclassified `FAILED_HARDENING_GATE` and
`PARKED_REQUIRES_RECONSTRUCTION`. Its old prose remains in place as visibly
fenced historical drafting evidence so provenance is not erased.

## Primary-overlap packet

The broad claims are already occupied in the literature, so novelty must live
in the projected-spin-3/2 delta and its joint proof:

- renormalizability of local quadratic gravity is the Stelle baseline, also
  treated as prior art by the candidate;
- the agravity beta system and the non-asymptotic-freedom of `f_0` are explicit
  in [Salvio--Strumia 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.4226) and
  [Salvio--Strumia 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.03896);
- PT-symmetric proposals for higher-derivative gravity are an existing claim
  family, including [Mannheim 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12743), whose
  own pure-fourth-order limit discussion is not a substitute for the missing
  interacting projected-RS construction here;
- nonperturbative fourth-derivative truncations already contain both
  asymptotically free and asymptotically safe fixed-point scenarios, e.g.
  [Sen--Wetterich--Yamada 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.04696);
- even the massless Rarita--Schwinger carrier and gamma-trace reduction require
  care: [Valenzuela--Zanelli 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.00106) derives a
  surviving spin-1/2 sector in the standard massless system and warns that the
  usual added elimination does not automatically describe the original
  Lagrangian system.

This bounded search does **not** prove no novelty. It proves that the only
defensible novelty target is narrower than the current paper: a complete,
typed projected-RS interacting calculation that distinguishes its action and
degrees of freedom from those prior classes. That calculation is absent.

## Correction-currency replay

The July package predates the August correction registry. `CC-02` requires
ambient indefiniteness to remain distinct from the still-open positivity of an
observed physical quotient. The candidate instead leaps from finite Krein/PT
models to a positive interacting physical sector; this is not the withdrawn
“positivity is impossible” reading, but it is still an unsupported bridge.
`CC-09` fixes the GU massive-spin-2 Yukawa coefficient at `-4/3`; the candidate
does not state the old `+1/3` coefficient, so no direct sign-text correction is
needed. Neither correction supplies the missing field-theory proof.

## Exact reopening packet

The package may re-enter hardening only when one coherent packet supplies:

1. the complete projected-spin-3/2 action on the claimed background class,
   including gauge symmetries, gauge fixing, ghost complex and domains;
2. an independent curved heat-kernel or equivalent divergent-effective-action
   calculation that closes the actual counterterm basis;
3. source-verified beta functions for every load-bearing coupling, including
   the projected-spin-3/2 `C^2` and `R^2` contributions and the physical
   coupling domain;
4. a typed interacting physical quotient/pairing and an optical-theorem or
   equivalent unitarity argument at the grade claimed; and
5. a primary-source novelty matrix binding the resulting theorem—not the
   generic quadratic-gravity baseline—to the exact literature delta.

Until then, use only the surviving conditional components: the degree-zero
projector identity, synthetic power-counting model, conditional beta-system
algebra, free-model metric obstruction at its stated scope, and conditional
quadratic-`f(R)` scalaron formula.

## Hostile review and ceiling

The strongest contrary response is that every W44--W80 script passes. That is
true and non-dispositive: most scripts verify implications **inside values and
operators they encode**, and several declare their load-bearing coefficient a
guess or their theorem extension unproved. Exit zero proves implementation
consistency with those assumptions, not the assumptions or the advertised
field theory.

The strongest overclaim in this audit would be “the theory is not
renormalizable/unitary/asymptotically complete.” No such conclusion follows.
Only the staged package and its current evidence entailment fail. No canon,
GU verdict, source ownership, prediction, confirmation or public posture
changes.
