# B5 constraint-surplus audit

## The question this settles, and the one it does not

An epistemic disagreement was blocking a construction route: is **positing** the
residual — building the piece that makes the rest of the puzzle fit — a
legitimate test, or is it curve-fitting?

The disagreement resolves into a number rather than an argument:

```text
surplus = (independent constraints expressible on the residual)
          - (free parameters of the residual)
```

Positive: success was not guaranteed, so a surviving posit is evidence. Zero or
negative: accommodation with freedom to spare.

The orthodox objection — *shaped to fit teaches nothing* — is true only in the
second case. Under **surplus constraint** it is simply false, and the canonical
demonstration is Standard Model hypercharge: fit to the observed particles,
derived by nobody, and then found to cancel six independent anomaly conditions
with no freedom left. That surplus is most of why the fit was informative.

**This investigation computes the number. It does not adjudicate the epistemics, which are
not in doubt.**

## Result

| side | value |
|---|---|
| free parameters | **1** observable integer, 11 admissible values (from 1024 assignments) |
| expressible FORCED rows | **0 of 8** |
| surplus | **uncomputable** |

Every one of `SA-Y1`, `SA-Y7a`, `SA-G9`, `SA-C2`, `SA-C4`, `SA-U1`, `SA-U3`,
`SA-U4` classifies OUTSIDE. Three have no test at all by the spec's own record
(`SA-C4` named unbuilt, `SA-U3`'s bound underived, `SA-U4` literature-anchored).
The other five have tests that share **no distinctive certified object** with the
B5 observer-symbol ledger.

## The decisive reading

**The blocker on positing is not epistemic. It is a missing bridge.**

Nothing here says the posit would be uninformative. It says the informativeness
**cannot currently be measured**, because no forced constraint and the residual
are expressed on common objects. The meter reads *unknown*, not *low*.

That is a much better problem to have, because it is buildable and small:

> Make **one** FORCED row expressible against the signed phase sum.

One bridge converts positing from an unmeasurable move into a measurable test,
and the surplus becomes computable the moment it exists. `SA-C2` is the natural
first candidate — it is FORCED, it has two live tests, and its `g = 1` cure acts
on the same `ker Gamma` structure the ledger's provenances name — but this run
does not establish that the bridge is constructible, only that none exists today.

## Controls, including one that fired

The first execution **FAILED control `N2`** and voided the run: the expressibility
proxy admitted single-character objects (`S`, `X` from the slot provenances),
which occur as ordinary identifiers in unrelated files and made every row
classify EXPRESSIBLE — including a deliberately unrelated planted row. Tightening
the proxy to distinctive objects (length `>= 3` plus colon-qualified slot names)
produced the reported result and both planted controls now fire correctly.

Recorded because the failure mode is instructive: **a permissive matcher makes a
surplus look large.** Any future surplus computation should be planted-tested
before its number is read.

- `P1` 1024 assignments, 11 observable values — matches both prior runs.
- `P2` distinctive object inventory nonempty; no short tokens survive.
- `N1` a planted B5-citing row classifies EXPRESSIBLE.
- `N2` a planted unrelated row classifies OUTSIDE.

## Proxy limit, binding

Object-sharing is a proxy for expressibility. A row classified OUTSIDE **may
still constrain the residual through a bridge nobody has built**. This investigation closes
no row and proves no inexpressibility; it reports which rows have no bridge
today.

## What moved

Nothing. No claim, canon, verdict, count, priority, posture, or packet field.
The contribution is a measured answer to a question that was being argued, and a
precise, small, buildable reopener in place of an impasse.
