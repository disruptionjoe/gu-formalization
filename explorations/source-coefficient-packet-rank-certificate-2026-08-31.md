# Frozen source coefficient packet and rank certificate

## Result

`lab/sources/source-coefficient-packet-v0.1.yaml` freezes an immutable
projection of the ratified source-claim register at SHA-256
`dc3ae173b4cc0eb2a0e343e3d92d5f110b8e4f6eef3e2852a1e897ab4371ade6`.
It selects thirteen action, fermion, family, and prediction rows by canonical
row hash. Quotations and evidence remain in the register; the packet owns only
edition selection, precedence, branch declarations, source-silent slots, and
forbidden imports.

The strict source matrix has zero equations on the four slots
`(d54_first,d54_second,d210_first,d210_second)`. Separately labeled
representation exchange contributes exactly two equations, one inside each
owner coordinate, and has rank two. Its solution space therefore retains two
independent owner coordinates. No equation in the packet supplies a family
covector, chooses an owner, relates `d54` to `d210`, or normalizes either one.

Lean proves the generic discriminator: a homogeneous coefficient constraint
is unique at zero exactly when its linear map is injective, and any nonzero
kernel direction gives a second solution through every solution. The strict
packet's zero constraint contains distinct `54`-axis and `210`-axis solutions,
so it cannot select an owner packet.

## Preflight and route selection

Retrieval searched the exact source rows, action coefficients, family
covectors, `54`/`210` owner language, exchange symmetry, normalizations, and
prior correction history before construction. The source corpus was already
extracted; re-mining transcripts would have created a second, less controlled
evidence path. The chosen route projects the existing ledger by content hash,
then asks only which exact equations the projection contains.

The representation-equivariant matrix is deliberately outside the strict
source model. It is a repository-derived structural extension, useful for
showing that equal coefficients on equivalent copies still leave the owner
axis free. Hypothetical normalization, family-covector, owner-ratio, and
real/Krein closures remain separately declared reconstruction extensions.

## Independent controls

The exact probe verifies the full register hash, all thirteen canonical row
hashes, polarity preservation, zero strict-source rank, rank two after family
exchange, and the two-dimensional surviving kernel. Hostile controls show
that inserting either a `54:210` relation or a single-owner equation raises
the rank; neither equation can be smuggled into the frozen packet without a
detectable change.

## Hostile review and claim ceiling

**Strongest overclaim.** Packet-relative source silence is not a theorem that
Weinstein never supplied or will never supply a coefficient relation. A later
source edition must mint a new packet and can change the rank.

**Strongest contrary route.** A complete action may supply a family covector,
real/Krein relation, normalization, or owner equation that closes the kernel.
The generic Lean theorem explicitly admits that route: uniqueness becomes
possible only when every nonzero kernel direction is removed.

**Weakest seam.** The representation matrix models exchange of two equivalent
copies inside each owner. It does not prove that a physical action uses these
four scalar slots, that the two families are observed families, or that `54`
and `210` are the only operators in a completed action.
