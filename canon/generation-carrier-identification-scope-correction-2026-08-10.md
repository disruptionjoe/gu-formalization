# Generation-carrier identification scope correction

## Canonical statement

The repository's exact theorems on the fixed `192`-dimensional subspace `W`
remain valid **on that supplied carrier**. They do not derive or uniquely
identify `W` as the physical generation carrier.

The formerly cited internal `Spin(10)` quadratic-Casimir value cannot perform
that selection. On the explicit substrate it is the same scalar on the whole
`4 tensor 128` block, so an unrelated planted `192`-dimensional subspace passes
the same test. The test verifies inherited representation content; it does not
distinguish `W` from rivals. Consequently, the `640/832/192` split and every
theorem proved after fixing `W` are conditional inputs to a physical-carrier
construction, not a derivation of three physical families.

This scope correction does not show that `W` is wrong. A future construction
may select exactly this subspace. The next admissible selector must be owned by
the action or source geometry and must distinguish the proposed `W` from at
least its mirror, planted random subspaces, and the `640`- and `832`-dimensional
complements. Ordinary observation pullback is not such a selector.

## Exact byproduct kept at its proper type

On the explicit fixed carrier and current Standard-Model embedding,
`ker Gamma` contains exactly `40` Standard-Model-neutral states: `32` in the
`16`-type sector and `8` in the `144`-type sector. This is a representation
count only. It does not establish that the states are light, survive the
action-owned reduction, are anomaly-free physical states, or are dark matter.

## Assumptions and failure modes

- The fixed-carrier results assume the declared `W`, pairing, grading and
  representation embedding.
- The correction fails if an independent source/action construction is shown
  to select `W`; that would upgrade the selection step, not invalidate the
  conditional theorems.
- The neutral count can change its physical meaning after the moving action
  reduction, mass operator and anomaly constraints are constructed.
- The ambient T1--T4 claim from the 2026-08-09 exploration remains unpromoted
  pending its own hostile verification.
