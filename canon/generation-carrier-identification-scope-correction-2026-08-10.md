# Generation-carrier identification scope correction

## Canonical statement

The repository's exact theorems on the fixed `192`-dimensional subspace `W`
remain valid **on that supplied carrier**. They do not derive or uniquely
identify `W` as the physical generation carrier.

The formerly cited internal `Spin(10)` quadratic-Casimir value cannot perform
that selection. On the explicit substrate it is the same scalar on the whole

least its mirror, planted random subspaces, and the `640`- and `832`-dimensional
