# Referee invitation guide: who to invite and what to test

*Internal companion to `REVIEWER.md`. This guide prepares a possible review
request. It does not authorize contacting anyone, sending the paper, or
describing the work as externally validated.*

## Review profiles

Prefer reviewers whose expertise matches a load-bearing boundary rather than
people chosen only because they are adjacent to Geometric Unity.

1. **Stable homotopy or framed-bordism specialist.** Ask them to inspect the
   `Z/24 -> Z/8 x Z/3` decomposition, the use and limits of the Adams
   `e`-invariant, and the absence of an integer-valued bridge from torsion.
2. **Clifford representation or spin-geometry specialist.** Ask them to test
   the finite equivariant class, the Pati-Salam-relative carrier statements,
   the real-form distinctions, and whether any excluded operator class can
   change the completeness ceiling.
3. **Index-theory or mathematical-physics specialist.** Ask them to inspect
   the finite Krein-transversality result, the boundary eta inputs, and the
   exact boundary between proved mathematics and the true-`Y14`/source-action
   interpretation.
4. **Formal-methods or reproducibility reviewer.** Ask them to run the pinned
   numerical and Lean surfaces in `REVIEWER.md`, audit the claim-and-premise
   ledger, and report any gap between executable statements and manuscript
   prose.

The cited bibliography is the first candidate pool because it identifies
people already working on the exact mathematical objects. Before any request,
verify current contact details, conflicts of interest, and the person's actual
fit from current public information. Do not infer willingness or endorsement
from citation alone.

## Minimum review questions

Every invitation should ask for criticism of a bounded object, not a general
verdict on GU:

- Which theorem statement, carrier, codomain, or premise is mistyped or too
  broad?
- Does the finite class omit a natural operator or map that defeats the stated
  ceiling?
- Is any torsion, index, or representation multiplicity being read as an
  integer generation count without a proved bridge?
- Does the manuscript claim more than the Lean and numerical artifacts
  establish?
- What is the strongest counterexample or nearest prior result the paper must
  address?

## Suggested packet

Send only if Joe separately authorizes the exact outreach. The packet should
contain a pinned paper revision, `REVIEWER.md`, the public repository link, the
claim-and-premise ledger, and one sentence stating that verification is
internal-tier and AI-produced. Ask for permission before naming the reviewer
or publishing their comments.

An endorsement request and a scientific referee request are different. The
former concerns category suitability; the latter tests correctness and scope.
Do not combine them in one message or treat either as agreement with the
paper's conclusions.
