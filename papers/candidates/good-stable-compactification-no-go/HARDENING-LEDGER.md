## Evidence rerun

repository dependency set. They passed 35/35, 44/44, 27/27, 46/46, 26/26, and
40/40 checks, respectively, under NumPy 2.5.1 and SciPy 1.18.0. The W241 program
is retained as historical evidence only; its false fixed-frame converse is not
accepted by the paper. The dependency file is unpinned, so environment-level
byte reproducibility is not claimed.

## Literature collision result

Cartan decomposition, maximal-compact conjugacy, compact averaging, real reductive
orbits, and parabolic/highest-weight stabilizers are standard. The paper makes no
priority claim over those results. The candidate's useful contribution is an
auditable theorem/application split for the fixed grading problem and the exact
classification of what a proposed escape must supply.
