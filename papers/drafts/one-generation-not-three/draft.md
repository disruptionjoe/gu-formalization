mechanism it analyzes produces them.** Its headline is the opposite: the one concrete generation-structure

not in the existence of the three-term branching (§13); **(e)** the prediction candidates of §14 are

## Abstract

The 2021 Geometric Unity draft proposes that its matter content yields "three families, really two plus
one," the third family being "an imposter for representation theoretic reasons" that "at low energy …
look[s] the same as the other two." We give the exact mathematical content, structure theory, and limits of
this mechanism on the program's reconstructed kinematic carrier, the gamma-traceless Rarita-Schwinger
module of Cl(9,5) ≅ M(64, ℍ) over the 14-dimensional metric bundle with base+fibre split 4 + 10.

The mechanism is a product rule: for an orthogonal split V ⊕ W with both parts even-dimensional, the
gamma-traceless Rarita-Schwinger functor satisfies RS(V ⊕ W) = RS(V)⊗S(W) ⊕ S(V)⊗RS(W) ⊕ S(V)⊗S(W),
verified by exact dimension count (1664 = 384 + 1152 + 128 on the 4+10 split) and at operator level. The
third term is the **exact defect of Leibniz locality under the joint constraint**: the traceful
vector-spinor obeys a clean two-term Leibniz rule with no third term, so the extra family-shaped block
comes precisely from gamma-tracelessness. We characterize it exactly: it is the (10, −4)-weighted
antidiagonal of the two trace-insertion directions — annihilated by neither factor's gamma-trace alone,
only by their sum — allocation-invariant across all sign allocations under both candidate signatures, an
orthogonal summand and exact tight frame, the unique cut vertex bridging the two native blocks, the unique
summand pairing like a spin-1/2 Dirac field under the ambient Krein form, and the unique block with
Lorentz-label-matched partners under the computed reunification asymmetry 10⊗16 = 144 ⊕ 16 (the spin-3/2
16s are stranded). A master arity law (b trace conditions on a k-part split yield b(k−1) defect copies) and
a coboundary theorem (the defect is exact, class zero) fix the mechanism's shape and its topological
triviality. The base+fibre split is canonical to the program's own tautological section, and 4 + 10 is the
unique mod-3-clean even/even split of 14 — block multiplicities {3⁰, 3¹, 3²} — a striking selectivity datum
that we hard-fence: multiplicity, not index.

The limits are the point. Five independent results kill every count reading; the block is kinematically
vectorlike under both signatures (the honest obstruction to any naive three-generations reading); the
program's own 2021 draft labels only the third term a generation, falsifying the popular reading of the
slogan; the referent of "imposter" is an open two-way fork which we state rather than resolve; and any
chiral use of the spin-3/2 reading owes an unpaid exit from the Witten (1983) Kaluza-Klein gravitino class.
One structural discriminator survives at candidate grade: the imposter is not a Leibniz partner, so it must
behave differently from the two true families at high energy — with the reunification scale honestly
unconstrained.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 What this paper is

Geometric Unity's public materials contain exactly one concrete mechanism for the number of fermion
families: the claim that the theory's matter decomposition "will yield you three families, really two plus
one," where "the third family is an imposter for representation theoretic reasons, but at low energy, it'll
look the same as the other two" (in-repo transcript; see §10). Predecessor work in this program — *Located,
Not Forced* (Hernandez 2026, hereafter LNF) — audited the question from the outside and found that the
interior kinematic structure cannot force a generation count. This paper goes inside the one mechanism
actually on offer and asks: what exactly does it do, what structure does it have, and what can it not do?

The answers, at the strength earned:

1. **The mechanism is real and exact.** The "2+1" arises from a product rule for the gamma-traceless
   Rarita-Schwinger functor over a split, and the "+1" is the precise failure of that functor to satisfy
   Leibniz locality under the joint gamma-trace constraint. The traceful control isolates the origin: no
   gamma-tracelessness, no third block (§3).
2. **The "+1" is fully characterized.** It is the (10, −4) relative gamma-trace mode — the weighted
   antidiagonal of the two factors' trace-insertion directions, killed by neither trace alone, only by
   their sum — with an allocation-invariant operator description, orthogonality and tight-frame structure,
   a cut-vertex separator property, a unique Krein form-type, and a computed reunification asymmetry
   (§§4–6).
3. **The split is selective.** The 4+10 split is canonical to the program's own tautological section, and
   it is the unique mod-3-clean even/even split of 14: block multiplicities {3⁰, 3¹, 3²}. This datum is
   striking and hard-fenced: it is a fact about multiplicities, not indices, and supplies no count (§7).
4. **The mechanism cannot count.** Five independent results close every route from this decomposition to a
   generation number; the block is kinematically vectorlike under both candidate signatures; the defect
   class is exact, hence topologically trivial; and the program's own primary text labels only the third
   term a generation (§§8–10).
5. **What remains** is a mechanism that *locates* one distinguished family-shaped block with unusually
   sharp structure, an open referent fork about which block the program's word "imposter" names, an unpaid
   prior-art burden at spin 3/2, and one structural high-energy discriminator at candidate grade
   (§§11–14).

The title states the result: this mechanism, as it stands on the reconstructed carrier, yields one
distinguished generation-shaped object — not three generations.

### 1.2 What this paper does not claim

This paper does not claim that GU is correct or incorrect physics. It does not claim a generation count of
any value, from this mechanism or any other; the five kill results of §8.4 are precisely the reasons no
such claim is available from this material. It does not claim that the kinematic carrier used throughout is
the physical carrier (no physical projector/quotient has been constructed; §2.3). It does not resolve the
imposter referent fork (§11). It does not claim the product-rule branching is new mathematics (§13). And
its prediction candidates (§14) are explicitly shape-level: no energy scale is derived, so no experiment is
named that must see anything.

### 1.3 Relation to prior program work

LNF proved a scoped two-primary result: within an explicitly delimited finite class built from the

interior cannot force three. A companion in-repo survey (*No-Go Theorems as Class-Relative Obstructions*)

## 3. The product rule and the Leibniz defect

### 3.1 The identity

For an orthogonal split V ⊕ W of the 14 directions with dim V and dim W both even, the gamma-traceless
Rarita-Schwinger functor RS(·) = ker Γ ⊂ (·) ⊗ S(·) satisfies the product rule

```text
RS(V ⊕ W)  =  RS(V) ⊗ S(W)   ⊕   S(V) ⊗ RS(W)   ⊕   S(V) ⊗ S(W)
              \_____________ Leibniz terms _____________/    \_ defect _/
```

On GU's base+fibre split 4 + 10 the dimensions are

```text
1664  =  384  +  1152  +  128 ,
```

with 384 = RS(V₄)⊗S(W₁₀) = 12·32, 1152 = S(V₄)⊗RS(W₁₀) = 4·288, and 128 = S(V₄)⊗S(W₁₀) = 4·32 — and
1664 is exactly dim ker Γ. The identity was verified by exact dimension count on every even/even split
tested, including a planted wrong-subtraction kill control, and the (10, −4) operator realization of §4
verifies it at operator level on the explicit Clifford builder.

**Grade.** This is exact multiplicity arithmetic plus operator verification, *not* a full
representation-theoretic proof: internal quantum numbers of the three factors are not checked here, and
"family-shaped" refers to dimension and form-type, not to verified Standard-Model content.

### 3.2 The traceful control

The decisive control: the **traceful** vector-spinor V ⊗ S(V) obeys a clean two-term Leibniz rule

```text
(V ⊕ W) ⊗ S(V ⊕ W)  =  [V ⊗ S(V)] ⊗ S(W)   ⊕   S(V) ⊗ [W ⊗ S(W)] ,
```

with **no third term**. The mechanism of the extra block is therefore isolated exactly: the two Leibniz
terms each subtract a full copy of S(V)⊗S(W) (one gamma-trace condition per factor — two conditions),
while the joint constraint on V ⊕ W subtracts only one. The difference is exactly one copy of
S(V)⊗S(W).

> The third family-shaped block comes **precisely** from gamma-tracelessness. Remove the gamma-trace
> constraint and it vanishes.

This is what makes "imposter" mechanically apt: the first two terms are Leibniz partners of the same kind;
the third is a correction of a different kind that happens to have the shape of a family. It also sources
the one surviving discriminator (§14.2): an object that is not a Leibniz partner has no reason to behave
like one when the constraint structure that created it becomes visible.

### 3.3 Scope: the even/even condition, carried by name

The first verification run *failed its own control* on the splits 3+11 and 9+5. Both are odd ⊕ odd
splits, where the ungraded spinor exponential property S(V ⊕ W) = S(V) ⊗ S(W) breaks by exactly a factor
of two (the odd Clifford algebra's central element). This is a known fact which the control forced into the
open as an explicit scope condition rather than a latent error: **the product rule as stated holds for
even/even splits; odd/odd splits are excluded by name.** GU's split 4+10 is even/even, so the result
applies under either ambient signature. (Layer-0 fence: "9+5" here is a *split* of 14; it is not the
ambient signature (9,5). The two are homonyms and neither implies anything about the other.)

### 3.4 The affine-functor form and the arity law

The structural home of the identity: S is an exponential functor (S(V ⊕ W) = S(V) ⊗ S(W) in the even
setting), the traceful vector-spinor functor is *additive* over splits (Leibniz), and the gamma-traceless
functor is **affine** — linear part minus a constant term — so its failure to be Leibniz is exactly its
constant term. The defect of an affine functor under a k-part split composes accordingly, giving the
master **arity law**:

- b independent trace conditions imposed over a k-part split produce **b(k−1)** defect copies;
- a single gamma-trace over a k-part split gives **2k − 1** blocks, of which k − 1 are defect copies.

For k = 2 this is the 3-block "2+1". For the author's own refinement 10 = 6 + 4 (a 3-part split of 14) the
law gives five blocks, "3+2" — the mechanism is not intrinsically a "three" machine; the number of blocks
is a function of how many times you cut, which is the first structural warning against reading it as a
count. The same law kills the "pairwise meeting" picture: k-part splits give k − 1 extras, not C(k,2);
in the graded-wall (Brauer-Wall) picture, walls compose, they do not pair.

### 3.5 The coboundary theorem: the defect is exact

At the level of the associated complex of factor assignments, the defect term is a coboundary: it equals
δR for an explicit one-factor assignment R, hence its class is **zero**. Two consequences are drawn at the
strength earned. Structurally: the "+1" is a canonical *coordinate artifact of constraint bookkeeping* —
real, exactly characterizable, and topologically trivial. Limitatively: **no protected quantity — no index,
no anomaly coefficient, no bordism class — can ever be extracted from the defect as a class**, because the
class vanishes identically. This is count kill #2 in §8.4.

## 4. The (10, −4) characterization

### 4.1 Trace-insertion maps and the antidiagonal

Define the metric-dual trace-insertion (embedding) maps ι_B, ι_F : S → C¹⁴ ⊗ S by ι_B(ψ)_a = η_aa e_a ψ
for a in the base directions and ι_F likewise for the fibre directions. Then, exactly and
allocation-invariantly,

```text
Γ ∘ ι_B = 4 · I     and     Γ ∘ ι_F = 10 · I ,
```

the coefficients being the dimensions of the two parts. Neither insertion direction alone lies in ker Γ.
The **imposter block is the (10, −4)-weighted antidiagonal**:

```text
Imposter  =  ker Γ ∩ span{ι_B, ι_F}  =  im(10·ι_B − 4·ι_F) ,     dim_C = 128 ,
```

annihilated by neither factor's gamma-trace alone, only by the weighted difference that cancels between
them. This is the sense — exact, not metaphorical — in which the third block is the *relative gamma-trace
mode* of the two geometries: the common spinor mode that the joint constraint deletes once instead of
twice.

### 4.2 Allocation invariance

The characterization was machine-verified on the explicit Clifford builder under **both** ambient
signatures and **all five** sign allocations to the 4+10 split per signature (ten runs): the identities
Γ∘ι_B = 4·I and Γ∘ι_F = 10·I hold exactly in every run, and the block is the same subspace in every run.
The mechanism does not care which real form the reconstruction picked — which is precisely why it survives
the signature fork of §2.3 while so many other program statements do not.

### 4.3 Orthogonality and the tight frame

Two exact facts sharpen the structure (found during the §9 computation; machine zero in all ten runs):

1. **The three blocks are mutually orthogonal.** The insertion maps are exactly the adjoints of the
   partial traces (ι_B = Γ_B†, ι_F = Γ_F†), so the imposter im(10·ι_B − 4·ι_F) is orthogonal to both
   native blocks *by construction*: the 3 : 9 : 1 decomposition is an orthogonal direct sum, not merely a
   direct sum.
2. **The antidiagonal is an exact tight frame**: with X = 10·ι_B − 4·ι_F, one has X†X = 560·I exactly
   (560 = 100·4 + 16·10). The imposter embedding is a rigid, isometry-up-to-scalar copy of S inside
   ker Γ.

## 5. Separator structure and the Krein form type

### 5.1 The cut-vertex theorem

On the frozen B5 observer-symbol multiplicity matrix (the program's banked symbol-level adjacency data),
the block decomposition has an exact separator structure: there are **zero direct symbol edges between the
two native blocks**, and **every imposter slot has exactly one neighbor in each**. In graph terms the
imposter is a **cut vertex**: the unique bridge through which the two geometries' matter sectors
communicate at symbol level. "The block where the two geometries meet" is thus true in exactly one sense —
adjacency — and false in every locational sense (§8.1).

### 5.2 Krein form type

Under the ambient indefinite (Krein) spinor pairing, all three blocks are **neutral** — inertia
(192,192) ⊕ (576,576) ⊕ (64,64), summing to the independently certified (832,832) on ker Γ; no block

distinction is finer: the imposter is the **unique summand whose induced pairing is the ambient spinor
form β itself (rescaled)** — it *pairs like a spin-1/2 Dirac field*, while both native blocks pair like
vector-spinors. This is the sharpest operator-level reading available of the author's phrase "at low
energy, it'll look the same as the other two": the imposter is form-indistinguishable from a true
spin-1/2 family at the level of its induced pairing. Fence: this is a kinematic form-type statement;
finite-rank Krein data are non-transportable to the interacting spectrum.
