\section{Coupled variation and local algebraic reduction}
\label{sec:reduction}

Use natural field coordinates $g=g_*+u$ and $T=T_*(g)+t$.
This is an invertible differential change of variables, not a restriction
of $t$ to the background ansatz. It transports boundary data.
The formal quadratic equations are
\begin{equation}
 Ct+Au=0\quad\hbox{on }X\times V,\qquad
 Mu+A^\dagger t=0\quad\hbox{on }X.\label{eq:coupled}
\end{equation}
The adjoint $A^\dagger$ in this notation includes integration over $V$.
The same $u(x)$ occurs at every fibre point; replacing its Euler equation
by pointwise fibre equations or section evaluation changes the action.
Here $C$ is the actual distortion Hessian, $A=D_gE_T(g,T_*(g))$, and
$M$ is the pure-base Hessian in these coordinates.

\subsection{The complete mixed operator}
For a base covector $q$ and symmetric polarization $u$, define
\begin{align}
 L_i{}^a{}_j&=\tfrac12\eta^{ab}(q_i u_{bj}+q_j u_{bi}-q_bu_{ij}),
 \nonumber\\
 C_i(h)&=L_i^Th+hL_i,\qquad
 k_{iA}=-D_h(C_i(h),A),\qquad k_{ij}=k_{AB}=0.\label{eq:lift}
\end{align}
With $Q=(q,0)$, the third-base-derivative Ricci coefficient is
\begin{equation}
 R^{(3)}_{ab}=\tfrac12\left(Q_aQ^ck_{bc}+Q_bQ^ck_{ac}
                -Q^2k_{ab}-Q_aQ_b\tr_Gk\right).\label{eq:A3}
\end{equation}
All contractions here use $G_Y$. The second-order coefficient is
\begin{equation}
 \delta\Ric_Y=\diag(\delta\Ric_g,0_{10}).\label{eq:A2}
\end{equation}
In each case apply the grade-one Shiab covector: if
$\mathsf E=\Ric^\sharp-\Scal I/2$, its row on $e^\mu\gamma_\nu$ is
$-\mathsf E^\mu{}_{\nu}$. The covector array must not be confused with
its Riesz endomorphism by dropping signature signs.

These formulas give $A=A_3+A_2$, with $A_0=A_1=0$.
The finite second-jet calculation spans all torsion-free connection jets
$N[k][i]^a_j=N[k][j]^a_i$ with closed trace
$\tr N[k][i]=\tr N[i][k]$. There are 170 explicit generators spanning
this 154-dimensional space. On all of them the radial variations vanish
and the full formal-adjoint contribution from the moving horizontal
projector cancels. Six anisotropic/sheared fibre controls supplement the
coordinate covariance argument \cite{Mixed,Algebraic}.
The zeroth-order term vanishes because a constant $g$ variation leaves
its connection unchanged. A first connection value is generated by a
coordinate change with identity first jet; naturality of the identically
zero background Euler field then gives $A_1=0$.
In particular $A(\mathcal L_\xi g_*)=0$.

At the observing section $h=\eta$, $C_i=q_i u$, so $k$ is a pure ambient
metric gauge symbol and $A_3$ vanishes. This is not true off section.
For example take $q=(1,1,0,0)$ and $u=\diag(0,0,1,-1)$.
Its base Ricci variation is zero. On $h=\diag(1+\rho,-1,-1,-1)$ the
nonzero mixed Ricci entries include
\begin{equation}
 R^{(3)}_{2,02}=-\frac{\rho^2}{2(1+\rho)^2},\qquad
 R^{(3)}_{3,03}=+\frac{\rho^2}{2(1+\rho)^2},\label{eq:nullcontrol}
\end{equation}
and their symmetric partners; $02,03$ denote fibre coordinates.
Thus the pure natural-metric lift does not intertwine the base Ricci
operator with the full ambient equation.

A leading compensator exists for $Q^2\ne0$. Set
$d_b=Q^ak_{ab}$ and
$k_\perp=k-(Q\otimes d+d\otimes Q)/Q^2$.
The transverse tracefree spin-connection variation
\begin{equation}
 y_i=\frac14\sum_{a<b}\eta_a\eta_b
       \left(Q_b(k_\perp)_{ai}-Q_a(k_\perp)_{ib}\right)\gamma_a\gamma_b
 \label{eq:compensator}
\end{equation}
satisfies $E(Q)y=A_3(q)u$, where $E$ is the skew Euler coefficient
from \eqref{eq:formal-adjoint}. The coefficient identity is checked on
the complete transverse tracefree basis and extends by covariance and
polynomiality after clearing the denominator. The unprojected spin
variation has additional Euler rows and fails this check.
Equation \eqref{eq:compensator} cancels a principal term only; it is
neither a full coupled mode nor an inverse at $Q^2=0$.

\subsection{Two different algebraic eliminations}
Ordinary coefficient conjugation fixes the action and background, so the
Hessian has independent $R$ and $J$ packets from \eqref{eq:packets}.
The metric lies in $R$; $J$ has no mixed metric source at quadratic order.
Clifford parity gives another decomposition, $t=o+e$, into odd and even
grades. Shiab reverses parity, so
\begin{equation}
 C=\begin{pmatrix}V_o&D_1\\D_1^\dagger&V_e\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 e=-V_e^{-1}D_1^\dagger o.\label{eq:even}
\end{equation}
The diagonal blocks are algebraic and $D_1$ is first order, including
connection and grade-two-background terms. For the selected background
$V_e$ is invertible: XOR labels give fourteen representative
$14\times14$ blocks, and all 39 distinct nonconstant determinant factors
have rational bounds excluding zero at \eqref{eq:rootinterval}.
For example the unphased weight-one radial block has determinant
\begin{equation}
 -\frac{\kappa(44a+12c-3\kappa)^{12}(528a+40c+3\kappa)}{1594323}.
 \label{eq:even-det}
\end{equation}
Complexified stabilizer covariance transports determinant nonvanishing;
it does not transport physical real energy signs. Phasing the real slice
preserves invertibility. This elimination introduces no covector
inverse, including at the null locus. The remaining odd equation is
$(V_o-D_1V_e^{-1}D_1^\dagger)o+Au=0$.
It is an equivalence of formal bulk equations, not inversion of $C$.

For the Hamiltonian it is more useful to eliminate the time kernel.
Write the unphased real distortion operator as
\begin{equation}
 C_R=E^\mu\nabla_\mu+V,
 \quad V=\kappa M_H+aH_a+cH_c+vH_v+wH_w.\label{eq:V}
\end{equation}
Each $H$ is the full cubic action Hessian, differentiating all three
slots. The Clifford tensors are parallel, hence $\nabla E=0$.
Fix the exact time covector $q=\dd x^0$ on its positive-square patch.

\begin{lemma}[Geometric primary kernel]
\label{lem:kernel}
If $N(q)$ is a smooth frame of $\ker E(q)$, then
\begin{equation}
 N^TE^AN=0,\qquad N^TE^\mu\nabla_\mu N=0.\label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}
Consequently the primary auxiliary matrix is exactly $W=N^TVN$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Orthogonal covariance gives constant rank on the positive-square locus.
Differentiate $E(q)N(q)=0$ once and twice in covector components and
multiply on the left by $N^T$, using skewness of $E(q)$:
\[
 N^TE^AN=0,\qquad
 N^T(E^AN_{,B}+E^BN_{,A})=0.
\]
Since $q=\dd x^0$, torsion freeness makes $\nabla_Aq_B$ symmetric.
Contracting the second identity proves the second part of
\eqref{eq:kernel} in a normal frame. A change of kernel frame adds a
within-kernel derivative, killed by the first identity.
\end{proof}

Choose a smooth complement with inclusion $\iota$ and write
$t=\iota s+Nz$. In the real packet put
\begin{equation}
 F=N^TC_R\iota,\quad B_0=N^TA,\qquad
 z=-W^{-1}(Fs+B_0u).\label{eq:primary}
\end{equation}
The operator $F$ has no time derivative. All forty primary classes in
each packet are invertible at the exact stationary root. Their labels
are quartets under toggling indices $0$ and $10$; representatives have
$0\le n_H\le3$, $0\le n_V\le9$, with multiplicity
$\binom3{n_H}\binom9{n_V}$.
Scaling even kernel coordinates by $\kappa$ and dividing $W$ by
$\kappa$ puts its entries in $\QQ(w_*)$. Every determinant is nonzero
there. Clifford parity $P$ gives
\begin{equation}
 W_-=-PW_+P,\qquad P^2=1,\label{eq:Wmirror}
\end{equation}
covering the mirror branch exactly.
The packet coefficient counts $(113792,115584)$ and time-form ranks
$(64482,66430)$ describe the raw real and imaginary coordinates; they
are not physical polarization counts.

Let $D_0=\iota^\dagger C_R\iota$ and $J_R=\iota^\dagger A$.
Completing the square gives
\begin{align}
 I_{R,\mathrm{red}}^{(2)}={}&\tfrac12\langle s,D_0s\rangle
 +\langle s,J_Ru\rangle+\tfrac12\langle u,Mu\rangle\nonumber\\
 &-\tfrac12\langle Fs+B_0u,W^{-1}(Fs+B_0u)\rangle.
 \label{eq:schur}
\end{align}
Every adjoint ending in the base equation includes fibre integration.
The remaining distortion time form is nondegenerate on its quotient.
These statements determine a local primary recovery, not a closed
physical domain or its boundary gauge charges.

\section{The metric Hessian and the boundary qualification}
\label{sec:boundary}

\subsection{Pure-base variation and acceleration}
For the natural field \eqref{eq:background} put $K=(11a+c)/2$ and define
\[
 F_{ij}=R(g)_{ij}^Th+hR(g)_{ij},\qquad
 \|F\|^2=h^{ik}h^{jl}D_h(F_{ij},F_{kl}).
\]
This contraction is indefinite. Direct adapted-connection contraction gives
\begin{align}
 \Scal(G_Y)&=-10+h^{ij}\Ric(g)_{ij}-\tfrac14\|F\|^2,
 \nonumber\\
 \Ric_Y(r,r)&=-\tfrac14,\nonumber\\
 I(g,T_*(g))&=\int\mu(h)
 \left(C_*+K h^{ij}\Ric(g)_{ij}-\tfrac K4\|F\|^2\right)\dd x\dd h.
 \label{eq:natural-action}
\end{align}
Here $C_*$ is independent of $g$; natural mass and cubic contractions
are constant. The possible curvature correction to the first-order
natural density cancels. The coordinate certificate checks the quadratic
identity in all 54 curvature components and 18 coordinate-jet controls
\cite{MetricHessian}.

For $L_i=\delta\Gamma_i[u]$, $F_1=\delta F$, and
$Q^{ij}=\int_V\mu(h)h^{ij}\dd h$, the Hessian is
\begin{align}
 M[u,u]={}&2K\int_X Q^{ij}

 &-\frac K2\int_{X\times V}\mu(h)\|F_1\|^2\dd x\dd h.
 \label{eq:metric-hessian}
\end{align}
This includes orders two and four. For homogeneous time dependence the
change $t=t'-y(h,\ddot u)$ cancels third-time mixing. Retaining every
covariant fibre and time-connection term in the zero-coordinate-time
operator $D_{\mathrm{conn}}$, the primary acceleration source is
$N^T(A_2-D_{\mathrm{conn}}y-Vy)$.

The boundary successor \cite{Boundary} checks a complete 37-dimensional

\begin{align}

All 1369 polarized Gram entries are checked, including cross terms.

If a boundary completion admitted these metric coordinates and velocities,

Those are substantive domain hypotheses. The following calculation shows

\subsection{Raw boundary form and a declared polarization}
Write $T=O+E$ by Clifford parity. Let $K^\mu$ be the raw derivative matrix
in $T^TK^\mu\nabla_\mu T/2$. Its bulk coefficient is
$E^\mu=(K^\mu-(K^\mu)^T)/2$, but its boundary one-form is
$T^TK(n)\delta T/2$, with oriented conormal density $n$.
For example, with $o=e^0\gamma_0$ and $e=e^0\gamma_0\gamma_4$,
\begin{equation}
 K^4(o,e)=0,\qquad K^4(e,o)=-2,\qquad E^4(o,e)=1.
 \label{eq:rawboundary}
\end{equation}
Replacing $K$ by its skew bulk coefficient would lose boundary data.
Add the explicit polarization term
\begin{equation}
 B_{\mathrm{pol}}=-\tfrac12\int_{\partial V}O^TK_{OE}(n)E.
 \label{eq:polarization}
\end{equation}
The derivative density then has form $E^TE_{EO}^\mu\nabla_\mu O$,
and its remaining boundary one-form is
$E^TE_{EO}(n)\delta O$.
Impose the odd natural perturbation $o=O-O_*(g)=0$ at the fibre boundary,
leaving the even perturbation free. The induced metric variation of
$O_*(g)$ contributes to the base equation and is not discarded.
This is a specified additional boundary choice, not a source-selected
physical law. It affects lower metric orders, not \eqref{eq:acceleration}.
Because $y$ is even, compensation preserves $o=0$ as a boundary condition;
that alone says nothing about its preservation under evolution.

\subsection{The compatibility chain restricts momentum}
After compensation and primary recovery write
$t'=\cR s$, where $\cR=\iota-NW^{-1}F$ is local and contains no
homogeneous metric acceleration. Define operators from the reduced action by
\begin{equation}
 \dot s=\cT s+\mathcal U\ddot u,\qquad
 \cB s=(P_{\mathrm{odd}}\cR s)|_{\partial V}.
 \label{eq:generator}
\end{equation}
Smooth solutions require, with $a_u=\ddot u$ and $j_u=\dddot u$,
\begin{align}
 C_0&:\quad \cB s=0,\nonumber\\
 C_1&:\quad \cB(\cT s+\mathcal Ua_u)=0,\nonumber\\
 C_2&:\quad \cB(\cT^2s+\cT\mathcal Ua_u+\mathcal Uj_u)=0.
 \label{eq:compatibility}
\end{align}
At $s=0$ the original compensated even equation reads
$E_{EO}(\dd t)\dot o=V_{EE}ya_u$.
Its five-polarization Gram is
\begin{equation}
 y^TV_{EE}y=-\frac9{256}\delta G_F,
 \qquad \delta=\kappa-\frac{52a+4c}{3},
 \qquad -0.401<\delta/\kappa<-0.399.\label{eq:boundaryrank}
\end{equation}
It is injective on that block and persists on the small patch. Thus
$\cB\mathcal U$ has rank five. Nonzero acceleration with $s=0$ fails
$C_1$; at $s=a_u=0$, nonzero jerk fails $C_2$.
If $\mathcal A$ is the integrated nondegenerate acceleration matrix,
then at fixed lower jets $\delta P_u=-\mathcal A\delta j_u$.
Consequently $C_2$ restricts five metric momenta at fixed distortion jets.
Arbitrary independent variation of $P_u$ does not remain in this domain.

A permanent distortion-free fibre collar is stronger still: the collar
bulk equation forces $A_3\dddot u+A_2\ddot u=0$, and the independent
receiver types imply $\ddot u=0$ in the tested homogeneous traceless class.
Neither statement establishes positive energy. They identify the limits
of the earlier free-momentum proof. The real theorem below uses a
different symmetry sector and does not need independently variable metric
momenta.
