\begin{abstract}
We study a specified bosonic realization of the first Geometric Unity action
on the bundle of Lorentzian metrics over a four-dimensional base. The
canonical connection metric remains curved when the observing metric is
flat. An intrinsic grade-one/two distortion yields an exact real local
stationary background on two certified opposite-coupling branches.
Differentiating the action before restriction gives the complete mixed
metric response, an algebraic distortion reduction, and the pure metric
Hessian. On each branch the constraint-reduced quadratic Noether energy
is unbounded above and below in both coefficient-conjugation packets.
For the real packet, a finite reflection character annihilates the full
fibre-integrated metric force. Compact oscillatory witnesses then satisfy
every finite-order formal compatibility condition of an explicitly declared
boundary polarization. This avoids the independent-metric-momentum
hypothesis required by an earlier conditional acceleration argument.
Bare observing-section evaluation, even augmented by the entire smooth
section germ, does not carry the energy. We retain the preceding flat
controls and distinguish these results from ordinary Yang--Mills,
complex Chern--Simons, scalar positivity donors, and separate cutoff
quantum/PDE controls. Exact coefficient calculations and five scoped Lean
deductions support identified parts of the argument. The energy theorems
concern a selected classical quadratic form and formal solution jets;
they do not construct a closed physical evolution, a quantum spectrum,
or a universal obstruction to every GU completion.
\end{abstract}
