\section{Evidence, reproducibility, and remaining physical questions}
\label{sec:verification}

The derivations have three distinct forms of support. Exact finite
calculations determine coefficient matrices, root isolation, determinant
nonvanishing, and rational sign bounds. Analytic arguments use naturality,
symmetry, compact support, integration by parts, and local differential
recovery to obtain the continuum statements. A small Lean module proves
five explicitly conditional deductions. These layers are complementary;
a successful finite script is not itself a proof of a global domain theorem.

The sparse Clifford implementation evaluates exterior/Clifford products
and differentiated action rows. The independent implementation realizes
fourteen gamma matrices on the 128 occupation states of seven fermionic
modes: positive generators are creation plus annihilation, negative ones
creation minus annihilation. It checks the Clifford relations on every
state and uses normalized matrix trace. It imports neither the sparse
engine nor a coefficient bank for the designated witness calculations.
This corroborates the finite energy inputs and selected Gram entries;
it is not an outside review of the analytic proofs.

The module \path{Lean/GUFormalization/NguyenPositivityKernels.lean}
proves: a witness refutes a universal failure predicate; a positive
conserved form excludes a supplied growing eigenvector; equal observation
images with a nonzero symmetric action difference prevent descent; and
the specified necessary three-block polynomial system has no real solution,
including its zero-coupling case. The first deduction has no axioms;
the others report only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and
\texttt{Quot.sound}. An external exact probe checks the action-to-polynomial
coefficients. Neither the full geometry and variational calculus nor
Witten's construction nor the curved energy theorems have been formalized
by that module.

All sixteen local Python certificates and the named Lean build passed
in the corpus integration at revision \texttt{f18b19f6}.
That integration also reconciled the upstream quantum/PDE sequence and
retained the existing contribution contracts. Earlier receipt hashes pin
the text verified at their own dates; later scope updates do not
retroactively change those runs. The evidence map and integration receipt
are \path{explorations/nguyen-gu-critique/README.md} and
\path{lab/process/native-i1b-corpus-integration.json}.
The manuscript's typesetting receipt separately checks compilation,
references, glyphs, and layout; it certifies no mathematics.

\subsection{The physical question after the energy calculation}
The direct result blocks semiboundedness of the specified quadratic form
on any domain that retains the constructed cores. The real restriction,
ordinary local boundary compatibility in the declared polarization,
overall sign reversal, and bare smooth observation do not remove that
result. The following objects remain unconstructed: a source-selected
physical admissibility rule, its propagated coupled constraint domain,
an observed energy map on that domain, and an interacting quantum
Hamiltonian with its physical pairing.

A future admissibility rule must identify precisely which witnesses it
excludes and why the intended dynamics preserve that exclusion.
A different background or source-supported action is a different
mathematical problem, not a sign change of the present theorem.
Fermionic closure, nonlinear evolution, and the source's actual shielding
mechanism also require separate derivations. In repository terms,
LT-SM8 and LT-GR6b remain \texttt{NEEDS}; the physical BV/BRST pairing
condition \texttt{INHERITANCE\_BRIDGE} remains undischarged;
SC-META-53 remains \texttt{UNCERTAIN}. These labels report scope, not
additional hypotheses or proof steps. No canon, source polarity, prediction
credit, or publication status is advanced by this manuscript.
